01 / The Construction

Begin With the Physical Picture

Particles are patterns of one field in a higher-dimensional space, like structures confined to layers of different dimensionality. The proposed electron has six spatial dimensions and the tau ten. Our three-dimensional view is a projection of this larger geometry. At least ten dimensions are proposed; the total is unknown. The current working geometry is flat, and the dynamics that confines and sustains the patterns remains under investigation.
