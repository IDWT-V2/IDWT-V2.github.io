02 / The research record

Follow the work as it develops

Read the latest calculations together with the assumptions, corrections, and open questions that give them context.

  • Five-dimensional CP3 selection and spectral discrimination

    A five-dimensional spinor-bivector model also selects a CP3 minimum family, but its exact spectral sum rule excludes the six-dimensional singlet-triplet gap within the linear-bivector operator class.

  • Reciprocal cylindrical molecule

    A 6D electron, relative angular source, moving finite cylindrical nuclei, and flexible nuclear-plane rotors form one translation-invariant reciprocal Hamiltonian with verified energy and Noether-charge conservation.

  • Nuclear-plane rotor

    A finite-inertia rank-three nuclear plane coupled reciprocally to a 6D electron preserves its projector constraints, energy, visible momentum, and combined rotational charge while undergoing nontrivial orientation response.

  • Localized polarization stationarity

    With the same localized Gaussian envelope and magnetic kernel, uniform five-dimensional polarization has a nonzero spatial first variation whereas six-dimensional pure polarization is stationary.

  • Frozen magnetic texture test

    A frozen-protocol test admits spatial polarization textures into the existing six-dimensional magnetic kernel and treats negative energy curvature as a failure of the uniform preparation.
