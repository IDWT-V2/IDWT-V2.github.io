01 / The Construction

Begin With the Physical Picture

Particles are patterns of one field in a higher-dimensional space, like structures confined to layers of different dimensionality. The proposed electron has six spatial dimensions and the tau ten. Our three-dimensional view is a projection of this larger geometry. The physical premise is ten spatial dimensions plus time. The current working geometry is flat, and the dynamics that confines and sustains the patterns remains under investigation.
