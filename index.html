01 / The construction

Begin with the physical picture

All sectors are nested submanifolds of one infinite-dimensional physical manifold. Every dimension is an ordinary macroscopic spatial dimension. An electron occupies six of the tau's ten dimensions and is lower-dimensional, or “flat,” relative to it. That relationship gives the tau no internal division, just as sharing three dimensions with us gives the electron none.

02 / The research record

Follow the work as it develops

Read the latest calculations together with the assumptions, corrections, and open questions that give them context.

  • Spatial polarization textures

    Allowing polarization to vary across a bound particle can preserve its scalar density while creating a full spatial kinetic residual, whose omitted components must be solved before the texture is a physical mode.

  • Spatial displacement action

    Full spatial deformation gives the exact constant-gradient action and a common leading interaction between two polarized displacement waves, with the complementary field retained explicitly.

  • Soft spatial current response

    The full spatial translation and velocity tangents determine the low-frequency electric current, its polarization correction, and the magnetic moment of a conditionally gauged scalar Dirac profile.

  • Soft polarized scattering

    Retaining the photon spatial phase and the target's complete polarization multiplet gives the low-frequency forward spin response in the conditionally gauged scalar Dirac model.

  • Retarded displacement response

    The full spatial quadrupole Hessian gives a positive frequency-dependent interaction kernel, with a derived logarithmic soft correction and outgoing radiation into modes of the same field.
