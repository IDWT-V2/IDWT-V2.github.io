Research context

  • Confining oscillator domain

    The harmonic spatial operator has a closed energy form, compact resolvent, and an explicit eigenbasis whose Gaussian tails justify specified boundary limits.

  • Fibre-integrated stress

    Integrating a conserved stress tensor over ordinary spatial fibres yields a lower-depth balance law with explicit metric, boundary-flux, and sampling terms.

  • Gaussian contact profiles

    Normalized Gaussian spatial densities give an exact contact overlap, force, and covariance rule under marginalization onto shared coordinates.
