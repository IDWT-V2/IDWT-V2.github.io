IDWT's physical premise is one infinite-dimensional manifold with all sectors nested as submanifolds. Particle depth counts macroscopic spatial dimensions; matrix and field-component counts in a calculation add no physical dimensions.

Calculation record

Verify geometric projections, spin bilinears and moment fixed points.

These are mathematical controls for stated models, not fitted particle
couplings or an observational neutrino test.

Research context

  • Nested moment coupling

    The dot-square kernel on centered isotropic nested subspaces gives a minimum-depth Gram matrix of full rank, despite factorized bare coefficients.

  • Five-dimensional spinor bilinears

    Explicit five-dimensional Clifford matrices have an invariant antisymmetric bilinear and a quaternionic reality map; allowed interaction terms additionally depend on field statistics and the full symmetry representation.

  • Hopf fibre and invariant channel projectors

    Circle averaging selects a definite fibre weight, while a colour singlet requires invariance under the full colour action; explicit projectors separate these conditions.

  • Self-consistent functional

    A reusable harmonic mean-field closure with explicit norm and variation assumptions.

Interpret numerical checks within the assumptions and limitations documented in these notes.
