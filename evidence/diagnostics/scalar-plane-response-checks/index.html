Research context

  • Spatially invariant scalar-null fields

    The minimal massless spatial Dirac module admits complete kinetic scalar-null classes at every odd spatial depth, while even depths have no such constant linear class; this parity result does not select a particle or a physical charge.

  • Full spatial response of a scalar-null plane

    A specified scalar-null Dirac plane has an exact full spatial spectrum, a sharp density-dependent linear stability threshold and a directional scalar response that cannot be replaced by a universal static overlap.

  • Moving nonlinear scalar planes and their response

    The local scalar Dirac equation has continuous nonnull traveling-plane branches whose complete self-consistent response has a covariant closed form, with proper density, conjugate modes and the conserved norm treated explicitly.
