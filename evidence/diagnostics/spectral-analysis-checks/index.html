Research context

  • Bounded spectral perturbations

    A bounded self-adjoint perturbation preserves compact resolvent, bounds every ordered eigenvalue shift, and preserves isolated spectral-cluster rank under an explicit gap condition.

  • Continuum mode memory

    Eliminating a coupled continuum produces an exact memory kernel, with exponential decay available only under additional weak-coupling and spectral assumptions.

  • Fixed-null calibration

    A specified null model permits exact combination of independent match probabilities, explicit treatment of probability floors, and honest finite-simulation uncertainty.

  • Gaussian mode geometry

    Normalized positive Gaussian profiles have an exact parameter-space metric determined by their mean and covariance, with zero Berry curvature for this real family.

  • Isotropic projection fractions

    An isotropically prepared spatial direction has a beta-distributed squared projection onto a fixed subspace, with explicit moments and observer-relative momentum bookkeeping.

  • Simplex spectral zeta

    Explicit analytic continuation of the one-eigenvalue-per-index simplex tower gives its value and derivative at zero, including the normalization dependence of its zeta determinant.

  • Spectral analysis tools

    Exact tools for recurrence, continuum response, spectral robustness, Gaussian profile geometry, simplex regularization, and statistical calibration.

  • Spectral survival recurrence

    Pure-point spectral evolution has a positive mean return weight and arbitrarily accurate recurrences, while an integrable continuous spectral density permits decay of the return amplitude.

  • Diagnostic catalog

    Reproduction commands and scope descriptions for every local finite diagnostic and helper.
