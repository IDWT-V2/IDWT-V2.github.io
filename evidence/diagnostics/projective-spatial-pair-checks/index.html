projective_spatial_pair_checks.py

IDWT's physical premise is one infinite-dimensional manifold with all sectors nested as submanifolds. Particle depth counts macroscopic spatial dimensions; matrix and field-component counts in a calculation add no physical dimensions.

Calculation record

Independent checks of a specified reciprocal spatial Dirac pair.

The projective geometry, scalar kernel and separate current families are
assumptions. These checks do not derive particle assignments or measured
interaction strengths. Every field component in the stated spatial symmetry
class, including complex phases and the higher two-form, is retained.

Research context

  • Reciprocal spatial Dirac pair

    The same spatial moment interaction drives reciprocal deformation of complete complex Dirac profiles in an exact CP3–CP5 symmetry class, including a generated two-form component and phase-dependent changes in interaction overlap.

  • One spatial kernel on a projective chain

    A single bounded spatial moment kernel gives reciprocal full-spinor Dirac equations on specified nested projective sectors, with conserved norms, global evolution and no independent interaction coefficient for each pair.

  • Diagnostic catalog

    Reproduction commands and scope descriptions for every local finite diagnostic and helper.
