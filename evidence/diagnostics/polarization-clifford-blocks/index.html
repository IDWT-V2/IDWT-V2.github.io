IDWT's physical premise is one infinite-dimensional manifold with all sectors nested as submanifolds. Particle depth counts macroscopic spatial dimensions; matrix and field-component counts in a calculation add no physical dimensions.

Calculation record

Complete local Clifford-orbit reduction for two physical momentum directions.

All matrix components are represented. Orbits are indexed by subsets of
Clifford generators outside the chosen radial/transverse plane. At odd depth
the unused generator completes the matrix representation, not physical space.
