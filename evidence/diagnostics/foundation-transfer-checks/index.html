Research context

    Centering the auxiliary scalar moment functional yields an explicit translation-covariant self-binding model with zero net self-force.

    A barrier continued cylindrically has no route around it through the added coordinates; a separated Hamiltonian reduces each transverse mode to a longitudinal crossing problem.

    Cylindrical function lifts require a projection, and exact inheritance of dynamics additionally requires an invariant domain and an intertwining operator relation.
