IDWT's physical premise is one infinite-dimensional manifold with all sectors nested as submanifolds. Particle depth counts macroscopic spatial dimensions; matrix and field-component counts in a calculation add no physical dimensions.

Calculation record

Checks for exact mode counting, evaluation, and spectral bounds.

Research context

  • Round-sphere Dirac counts

    The round-sphere Dirac spectrum has a dimension-dependent spinor multiplicity, giving two times the simplex count on S3 and four times it on S5.

  • Simplex band level moments

    Uniform counting over a cumulative oscillator band gives exact mean and variance of the excitation level, without selecting a physical occupation or mass correction.

  • Simplex heat trace

    The tower with one eigenvalue per simplex index has an explicit heat-trace bound and convergence domain, distinct from the spatial dimension of a sector operator.

  • Weighted mode evaluation

    A weighted coefficient norm bounds point evaluation exactly when the corresponding inverse-weighted evaluation sum is finite; counts alone do not establish that condition.

  • Normalized oscillator modes

    Explicit normalized modes and density overlaps supply calculational inputs without identifying them with mixing amplitudes.

  • Diagnostic catalog

    Reproduction commands and scope descriptions for every local finite diagnostic and helper.
