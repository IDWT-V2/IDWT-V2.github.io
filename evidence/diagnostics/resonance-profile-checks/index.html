Research context

  • Chiral pseudoscalar mass term

    A specified leading chiral action makes pseudoscalar mass squared proportional to light-quark masses, with the response scale left as matched input.

  • Coulomb exponential profiles

    Normalized exponential profiles give exact Coulomb integrals, a two-particle variational bound, and finite-range contact estimates.

  • Cyclic resonance spectrum

    A nearest-neighbour amplitude ring has exact Fourier resonances, paired levels, and a calculable response to alternating couplings.

  • Dipole dispersion response

    Second-order dipole coupling yields an attractive inverse-sixth-power energy with a coefficient fixed by the specified excitation response.

  • Oscillator rotation branching

    Harmonic-polynomial decomposition gives exact angular multiplicities for a flat oscillator shell and its restriction to an observer-selected spatial three-plane.

  • Radial confinement bounds

    The radial spatial measure fixes the centrifugal term, while a Hardy bound gives an explicit sufficient condition excluding levels below a saturating threshold.

  • Resonance and profile tools

    Tools for Coulomb profiles, angular functions, cyclic and quartic resonance, dispersion response, radial bounds, and pseudoscalar symmetry protection.

  • Resonant quartet dynamics

    A conservative quartic amplitude interaction has exact action invariants and a detuning-controlled exchange law under specified pump preparation.

  • Zonal hybrid geometry

    Spherical-harmonic addition gives exact directed-state overlaps and conditional trigonal, tetrahedral, and octahedral orthogonality.

  • Diagnostic catalog

    Reproduction commands and scope descriptions for every local finite diagnostic and helper.
