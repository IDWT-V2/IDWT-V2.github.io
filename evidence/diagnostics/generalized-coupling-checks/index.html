generalized_coupling_checks.py

IDWT's physical premise is one infinite-dimensional manifold with all sectors nested as submanifolds. Particle depth counts macroscopic spatial dimensions; matrix and field-component counts in a calculation add no physical dimensions.

Calculation record

Finite checks of generalized coupling on nested spatial sectors.

No empirical coupling constants are fitted. Independent energy variations,
time evolution, spatial quadrature and basis changes test conditional models.
Run directly; this diagnostic writes no files and remains active under -O.

Research context

  • Canonical coupling normalization

    Kinetic Gram matrices fix normalized transition vertices and conserved generators, separating charge universality from profile-dependent interaction strength.

  • Common field response

    Eliminating stable response modes of the same field gives a Gram correction to the direct coupling, with exact reciprocity and a proportional-profile criterion for rank one.

  • Generalized sector coupling

    A common interaction on arbitrary nested sectors yields reciprocal responses, normalized vertices, and testable coupling relations, with neutrinos as a channel-specific control.

  • Nested moment coupling

    The dot-square kernel on centered isotropic nested subspaces gives a minimum-depth Gram matrix of full rank, despite factorized bare coefficients.

  • Nested sector reciprocity

    Compatible spatial marginals and their dual pullbacks give reciprocal coupling equations on arbitrary nested sectors, with exact composition along a chain.

  • Neutrino channel control

    A neutrino coupling test requires vanishing electric charge, a colour singlet, and nonzero weak vertices, with low-energy suppression distinguished from a zero interaction.
