Research context

  • Angular kernel parity

    The dot-square angular integral has an exact finite spectrum, while its density-sourced Hartree potential acts by multiplication and preserves parity.

  • Anomaly-free charge family

    The one-family anomaly equations admit a hypercharge and B-minus-L family when a right-handed neutrino is included; selecting observed charges needs additional conditions.

  • Colour singlet criterion

    Vanishing mean colour charge is weaker than the singlet condition; the positive total quadratic Casimir distinguishes them exactly.

  • Dirac heat curvature

    The untwisted spatial Dirac heat kernel has local relative coefficient minus scalar curvature over twelve, with an explicit round-sphere spectral benchmark.

  • Green function marginal

    Full flat-fibre integration of a normalized Laplace Green function gives the lower-dimensional Green function, while restriction to a slice does not.

  • Interaction operator tools

    Self-contained tools for spatial projection, localized profiles, interaction operators, and conservation laws.

  • Projected bilocal variation

    Varying an exchange-symmetric interaction between projected densities retains the channel projector and one kernel convolution in each mean-field term.

  • Diagnostic catalog

    Reproduction commands and scope descriptions for every local finite diagnostic and helper.
