IDWT's physical premise is one infinite-dimensional manifold with all sectors nested as submanifolds. Particle depth counts macroscopic spatial dimensions; matrix and field-component counts in a calculation add no physical dimensions.

Research context

  • Complete spatial polarization data

    Complete spatial Clifford tensors retain the amplitudes and phases of a polarized field, exposing information that a circulation tensor captures at six dimensions but can omit at ten.

  • Equal-current spatial polarizations

    Complete ten-dimensional fields can share density and circulation yet respond differently to the same six-dimensional partner, because reciprocal rotation dynamics retains polarization information beyond the current tensor.

  • Spatial polarization textures

    Allowing polarization to vary across a bound particle can preserve its scalar density while creating a full spatial kinetic residual, whose omitted components must be solved before the texture is a physical mode.
