Research context

  • Generalized sector coupling

    A common interaction on arbitrary nested sectors yields reciprocal responses, normalized vertices, and testable coupling relations, with neutrinos as a channel-specific control.

  • Rotations of nested spatial sectors

    A rotation preserving every fixed member of the nested spatial chain has a restricted block structure, while rotating the whole chain requires the moving embeddings in the projected dynamics.

  • Spatial transport of a neutral doublet

    The two neutral polarizations of a balanced five-dimensional rotation carry a nonzero traceless geometric curvature when its physical null axis varies, with an exact spatial kinetic-energy decomposition.

  • Polarized photon transition strength

    Summing all spinor response directions gives an exact two-polarization transition matrix for a simple spatial Pauli probe, including helicity-sensitive zeros and a nonzero total-strength bound at every tested depth.

  • Reciprocal scattering of spatial modes

    A solved conservative wave model turns a two-sided spatial overlap into reflection and transmission, separating protected polarization mismatch from narrow resonances and coherent transparency.

  • Rotation-neutral spatial modes

    A common rotation of two spatial planes has an exact neutral spinor subspace, while three equal planes have none; full spatial motion and dynamical selection determine whether this can become a particle coupling rule.

  • Transition-density coupling

    The complete derivative of a bilocal density interaction pairs both objects' transition densities, giving reciprocal mode vertices and an exact signed quadrupole matching law from the existing spatial kernel.

    Reproduction commands and scope descriptions for every local finite diagnostic and helper.
