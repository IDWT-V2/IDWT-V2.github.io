Coordinate containment vs coupling filter. These are two distinct necessary conditions. Coordinate containment (§0.8) governs which forces can reach a particle: a force couples to a particle only if the particle's sector contains that force's sector. The coupling filter governs what happens within that sector: even with support in the sector, the particle's own sector geometry may project the relevant representation to zero. Both must hold for coupling to occur.
