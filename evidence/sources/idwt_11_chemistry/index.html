Proposals of such deviations are not open items — they are excluded.

🔶 Scope condition. The theorem covers the established potentials. Whether the cross-sector kernel contact terms contribute any residue at chemical energy scales is the one stated caveat. §6.3 bounds it.

6.2 What 6D chemistry consists of

Given §6.1, the content of 6D chemistry is three things, none of which is a deviating lab number:

The falsifiable content of the framework is carried by its derived numbers — the mass spectrum, mixing angles, δCP\delta_{CP}δCP​, Σmν\Sigma m_\nuΣmν​, and the Part 5 falsifier catalogue — not by new 6D phenomena in chemistry.
  • Frontier. Independent of observables, the orbit trajectory geometry in the CP3\mathbb{CP}^3CP3 coordinates (Part 8 §14.4) remains the structural frontier. 6D chemistry is the foundation and the ontology.
