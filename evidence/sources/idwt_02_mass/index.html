10b. g22g_{22}g22​ — the kernel back-reaction fixed-point 🔶

Status: 🔶 Structurally motivated (state-counting). g22=p2q/2=722.5g_{22} = p^2q/2 = 722.5g22​=p2q/2=722.5 is a multiplicity count: the product of available Dirac-eigenspace dimensions across the d=3d=3d=3 sector (two kernel legs → p2p^2p2) and the d=4d=4d=4 sector (one leg → q), with the 1/2 from the ξ↔ξ′\xi\leftrightarrow\xi'ξ↔ξ′ symmetry of the two-body kernel. It is not a kernel matrix element. Testing whether a genuine (ξ⋅ξ′)2(\xi\cdot\xi')^2(ξ⋅ξ′)2 trace yields p2q/2p^2q/2p2q/2 (files/idwt.py STEP 2d): the literal Tr[G232+G242G_{23}^2 + G_{24}^2G232​+G242​] is additive (∼ p2+qp^2+qp2+q, the wrong structure for a product), and the actual (ξ⋅ξ′)2(\xi\cdot\xi')^2(ξ⋅ξ′)2 overlap returns ⟨r2⟩\langle r^2\rangle⟨r2⟩ magnitudes that scale as the mode index nnn — O(1), orders of magnitude below the multiplicity product. p2qp^2qp2q appears only as a trace of the identity over rank-(p,p,q) eigenspaces, where the multiplicities are the input. So g22g_{22}g22​ is a state-count (IDOS), on the same footing as the CKM formula: combinatorial, empirically exact, mechanism = counting. The 1/2 and the leg-counting are kernel-motivated; the magnitude is a count of eigenstates, not a dynamical overlap. A count is not a foreign mechanism here — counting is the framework's native currency: the mass law itself is the integrated density of states (Part 8 §5, Theorem S1), and the CKM formula is a relative state count of the Born-ratio form (Part 3 §0.9, Part 1 §2.3). What remains open (🔶) is the operator-level counting theorem — a dynamical selection operator whose spectral-projector trace makes the eigenspace dimensions an output rather than an input.

The d=3d=3d=3 self-coupling g33g_{33}g33​ is fixed by the intra-sector confinement condition geff(ns,3)=g33/S(ns,3)≈1g_{\rm eff}(n_s,3) = g_{33}/S(n_s,3) \approx 1geff​(ns​,3)=g33​/S(ns​,3)≈1 (Part 2 §8). The d=2d=2d=2 sector has no self-confinement — the W is massive but not confined in the quark sense. Its self-coupling g22g_{22}g22​ is instead fixed by the cross-sector back-reaction: the requirement that the d=2d=2d=2 vacuum amplitude is consistent with the d=3d=3d=3 and d=4d=4d=4 quark sector structures at the level ns=4n_s=4ns​=4.
