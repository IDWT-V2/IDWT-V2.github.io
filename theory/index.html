Theory

Physical spacetime has infinitely many ordinary spatial dimensions plus time. Matter occupies up to ten spatial dimensions; all remaining spatial directions are unbound for every particle. Particles are patterns of one field with different dimensional involvement.

The current working geometry is flat. Older curved and projective constructions retain their stated mathematical assumptions. Read the updated sector case for their relation to the current picture.
