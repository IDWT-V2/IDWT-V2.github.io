60. Generic Dirac-induced geometry does not uniquely predict the required curvature coefficient

The remaining induced-action route was tested against the standard heat-kernel structure of a Dirac one-loop effective action.

For a Dirac-type operator, integrating out the fermion gives schematically

Γ1loop=−log⁡det⁡D=−12log⁡det⁡D2. \Gamma_{\rm 1loop} =-\log\det D =-\frac12\log\det D^2. Γ1loop​=−logdetD=−21​logdetD2.

The heat-kernel expansion of D2D^2D2 generates local geometric invariants. On a smooth bulk manifold these include terms of the schematic form

∫ddxg [c0Λd+c1Λd−2R+c2R2+c3RabRab+⋯ ]. \int d^dx\sqrt g\, \left[ c_0\Lambda^d +c_1\Lambda^{d-2}R +c_2R^2 +c_3R_{ab}R^{ab} +\cdots \right]. ∫ddxg​[c0​Λd+c1​Λd−2R+c2​R2+c3​Rab​Rab+⋯].

For manifolds with boundaries, interfaces, or hypersurface structures, the corresponding heat-kernel coefficients also contain local boundary/extrinsic invariants involving the second fundamental form and mean curvature, schematically

∫∂Mdd−1xh [b0Λd−1+b1Λd−2H+b2H2+b3KabKab+⋯ ]. \int_{\partial M}d^{d-1}x\sqrt h\, \left[ b_0\Lambda^{d-1} +b_1\Lambda^{d-2}H +b_2H^2 +b_3K_{ab}K^{ab} +\cdots \right]. ∫∂M​dd−1xh​[b0​Λd−1+b1​Λd−2H+b2​H2+b3​Kab​Kab+⋯].

This establishes an important positive result:

However, it does not close the V2 hadronic calculation by itself.

The local heat-kernel terms are also the terms that carry ultraviolet divergences and require local counterterms. Their renormalized coefficients depend on the underlying bare geometric action, regulator/renormalization prescription, and renormalization conditions unless a more fundamental UV principle fixes them.

Consequently, a generic induced action does not uniquely predict a finite coefficient multiplying

H,H2,KabKab, H, \qquad H^2, \qquad K_{ab}K^{ab}, H,H2,Kab​Kab,

from the fermion determinant alone.

Therefore it cannot, without an additional V2 principle, force

Thus

generic one-loop induced geometry supplies the right operator class but not a unique V2 curvature normalization. \boxed{ \text{generic one-loop induced geometry supplies the right operator class but not a unique V2 curvature normalization.} } generic one-loop induced geometry supplies the right operator class but not a unique V2 curvature normalization.​

60.1 Consequence for the factor-two problem

At this point the independent extrinsic Dirac contribution remains mathematically viable, but neither the current classical V2 action nor a generic fermion-induced effective action fixes its magnitude uniquely.

The factor-two closure therefore requires at least one additional principle of one of the following kinds:

  1. a UV/master geometric action fixing the projector/embedding counterterms;
  2. a symmetry or topological quantization condition fixing the relevant curvature coefficient;
  3. a normalization theorem relating the embedding curvature directly to the already fixed contact scale μ3g33\mu_3g_{33}μ3​g33​;
  4. a different independent operator contribution whose coefficient is already fixed by existing V2 data.

Without one of these, the strongest derived band-blind constriction contribution remains

εconstr=154.808228 MeV, \boxed{ \varepsilon_{\rm constr}=154.808228\ {\rm MeV}, } εconstr​=154.808228 MeV,​

while

2εconstr=309.616456 MeV 2\varepsilon_{\rm constr}=309.616456\ {\rm MeV} 2εconstr​=309.616456 MeV

remains an unexplained but unusually precise numerical clue.

61. Topological quantization fixes discrete sectors, not the absolute embedding curvature scale

The V2 topological modules were checked for a quantization condition capable of fixing the mean curvature required by the independent extrinsic Dirac contribution.

62. Scope of the existing covariance normalization theorem

The final existing normalization candidate was checked carefully.

Section 34 already establishes a useful conditional result. If the dimensionless normal quadratic constriction block is the normal/extrinsic component of the same projected scalar contact vertex that defines the depth-three mass normalization, then covariance of that single scalar vertex requires

Q34phys=μ3Q^34. Q_{34}^{\rm phys}=\mu_3\widehat Q_{34}. Q34phys​=μ3​Q​34​.

g342g44=g33, \frac{g_{34}^2}{g_{44}}=g_{33}, g44​g342​​=g33​,

Estiffness=μ3g33. \boxed{ E_{\rm stiffness}=\mu_3g_{33}. } Estiffness​=μ3​g33​.​

This is a real normalization theorem conditional on the same-scalar-vertex identification. It is the reason the first constriction contribution can be normalized without a new dimensionful constant.

However, the independent codimension-one mean-curvature term has a different operator origin. It arises from decomposition of the Dirac kinetic geometry,

D4=γ(n)(∇n+D3−32H), D_4 =\gamma(n)\left(\nabla_n+D_3-\frac32H\right), D4​=γ(n)(∇n​+D3​−23​H),

Thus the existing V2 normalization result has the precise scope

same scalar vertex⟹E34=μ3, \boxed{ \text{same scalar vertex}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_{34}=\mu_3, } same scalar vertex⟹E34​=μ3​,​

The investigation has now separated two distinct channels cleanly:

  1. Scalar normal-singlet constriction εconstr=149μ3g33=154.808228 MeV, \varepsilon_{\rm constr} =\frac{14}{9}\mu_3g_{33} =154.808228\ {\rm MeV}, εconstr​=914​μ3​g33​=154.808228 MeV, under the already stated scalar-channel and assumed frequency-rescaling assumptions.

To obtain the observed light-nucleon bulk term from their sum would require

EH=εconstr, E_H=\varepsilon_{\rm constr}, EH​=εconstr​,

The factor-two investigation has therefore reached a genuine boundary of the presently written V2 theory rather than an overlooked algebraic identity.

63.1 Remaining freedom is now explicit

The missing geometric closure can be stated more concretely than before. A completed V2 embedding theory must determine at least the combination

∇nΨ∣Q3−γ(n)−1Mψ \boxed{ \nabla_n\Psi|_{Q_3} -\gamma(n)^{-1}M\psi } ∇n​Ψ∣Q3​​−γ(n)−1Mψ​

Without that equation, the mean curvature is not an eigenvalue prediction but one member of an underdetermined lower-order operator combination.

This strengthens the previous conclusion: the unresolved hadronic factor is not hiding in the standard hypersurface Dirac identity. It is tied to the still-missing normal/projector dynamics of the V2 embedding.
