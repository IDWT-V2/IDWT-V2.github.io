43. Factor-of-two correction to the reciprocal zero-point trace

The earlier interpretation of the reciprocal zero-point trace contained a hidden factor-of-two ambiguity that becomes clear once the coordinate harmonic kernel is quantized directly.

For a common oscillator gap

E0,4+E0,3=72ε. E_{0,4}+E_{0,3} =\frac72\varepsilon. E0,4​+E0,3​=27​ε.

Distributed over the three surviving directions, the reciprocal zero-point energy per direction is instead

Therefore the consistent reciprocal energy factor is

not 7/37/37/3, when applied to an oscillator energy quantum.

45. No-go for obtaining the missing factor two from reciprocity or Dirac doubling

The remaining discrepancy is exactly a factor of two, so the obvious possibilities are two reciprocal variations, two chiral halves, or particle/antiparticle doubling. The written V2 action does not support any of these as an independent second copy of the same scalar confinement energy.

45.1 The scalar quartic variation already removes the combinatorial factor two

The leading scalar self-interaction is

Lsc=−κd2Jd2,Jd=ΨˉdΨd. \mathcal L_{\rm sc} =-\frac{\kappa_d}{2}J_d^2, \qquad J_d=\bar\Psi_d\Psi_d. Lsc​=−2κd​​Jd2​,Jd​=Ψˉd​Ψd​.

Varying with respect to Ψˉd\bar\Psi_dΨˉd​ gives

∂Lsc∂Ψˉd=−κd2 2JdΨd=−κdJdΨd. \frac{\partial\mathcal L_{\rm sc}}{\partial\bar\Psi_d} =-\frac{\kappa_d}{2}\,2J_d\Psi_d =-\kappa_dJ_d\Psi_d. ∂Ψˉd​∂Lsc​​=−2κd​​2Jd​Ψd​=−κd​Jd​Ψd​.

Thus the two identical factors in Jd2J_d^2Jd2​ are already compensated by the conventional 1/21/21/2 in the action.

There is therefore no additional factor of two available from saying that the self-contact acts in two reciprocal directions. Counting both variations of the same quadratic interaction would double-count one action term.

45.2 Cross-depth reciprocity is also one action term

The V2 cross-depth interaction is written as

S34=g34∫dt I34. S_{34}=g_{34}\int dt\,I_{34}. S34​=g34​∫dtI34​.

Functional differentiation with respect to the depth-three and depth-four densities gives two equations of motion, but both are variations of this same interaction functional. They do not represent two additive copies of its energy.

Hence

reciprocity does not provide the missing factor 2. \boxed{ \text{reciprocity does not provide the missing factor }2. } reciprocity does not provide the missing factor 2.​

45.3 Chiral halves are not two independent scalar energies

A Dirac spinor may decompose into two chiral halves, but the scalar bilinear

ΨˉΨ \bar\Psi\Psi ΨˉΨ

is a single Lorentz-scalar contraction of the full field. The V2 scalar contact normalization μd\mu_dμd​ is obtained after the scalar spinor contraction BdB_dBd​ has already been performed.

Consequently, multiplying the resulting scalar energy again by two merely because the underlying Dirac fibre has two chiral components would count spinor components already included in BdB_dBd​.

Likewise, positive- and negative-frequency solutions of one Dirac operator are not simultaneous additive rest-energy contributions to one one-particle state.

Therefore neither chirality nor particle/antiparticle structure supplies a justified universal doubling.

47.1 Decisive bookkeeping question

The graph factor two is physically legitimate only if a symmetric pair-contact action produces one induced one-particle rest-energy operator on each endpoint, while the underlying interaction action itself remains counted once.

The required distinction is

one pair energy functional≠one endpoint self-energy only. \boxed{ \text{one pair energy functional} \neq \text{one endpoint self-energy only}. } one pair energy functional=one endpoint self-energy only.​

For a symmetric pair functional SijS_{ij}Sij​, variation with respect to particle iii and particle jjj may produce two endpoint equations of motion without implying that SijS_{ij}Sij​ itself should be summed twice in the total Hamiltonian.

The next test is to derive this endpoint-versus-pair distinction explicitly for the V2 cross-depth contact functional.

48. Exact K3K_3K3​ collective-mode test: endpoint incidence is not an independent doubling
