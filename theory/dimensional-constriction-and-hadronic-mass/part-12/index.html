54. Tetrahedral/projective geometry does not fix the extrinsic curvature scale

The tetrahedral and projective observer modules were checked for a dimensionful embedding scale that could determine the mean curvature required by the extrinsic Dirac term.

They do derive exact dimensionless geometry. In particular, the centred occupation simplex gives

ni⋅nj=−13, \mathbf n_i\cdot\mathbf n_j=-\frac13, ni​⋅nj​=−31​,

and hence the regular tetrahedral angle

θtet=arccos⁡(−1/3). \theta_{\rm tet}=\arccos(-1/3). θtet​=arccos(−1/3).

The projective observer map similarly fixes normalized occupations and the covariant form

W=Rf(q)eiϕ, W=R f(q)e^{i\phi}, W=Rf(q)eiϕ,

with the squared-radius coordinate-readout choice

f(q)=q. f(q)=\sqrt q. f(q)=q​.

But the overall spatial scale

R R R

remains an external response/embedding scale in that construction. The projective geometry fixes the normalized shape, not the absolute physical radius.

Therefore neither the centred-simplex geometry nor the projective observer map implies

∣H∣=2827ℏcμ3g33. |H|=\frac{28}{27\hbar c}\mu_3g_{33}. ∣H∣=27ℏc28​μ3​g33​.

In particular, the regular tetrahedral angle does not determine a unique circumradius or mean curvature until an external length normalization is supplied.

Thus

the projective/tetrahedral modules fix shape but not the dimensionful curvature scale needed for EH. \boxed{ \text{the projective/tetrahedral modules fix shape but not the dimensionful curvature scale needed for }E_H. } the projective/tetrahedral modules fix shape but not the dimensionful curvature scale needed for EH​.​

The extrinsic Dirac term remains a legitimate independent operator channel, but its magnitude is still unclosed.

The remaining V2-native possibility is that the scale enters through the embedding projector itself: derivatives of PdP_dPd​, the second fundamental form, or the normal connection may carry a dimensionful norm fixed by the same master operator that fixes the sector mass scales. That requires an explicit projector/embedding equation; it cannot be inferred from simplex angles alone.

55. The sector projector is kinematically specified but not dynamically closed

The projector/embe_ding audit sharpens the status of the extrinsic-curvature route.

The V2 projected-principal-symbol theorem assumes

id:Qd↪M∞ i_d:Q_d\hookrightarrow M_\infty id​:Qd​↪M∞​

with an orthogonal tangent--normal splitting and a smooth zeroth-order orthogonal projector

Pd:Σ∞∣Qd→Σd. P_d:\Sigma_\infty|_{Q_d}\to\Sigma_d. Pd​:Σ∞​∣Qd​​→Σd​.

The sector operator is defined by

Dd=PdD∞∥Pd. \mathcal D_d=P_d\mathcal D_\infty^{\parallel}P_d. Dd​=Pd​D∞∥​Pd​.

Because PdP_dPd​ is order zero, derivatives of PdP_dPd​ enter only in lower-order connection and algebraic terms. This is exactly where normal connection, second-fundamental-form, and extrinsic-curvature contributions can appear.

The cross-sector construction likewise says that PdP_dPd​ is a "dynamically defined projection" and identifies the decisive object

PdΓA∇APe. P_d\Gamma^A\nabla_A P_e. Pd​ΓA∇A​Pe​.

However, the current V2 files do not provide a dynamical action for PdP_dPd​ itself. In particular, no equation of the form

δSδPd=0, \frac{\delta S}{\delta P_d}=0, δPd​δS​=0,

or an equivalent evolution/minimization equation for

∇Pd,IId,Hd, \nabla P_d, \qquad \mathrm{II}_d, \qquad H_d, ∇Pd​,IId​,Hd​,

is presently written.

Therefore the phrase "dynamically defined projection" is currently a physical requirement or construction target rather than a completed field equation.

This has a direct consequence for the hadronic closure problem:

V2 permits an extrinsic Dirac contribution, but does not yet determine its absolute curvature scale. \boxed{ \text{V2 permits an extrinsic Dirac contribution, but does not yet determine its absolute curvature scale.} } V2 permits an extrinsic Dirac contribution, but does not yet determine its absolute curvature scale.​

Hence the candidate equality

cannot presently be derived from projector dynamics because those dynamics have not yet been specified.

This is not a numerical failure. It identifies a concrete missing equation in the V2 master geometry.

56. Full cross-depth marginalization fixes weight, not embedding curvature

The bound-within-free-without contact theorem was checked for an indirect constraint on the embedding scale.

For a depth-aaa density ρa(x)\rho_a(x)ρa​(x) and a higher-depth density ρb(x,y)\rho_b(x,y)ρb​(x,y), the theorem gives

Iab=∫Ξaρa(x)ρˉb→a(x) dμa(x), I_{ab} =\int_{\Xi_a}\rho_a(x)\bar\rho_{b\to a}(x)\,d\mu_a(x), Iab​=∫Ξa​​ρa​(x)ρˉ​b→a​(x)dμa​(x),

with

ρˉb→a(x)=∫Fb/aρb(x,y) dμF(y). \bar\rho_{b\to a}(x) =\int_{F_{b/a}}\rho_b(x,y)\,d\mu_F(y). ρˉ​b→a​(x)=∫Fb/a​​ρb​(x,y)dμF​(y).

For normalized nonnegative ρb\rho_bρb​,

∫Ξaρˉb→a dμa=1. \int_{\Xi_a}\bar\rho_{b\to a}\,d\mu_a=1. ∫Ξa​​ρˉ​b→a​dμa​=1.

This proves that the complete higher-depth marginal weight survives projection. It also proves reciprocal functional response from a single interaction action.

But the theorem explicitly leaves the physical magnitude to

It therefore does not determine a physical radius, normal localization length, second fundamental form, or mean curvature.

Consequently the cross-depth marginal theorem cannot supply the missing equation

full marginalization fixes contact normalization, not the dimensionful embedding scale. \boxed{ \text{full marginalization fixes contact normalization, not the dimensionful embedding scale.} } full marginalization fixes contact normalization, not the dimensionful embedding scale.​

Thus the unresolved hadronic closure is now traceable to the absence of dynamics for the embedding/projector geometry rather than to an ambiguity in fibre normalization.

57. Exact master-geometry gap: no action presently determines the embedding curvature

A corpus-wide search was made for a geometric action capable of determining the sector embedding or projector dynamically. The current V2 files contain no written term of the following standard types:

  • Einstein--Hilbert or scalar-curvature action of the spatial metric;
  • sigma-model or Grassmannian kinetic term for the sector projector, such as tr⁡(∇Pd∇Pd)\operatorname{tr}(\nabla P_d\nabla P_d)tr(∇Pd​∇Pd​);
  • Nambu--Goto, Polyakov, or other embedding-volume action;
  • Willmore or extrinsic-curvature/bending functional;
  • second-fundamental-form energy;
  • variational equation for the embedding metric, projector, or normal geometry.

This makes the current status precise.

V2 already specifies the kinematics of sector restriction:

Qd↪M∞,Dd=PdD∞∥Pd, Q_d\hookrightarrow M_\infty, \qquad \mathcal D_d=P_d\mathcal D_\infty^{\parallel}P_d, Qd​↪M∞​,Dd​=Pd​D∞∥​Pd​,

and it proves that derivatives of the zeroth-order projector enter the lower-order algebraic/connection sector. It therefore has a mathematically valid location for extrinsic curvature and normal-connection contributions.

What it does not yet possess is the action whose variation fixes those lower-order geometric fields.

Consequently there is presently no V2 Euler--Lagrange equation capable of deriving

Hd,IId,∇Pd, H_d, \qquad \mathrm{II}_d, \qquad \nabla P_d, Hd​,IId​,∇Pd​,

or an absolute embedding radius from the already fixed mass and coupling data.

For the hadronic problem this means that the attractive closure condition

32ℏc ∣H3∣=149μ3g33 \frac{3}{2}\hbar c\,|H_3| =\frac{14}{9}\mu_3g_{33} 23​ℏc∣H3​∣=914​μ3​g33​

is not contradicted by existing V2, but neither is it derived by it.

The required mean curvature would be

∣H3∣=2827ℏcμ3g33≈0.52301761 fm−1. \boxed{ |H_3| =\frac{28}{27\hbar c}\mu_3g_{33} \approx0.52301761\ {\rm fm}^{-1}. } ∣H3​∣=27ℏc28​μ3​g33​≈0.52301761 fm−1.​

Thus the unresolved factor-two problem has been reduced to a sharply defined missing piece of the master theory:

derive the sector embedding/projector dynamics and compute its lower-order Dirac curvature term. \boxed{ \text{derive the sector embedding/projector dynamics and compute its lower-order Dirac curvature term.} } derive the sector embedding/projector dynamics and compute its lower-order Dirac curvature term.​

This is a stronger statement than merely saying that a numerical coefficient is unknown. The current V2 action is incomplete specifically in the geometric sector that would decide whether the independent extrinsic contribution exists at the required scale.
