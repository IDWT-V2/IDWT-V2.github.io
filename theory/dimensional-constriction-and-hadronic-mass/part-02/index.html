Thus the dimensional mechanism is better interpreted as a relation between confinement scale and mass than as a universal fixed mass increment.

7. The pseudoscalar exception

The pion and kaon do not follow the vector/baryon pattern.

Their inferred constriction energies are much smaller:

ϵπ≃66 MeV, \epsilon_\pi\simeq66\ {\rm MeV}, ϵπ​≃66 MeV,

ϵK≃199 MeV. \epsilon_K\simeq199\ {\rm MeV}. ϵK​≃199 MeV.

This is expected to be a decisive test rather than a nuisance. In QCD the light pseudoscalar mesons are special because their masses are controlled strongly by chiral symmetry and its breaking. A purely geometric confinement contribution cannot simply be added to them in the same way as a generic constituent-energy estimate.

Therefore any V2 hadron model based on dimensional constriction must contain a channel-dependent cancellation or symmetry protection for pseudoscalar states. Without such a mechanism, the model fails on the pion.

8. What the calculation establishes

The 4→34\to34→3 oscillator constriction gives an exact relative statement under fixed total action:

ω3=43ω4. \boxed{ \omega_3=\frac43\omega_4. } ω3​=34​ω4​.​

When the large nucleon mass excess is interpreted as the resulting three-dimensional confinement energy, the proton and neutron independently imply

ℏω3≃309.5 MeV \boxed{ \hbar\omega_3\simeq309.5\ {\rm MeV} } ℏω3​≃309.5 MeV​

and

ℏω4≃232.2 MeV. \boxed{ \hbar\omega_4\simeq232.2\ {\rm MeV}. } ℏω4​≃232.2 MeV.​

This corresponds to

RN≃0.850 fm, \boxed{ R_N\simeq0.850\ {\rm fm}, } RN​≃0.850 fm,​

which is remarkably close to the physical proton-size scale.

The broader spectrum rules out the stronger claim that every valence quark simply receives the same 309.5309.5309.5 MeV additive mass. Instead the data require a state-dependent constriction scale

Rh, R_h, Rh​,

or equivalently a state-dependent projected oscillator frequency.

The natural V2 interpretation is therefore

Mh=∑imqi+Econstriction[Ψh]+Espin/flavor[Ψh], \boxed{ M_h =\sum_i m_{q_i} +E_{\rm constriction}[\Psi_h] +E_{\rm spin/flavor}[\Psi_h], } Mh​=i∑​mqi​​+Econstriction​[Ψh​]+Espin/flavor​[Ψh​],​

where the first large term is generated by the loss of one accessible spatial degree of freedom and the remaining splittings come from the projected polarization, spin, flavor, and curvature structure of the hadronic state.

9. Why this is compatible with known hadronic mass generation

The light-quark current masses account for only a small fraction of the nucleon rest mass. Most nucleon mass is dynamical field and confinement energy rather than the sum of the light current-quark masses.

The dimensional-constriction hypothesis does not contradict that fact. It proposes a geometric origin for the dynamical energy:

four-dimensional quark mode⟶three-dimensional constrained mode⟶higher oscillator frequency⟶large rest-energy contribution. \text{four-dimensional quark mode} \longrightarrow \text{three-dimensional constrained mode} \longrightarrow \text{higher oscillator frequency} \longrightarrow \text{large rest-energy contribution}. four-dimensional quark mode⟶three-dimensional constrained mode⟶higher oscillator frequency⟶large rest-energy contribution.

In a complete V2 derivation, this contribution must emerge from the projected master Dirac operator rather than being inserted phenomenologically.

10. Connection with the CKM obstruction

The CKM calculation independently showed that the 4→34\to34→3 transition cannot be a passive partial trace alone. A pure loss map cannot generate the observed u→su\to su→s transition because it cannot increase cumulative mode degree.

The hadronic-mass calculation points in the same direction. If dimensional reduction constricts the oscillator rather than merely deleting a coordinate, the projected operator must redistribute action among the surviving directions.

Therefore the same unresolved cross-sector operator

R34 \mathcal R_{34} R34​

may be responsible for both

mode redistribution / flavor mixing \text{mode redistribution / flavor mixing} mode redistribution / flavor mixing

and

confinement energy / hadronic mass generation. \text{confinement energy / hadronic mass generation}. confinement energy / hadronic mass generation.

This makes R34\mathcal R_{34}R34​ a central dynamical object rather than a small correction.

11. The missing first-principles calculation

The remaining task is sharply defined.

Starting from the master operator,

D34=P3D∞P4, D_{34}=P_3D_\infty P_4, D34​=P3​D∞​P4​,

one must derive its decomposition into surviving tangential modes and the eliminated normal mode. If the four-dimensional oscillator action is conserved during projection, the operator should contain a term whose expectation value satisfies

⟨H3⟩=43⟨H4⟩ \langle H_3\rangle =\frac43\langle H_4\rangle ⟨H3​⟩=34​⟨H4​⟩

for the isotropic ground configuration.

The hadronic mass contribution would then be

Econstriction=⟨Ψh∣H3−P3H4P3∣Ψh⟩. \boxed{ E_{\rm constriction} = \langle\Psi_h| H_3-P_3H_4P_3 |\Psi_h\rangle. } Econstriction​=⟨Ψh​∣H3​−P3​H4​P3​∣Ψh​⟩.​

A successful calculation must then derive, rather than fit, the state-dependent length scale RhR_hRh​ or its equivalent oscillator stiffness from the d=3d=3d=3 projected geometry.

For the nucleon the target is

RN≈0.85 fm \boxed{ R_N\approx0.85\ {\rm fm} } RN​≈0.85 fm​

or equivalently

ℏω4≈232 MeV,ℏω3≈310 MeV. \boxed{ \hbar\omega_4\approx232\ {\rm MeV}, \qquad \hbar\omega_3\approx310\ {\rm MeV}. } ℏω4​≈232 MeV,ℏω3​≈310 MeV.​

If the projected V2 operator independently produces that scale, the bulk of the nucleon mass follows without using the nucleon mass as an input.

If it does not, the numerical agreement found here remains dimensional phenomenology rather than a derivation.

12. Status

The present result can be stated compactly:

4→3 oscillator constriction at assumed frequency rescaling⟹ω→43ω. \boxed{ 4\to3\ \text{oscillator constriction at assumed frequency rescaling} \Longrightarrow \omega\to\frac43\omega. } 4→3 oscillator constriction at assumed frequency rescaling⟹ω→34​ω.​

Applied to the observed nucleon mass excess, this yields an inferred pre-constriction scale

ℏω4≃232 MeV \boxed{ \hbar\omega_4\simeq232\ {\rm MeV} } ℏω4​≃232 MeV​

and radius

RN≃0.850 fm, \boxed{ R_N\simeq0.850\ {\rm fm}, } RN​≃0.850 fm,​

which closely matches the physical nucleon-size scale.

The wider hadron spectrum shows that dimensional constriction can plausibly account for the dominant confinement-scale mass but cannot by itself explain all hadron masses. Spin, flavor, chiral structure, and state-dependent geometry remain required.

The next decisive calculation is therefore not another numerical fit. It is the explicit spectrum of the normal/extrinsic part of

P3D∞P4. P_3D_\infty P_4. P3​D∞​P4​.

That spectrum must simultaneously address the hadronic confinement scale and the mode-changing transitions already demanded by CKM.
