75. Three-body relative coordinates: an effective domain, not a new particle depth

75.1 The observed-coordinate kinematics

For three positions in a common observed R3\mathbb R^3R3, an orthogonal Jacobi transformation separates the center coordinate and two vectors ρ,λ∈R3\rho,\lambda\in\mathbb R^3ρ,λ∈R3. Their relative configuration space is R6\mathbb R^6R6.

With the Euclidean metric and R2=∣ρ∣2+∣λ∣2, R^2=|\rho|^2+|\lambda|^2, R2=∣ρ∣2+∣λ∣2, a fixed-RRR surface is S5S^5S5. Mass-weighted Jacobi coordinates can be used when the kinetic metric requires them. These statements concern three-body configuration coordinates, not six spatial directions available to a single particle.

75.2 Native depth is not configuration-space dimension

V2 assigns the constituent fields to their native manifolds QdQ_dQd​. Observing three positions in a common three-space does not prove that the native higher-depth coordinates disappear from the dynamics.

Consequently neither the six Jacobi coordinates nor their S5S^5S5 angular surface can be identified with the electron's depth-six manifold or the neutrino's depth-five sector merely by matching dimensions or manifold names. Such an identification needs an explicit map of operators, metrics, bundles, measures, and constraints.

75.3 Degeneracy is not automatically a frequency

For scalar harmonics on the unit round S5S^5S5, −ΔS5Yℓ=ℓ(ℓ+4)Yℓ,hℓ=(ℓ+55)−(ℓ+35). -\Delta_{S^5}Y_{\ell}=\ell(\ell+4)Y_{\ell}, \qquad h_\ell=\binom{\ell+5}{5}-\binom{\ell+3}{5}. −ΔS5​Yℓ​=ℓ(ℓ+4)Yℓ​,hℓ​=(5ℓ+5​)−(5ℓ+3​). The Laplacian eigenvalue and its degeneracy are different quantities. Neither is automatically the spectrum of the composite Dirac/contact frequency generator. The previous degeneracy scan therefore did not test a specified V2 baryon operator.

75.4 A conditional valence-space representation

After deriving an appropriate observed-coordinate reduction, a three-quark valence model could use a physical subspace of L2(Rρ,λ6)⊗Λ3Ucolor⊗Hspin/flavor, L^2(\mathbb R^6_{\rho,\lambda}) \otimes\Lambda^3U_{\rm color} \otimes\mathcal H_{\rm spin/flavor}, L2(Rρ,λ6​)⊗Λ3Ucolor​⊗Hspin/flavor​, with the total fermionic permutation constraint enforced. This is a possible effective representation, not a derivation of the complete baryon Hilbert space. Native coordinates and other dynamical channels must either be retained or eliminated with their induced spectral terms.

75.5 Predict the whole baryon pole

The primary target is the complete rest-frequency ratio zB=ωB/ωe. \boxed{z_B=\omega_B/\omega_e.} zB​=ωB​/ωe​.​ The earlier approximately 309309309--310310310 MeV quantity was a per-constituent allocation of a residual after subtracting chosen quark contributions. It is not, without an additional decomposition theorem, a separate measured eigenfrequency that the operator must contain.

75.6 Corrected status

The Jacobi construction provides useful observed three-body kinematics. It does not establish a new particle sector, a baryon-to-neutrino identification, or the elimination of native coordinates. Passive projection alone does not generate a larger frequency. A physical frequency shift requires the interacting generator or a specified change of dynamics; exact elimination represents that interaction rather than replacing it with dimension counting.

Applying them to the full master operator additionally requires specified domains, a stationary background where needed, and a controlled treatment of its infinite-dimensional channels.

76.4 What has and has not been solved

The static/spectral Schur distinction, the projection-versus-frequency error, and the singlet pair coefficient have been resolved exactly. A normalized eigenvector may be used to represent a mode, but no measured field amplitude is used as an input.

Electron anchoring fixes the unit. It does not, by itself, determine every dimensionless interaction coefficient. The next calculation must derive the remaining blocks and their spectral dependence from the V2 dynamics, retaining induced terms rather than guessing an integer near the desired mass. No numerical baryon-frequency prediction is claimed by this revision.

A local-power action attains curvature ratio two but has only one frozen bound level. The sech-power classification extends the positive finite-width kernel construction to all exponents p>1p>1p>1 and proves Rcurv≤2R_{\rm curv}\le\sqrt2Rcurv​≤2​ in this exact finite-measure convolution family.
