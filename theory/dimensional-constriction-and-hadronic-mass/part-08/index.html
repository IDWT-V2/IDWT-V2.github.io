34. Projection-normalization lemma for the constriction energy

The remaining normalization question can be stated sharply:

If the 4→34\to34→3 constriction quadratic form is projected into the same normalized depth-three scalar-contact channel that defines the ordinary d=3d=3d=3 mass operator, what dimensionful coefficient multiplies its dimensionless coupling matrix?

The answer is fixed by the existing projection theorem, provided the constriction term belongs to that same scalar channel.

34.1 The depth-three projected scalar channel has one energy coefficient

The V2 scalar self-contact projection is

P3,N(κ3J3δ[z])P3,N=μ3PNδ[z]PN, P_{3,N} \left(\kappa_3J_3\delta_{[z]}\right) P_{3,N} = \mu_3P_N\delta_{[z]}P_N, P3,N​(κ3​J3​δ[z]​)P3,N​=μ3​PN​δ[z]​PN​,

with

μ3=κ3R3B3. \mu_3=\kappa_3R_3B_3. μ3​=κ3​R3​B3​.

After the normalized spatial profile and scalar spinor contraction have been performed, μ3\mu_3μ3​ is the coefficient of the scalar rest-energy endomorphism on the depth-three mode fibre.

The remaining mode dependence is dimensionless:

PNδ[z]PN=S(N+1,3)∣z;N⟩⟨z;N∣. P_N\delta_{[z]}P_N =S(N+1,3)|z;N\rangle\langle z;N|. PN​δ[z]​PN​=S(N+1,3)∣z;N⟩⟨z;N∣.

Thus, before inserting a particular occupied band, the normalized depth-three scalar channel has the operator form

H3scalar=μ3 H^3, \boxed{ H_3^{\rm scalar} =\mu_3\,\widehat H_3, } H3scalar​=μ3​H3​,​

where H^3\widehat H_3H3​ is dimensionless.

34.2 The coupling matrix is dimensionless

The conditional fixed-point equation is

(Md⋆)2gdd=me2g66. \frac{(M_d^\star)^2}{g_{dd}} = \frac{m_e^2}{g_{66}}. gdd​(Md⋆​)2​=g66​me2​​.

Since both numerators have dimensions of energy squared, the self-couplings gddg_{dd}gdd​ are dimensionless. The rank-one relation

g342=g33g44 g_{34}^2=g_{33}g_{44} g342​=g33​g44​

therefore also makes g34g_{34}g34​ dimensionless.

Consequently the reduced constriction stiffness

gcon=g342g44=g33 g_{\rm con} =\frac{g_{34}^2}{g_{44}} =g_{33} gcon​=g44​g342​​=g33​

is dimensionless.

34.3 Projection of the quadratic constriction block

Suppose the normal/extrinsic 4→34\to34→3 block, after the fourth coordinate is constrained and the non-mode factors are projected onto the same normalized depth-three scalar channel, has dimensionless quadratic form

Q^34=12(xy)(g33g34g34g44)(xy). \widehat Q_{34} =\frac12 \begin{pmatrix}x&y\end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} g_{33}&g_{34}\\ g_{34}&g_{44} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}. Q​34​=21​(x​y​)(g33​g34​​g34​g44​​)(xy​).

Because the ggg matrix and normalized amplitudes are dimensionless, the projected physical quadratic operator must have the form

Q34phys=E34Q^34 Q_{34}^{\rm phys} =E_{34}\widehat Q_{34} Q34phys​=E34​Q​34​

for some energy coefficient E34E_{34}E34​.

If this block is not a new independent interaction channel, but is the normal/extrinsic component of the same projected scalar contact whose tangential depth-three component is already normalized by μ3\mu_3μ3​, covariance of the single projected scalar vertex requires

E34=μ3. \boxed{ E_{34}=\mu_3. } E34​=μ3​.​

Introducing a different coefficient would amount to introducing an additional sector-dependent scalar normalization not present in the assumed single-vertex master action.

Therefore, under the same projected scalar vertex hypothesis,

Q34phys=μ3Q^34. \boxed{ Q_{34}^{\rm phys} =\mu_3\widehat Q_{34}. } Q34phys​=μ3​Q​34​.​

The constrained-versus-relaxed energy penalty is then

ΔQ34phys=12μ3g342g44x2=12μ3g33x2. \Delta Q_{34}^{\rm phys} = \frac12\mu_3 \frac{g_{34}^2}{g_{44}}x^2 = \frac12\mu_3g_{33}x^2. ΔQ34phys​=21​μ3​g44​g342​​x2=21​μ3​g33​x2.

Hence the natural physical stiffness unit of the constriction block is

μ3gcon=μ3g33. \boxed{ \mu_3g_{\rm con} =\mu_3g_{33}. } μ3​gcon​=μ3​g33​.​

34.4 No simplex-factor double counting

The factor S(n,3)S(n,3)S(n,3) is the eigenvalue generated when the localized projective point contact is evaluated on a particular cumulative mode band.

The hadronic constriction term above is instead the band-independent normal stiffness of the projected scalar vertex before selecting a quark excitation level. Multiplying it by another S(n,3)S(n,3)S(n,3) would therefore count the occupied-ray contact multiplicity a second time and would destroy the observed nearly universal bulk nucleon contribution per valence quark.

The appropriate energy unit for the universal normal block is thus μ3\mu_3μ3​, not an individual quark mass μ3S(n,3)\mu_3S(n,3)μ3​S(n,3).

34.5 Consequence

Combining the projection lemma with the previous Schur-complement result gives

Estiffness=μ3g342g44=μ3g33. \boxed{ E_{\rm stiffness} =\mu_3\frac{g_{34}^2}{g_{44}} =\mu_3g_{33}. } Estiffness​=μ3​g44​g342​​=μ3​g33​.​

Canonical reciprocal normalization then gives the central well depth

A34=76μ3g33. \boxed{ A_{34} =\frac76\mu_3g_{33}. } A34​=67​μ3​g33​.​

Gaussian density curvature gives

ℏωcommon=2A34=73μ3g33. \boxed{ \hbar\omega_{\rm common} =2A_{34} =\frac73\mu_3g_{33}. } ℏωcommon​=2A34​=37​μ3​g33​.​

Fixed-action 4→34\to34→3 redistribution finally gives

ℏω3=43ℏωcommon=289μ3g33=309.616456 MeV. \boxed{ \hbar\omega_3 =\frac43\hbar\omega_{\rm common} =\frac{28}{9}\mu_3g_{33} =309.616456\ {\rm MeV}. } ℏω3​=34​ℏωcommon​=928​μ3​g33​=309.616456 MeV.​

35. Exact status of the closure

The normalization problem is therefore closed conditional on one identifiable V2 statement:

R34normal is a component of the same normalized scalar master vertex that defines μ3. \boxed{ \mathcal R_{34}^{\rm normal} \text{ is a component of the same normalized scalar master vertex that defines }\mu_3. } R34normal​ is a component of the same normalized scalar master vertex that defines μ3​.​

Under that statement, no additional dimensional constant is allowed in the projected normal quadratic block, and the μ3\mu_3μ3​ prefactor follows from the existing sector normalization.

If instead R34normal\mathcal R_{34}^{\rm normal}R34normal​ is an independent derivative, tensor, or nonlocal vertex with its own projected coefficient, then a new energy normalization is allowed and the hadronic formula remains conditional.

So the remaining question is no longer numerical or algebraic. It is operator-theoretic:

Is the constriction block contained in the scalar self-contact vertex, or is it an independent master-action vertex? \boxed{ \text{Is the constriction block contained in the scalar self-contact vertex, or is it an independent master-action vertex?} } Is the constriction block contained in the scalar self-contact vertex, or is it an independent master-action vertex?​

36. Scalar normal-singlet route: the confinement block need not be in R34\mathcal R_{34}R34​

The operator-classification issue can be resolved without reclassifying the remainder R34\mathcal R_{34}R34​.

The V2 coupling geometry already states that when a lower-depth operator is a scalar under the normal group Spin(q)Spin(q)Spin(q), integration over the normal fibre retains the Spin(q)Spin(q)Spin(q)-singlet part of the higher-dimensional current.

For a 4→34\to34→3 reduction the normal fibre is one-dimensional, so a scalar lower-depth contact has a surviving normal-singlet channel under fibre integration.

The leading cross-sector decomposition is

PdD∞Pe=Idepol⊗Cdemode+Rde, P_dD_\infty P_e = \mathcal I_{de}^{\rm pol} \otimes \mathcal C_{de}^{\rm mode} + \mathcal R_{de}, Pd​D∞​Pe​=Idepol​⊗Cdemode​+Rde​,

where Cdemode\mathcal C_{de}^{\rm mode}Cdemode​ is the pushed-forward coherent contact and Rde\mathcal R_{de}Rde​ contains extrinsic, derivative, and nonlocal corrections.

Therefore the order-zero scalar constriction energy does not have to be assigned to R34\mathcal R_{34}R34​. It can arise from the scalar-singlet component of the leading pushed-forward coherent contact:

C34scalar⊂I34singlet⊗C34mode. \boxed{ \mathcal C_{34}^{\rm scalar} \subset \mathcal I_{34}^{\rm singlet} \otimes \mathcal C_{34}^{\rm mode}. } C34scalar​⊂I34singlet​⊗C34mode​.​

This matters because the leading coherent-contact channel is precisely the one for which the scalar self-contact projection supplies the normalized energy coefficient μ3\mu_3μ3​.

The operator picture can therefore be separated consistently:

bulk hadronic constriction energy⟵C34scalar \boxed{ \text{bulk hadronic constriction energy} \longleftarrow \mathcal C_{34}^{\rm scalar} } bulk hadronic constriction energy⟵C34scalar​​

while

generation-changing CKM structure⟵R34 and/or nontrivial mode contact. \boxed{ \text{generation-changing CKM structure} \longleftarrow \mathcal R_{34} \text{ and/or nontrivial mode contact}. } generation-changing CKM structure⟵R34​ and/or nontrivial mode contact.​

This removes the apparent conflict between the hadronic normalization argument and the explicit definition of R34\mathcal R_{34}R34​ as an extrinsic/derivative/nonlocal remainder.

37. Conditional closure of the bulk nucleon scale

Under the following V2-native assumptions:

  1. the 4→34\to34→3 scalar constriction is the normal-singlet component of the leading coherent-contact channel;
  2. that scalar channel inherits the same normalized depth-three contact-energy unit μ3\mu_3μ3​;
  3. the coupling block obeys the rank-one relation g342=g33g44g_{34}^2=g_{33}g_{44}g342​=g33​g44​;
  4. the reciprocal normalized phase-space stiffness contributes the inverse symmetric factor 7/67/67/6;
  5. the localized normal mean field is self-consistent and Gaussian near its minimum;
  6. total isotropic oscillator action is conserved during the 4→34\to34→3 constriction;

the chain is

g342g44=g33, \frac{g_{34}^2}{g_{44}} =g_{33}, g44​g342​​=g33​,

A34=76μ3g33, A_{34} =\frac76\mu_3g_{33}, A34​=67​μ3​g33​,

ℏωcommon=2A34=73μ3g33, \hbar\omega_{\rm common} =2A_{34} =\frac73\mu_3g_{33}, ℏωcommon​=2A34​=37​μ3​g33​,

and

ℏω3=43ℏωcommon=289μ3g33. \boxed{ \hbar\omega_3 =\frac43\hbar\omega_{\rm common} =\frac{28}{9}\mu_3g_{33}. } ℏω3​=34​ℏωcommon​=928​μ3​g33​.​

With the current V2 values,

ℏω3=309.616456 MeV. \boxed{ \hbar\omega_3 =309.616456\ {\rm MeV}. } ℏω3​=309.616456 MeV.​

The remaining gap to a theorem is concentrated in two assumptions rather than an unexplained numerical coefficient:

  • prove that the scalar normal-singlet pushed-forward contact inherits exactly the μ3\mu_3μ3​ normalization;
  • derive the self-consistent normal profile, or replace the Gaussian approximation with the exact profile and compute its curvature factor.

The g33g_{33}g33​ factor, the 7/67/67/6 reciprocal normalization, and the 4/34/34/3 redistribution no longer need to be inserted ad hoc once these operator identifications are adopted.
