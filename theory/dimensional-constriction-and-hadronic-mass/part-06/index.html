28. Reciprocal energy-trace test of the 7/37/37/3 coefficient

The failure of the normalized amplitude bilinear leaves one distinct possibility: perhaps the relevant cross-sector normalization is an additive energy trace, rather than an amplitude overlap.

For an isotropic oscillator,

E0,d=d2ℏωd. E_{0,d}=\frac d2\hbar\omega_d. E0,d​=2d​ℏωd​.

If the incoming d=4d=4d=4 and outgoing d=3d=3d=3 carriers are first compared at one common frequency ω\omegaω, then

E0,4=2ℏω,E0,3=32ℏω. E_{0,4}=2\hbar\omega, \qquad E_{0,3}=\frac32\hbar\omega. E0,4​=2ℏω,E0,3​=23​ℏω.

Their reciprocal sum normalized by the three-dimensional contribution is

E0,4+E0,3E0,3=2+3/23/2=73. \frac{E_{0,4}+E_{0,3}}{E_{0,3}} = \frac{2+3/2}{3/2} = \boxed{\frac73}. E0,3​E0,4​+E0,3​​=3/22+3/2​=37​​.

So the 7/37/37/3 factor does arise exactly from an additive zero-point energy trace evaluated at a common oscillator frequency.

However, this must be tested against the independently assumed frequency-rescaling constriction law. That law gives

ω3=43ω4. \omega_3=\frac43\omega_4. ω3​=34​ω4​.

Therefore after constriction

E0,3=32ℏω3=3243ℏω4=2ℏω4=E0,4. E_{0,3} =\frac32\hbar\omega_3 =\frac32\frac43\hbar\omega_4 =2\hbar\omega_4 =E_{0,4}. E0,3​=23​ℏω3​=23​34​ℏω4​=2ℏω4​=E0,4​.

The reciprocal post-constriction energy sum is consequently

E0,4+E0,3E0,3=2, \frac{E_{0,4}+E_{0,3}}{E_{0,3}} = \boxed{2}, E0,3​E0,4​+E0,3​​=2​,

not 7/37/37/3.

This gives a sharp consistency result:

73 is the reciprocal dimension trace only before frequency redistribution. \boxed{ \frac73 \text{ is the reciprocal dimension trace only before frequency redistribution.} } 37​ is the reciprocal dimension trace only before frequency redistribution.​

It cannot simultaneously be interpreted as the reciprocal post-constriction oscillator-energy normalization if the 4/34/34/3 assumed frequency-rescaling law is retained.

Therefore a valid derivation of

ΔE⊥=73μ3g33 \Delta E_\perp=\frac73\mu_3g_{33} ΔE⊥​=37​μ3​g33​

would have to show that μ3g33\mu_3g_{33}μ3​g33​ belongs to a common-frequency pre-constriction cross-sector energy functional, and that the subsequent 4/34/34/3 redistribution acts only afterward. The currently written V2 action does not yet establish that ordering or identify μ3g33\mu_3g_{33}μ3​g33​ as that common-frequency energy quantum.

This is more restrictive than the earlier normalization test. The 7/37/37/3 factor now has a mathematically natural source, but only in a very specific pre-constriction energy trace. It remains unproved that the V2 master operator realizes precisely that trace.

29. Structural test of the product μ3g33\mu_3g_{33}μ3​g33​

The 7/37/37/3 coefficient now has a mathematically natural interpretation as a reciprocal pre-constriction zero-point energy trace. That still leaves the second half of the proposed formula to justify:

ΔE⊥=?73μ3g33. \Delta E_\perp \stackrel{?}{=} \frac73\mu_3g_{33}. ΔE⊥​=?37​μ3​g33​.

The current V2 mass operator gives the depth-three contact scale

μ3=κ3R3B3, \mu_3=\kappa_3R_3B_3, μ3​=κ3​R3​B3​,

and the localized contact operator is explicitly interpreted as a rest-energy endomorphism.

The self-coupling g33g_{33}g33​ enters separately through the conditional fixed-point equation

(M3⋆)2g33=me2g66, \frac{(M_3^\star)^2}{g_{33}} = \frac{m_e^2}{g_{66}}, g33​(M3⋆​)2​=g66​me2​​,

which implies

μ3=meg33g66 \mu_3=m_e\sqrt{\frac{g_{33}}{g_{66}}} μ3​=me​g66​g33​​​

for the chosen depth-three reference mode.

A direct search of the present V2 action and companion derivations finds no term proportional to

g33μ3,g33κ3, g_{33}\mu_3, \qquad g_{33}\kappa_3, g33​μ3​,g33​κ3​,

or an equivalent linear product of the depth-three self-coupling with the projected contact-energy scale.

Therefore

μ3g33 \boxed{ \mu_3g_{33} } μ3​g33​​

is presently an algebraically constructible energy scale, but it is not an eigenvalue or interaction coefficient derived by the written master operator.

This sharpens the status of the nucleon formula.

The coefficient

73 \frac73 37​

But the identification

ℏωcommon=?μ3g33 \hbar\omega_{\rm common} \stackrel{?}{=}\mu_3g_{33} ℏωcommon​=?μ3​g33​

remains unsupported by the current V2 equations.

Thus the missing derivation has been reduced to one precise statement:

derive ℏωcommon=μ3g33 from the master action. \boxed{ \text{derive }\hbar\omega_{\rm common}=\mu_3g_{33} \text{ from the master action.} } derive ℏωcommon​=μ3​g33​ from the master action.​

If that identity follows independently, then the full chain becomes

ℏωcommon=μ3g33, \hbar\omega_{\rm common}=\mu_3g_{33}, ℏωcommon​=μ3​g33​,

ΔE⊥=73ℏωcommon=73μ3g33, \Delta E_\perp =\frac73\hbar\omega_{\rm common} =\frac73\mu_3g_{33}, ΔE⊥​=37​ℏωcommon​=37​μ3​g33​,

Without the first identity, however, the numerical success is not yet a V2 prediction.

30. Present stopping point

Inspection of the explicit projected master-action derivation confirms that the two quantities entering the close nucleon formula arise at different logical stages.

The scalar self-contact term is

Lsc=−κd2Jd2, \mathcal L_{\rm sc} =-\frac{\kappa_d}{2}J_d^2, Lsc​=−2κd​​Jd2​,

and after profile, spinor, and projective normalization it produces

μd=κdRdBd \mu_d=\kappa_dR_dB_d μd​=κd​Rd​Bd​

as the rest-energy scale of the localized contact operator.

By contrast, gddg_{dd}gdd​ appears in the later conditional fixed-point relation

(Md⋆)2gdd=common anchor, \frac{(M_d^\star)^2}{g_{dd}} =\text{common anchor}, gdd​(Md⋆​)2​=common anchor,

not as a multiplicative coefficient of the scalar self-contact Hamiltonian.

Therefore the current written action does not imply

ℏωcommon=μ3g33. \hbar\omega_{\rm common}=\mu_3g_{33}. ℏωcommon​=μ3​g33​.

The hadronic result has consequently been reduced as far as the present V2 equations allow:

73 \boxed{ \frac73 } 37​​

has a natural derivation as the reciprocal pre-constriction zero-point energy trace at common frequency,

43 \boxed{ \frac43 } 34​​

has a natural conditional derivation from assumed frequency-rescaling 4→34\to34→3 redistribution,

but the middle identification

ℏωcommon=μ3g33 \boxed{ \hbar\omega_{\rm common}=\mu_3g_{33} } ℏωcommon​=μ3​g33​​

is absent from the current master action.

Thus

ΔE3=289μ3g33=309.616456 MeV \boxed{ \Delta E_3 =\frac{28}{9}\mu_3g_{33} =309.616456\ {\rm MeV} } ΔE3​=928​μ3​g33​=309.616456 MeV​

is an unusually precise and structurally suggestive target formula, but not yet a theorem or prediction of V2.

Any further derivation must come from an explicit equation connecting the self-coupling/fixed-point sector to the curvature of the coordinate harmonic kernel. Without such an equation, multiplying μ3\mu_3μ3​ by g33g_{33}g33​ would be an added postulate.
