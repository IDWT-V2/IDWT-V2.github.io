49. Status after the baryon-topology test

The following candidate sources of the missing doubling have now been explicitly rejected as automatic consequences of the present V2 structure:

  • canonical coordinate/momentum quadratures;
  • reciprocal variation of one interaction action;
  • Dirac chirality;
  • particle/antiparticle structure;
  • local cubic Schrödinger self-contact;
  • local scalar-Soler self-contact;
  • finite-band Fubini--Study contact curvature;
  • naive K3K_3K3​ endpoint-incidence counting.

The baryon graph still supplies two independent relative Jacobi modes per spatial axis, but ordinary harmonic quantization does not turn those into a universal extra factor two multiplying the previously derived band-blind constriction scale.

Therefore the remaining factor two must come from a genuinely additional operator contribution or from a revision of which V2 quantity has been identified with the physical oscillator gap.

51. Virial bookkeeping and the Dirac-oscillator test

A possible rescue of the missing factor two is to interpret

ΔE=ε. \boxed{\Delta E=\varepsilon.} ΔE=ε.​

The virial theorem gives equal kinetic and potential expectation values in an eigenstate, but their sum is already the expectation value of this single Hamiltonian. It does not multiply the level spacing by two.

Therefore the factor-two discrepancy cannot be repaired by adding a second copy of the canonical kinetic energy to the harmonic constriction energy.

51.1 Relativistic Dirac oscillator

The V2 master operator is Dirac type, so the normal oscillator can also be tested in the standard one-dimensional Dirac-oscillator form

HD=α(p−iβmωx)+βm. H_D =\alpha\left(p-i\beta m\omega x\right)+\beta m. HD​=α(p−iβmωx)+βm.

En2=m2+2mωn \boxed{ E_n^2=m^2+2m\omega n } En2​=m2+2mωn​

The important point is structural: the spinor term shifts the two components relative to one another; it does not create two equal additive copies of the oscillator energy.
