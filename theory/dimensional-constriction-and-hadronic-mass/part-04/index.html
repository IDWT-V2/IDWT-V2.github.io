18.1 Why the 7/37/37/3 coefficient is not yet derived

The numerical agreement alone is not sufficient to establish

ΔE⊥=73μ3g33. \Delta E_\perp=\frac73\mu_3g_{33}. ΔE⊥​=37​μ3​g33​.

There are two structural observations behind the candidate coefficient:

  1. the spatial transition has incoming depth four and surviving depth three, so

    din+doutdout=73; \frac{d_{\rm in}+d_{\rm out}}{d_{\rm out}}=\frac73; dout​din​+dout​​=37​;

  2. each spatial direction carries a position and momentum quadrature, so the reciprocal 4→34\to34→3 phase-space contact involves eight incoming and six surviving real quadratures, giving the same ratio

    8+66=73. \frac{8+6}{6}=\frac73. 68+6​=37​.

The V2 cross-depth action is reciprocal: the lower-depth state samples the complete marginal of the higher-depth state, while the higher-depth state feels the lower-depth state uniformly across the additional fibre. This makes a normalization involving both incident and surviving sectors plausible.

However, the currently written action does not contain a theorem saying that the reciprocal contact energy is normalized by

2din+2dout2dout. \frac{2d_{\rm in}+2d_{\rm out}}{2d_{\rm out}}. 2dout​2din​+2dout​​.

Therefore the factor 7/37/37/3 remains a conjectured cross-sector normalization, not a derived result.

The hadronic calculation now has a clean hierarchy of confidence.

The close formula
