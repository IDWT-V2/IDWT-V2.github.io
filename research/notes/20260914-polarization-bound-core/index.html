Research / Mass and normalization

A localized core determined by polarization ratios

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

A reconstructed local current action admits a localized spatial core whose shape is determined by polarization ratios. Its defining equation is independent of the overall field magnitude. The same coefficients and profile support every coherent grade of the existing simplex current hierarchy. In that hierarchy the assigned electron and muon frequencies remain different, while their common spatial size is unchanged.

This is a specified mathematical model with testable consequences. The binomial current weights, their physical spatial carrier and the common unit of frequency remain premises. This action has not been identified with the separate gapped normal operator. Neither its nonlinear stability nor the physical selection of occupied particle grades is assumed below. Mass remains the selected frequency in equivalent units.

Energy, phase and complete inertial response
