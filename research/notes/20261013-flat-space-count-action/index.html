A Common Flat-Space Action with Exact Count Clocks and Bounded Localization

Updated October 13, 2026

One explicit action can support the maintained depth classifications as different patterns in the same ordinary ten-dimensional space. It has exact orthogonal mode families with the target simplex clocks, positive complete energy, center motion in every spatial direction, and a theorem of persistent bounded localization for an open set around every maintained branch.

This is an inverse realization of the frequency proposal. Its constitutive function includes the simplex law; its width scales and positive penalties are chosen. The result establishes that these requirements can coexist in one equation. It does not derive the constitutive law, identify the observed occupied states, or reproduce interactions between separated particles.
