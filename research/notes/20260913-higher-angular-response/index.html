Higher angular response of the localized current field

Updated September 13, 2026

Summary: Homogeneous closed and coclosed polynomial forms give complete angular blocks for the even-depth matrix field; independent Cartesian checks and expanded electron and muon scans retain the distinction between angular classification and full spatial stability.

Three different indices

The earlier localized response calculation used constant Clifford forms. Those angular families include translations but only a bounded range of ordinary spherical harmonics. Here ddd is an even ordinary spatial depth, kkk is a Clifford form degree, and ℓ\ellℓ is a polynomial angular degree. Neither kkk nor ℓ\ellℓ is the occupied mass grade NNN. The electron and muon retain N=12N=12N=12 and N=34N=34N=34 at d=6d=6d=6.

At even depth, the full complex r×rr\times rr×r matrix space, r=2d/2r=2^{d/2}r=2d/2, is represented by all Clifford forms. No spinor or partner directions are discarded by using that matrix basis.

Why the angular blocks cover the matrix field

The Fischer decomposition for closed and coclosed polynomial forms states that arbitrary Clifford-valued polynomials are generated from solutions of (1) by radial powers, exterior multiplication by xxx, and contraction with xxx. On the sphere these operations are exactly the span (3), with the two exceptional constant families. This gives the angular classification; the radial functions remain unrestricted. See Theorem 1 of Delanghe, Lávička and Souček.

Reproduction and limits

localized_harmonic_response.py constructs the angular matrices and multiplicities. localized_harmonic_response_checks.py performs the independent Cartesian and full-Hessian checks, exact dimension counts, and all 200 spectra using localized_clifford_response.py. The report preserves the full finite eigenvalue arrays and each discretization, rather than only favorable selected frequencies.

This supplies the angular blocks needed for a complete future spatial analysis. It does not prove nonlinear stability, determine the physical mass carrier, derive the shell weights, or identify observed interaction vertices. The matrix decomposition and the absence of resolved growth in these finite calculations have precisely those separate scopes.
