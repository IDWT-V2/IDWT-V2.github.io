Updated October 13, 2026

Candidate and Its Physical Status

Let the ordinary sector spacetime have metric eta with signature (+,−,…,−). Keep the orientation target, metric and timelike determinant law of the preceding calculation. In a local adapted normal frame, write its canonical connection as a = A_A(phi) d phi^A, with curvature F_AB. Let rho be a unitary representation of the normal group on retained coefficients chi. All coefficient spaces are field values, not additional ordinary spatial dimensions.

The following positive-energy wave action is a comparison realization of retained same-field modes:

L=T(1−Δ)+(Dμχ)†Dμχ−V(χ†χ),Dμχ=∂μχ+ρ(AA)χ ∂μϕA,V≥0. \mathcal L=T(1-\sqrt\Delta)+(D_\mu\chi)^\dagger D^\mu\chi-V(\chi^\dagger\chi), \quad D_\mu\chi=\partial_\mu\chi+\rho(A_A)\chi\,\partial_\mu\phi^A, \quad V\ge0. L=T(1−Δ​)+(Dμ​χ)†Dμχ−V(χ†χ),Dμ​χ=∂μ​χ+ρ(AA​)χ∂μ​ϕA,V≥0.

It does not derive this decomposition from Psi_infinity, does not establish fermionic dynamics, and does not identify a chosen bare restoring clock with the maintained particle frequencies. In this candidate chi describes retained coefficients independent of the ray that defines J. If instead chi is that same complete chiral ray, treating both as independent variables overcounts its degrees of freedom and the action needs a different reduction. The connection has no independent variation or independent mediator. A Dirac comparison below retains the same geometric source identity but requires its own energy interpretation.

Constrained Source Variation

This is a consequence of the constrained connection variation, not a proof that a physical charged particle cannot interact.

Principal Structure of the Coupled Wave Action

An Exact Current-Induced Instability Test

Take constant J and use a root tangent pair in the normal indices a,b. Let X_E and X_iE be the real antilinear generators with B=E_ab and iE_ab. The coordinates pi = (u X_E + v X_iE)/4 have unit orientation metric at the origin because norm([X_E,J0]) squared = 16. The canonical root curvature is

Fuv=−i8(Da+Db). F_{uv}=-\frac{i}{8}(D_a+D_b). Fuv​=−8i​(Da​+Db​).

For a constant diagonal normal current, distinct root planes decouple in the linearized orientation equation. Put c^mu = j^mu(F_uv) and alpha^mu = c^mu/f^2. The root equations and their complex form are

□u=αμ∂μv,□v=−αμ∂μu,w=u+iv:□w+iαμ∂μw=0. \Box u=\alpha^\mu\partial_\mu v,\quad \Box v=-\alpha^\mu\partial_\mu u,\quad w=u+iv:\quad \Box w+i\alpha^\mu\partial_\mu w=0. □u=αμ∂μ​v,□v=−αμ∂μ​u,w=u+iv:□w+iαμ∂μ​w=0.

For constant alpha, w = exp(−i alpha_mu x^mu/2) z reduces this exactly to

□z+αμαμ4z=0. \Box z+\frac{\alpha_\mu\alpha^\mu}{4}z=0. □z+4αμ​αμ​z=0.

All Fourier frequencies are real if and only if alpha is timelike or null. For a spacelike alpha there is a growing finite-wavenumber band. The original principal symbol remains the ordinary wave symbol. This distinguishes a lower-order current instability from the earlier loss of principal hyperbolicity.

The Spin representation supplies an explicit counterflow control without changing the connection. It is Lambda-star W tensor (det W) to the power minus one half, so rho(i(D_a+D_b)) = i(N_a+N_b−1). Its vacuum and grade-ab state have opposite root weights. Equal-amplitude positive-frequency wave solutions in these two orthogonal components with opposite spatial momenta p_plus=(omega,k) and p_minus=(omega,−k) give

The full field has constant J and two free wave components, so it is an exact background of the candidate action. Its normal current is diagonal and constant; cross terms between the vacuum and grade two vanish because u(q) preserves grade. The root-ab perturbation is therefore a closed linearized subsystem. It is unstable for nonzero k. These ideal plane backgrounds have infinite total energy on an infinite sector; their exact dispersion is a background test, not an identified finite-energy particle decay or a finite-time concentration theorem.

This same construction exists at every nonempty occupied q=2,3,4 carrier. At q=2 the canonical traceless connection is absent, but its central Spin current still produces the root response. Neither current susceptibility nor this instability isolates depths three/four. Depth ten has no such normal-orientation channel in the declared ten-parent rule.

Dirac Coefficients and Remaining Questions

A first-order comparison instead uses

Lχ=i2[χˉγμDμχ−(Dμχˉ)γμχ]. \mathcal L_\chi=\frac i2[\bar\chi\gamma^\mu D_\mu\chi-(D_\mu\bar\chi)\gamma^\mu\chi]. Lχ​=2i​[χˉ​γμDμ​χ−(Dμ​χˉ​)γμχ].

Its current convention is j_D^mu(H) = i bar chi gamma^mu rho(H) chi. The covariant variation gives the same constrained curvature force with this current. In an adapted frame with A_A=0 at the point, the orientation and Dirac characteristic blocks retain the determinant and ordinary light cones. Normal even Clifford rotations commute with the complete tangent Dirac symbol on the full parent carrier. The bare classical Dirac Hamiltonian, however, is not nonnegative on unrestricted complex commuting coefficients; the positive wave-energy formula above must not be transferred to it.

The constructive results are an explicit constrained source equation, complete reciprocal stress and a coupled causal principal structure for the wave comparison. The counterflow calculation identifies a dynamical acceptance condition to test rather than an inserted sector coefficient. The same-field reduction, observed electric weights, compatible occupied projections, finite localized states, frequency selection, and suppression of the allowed depth-two larger response remain open.
