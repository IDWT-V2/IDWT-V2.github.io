Scope of the spatial solution

Multiplying (15) by the complete profiles and radial carrier phases gives solutions of the declared nonlinear spatial channel equations including (13). Rotations preserve the radial scalar density, so their original self-binding sources remain valid. The derivative part of (13) also adds the corresponding rotational advection to the local conserved number current. That drift is the same in the two compared preparations; (3) names the Dirac current rather than silently including this extra term.

The example uses two individual spatial field channels. It does not introduce an atom, a mediator particle, or a new physical dimension. It also does not identify the lower field as an actual electron or the higher field as an actual tau merely from their depths. Their occupied mode, physical interaction operator and stability remain separate tasks. The scalar branch's known radial instabilities are not removed by (15).

The normal polarization waves provide a different exact motion with a preserved scalar source. Allowing polarization to vary across the bound coordinates instead requires the full kinetic remainder derived in Spatial polarization textures.
