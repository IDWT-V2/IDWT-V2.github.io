Literal Orbit Precession in Flat Spatial Coordinates

Updated October 17, 2026

The premise is that the six-dimensional electron itself follows a literal deterministic trajectory around the nucleus in the surrounding higher-dimensional physical space. The position q(t) below is relative to the nuclear center, taken as the origin; this external motion is distinct from the electron's internal classical field oscillations. A useful corrected result identifies the equation obeyed by the maintained orbit curve in flat coordinates, while keeping it distinct from the older curved-space action that generated the same curve.
