Adjacent-Depth Polarization Geometry

Updated November 5, 2026

conditional research

The polarization geometry at neighboring depths can be connected explicitly. At depth five, a spinor seed determines a unit axis and two equal-strength rotation planes perpendicular to it. With a chosen embedding into six ordinary spatial directions, that axis pairs with the additional direction to complete a three-plane rotation structure. The same construction joins depths three and four, with one perpendicular plane becoming two. Thus an axis in an odd number of directions and a full rotation structure in the neighboring even number need not be unrelated ingredients.

Here is the common calculation. Set D=d+1D=d+1D=d+1, with (d,D)=(3,4)(d,D)=(3,4)(d,D)=(3,4) or (5,6)(5,6)(5,6). Take the even-dimensional Hermitian Clifford matrices and a chiral seed χ\chiχ of squared norm s>0s>0s>0. On that chirality define

Γi=−iγiγD,vi=χ†Γiχ,Bij=−iχ†ΓiΓjχ(i≠j),Bii=0.(1) \Gamma_i=-i\gamma_i\gamma_D, \qquad v_i=\chi^\dagger\Gamma_i\chi, \qquad B_{ij}=-i\chi^\dagger\Gamma_i\Gamma_j\chi \quad(i\ne j),\qquad B_{ii}=0. \tag{1} Γi​=−iγi​γD​,vi​=χ†Γi​χ,Bij​=−iχ†Γi​Γj​χ(i=j),Bii​=0.(1)

The Γi\Gamma_iΓi​ obey the Clifford relation in ddd dimensions. The same seed's even-dimensional polarization matrix is

Beven=(Bv−vT0),Beven2=−s2ID.(2) B_{\rm even}=\begin{pmatrix}B&v\\-v^T&0\end{pmatrix}, \qquad B_{\rm even}^2=-s^2I_D. \tag{2} Beven​=(B−vT​v0​),Beven2​=−s2ID​.(2)

The square identity holds for every chiral seed at four and six. Multiplying the blocks gives

∣v∣2=s2,Bv=0,B2=vvT−s2Id.(3) |v|^2=s^2,\qquad Bv=0,\qquad B^2=vv^T-s^2I_d. \tag{3} ∣v∣2=s2,Bv=0,B2=vvT−s2Id​.(3)

In plain terms, v/sv/sv/s is the axis. The matrix B/sB/sB/s vanishes along it and acts as a right-angle rotation on its perpendicular directions. The extra direction completes the missing plane. Overall field amplitude cancels from these normalized geometric quantities. The five/six comparison uses the same four complex spinor coefficients; the three/four comparison uses two. These coefficients describe the field on ordinary space, not another physical space.

This relates the polarization structures used by the spatial-current and normal-geometry constructions. It does not require different particles to share one seed or derive their relative embedding. It is shared by depths three and five, so it does not uniquely explain neutrino neutrality. At ten, the corresponding full rotation structure requires the additional pure-seed restriction; chirality alone does not suffice. The result adds a concrete compatibility relation, not a new independent charge or mass prediction.
