An Exact Scalar-Curvature Constraint on Pure Count Carriers

Updated October 12, 2026

The minimum-carrier comparison can be sharpened without a parameter scan. At the common ground preparation c=3/5, the proper-current count action has a growing spatial disturbance at depths six and ten for every real choice of its local scalar-ratio curvature. The result concerns the one-column carrier, rather than borrowing the earlier full-matrix rigidity theorem.

This limits one derivative-free Lorentz-scalar interaction class and preparation. It does not reject the assigned excited particles, other physical geometries, additional same-field degrees of freedom, derivative interactions, or finite-lived states. The known preferred-time persistence and actual count/rotation identities remain valid in their own actions.

5. Occupied-Depth Controls and Verification

The same exact coefficient calculation tests all occupied depths. At two, three and four it gives intervals allowing nonnegative real squared frequencies in this low family: gamma is at or below approximately -0.9222, 0.2791 and -0.5762, respectively. The exact algebraic endpoints and cubics are in the report. These intervals are not all-harmonic or nonlinear stability windows. Depth five has failed fixed-label closure and requires its larger coupled family. Depth six and the axial depth-ten family have empty allowed sets, as proved above.

minimal-carrier-exact-curvature.py and its report retain the exact real matrices, coefficient identities, cubic discriminants, complete real coefficient inequalities and Sturm counts. Independent direct NumPy field sources at five new positions and three curvatures per closed depth agree with the exact matrices to below 1.3e-15 relatively, including nonzero curvatures. This checks the operator as well as its polynomial certificate.

An initial implementation used a real-assumption polynomial variable that SymPy's characteristic-polynomial routine replaced by a distinct symbol of the same name. The resulting degree-zero interpretation was corrected by explicitly substituting the routine's returned generator. Its exact source and failure record remain preserved. The repaired calculation and the independently verified final calculation agree.

The useful conclusion is a constraint on a simplification of the count-supporting action. Changing its scalar curvature alone cannot repair these six-/ten-dimensional ground controls. A successful covariant continuation must change a premise that this calculation actually holds fixed, or establish viable assigned excited states despite these controls.
