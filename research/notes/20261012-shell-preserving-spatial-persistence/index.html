Shell-Preserving Potentials with Full Spatial Persistence

Updated October 12, 2026

Preserving a stationary mass-frequency does not require retaining every derivative of the earlier constitutive law. This distinction supplies a concrete inverse construction beyond the pair-preserving repair obstruction. One common formula retains all integer-shell backgrounds and their energy while changing their response to spatial disturbances. It uses no fitted field amplitude and no separate adjustment for an individual particle.

The successful persistence result below uses the preferred-time density ρ\rhoρ, rather than a Lorentz proper-current norm. It is a conditional mathematical construction on the earlier ordinary sphere, not a completed physical particle model. Known mass targets are legitimate inputs to this inverse investigation; preserving them is distinct from deriving the action or demonstrating observational agreement beyond those targets.

Measured masses are inputs to the upstream constrained spectral inference. The sector depths and mode indices are inferred assignments under the shared counting and scale rules, rather than additional independently supplied measurements. Those inferred assignments become inputs to this downstream dynamical realization test. Assignment uniqueness is a separate question, restricted to the documented search domain and constraints.

5. Physical and Evidential Scope

The successful action uses ρ\rhoρ, a density in a chosen time frame. It lacks the original potential's Lorentz spinor-boost invariance. Replacing ρ\rhoρ by the proper vector norm restores additional current derivatives, so (5) cannot be carried over to that action. A covariant same-field time structure would need its own action and complete disturbance analysis; its fluctuations cannot be frozen silently.

These states occupy the conditional ordinary sphere. Persistence on that carrier does not establish a localized finite-energy object in unbounded space, complete translational inertia, observed spin statistics, electric charge, or observed lifetimes. This construction demonstrates that an inverse count clock can coexist mathematically with full spatial persistence. The recipe also works for other admissible monotone spectral functions, so it does not independently select IDWT's assigned dimensions.
