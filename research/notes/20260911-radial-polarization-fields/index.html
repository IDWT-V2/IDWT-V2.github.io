A spatial family available in even dimensions

The localized axial family uses odd-dimensional kinetic chiral blocks. A separate spherical family works for every D≥2D\ge2D≥2, including the proposed depths six and ten. It extends the fixed-polarization radial construction by allowing every radial polarization component to have its own complex profile. The spatial domain here is a specified flat chart, not a selected global particle geometry.

Verification and scope

radial_polarization_fields.py implements reconstruction, the vector time derivatives and the complete spatial current. radial_polarization_checks.py runs 234 checks of complete Clifford modules in every dimension from two through ten. Independent Cartesian derivatives verify (5) on complex profiles whose polarizations vary with radius. The checks also reconstruct every current component, rotate the full field, retain the complete scalar Hessian and integrate the derivative of the full conserved matrix (10).

This supplies a controlled spatial family at the even particle depths as well as at odd depths. Its patterns share one radial center. General separated-center encounters, arbitrary angular textures, stable occupied particle states and physically normalized cross-depth coupling require additional calculations. The family does not select the global geometry of our three-dimensional space or of a higher sector.
