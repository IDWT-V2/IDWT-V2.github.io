A conformal spatial operator with the simplex spectrum

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: The critical conformal scalar operator on a round even-dimensional sphere has exactly the normalized simplex spectrum, while its spinor realization, spatial geometry and master interaction remain additional requirements.

An exact geometric occurrence of the mass-count polynomial

There is a known natural differential operator whose normalized eigenvalues are exactly

S(n,d)=(n+d−1d).(1) S(n,d)=\binom{n+d-1}{d}. \tag{1} S(n,d)=(dn+d−1​).(1)

For even ddd, it is the critical conformal scalar operator on a round ddd-sphere. The result follows from spatial conformal covariance and can also be verified by direct differentiation. It gives a distinct mathematical origin for the same sequence used in the mass-spectrum construction.

This calculation specifies a round physical spatial geometry SRdS_R^dSRd​, with undetermined radius RRR. It is a conditional comparison, not a replacement of the CP3 candidate or a determination of physical topology. Its directions are ordinary macroscopic spatial directions; there is no compactification or hidden small radius. Spherical harmonic coefficients do not add dimensions.

The operator acts on scalar spatial functions. Its appearance in the master spinor interaction, and the identification of its harmonic degree with an occupied particle mode, must be derived separately.

Scalar covariance is not automatically spinor covariance

Equation (19) contains derivatives of the current. It is an additional interaction, beyond the written algebraic scalar term tested by the local derivative calculation. Any claim that it arises by eliminating modes must identify those modes of the same master field and retain the induced kinetic normalization.

The complete response on a zero-current Killing background provides one exact spatial test of this proposed interaction. Its simplex coefficient enters collective frequencies; any nonzero derivative-current coefficient gives unstable high harmonics on that specified branch.

The exact result is therefore a geometric origin for the simplex scalar spectrum, with conformal covariance fixing the integer recurrence. The physical mass-operator problem still requires the actual spatial geometry, a scalar or spinor realization inherited from the master field, a selected harmonic mode, and a justified connection between the resulting frequency or energy and rest mass.
