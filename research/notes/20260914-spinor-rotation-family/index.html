A complete rotation family for the depth-six fixed point

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The actual spinor parent admits a manageable spatial symmetry class for testing a circulating nonlinear branch. Fix the two rotation quantum numbers of a highest-weight spinor pattern, then retain every spinor component and every compatible scalar harmonic. This supplies a basis for a fixed-point calculation; it does not establish the fixed point or stability against perturbations in other rotation classes.

Use the unit four-sphere feature in ordinary six-dimensional space, with the spinor operator B_K in A spatial spinor parent for the curvature frequency. The underlying four-sphere sits in five ambient directions. Its second ordinary normal direction and its physical sustaining field are still outside this surface calculation.

Every compatible spinor component

Choose the standard ambient Clifford frame with i Gamma_1 Gamma_2=-Z tensor I and i Gamma_3 Gamma_4=-I tensor Z. Let L_0 be the circulating base degree. For spinor components 0,1,2,3 the scalar azimuthal charges are

(q1,q2)=(L0,0), (L0,1), (L0+1,0), (L0+1,1).(3) (q_1,q_2)=(L_0,0),\ (L_0,1),\ (L_0+1,0),\ (L_0+1,1). \tag{3} (q1​,q2​)=(L0​,0), (L0​,1), (L0​+1,0), (L0​+1,1).(3)

Together with the spin rotation, all four have the same total rotation labels. The component-zero polynomial (x_1+i x_2)^(L_0) is a monogenic positive B_K mode. In the parity class of this initial field, all compatible scalar degrees are K=L_0+2s, s nonnegative.

For one component and fixed K, choose a rest-sphere degree l satisfying K=q_1+l+2j, j nonnegative, l at least q_2. The complete scalar basis is

Here the second P is an associated Legendre polynomial, and C normalizes (4) using (2). Jacobi and associated-Legendre orthogonality give the full canonical norm; different spinor components are also orthogonal. Up to s=J the basis has (J+1)(2J+1) scalar-spinor modes. Each coefficient may be complex.

All four physical tangent derivatives are used. In particular the azimuthal gradients are i q_1/sqrt(t) and i q_2/sqrt((1-t)(1-u^2)); omitting them would remove the circulating motion and change the spinor operator.

Complete first-order closure

Apply the actual ambient formula B_K=3/2-Gamma_x Gamma_grad to (4) and integrate its full spinor matrix elements. Each K block closes exactly and satisfies

BK†=BK,BK2=(K+3/2)2I.(5) B_K^\dagger=B_K,\qquad B_K^2=(K+3/2)^2I. \tag{5} BK†​=BK​,BK2​=(K+3/2)2I.(5)

At order s its dimension is 4s+1. Its positive and negative B_K multiplicities are respectively 2s+1 and 2s. The first block therefore contains the single monogenic positive mode; the other spinor branches enter at higher compatible spatial degree. This is a consequence of the rotation labels, not an imposed removal of their components.

The same low-frequency chain supplies both positive and negative parent frequencies on each block. Both must be retained in a nonlinear fixed-point and response calculation. An efficient canonical embedding can use the two exact B projections in each block, avoiding a discretionary choice of basis inside degenerate eigenspaces.
