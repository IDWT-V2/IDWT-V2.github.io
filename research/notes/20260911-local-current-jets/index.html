Local scalar contact on physical field derivatives

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: Prolonging the written local scalar interaction to physical field derivatives repeats its local source blocks without generating a simplex coupling, and finite derivative data do not close the spatial kinetic equation.

Test the local interaction before changing its action

The mass-operator construction identifies the filtered derivative symbols of the physical field with a cumulative symmetric carrier. That identification gives the correct Hockey-Stick rank. It does not yet determine how the written interaction acts on those derivatives.

This calculation applies the actual product rule to the local scalar term. It uses the same smooth physical spinor field throughout. Its coordinate directions are ordinary spatial directions on QdQ_dQd​; Taylor coefficients introduce no additional physical dimensions and require no compactification. The result is local and does not choose a global spatial topology.

In a local orthonormal spin frame, write

Esc=κ2∫(ψ†βψ)2dμ,N(ψ)=κ(ψ†βψ)βψ,β†=β,β2=I.(1) \mathcal E_{\rm sc} =\frac{\kappa}{2}\int(\psi^\dagger\beta\psi)^2d\mu,\qquad \mathcal N(\psi)=\kappa(\psi^\dagger\beta\psi)\beta\psi, \qquad \beta^\dagger=\beta,\quad\beta^2=I. \tag{1} Esc​=2κ​∫(ψ†βψ)2dμ,N(ψ)=κ(ψ†βψ)βψ,β†=β,β2=I.(1)

The metric, β\betaβ and κ\kappaκ are fixed background data in the variation. The full equation also contains its spatial Dirac operator. No occupied profile or physical mass follows from the algebraic source alone.

Curvature and the physical kinetic norm

On a curved spatial manifold, a connection splits the finite derivative filtration into symmetrized covariant derivatives. The change from ordinary coordinate derivatives to this splitting is triangular and invertible, with the same highest-order symbols. A connection preserving the spinor pairing obeys the covariant product rule. The associated graded operator of scalar multiplication remains J(q)J(q)J(q) on every degree, so (7) survives. The full source variation transforms with the same fixed linear change of jet coordinates, preserving (16). Curvature does not turn a coordinate transformation into a new scalar coupling.

There is a useful warning about the norm implicit in such a rewriting. If some retained derivative of JJJ is nonzero, KKK in (6) is a nonzero nilpotent matrix. Then MN[J]M_N[J]MN​[J] is not diagonalizable and cannot be self-adjoint in any positive definite metric on the independent finite jet fibre. For example, at d=N=1d=N=1d=N=1 and J=1+xJ=1+xJ=1+x,

M=(1011).(18) M=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix}. \tag{18} M=(11​01​).(18)

The equation GM=M†GGM=M^\dagger GGM=M†G forces G22=0G_{22}=0G22​=0, which excludes a positive definite GGG. Ordinary real multiplication on the physical L2L^2L2 field space is nevertheless self-adjoint. There is no contradiction: the Taylor map is not a unitary identification of that space with an independent finite coefficient fibre at one point.

The original canonical norm remains

Q[ψ]=∫ψ†ψ dμ.(19) Q[\psi]=\int\psi^\dagger\psi\,d\mu. \tag{19} Q[ψ]=∫ψ†ψdμ.(19)

Expressing its field through holonomic derivatives does not replace (19) with an independent sum over all derivative slots. A positive norm that weights all spatial derivatives defines a different, usually Sobolev, metric; its induced operator and constraints must be transformed as well. The canonical normalization calculation explains why the physical kinetic metric must accompany a projected interaction.

There is also a kinetic closure issue. Already for a flat first-order Dirac operator D=−i∑jaj∂j\mathscr D=-i\sum_j\mathsf a_j\partial_jD=−i∑j​aj​∂j​,

The highest retained derivative needs order N+1N+1N+1 of the field. For example, ψ1=u\psi_1=uψ1​=u and ψ2=u+ϵx1N+1v\psi_2=u+\epsilon x_1^{N+1}vψ2​=u+ϵx1N+1​v have identical NNN-jets at zero, but their degree-NNN Dirac jets differ by −iϵ(N+1)a1v-i\epsilon(N+1)\mathsf a_1v−iϵ(N+1)a1​v. Thus a finite jet at one point does not define a closed algebraic kinetic matrix. A spatial PDE on jet fields can retain their derivatives and compatibility equations; a finite oscillator reduction instead needs an actual projection and control of its complementary spatial modes.

Consequence for the mass-operator derivation

The count in (3) is exact. The prolonged local action gives (13) and (14), rather than the auxiliary rank-one contact. Deriving the latter requires an additional physical reduction, such as a specific source pairing and its canonical normalization. The defined graph pairing supplies one conditional action with a simplex coefficient, but its kernel is an additional premise.

This also clarifies the scope of the coherent curved mode-bundle fields and their physical contact spectrum. Those calculations use sections of a specified independently twisted kinetic bundle. Realizing them as derivatives of the master field would require the holonomic constraints and the actual induced kinetic operator. Equality of carrier ranks alone does not provide that realization.

The result concerns the written local scalar term and changes of its derivative coordinates. It does not exclude different nonlinear spatial branches, a derived interaction from complementary modes of the same field, or a different master term. It leaves the assigned particle dimensionalities and mode indices unchanged.

Verification

local_current_jet_checks.py compares (13) with independently expanded complex spatial polynomials. It checks the complete real-linear variation, trace identities, both signs of β\betaβ, scalar-null fields, and covariance when all spin data are rotated together. The largest explicit real Jacobian has dimension 448448448, retaining every derivative through order two in six dimensions for an eight-component spinor.

A nonflat metric-compatible connection is checked in two independent ways: symmetrized ordered covariant differentiation and a matrix exponential along radial lines. Their current jets and full nonlinear variations agree. Exact binomial identities verify the plane-wave cancellation at arbitrary multiindices through the assigned degrees, without constructing millions of coefficient entries. Symbolic differentiation also verifies the kinetic counterexample (20).
