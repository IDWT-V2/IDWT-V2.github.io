Spatial dimension and component frames of the mass carrier

Updated September 16, 2026

conditional calculation

The completed radial calculation uses a genuine spatial differential operator, but a physical interpretation also requires its surface and component-frame data. Two independent checks clarify those requirements.

The round-feature surface operator works in each positive tangent dimension used by the construction, with its correct spinor rank. At depth two, however, the surface has dimension zero and cannot supply the assigned harmonic tower. Separately, the bounded angular completion is written in a specified component frame. A change of local phase must transport its angular operator along with the scalar, connection and wave.

These checks concern ordinary spatial dimensions and representations of field components. They introduce no compactified dimensions or new interacting particles.
