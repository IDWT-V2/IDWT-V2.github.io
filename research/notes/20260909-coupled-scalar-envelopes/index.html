Reciprocal scalar envelopes

Updated September 9, 2026

Summary: Readjusting both nested spatial Dirac profiles derives a reciprocal scalar coupling matrix and distinct common and relative amplitude responses from one interaction coefficient.

Couple both envelopes through their actual spatial profiles

The nested Dirac pair determines both spatial profiles from one scalar moment interaction. The scalar envelope reduction determines how one profile responds when its amplitude changes. Combining these constructions gives the mutual amplitude response without introducing a new coefficient for each pair.

Use nested spatial sectors Qp⊂Qp+qQ_p\subset Q_{p+q}Qp​⊂Qp+q​ and one ordinary free coordinate in a sector containing both. All these directions belong to the same infinite physical manifold. The two envelope amplitudes describe orthogonal projected channels of Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​ with the kinetic measures specified in the nested-pair construction; they are not independent interaction particles or further physical dimensions.

Keep the two centers and rest polarizations fixed in this scalar envelope reduction. The scalar stationary profiles retain that polarization degeneracy; changing spatial shapes and orientations has the additional responses in Dynamical shape opening. The reduction here does not establish that those other motions decouple from a physical experiment.

The calculation provides a reciprocal amplitude interaction and its reduced spectral criterion; full spatial particle stability requires the other response channels too.
