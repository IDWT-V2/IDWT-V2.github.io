Relaxed Frame Twists Link Guide Force and Longitudinal Stiffness

Updated October 13, 2026

The balanced neutral frame has continuous orientation modes as well as its gapped imbalance channels. A common rotation of its two vectors leaves the dielectric unchanged. Its longitudinal gradient costs energy, and the transverse guide can respond by changing its profile. This response can be calculated from the existing guide action rather than choosing a new potential or holding the profile fixed.

The construction remains in the independently varied commuting connection action. Its neutral vectors are retained deformation modes of the proposed single field, not a derived master-field reduction. End cores, full non-Abelian response and physical normalizations remain open.

Their global mode-rotation symmetry has not been declared a local gauge redundancy. This is an exact bulk family within the stated frame action, not a complete finite-source solution. The area-preserving rotation exists in ordinary three- and four-dimensional directions; it does not add a spatial dimension. It retains the existing odd-sector source-carrier premise where required.
