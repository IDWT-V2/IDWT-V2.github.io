A Local Action with Its Complete Current Variation

The signed scalar-source calculation shows why a positive-band trial cannot freeze its opposite-sign source. Here a changed inverse action removes that source leakage for a different spatial family. It does not derive the master interaction or replace the maintained frequency law.

The carrier is an additional field-component premise, following the earlier balanced-multiplet comparison; the columns are neither extra spatial dimensions nor separate mediator particles. A reduction from the single field Ψ∞, and observed spin and charge, remain unestablished. In particular, balancing all columns cannot be assumed to preserve an observed particle's spin moments.

On the timelike-current domain n>0, use the ordinary Dirac kinetic term and the changed local potential

U=κJ22n,κ>0,E=∫Tr⁡Φ†DΦ dv+∫U dv. U=\frac{\kappa J^2}{2n},\qquad \kappa>0,\qquad E=\int\operatorname{Tr}\Phi^\dagger\mathscr D\Phi\,dv+\int U\,dv. U=2nκJ2​,κ>0,E=∫TrΦ†DΦdv+∫Udv.
