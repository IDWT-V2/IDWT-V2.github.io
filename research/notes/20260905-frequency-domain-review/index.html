Frequency-domain review

Updated September 8, 2026

exploratory not a module

Physical premise (IDWT): There is one infinite-dimensional physical manifold. All sectors form one nested chain of submanifolds within it. Particle depth counts literal macroscopic spatial dimensions. The electron's CP3 sector has six; nuclear 3+3 locking constrains ordinary matter and observers to an experienced three-dimensional sector. Unobserved spatial directions remain physical. See spatial meaning and mathematical scope.

Summary: Corrected frequency-domain conclusions and the remaining hadronic proof target.

RESEARCH NOTES, NOT A PHYSICS MODULE. This is a record of calculations, corrections, and unresolved hypotheses. It does not assert a completed hadronic-mass derivation or a validated replacement for the V2 master dynamics.

1. Where the investigation stands

The most useful progress was not another numerical factor close to a nucleon residual. Several earlier exclusions depended on confusing a static matrix, an observed projection, or an operator coefficient with a frequency generator. Those exclusions were narrowed or withdrawn after exact checks.

Two later results had not yet been recorded in the inspected ledger. First, the point-contact construction and a smooth coherent-density construction have different spectra; the latter does not automatically replace or disprove the graph-supported prescription explicitly chosen in V2. Second, a rank-one source-to-scalar-self-energy response supplies a concrete sufficient mechanism for the common-anchor ratios without measuring or solving for the sources. The unresolved issue is whether that response applies to complete occupied reference frequencies or to coefficients before the simplex multiplier.

The companion contact-kernel note and rank-one-response note contain those previously undocumented calculations. They are live possibilities and exact comparison tests, not a numerical baryon prediction.

The last inspected hadronic ledger ends at Section 76. Its Sections 67--76 contain the earlier saved corrections. The returned execution report confirmed that write and its verification. Later read-only tests did not, by themselves, save the additional findings. This notes package preserves them separately rather than extending an exploratory ledger as though it were a finished module.

7. The constructive spectral target

Three positions in a common observed three-space give six relative Jacobi coordinates after center-of-mass removal. A fixed Euclidean hyperradius has an S5S^5S5 angular surface. This is useful effective three-body kinematics, not proof that the baryon is the depth-six electron sector or the depth-five neutrino sector. Native higher-depth coordinates, spinor bundles, and eliminated channels must be accounted for. Ledger Section 75 records the corrected distinction.

8. Scope of verification

The packaged regression checks reproduce finite algebra, the illustrative resolvent, color contractions, moving-projector identities, smooth-symbol spectra, and the source-cancellation/count-normalization distinction. They are not Monte Carlo significance tests, a full variational derivation, or a proof of empirical agreement.

Source audits here are limited to the inspected passages and bounded searches returned during this session. An unsuccessful search for a formula is not proof of absence from every possible V2 companion. No prior claimed extensive null tests have been re-executed in this session.
