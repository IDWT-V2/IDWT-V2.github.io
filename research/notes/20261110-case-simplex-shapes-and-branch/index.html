Shapes

The count C = S(n, d) is the number of sites of a complete simplex of side n - 1 in d directions (spacing 1). Exact (rational) statistics, no enumeration; checked against the enumerated electron run. Electron d = 6, side 12: 18,564 sites; muon d = 6, side 34: 3,838,380; tau d = 10, side 22: 64,512,240, the only complete simplex of that count (the tau count is not a complete simplex in six directions). The exact branch

For d bound directions the stationary branch of i Phi_t = -i gamma.grad Phi + M beta Phi - g rho^(1/9) Phi is f = A (1+r^2/a^2)^(-5), a = (10-d)/(2M), g (s0 A^2)^(1/9) = 20M/(10-d), energy per norm -(8M/9)(9-d)/(10-d), the same tail exponent for every d, checked on explicit Clifford matrices. Independent verification (Opus agent, 10 November 2026)

Verified exactly: the counts 18,564, 3,838,380, 64,512,240; the second moments in closed form, lambda = k(k+m)/(2m(m+1)) with m = d+1 (57/28, 697/56, 11/4); circumradii and rms radii; the face table (sites with s nonzero barycentric parts number C(m,s) C(k-1,s-1)) and interior fractions 2.489%, 28.855%, 0.5467%; the branch by hand derivation (p = 5, a = (10-d)/(2M), g(s0 A^2)^(1/9) = 20M/(10-d), E/N = -(8M/9)(9-d)/(10-d), valid for d < 10, convergent for d < 18); both branch scripts rerun with residuals of 1e-11 or below and quadrature agreeing to about 1e-13.

Corrections accepted:

  1. 64,512,240 = binom(n+d-1, d) has exactly three solutions for d = 1..40: d = 1 (n = 64,512,240), d = 10 (n = 23, side 22) and d = 22 (n = 11, side 10). The tau is the only complete simplex of that count with 2 <= d <= 21; in particular none in six directions. The Case now says one matter-domain dimension, ten.
  2. 18,564 counts weak compositions of 12 into 7 parts (zeros allowed); positive compositions number 462, the interior count.
  3. The simplex is the programme's reading of the label count (Case section 3: 'a simplex count in exponent labels', slack integer is bookkeeping). The isotropic second moments hold in the regular embedding, which treats the slack coordinate symmetrically; in the corner embedding of section 3 (d unit axes, slack as bookkeeping) the covariance has a (d+1):1 anisotropy and the electron's per-axis value is 3.49. Isotropy of the second moment follows for any arrangement with S_{d+1} symmetry. The fourth moment is anisotropic (10.0 to 18.5 across directions, electron run). The Case now states the premise and the embedding.
  4. The branch sits at frequency -M, the continuum edge, so the tail is algebraic (f ~ r^-10). E/N is zero at d = 9 and positive for 9 < d < 10. At d = 10 there is no solution of this form: a = 0, the required coupling diverges, E/N diverges; it exists only as the limit d -> 10. The Case now says so. The explicit Clifford check covers d = 4, 6, 8; the statement for other d rests on the analytic derivation.
