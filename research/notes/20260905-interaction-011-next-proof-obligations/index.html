11. Next proof obligations

The next pair calculation must use actual isolated field patterns at their own frequencies and evolve both through the same causal field law. Retain their extended tails, complete spatial and orientation structure, relative phases and reciprocal deformation.

Derive forces and any changes of shape from that evolution with complete energy and momentum accounting. Determine how the response depends on bound/free orientation and the detector's resolved coordinates. No centre-coincidence requirement, coordinate-mirroring constraint or independent random-offset ensemble is supplied in advance.

If a reduced current or kernel is useful, derive its maps, kinetic normalization, causal history and observable interpretation. Electromagnetic identification and any reaction rate remain outputs to calculate, rather than assumptions fixing the interaction's dimensionality.
