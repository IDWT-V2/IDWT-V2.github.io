Counting mode tools

Updated September 8, 2026

Exact counting and spectral tools with explicit operator, normalization, and occupation assumptions.

All physical coordinates belong to one nested chain of ordinary macroscopic spatial submanifolds. The scalar models and coefficient spaces here are mathematical tools whose physical application requires a specified operator.

  • Simplex band level moments — derives exact mean and variance for a uniform cumulative band.
  • Weighted mode evaluation — states the necessary and sufficient point-evaluation bound.
  • Simplex heat trace — bounds a specified positive spectral tower and its truncation error.
  • Round sphere dirac counts — keeps eigenvalues and dimension-dependent spinor multiplicities explicit.
  • Normalized oscillator modes — supplies normalized functions, shell convolutions, and weighted overlaps.
