Self-mass from a hierarchy of currents

Updated September 13, 2026

Summary: One canonically normalized nonlinear action generates a simplex self-source and a common lower-order probe response; its shell weights and physical carrier remain inverse-model premises.

A self-interaction that also determines coupling

The target is a single field law that gives different masses and similar coupling responses within a spatial depth. The construction below generates its self-source from the same nonlinear interaction used for a second field pattern. It contains no separately prescribed quadratic mass term. Its weighting is reconstructed from the desired simplex rule, however; it has not been derived from the existing master scalar interaction.

The fixed-order current construction supplies the exact contractions used here. Retaining the whole hierarchy allows those currents to generate the isolated self-source as well.

Three self-interaction laws

Other homogeneous coherent actions retain the same occupied mass while giving different complete responses. The chosen self-mass sequence does not uniquely specify the interaction.

Coupling and the remaining operator question

Because the grades enter the combined currents RkR_kRk​ in (7), the action also fixes their reciprocal interaction. A fixed probe of degree MMM sees an identical coherent target current through every order it can access whenever N≥MN\ge MN≥M. The complete probe calculation states the required density and ray conditions, the full target response, and the root law's delicate vacuum limit.

This supplies a joint inverse model for mass and coupling. It still needs the master field to produce the canonical carrier, the shell weighting, the scalar law and the selected spatial states. Merely counting independent copies of a field does not do so: those copies contribute to the kinetic norm as well as to the potential.

The full-field localization condition now excludes flat finite-norm stationary fields in the convex hierarchy's positive-current cone under its stated virial hypotheses. A fixed split into positive and negative insertion channels has the same obstruction. The calculation retains spatially changing sign projectors explicitly, giving a necessary geometric term for a possible textured solution.

Verification

The later localized current construction retains the hierarchy while adding matrix partners and a local binding interaction. It supplies actual localized spatial solutions, their complete boost stress and an exact frequency-proportional canonical inertia at a common norm. This is a different reconstructed action: it does not remove the positivity obstruction for the action studied above, nor derive the shell weights or physical mode carrier from the master field.

An explicit complementary Dirac reduction now derives a spectral square-root source from specified canonical normal occupations and checks its complete response. Its actual scalar projection counts the populated normal band; restoring both self-terms identifies the remaining band matching, energy and occupation questions.
