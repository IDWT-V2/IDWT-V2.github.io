Periodic spatial source response

Updated September 9, 2026

Summary: The full response of a two-frequency spatial Dirac field reduces to a frequency-indexed moment equation that retains scalar-center motion, normal propagation and the conditions for source-sideband cancellation.

Follow the complete periodic background

The two-frequency spatial field has an unchanging scalar binding source and a changing number-current pattern. Its linearized field equation nevertheless has periodic coefficients. The response can be derived without assigning its bound frequency to an external forcing frequency or discarding one of its two components.

All coordinates describe ordinary macroscopic spatial directions of the one nested manifold. The bound coordinates are x∈Rdx\in\mathbb R^dx∈Rd and yyy is one existing containing direction. The field-component indices, source moments and temporal harmonics below are mathematical descriptions of that same field; they add no physical spatial dimensions or particles. The present normal momentum is along the direction used to construct the opposite-frequency partner.

A periodic field can have a simpler scalar readout at rest

Periodic field coefficients do not guarantee a nonzero sideband in every observable. At q=0q=0q=0, let PEP_EPE​ be the complete spectral projector of HbH_bHb​ at eigenvalue EEE in a finite spatial regulator. C−†PEC+=(C−†P−EC+)T.(11) \boxed{C_-^\dagger P_EC_+ =\left(C_-^\dagger P_{-E}C_+\right)^T.} \tag{11} C−†​PE​C+​=(C−†​P−E​C+​)T.​(11)

The negative sideband has the corresponding conjugate relation. This criterion follows by expanding both resolvents in spectral projectors. It checks an identity at every pole rather than inferring cancellation from a few sampled frequencies. A continuum statement would require the corresponding equality of spectral measures and valid operator domains.

For the centered Cartesian radial examples at depths three, five and six, all complete projectors satisfy (11) for the scalar second moments and dipoles. The direct source response has no ω±2M\omega\pm2Mω±2M scalar output at rest, even though its field perturbation contains both carriers and its full field operator is periodic. This is a verified property of the declared regulators; it is not an assumption about an arbitrary source, an arbitrary nonlocal probe or a physical photon current.

Propagation and the second object's response remain essential

In the same spatial calculations, normal momentum makes the scalar shape sidebands nonzero. Their first small-qqq term is linear in qqq; the positive and negative normal momenta give opposite sideband amplitudes for the tested preparation. Thus the rest cancellation does not authorize using a stationary scalar susceptibility for the whole propagating periodic field.

A reciprocal nested interaction uses the curvature of the common energy, including its off-diagonal shape restriction and scalar-center terms. The readjusted nested pair supplies the stationary source requirement. If two periodic backgrounds have different bound frequencies, their common response generally needs both frequency indices; a single period cannot be assumed for an irrational frequency ratio. Both objects' spatial polarizations and their feedback must be retained.

The source-frequency analysis explains why an actual photon output map is still necessary. A scalar moment cancellation is a statement about that readout, while a photon could sample a different spatial current with different surviving sidebands. Spatial current response derives those output columns from the same field and verifies the normal continuity identity before and after source feedback.

Verification

Complete spectral projectors check (11). Dense normal Dirac resolvents independently check (6)–(7). Explicit time-dependent forced fields retain both spatial polarizations; their temporal Fourier transforms recover the predicted moment sidebands. Matched full-field and moment harmonic cutoffs check (9)–(10), retaining ordinary and conjugate fluctuations. Full-period evolution independently verifies the reconstructed growing modes, their Floquet multipliers and preservation of the canonical form. Reports use timestamped filenames and checks remain active under Python optimization. These calculations establish the stated algebra and regulated response; they do not identify physical particle masses or interaction rates.
