Verification and mathematical context

scalar_born_response.py implements the full projectors, cubic vertex, stable angular detuning and both time-window kernels. scalar_born_checks.py runs 1,205 checks of full Clifford modules at every spatial dimension from two through ten, including nearly collinear bands and both massive and massless detuning identities. An independent spatial Fourier extraction retains both coherent permutations. Direct forced evolution of the complete signed Dirac mode verifies its nonzero finite-time response and its normalization. Common physical rotations preserve the full vector source.

The broader nonlinear theory already exploits related scalar null structure. Bournaveas and Candy, Theorem 1.1 prove small-data global existence and scattering for the massless cubic Soler and Thirring equations at the critical homogeneous Sobolev regularity in two and three spatial dimensions. Their Remark 1.4 explains the inhomogeneous version using conserved L2L^2L2 norm. That theorem is not a global-existence claim for the higher-dimensional finite-amplitude preparations considered here.

These frequency restrictions constrain a specified classical scalar term. They supply no charge assignment, bound-particle identification, or observed coupling rate. The conserved spatial charge question remains a separate physical requirement.
