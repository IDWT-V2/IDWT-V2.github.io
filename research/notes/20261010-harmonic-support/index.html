Updated October 10, 2026

The sawtooth calculation establishes a useful conditional connection: adjacent occupied harmonics produce a strong source beat at the sector fundamental even when the selected mass-frequency is a much higher harmonic. The connection depends on which harmonics are occupied and how the physical current reads their spatial modes. The square-wave comparison makes those conditions explicit.
