38. Correction: do not extrapolate ordinary 3D field equations into the higher sector

A conceptual error was identified in the electromagnetic audit.

The earlier Green-function test assumed that if the electron has six real spatial directions, then electromagnetism in that sector should be obtained by taking the ordinary Maxwell/Laplacian equations and merely changing the spatial dimension from three to six. That assumption is not licensed by IDWT.

IDWT's claim is instead that the master higher-dimensional dynamics and their projection must produce the lower-dimensional laws actually observed. Therefore one cannot begin with the three-dimensional effective field equation, extrapolate it to six dimensions, and then reject the result because that extrapolation no longer reproduces the three-dimensional effective law.

The correct logical direction is

higher-dimensional master operator⟶sector projection / marginalization⟶observed lower-dimensional propagator. \boxed{ \text{higher-dimensional master operator} \longrightarrow \text{sector projection / marginalization} \longrightarrow \text{observed lower-dimensional propagator}. } higher-dimensional master operator⟶sector projection / marginalization⟶observed lower-dimensional propagator.​

not

observed 3D equation⟶replace 3 by 6⟶compare back to 3D. \text{observed 3D equation} \longrightarrow \text{replace }3\text{ by }6 \longrightarrow \text{compare back to 3D}. observed 3D equation⟶replace 3 by 6⟶compare back to 3D.

Consequently the calculation

G6(R)∝R−4,E6(R)∝R−5 G_6(R)\propto R^{-4}, \qquad E_6(R)\propto R^{-5} G6​(R)∝R−4,E6​(R)∝R−5

has only the following valid status:

direct slice restriction of the flat six-dimensional static kernel cannot reproduce the three-dimensional Coulomb potential. \boxed{ \text{direct slice restriction of the flat six-dimensional static kernel cannot reproduce the three-dimensional Coulomb potential.} } direct slice restriction of the flat six-dimensional static kernel cannot reproduce the three-dimensional Coulomb potential.​

It does not show that electromagnetic influence cannot involve all six electron directions.

September 8 correction: the previous wording also excluded a general observable projection, which was too strong. The Green function marginal gives an explicit counterexample: full unweighted integration of the normalized flat six-dimensional kernel over three coordinates produces 1/(4πr)1/(4\pi r)1/(4πr). This is a conditional static identity; the physical projection, normalization, and complete electromagnetic dynamics still need to be derived.

Likewise, the pure-pullback result

A(6)=π62∗A(2) A^{(6)}=\pi_{62}^*A^{(2)} A(6)=π62∗​A(2)

with no vertical legs is only a theorem about that particular ansatz. IDWT is not required to use a pure pullback. A genuine higher-dimensional connection may have mixed or vertical structure while its observable projection still yields the familiar electromagnetic law.

This substantially reopens the unobserved-steering question. The task is no longer to ask whether ordinary six-dimensional Maxwell theory focuses unobserved coordinates. The task is to derive the IDWT higher-dimensional electromagnetic/master operator and prove that its projection simultaneously:

  1. reproduces the observed Coulomb/Maxwell behavior;
  2. acts consistently on a depth-six electron;
  3. determines whether visible and unobserved relative motion are dynamically correlated;
  4. preserves photon transversality and its depth-two bound oscillation plane, while allowing phase momentum in the containing spatial directions;
  5. introduces no arbitrary phenomenological corrections.

The previous scalar-contact result remains unchanged: the order-zero scalar quartic acts only at overlap and does not by itself generate pre-contact focusing. What is retracted is the stronger electromagnetic no-go based on naive dimensional extrapolation.
