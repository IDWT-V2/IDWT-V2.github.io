Full Observer Rotation of the Actual Count Fields

Updated October 12, 2026

The same explicit fields that solve the conditional count-clock equation have a simple full observer transformation law. At maintained matter depths three, four, five, six and ten, their observer Spin3 Casimir is exactly 3/4. This includes rotation of the spatial profile and the spin connection, rather than applying a matrix to the spinor while holding its spatial dependence fixed.

This is an exact half-spin transformation property of the actual fields. The balanced classical preparations have zero mean rotation Noether charge. The representation result and the mechanical charge are different quantities; identifying an observed particle spin requires their physical interpretation and readout. No probability-cloud description of electron position is introduced.

1. One Center-Fixing Observer Action

Use the conditional round-sphere geometry and the actual kernels in the reduced-carrier construction. Choose a kernel center p and three orthogonal tangent directions there. The observer Spin3 rotates those three ordinary spatial directions and fixes p. Its extension to the sphere is an ordinary isometry, and its lift to the spinor bundle is the canonical spin lift. Columns are scalar-labelled spinor fields in this calculation. A different transformation of the column label is a different representation premise.

The positive Dirac projector Pi_l has an equivariant kernel:

UgΠl(⋅,p)C=Πl(⋅,p)Sp(g)C,gp=p.(1) U_g\Pi_l(\cdot,p)C=\Pi_l(\cdot,p)S_p(g)C, \qquad g p=p. \tag{1} Ug​Πl​(⋅,p)C=Πl​(⋅,p)Sp​(g)C,gp=p.(1)

Here U_g acts on both the argument and fibre, and S_p is the spin lift at p. This follows because the Dirac operator commutes with isometries, so its spectral projector does too. The zonal map I_l:C maps to E_l(C) is the normalized kernel Pi_l(.,p). Its value at p is sqrt(g_l) C, making the map injective. Thus (1) is an actual intertwining subspace of the spatial shell, not only a list of representations allowed elsewhere in it.

For three tangent directions, use the Hermitian generators

Every spinor seed component therefore belongs to an observer spin-half copy. Extra spinor components give multiplicity, rather than larger observer spin within this center-fixing kernel subspace. The full spatial shell has other states; this argument does not remove them dynamically.

3. The Count Map Preserves the Full Observer Casimir

The massive-shell map Z_l=a_l+b_l beta commutes with the isometry lift, because beta is parallel and rotation invariant. Hence the actual stationary count field obeys

One explicit family now has both the count clock and the full half-spin transformation, with no new coefficient for the observer spin. This is conditional on the sphere, kernel preparation, spin lift and effective count action. The kernel preparation selects an intertwining subspace; the action has not been shown to select it uniquely among all stationary spatial shapes or higher-spin shell components.

4. Casimir and Classical Rotation Charge Must Remain Distinct

The normalized projector map is an isometry from seed space to the positive shell. The massive map retains its integrated norm because beta maps that shell into the orthogonal negative Dirac shell. Thus the complete canonical rotation Noether charge is

La=Qtr⁡C†TaC.(6) L_a=Q\operatorname{tr}C^\dagger T_a C. \tag{6} La​=QtrC†Ta​C.(6)

For the balanced seeds, all these bivector expectations vanish. Therefore L_a=0, even though (5) has Casimir 3/4. A Casimir acting on a field is not the square of that field's integrated mean Noether charge. Local spin-density planes in the preceding note likewise do not replace the orbital term in (3).

The balanced nonlinear family is invariant under spin rotations, but generally not under arbitrary complex superposition. Its ambient linear field subspace carries the half-spin representation; not every vector in that subspace is asserted to solve the same nonlinear stationary source. Deriving a measured spin magnitude, polarization response or magnetic moment remains an additional physical task. These limitations do not alter the exact full transformation identity.

5. The Depth-Two Control and Reproducibility

Depth two has only a center-fixing Spin2 circle. Its seed generator squares to 1/4, rather than supplying three center-fixing axes with Casimir 3/4. The full Spin3 isometry of a round two-sphere moves the kernel center and is a different action. This calculation does not derive photon polarization or the observed integer spin of the massive depth-two family.

count-carrier-observer-rotation.py and its report pass 800 evaluations over all fourteen maintained assignments at every occupied depth. The actual spatial Dirac residual is below 5.2e-15 relatively. Full orbital-plus-spin intertwining residuals are below 1.4e-14. Both terms of (3) are retained; the observer seed Casimirs and mean generator expectations are exact in the Clifford matrices.

The mathematical result is therefore more concrete than unrelated mass and spin availability checks: the actual conditional count solutions have the declared observer half-spin transformation. It supplies common model compatibility, rather than independent preference for a particular matter depth or a complete physical spin derivation.
