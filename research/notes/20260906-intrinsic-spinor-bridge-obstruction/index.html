Intrinsic spinor bridge obstruction

Updated September 8, 2026

Summary: Characteristic classes exclude a full-rank linear identification of intrinsic CP3 half-spinors with homogeneous coordinates.

Three different objects

The base is Q = CP3. A homogeneous lift Z belongs to the tautological line L = O(-1) inside the constant bundle Q × C4. An intrinsic Dirac field at q belongs instead to the spinor bundle of TQ, possibly with a specified twist. The model R6 used in CP3 = SO(6)/U(3) is also not the intrinsic real tangent bundle over CP3. The latter has Euler class 4H³ and first Pontryagin class 4H²; the constant model bundle has neither. Local Clifford matrices alone cannot supply a global identification.

Half-spin characteristic classes

For a spin Kähler manifold the intrinsic spinor bundle is Λ^(0,*) ⊗ K^(1/2). This standard bundle construction is stated in Moore, Royston and Van den Bleeken, Appendix E, equation E.1. Here K^(1/2) = O(-2). The Hermitian metric identifies the smooth complex bundle of (0,1)-forms with the holomorphic tangent bundle. Thus even chirality has the smooth bundle type O(-2) ⊕ Ω¹(2). The Euler sequence gives [Ω¹(2)] = 4[O(1)] − [O(2)] in K theory. Consequently

ch⁡(Σ+)=e−2H+4eH−e2H=4+2H2−2H3. \operatorname{ch}(\Sigma^+) =e^{-2H}+4e^H-e^{2H}=4+2H^2-2H^3. ch(Σ+)=e−2H+4eH−e2H=4+2H2−2H3.

Opposite chirality changes the sign of the degree-six term, not ch₂. In this convention c₁ = 0, c₂ = −2H², c₃ = −4H³. Twisting by O(k) gives c₁ = 4kH. An isomorphism with the trivial rank-four bundle forces k = 0, then fails because ch₂ = 2H² ≠ 0. This excludes a nowhere-singular full-rank complex-linear bundle identification, including any electromagnetic line twist. It does not exclude local frames or a bundle map that becomes singular.

What remains possible

A selected spinor line is a weaker object than the whole spinor bundle. The canonical even Kähler vacuum line is O(-2), while the homogeneous tautological line is O(-1). They are not linearly isomorphic, although O(-2) = L² supplies a possible nonlinear relation. A chosen twist can also change the selected line without trivializing the whole half-spin bundle. Neither possibility by itself is the projected master-field dynamics.

A subsequent construction makes one selected-line possibility explicit: twist by O(L+2) so the degree-zero spinor component is O(L), then embed the demo's holomorphic sections there. They are spatial Dirac zero modes; an added, consistently gauged Killing drive is required for the actual orbit and its transport current. This preserves the full-bundle obstruction and does not establish that the master action selects this twist or drive.

There is a second independent requirement. A fiber Hamiltonian H(q) can have a unique eigenline at every q without preferring any q. If homogeneity gives H(gq) = U_g H(q) U_g⁻¹ for a transitive group action, its lowest eigenvalue ε₀(q) is constant. Fiber selection then produces no base potential minimum. A nuclear background or another derived symmetry-breaking source must enter the q-dependent action before a preferred base lock can be inferred.

Reproduction

Run python3 -B experiments/intrinsic_spinor_bundle_checks.py from the repository root. This checks characteristic-class algebra, not the existence of a nonlinear bridge. The main pure-spinor module now states this obstruction explicitly.
