Spatial current response of a two-frequency field

Updated September 9, 2026

Summary: The spatial number current retains polarization-dependent frequency sidebands that can cancel in the scalar binding source, with an exact continuity identity and the full source feedback preserved.

The source and the measured current are different bilinears

The periodic source equation follows the complete spatial Dirac perturbation through its scalar binding moments. The same perturbation also changes the spatial number current. A cancellation in one readout does not automatically cancel the other.

All spatial coordinates belong to the one nested manifold of ordinary macroscopic dimensions. Let xxx span the ddd bound directions and yyy be the existing containing direction used for the normal partner. For

i∂tΨ=[−iαi∂i−iαy∂y+βm]Ψ,m∈R,(1) i\partial_t\Psi= [-i\alpha_i\partial_i-i\alpha_y\partial_y+\beta\mathfrak m]\Psi, \qquad \mathfrak m\in\mathbb R, \tag{1} i∂t​Ψ=[−iαi​∂i​−iαy​∂y​+βm]Ψ,m∈R,(1)

direct differentiation gives

∂tρ+∂iji+∂yjy=0,ρ=Ψ†Ψ,jμ=Ψ†αμΨ.(2) \boxed{\partial_t\rho+\partial_i j_i+\partial_y j_y=0, \qquad \rho=\Psi^\dagger\Psi,\quad j_\mu=\Psi^\dagger\alpha_\mu\Psi.} \tag{2} ∂t​ρ+∂i​ji​+∂y​jy​=0,ρ=Ψ†Ψ,jμ​=Ψ†αμ​Ψ.​(2)

The scalar potential cancels pointwise because it is Hermitian and multiplies the field at that point. Its coefficient may depend on the field's spatial moments without changing this identity. These are the number density and Noether current of the specified field action. Identifying their physical photon projection requires the additional current map discussed in source frequencies.

Preserve source feedback before reading the current

Let hjh_jhj​ and fjf_jfj​ be scalar moment and force amplitudes at ω+2jM\omega+2jMω+2jM, and let P,K\mathbb P,\mathbb KP,K be the complete moment response and curvature from the periodic source equation. The output retains every induced source harmonic. A matched full-field frequency cutoff gives an independent implementation: solve the complete ordinary/conjugate field matrix, then apply the current readout directly. Interior harmonics satisfy (6) after feedback. Cutoff boundaries must use the same retained field harmonics in both calculations.

For two interacting objects, their common action supplies the cross curvature and both responses. Equation (9) describes how to read an output from that solved field; it does not replace the partner's response with a fixed detector or introduce an additional interaction particle.

Verification

periodic_current_checks.py uses the actual centered Cartesian Dirac fields, independent occupied polarizations and one common interaction coefficient. It checks the full normal continuity relation, the analytic rest-current residues and the distinction between integrated and resolved spatial outputs.

A complete ordinary/conjugate harmonic solve independently verifies the current after source feedback. Direct time-dependent spatial bilinears and their Fourier transform reproduce the output sidebands. Reports have timestamped filenames and verification remains active under Python optimization. These results identify geometric selection within the stated model; the physical photon map and normalized interaction rates remain open.
