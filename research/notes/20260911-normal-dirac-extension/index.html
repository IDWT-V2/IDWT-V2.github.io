Summary: A full Dirac field on a conditional spatial half-cylinder induces the absolute tangential Dirac operator, while its kinetic norm cancels a constant bulk mass factor and local scalar contact excites complementary normal profiles.

Obtain a nonlocal operator from ordinary spatial continuation

The Killing-spinor transfer gives a Hermitian scalar-mass candidate when its polynomial is evaluated on the absolute spatial Dirac operator:

It has the desired simplex eigenvalues on both Dirac signs, under the specified round geometry. The absolute value makes it spatially nonlocal. A natural question is whether ordinary spatial continuation of the same field can supply that absolute value.

An exact product-space calculation does supply it as a map from a field's value to its perpendicular derivative. However, that same continuation induces a nontrivial time-kinetic norm. A constant bulk scalar coefficient remains constant after canonical normalization. A local nonlinear scalar source also drives normal profiles outside the initially selected decaying profile.

These statements concern a conditional geometry and boundary problem. Take a six-dimensional spatial manifold Q6Q_6Q6​ and one ordinary perpendicular coordinate y≥0y\ge0y≥0, with metric dy2+gQ6dy^2+g_{Q_6}dy2+gQ6​​. The six tangential directions remain equivalent in kind. No dimension is compactified and no small geometric radius is assumed. This is a local normal-direction benchmark, not an assignment of a seven-dimensional particle or a derivation of the full six-to-ten nesting. Multiple normal directions require their complete Clifford structure and cannot be supplied by independent scalar copies.

A nested spatial submanifold does not itself impose a hard boundary, a one-sided domain or a decaying boundary condition. Those are explicit extra assumptions here. All retained and complementary profiles below are modes of the same Dirac field.

This concerns this product-space construction. It does not exclude different normal geometries or a fully solved nonlinear profile. It also does not replace IDWT's cylindrical free continuation by an already established localized normal profile.
