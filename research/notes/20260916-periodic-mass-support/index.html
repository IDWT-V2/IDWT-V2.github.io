Research / Mass and normalization

Periodic motion, preserved mass ratios and reciprocal support

Updated September 16, 2026

conditional calculation

A supporting pattern can keep moving while retaining a common frequency multiplier across a particle family. This can be demonstrated in the endpoint operator already obtained from the spatial carrier. The same calculation also determines the force exerted on that pattern. Preserving the frequency ratios does not automatically make the supporting motion a solution of its own equations.

The results below retain the complete two-state wave evolution, relative phase, a reacting positive geometric oscillator and the temporal frame terms. The reduction from the entire moving normal field is still open. All coordinates describe proposed modes of the same field; no additional interaction particle or particle-exchange mechanism is introduced.

Mass remains the selected particle frequency in equivalent units. A Floquet phase of a prescribed periodically varying subsystem is a candidate frequency, not yet the frequency of a complete particle state. Its branch, other temporal harmonics, supporting energy and inertia must also be identified. No frequency is chosen modulo the drive frequency merely to obtain a desired mass.
