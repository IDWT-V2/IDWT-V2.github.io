Research / Mass and normalization

The linked shape response after geometric normalization

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The linked mass carrier gives different frequencies with nearly equal absolute shape responses within a depth. The coefficient written in its polynomial coordinate also becomes much smaller with increasing depth. Normalizing that coordinate by its actual geometric metric changes the second observation substantially: the normalized coefficient is already within a few percent of one common value across depths 3 through 10.

This comparison retains the entire finite wave branch and its spatial normal modes. It uses the stated positive geometric metric, whose physical coefficient remains to be derived from the one field. It does not establish measured interaction strengths or neutrino suppression.
