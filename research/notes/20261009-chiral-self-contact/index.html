A Parity-Even Self-Contact Produces the Occupied-Sector Hodge Rule

Updated October 9, 2026

The normal-color Hodge current has a more direct local-action formulation. Pair each real spatial chiral current with itself using exterior multiplication, and weight its two chiralities by their oriented Clifford signs. The resulting scalar energy is positive at three/four and identically zero at the other occupied depths. It equals the earlier positive current action there, including its complete field variation.

This is a concrete candidate interaction with a common geometric rule. Choosing this self-contact as the field's interaction remains a physical premise. The calculation neither derives the complete strong force nor excludes other allowed interactions.

7. Physical Scope

One local polynomial contact now supplies the maintained-sector cancellation, positive source energy, both spatial chiralities, independent color/spin at three/four and a legitimate three-shadow contraction. These properties reuse the same spatial Clifford and exterior algebra.

The choice of self-wedge contact is still a hypothesis. Generic metric-norm interactions, complementary Hodge response and a separately prescribed normal kinetic connection are not removed. Noncommuting matrix-valued forms also need their own ordering analysis; the cancellation proved here uses real commuting current components and the invariant diagonal color contraction.

The action's existing scalar and spin-dependent pair terms remain. Recovering a color-Casimir term does not fix its physical force sign, remove scalar offsets or reproduce confinement. The current preparation must also belong to the full spatial kinetics. These are specific remaining dynamical questions, not defects in the exact cancellation identity.
