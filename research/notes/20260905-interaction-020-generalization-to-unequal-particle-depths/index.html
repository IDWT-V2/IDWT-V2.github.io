20. Generalization to unequal particle depths

For a conditional uniform-extension control, write ordinary spatial coordinates as (x,y)(x,y)(x,y) and suppose one field-density profile is independent of yyy:

ρa(x,y)=ρa(x). \rho_a(x,y)=\rho_a(x). ρa​(x,y)=ρa​(x).

For an integrable second density and a specifically chosen scalar overlap, iterated integration gives

Iab=∫dx ρa(x)∫dy ρb(x,y). I_{ab}=\int dx\,\rho_a(x)\int dy\,\rho_b(x,y). Iab​=∫dxρa​(x)∫dyρb​(x,y).

This identity requires the stated profiles, measure and integrability. It concerns that scalar overlap. Uniformity in a direction means that profile has no independently localized centre offset in that direction; it does not make the complete interaction independent of the other pattern's polarization or shape.

The actual tails and their bound/free orientation determine which coordinates affect a pair response. No encounter-suppression exponent or strength hierarchy follows from the depth difference alone. In particular, an assumed scalar marginal does not derive an electromagnetic or weak interaction. Those identifications require the complete same-field dynamics and a causal observable response.
