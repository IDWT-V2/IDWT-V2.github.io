Updated September 11, 2026

Uniform spectral stability and its limits

The local blocks have frequencies ±λ\pm\lambda±λ and ±ν\pm\nu±ν, all real. Together with the self-adjoint complement, this excludes exponential linear eigenmodes for every spatial polarization at the uniform solution. The constant phase and norm-frequency pair supplies the usual zero Jordan block; fixing the norm and quotienting the overall phase removes it. This is a spectral statement, not a nonlinear stability theorem.

More generally, replace the local scalar convolution by a real isotropic kernel normalized to preserve constants. Moving flat backgrounds

Moving nonlinear scalar planes derive the covariant flat-space continuation with the proper rest density and both conjugate modes retained. Its scalar response reduces to (6) at rest, while a spatially constant probe has a different response when the background carries momentum. The scalar-null plane supplies the continuous zero-scalar threshold, including its instability boundary. These are flat unbound controls, distinct from a global CPmmm assignment.
