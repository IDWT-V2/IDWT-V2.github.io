An exact flat-ball count clock and its conformal characterization

Updated October 14, 2026

A scalar classical field on an ordinary spatial ball can have both the maintained cumulative mode count and the corresponding simplex frequency law. The construction below is inverse: the ball, its density and stiffness family, and the relation between its spatial clock and time evolution are specified. Requiring the exact infinite squared-count law then fixes the density exponent within that family. The conformal characterization supplies a second constraint and useful static and nonlinear identities.

The active depth is d∈{2,3,4,5,6,10}d\in\{2,3,4,5,6,10\}d∈{2,3,4,5,6,10} in a shared ordinary flat containing space, currently modeled by R10\mathbb R^{10}R10. A depth classifies a pattern's dimensional involvement; it is not an independent physical sector manifold. The auxiliary spheres below are mathematical lifts with explicitly counted extra coordinates. They are not physical embeddings in that containing space and introduce no compactification or Kaluza--Klein tower. Spatial field profiles and spectral coefficients describe classical internal structure; they do not replace an electron's deterministic six-dimensional trajectory or define a position-probability law.

6. Exact surviving claim and missing physics

The surviving claim is an explicit positive local scalar wave action on a prescribed d-dimensional ball in ordinary flat coordinates, having the exact maintained cumulative mode count and exact simplex frequency law simultaneously for every N>=0 at all six maintained depths. It is an inverse spatial constitutive model. It improves the oscillator's squared-action spatial order from 4d to 2d, but does not derive the master law.

Its interior high-wave-number dispersion scales omega approximately |k|^d, so group speed is unbounded for d>1. Positivity and second order in time do not establish finite propagation speed. A low-frequency effective use would require a demonstrated regime and a causal completion; the exact infinite ladder cannot simply be asserted for a bounded-speed first-order parent.

The support radius, position, radial material coefficients, moving support and sustaining stress remain prescribed. Deriving them as responding components or bilinears of Psi_infinity is the next physical task. Their omitted dynamics may alter both normalization and spectrum. No mediator field or independent particles have been introduced.

The scalar construction supplies spatial shape degeneracies, not spin, vector polarization, electric charge or a photon zero. Its lowest frequency is mu_d at every d. A physical photon remains a separate control to realize. Arbitrary amplitudes are allowed and do not change (10), while total energy is quadratic in amplitude; no amplitude is fitted to a mass. Complete translation inertia and the mass-energy consistency check require a responding full action.

To prepare a finite field in R10 when d<10, a normalized transverse profile may multiply the ball mode, but its support and derivatives must enter a completed action. A law blind to those coordinates leaves an unlimited spectator degeneracy. Uniform unused directions instead give an infinite-volume norm. This construction therefore supplies the active d-direction carrier, not a complete finite-energy R10 particle. It does not replace deterministic electron center trajectories with scalar waves.
