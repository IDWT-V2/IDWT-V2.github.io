This is a different space of spatial fields from the holomorphic tangent-mode band: the line degree here stays fixed while nonholomorphic spatial harmonics are added. Consequence for the contact derivation

There is now a fixed-connection band of actual spatial functions with the exact simplex dimension up to one sector constant. Its polarized physical point operator has the matching eigenvalue. These are conditional mathematical constructions with a canonical spatial norm.

The remaining step is to derive that particular point interaction and its occupied full spinor field from the master action. The tested smooth local scalar source does not supply it directly. A different self-consistent spatial state remains possible and must be solved with its complementary components.

The consistent-band analysis explains why prescribing a point form and deriving a local self-interaction are separate operations. The spatial scalar Dirac equation provides the full variational setting for continuing the occupied field.
