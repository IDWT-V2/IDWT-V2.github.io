Chiral pseudoscalar mass term

Updated September 8, 2026

Conditional symmetry protection

The V2 hadronic problem needs a mechanism for light pseudoscalar masses. A useful adjacent benchmark is a leading chiral effective action. Assume spontaneous SU(3)L×SU(3)R→SU(3)VSU(3)_L\times SU(3)_R\to SU(3)_VSU(3)L​×SU(3)R​→SU(3)V​ breaking, positive light quark masses, and momenta and masses within its low-energy expansion. The quadratic mass vanishes with MMM. This is the explicit cancellation pattern supplied by chiral symmetry in this action. In the two-flavour restriction it gives F2mπ2=−(mu+md)⟨qˉq⟩0F^2m_\pi^2=-(m_u+m_d)\langle\bar q q\rangle_0F2mπ2​=−(mu​+md​)⟨qˉ​q⟩0​ with the per-flavour convention ⟨qˉq⟩0=−F2B0\langle\bar q q\rangle_0=-F^2B_0⟨qˉ​q⟩0​=−F2B0​. This condensate relation uses chiral-limit parameters and holds to leading order in the light masses.

The leading mass relations and their scale limitation are reviewed in PDG, Quark Masses, §60.5.2. The scheme dependence of B0B_0B0​ compensates that of the quark masses. Electromagnetic effects and higher orders require additional terms. This benchmark identifies what a V2 pseudoscalar mechanism must reproduce if it realizes these chiral premises; it does not determine FFF or B0B_0B0​.
