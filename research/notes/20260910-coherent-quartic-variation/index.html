Coherent states are precisely the sharp extrema

Orthogonal projection cannot increase a norm. Consequently

A tensor with a pure one-index contraction factors across that index; symmetry forces all remaining indices to carry the same vector. Finite-mode nonlinear orbital stability

vanishes exactly on the compact coherent orbit. It has a strictly positive minimum on every closed subset a positive distance from that orbit. Norm and energy conservation therefore imply nonlinear orbital stability of the coherent set under (3), for either nonzero sign of κ\kappaκ. The polynomial vector field exists globally because the norm remains bounded. This argument supplies finite-mode nonlinear stability, rather than inferring it solely from real linearized frequencies.

The conclusion concerns the whole coherent set, including phase and ray rotations. It is not attraction to one prepared ray. Physical spatial variation, omitted bands, Dirac components, nuclear sources and additional interaction terms require their own stability analysis.
