63.3 Ricci curvature is not automatically the locking Hessian

The intrinsic identity

Ric⁡♯=4I9 \operatorname{Ric}^{\sharp}=4I_9 Ric♯=4I9​

and the projector mismatch Hessian are different mathematical objects.

The former is fixed by the homogeneous geometry. The latter is fixed only after an action functional and its normalization have been specified.

Therefore the tempting chain

Ric⁡=4g⇒κ^=4 \operatorname{Ric}=4g \quad\Rightarrow\quad \widehat\kappa=4 Ric=4g⇒κ=4

is not valid without an action-level theorem.

Likewise,

being the reciprocal of the Ricci eigenvalue remains an exact and interesting normalization relation, but not yet a derived dynamical identity.
