Spinor Polarization and Spatial Circulation

Updated November 3, 2026

conditional research

The spatial spinor profile already used in the sector case supplies a concrete connection between polarization and internal field circulation. Its seed orientation determines an actual current of that profile. The current can be nonzero locally while its integral is exactly zero. These are conditional results for the specified field equation; identifying the current with electric current, energy flow or the particle's fundamental clock remains open.

All coordinates refer to the same ten ordinary spatial directions plus time. A depth-d calculation describes dependence on d of those directions. It does not introduce another physical manifold. Field components and their squared norm are classical field data, not a probability distribution for the electron's position. The electron's centre trajectory is a separate physical variable.

3. Which Depths Give Which Circulation?

At depth four every nonzero chiral seed gives B2=−s2I4B^2=-s^2I_4B2=−s2I4​. In the positive-chirality convention of the connections note, B is self-dual. It has two rotation planes and form norm squared ∥B∥2=2s2\|B\|^2=2s^2∥B∥2=2s2. It is not the rank-two area tensor of one vector ellipse.

At six every chiral seed is Cartan-pure, and B2=−s2I6B^2=-s^2I_6B2=−s2I6​ gives three planes. At ten that conclusion requires the additional Cartan-purity condition. General chiral seeds need not give an orthogonal complex structure or full rank. 5. What Makes This a Conserved Current?

A specified candidate time equation is

i∂tΨ=DΨ−g(Ψ†Ψ)1/(d−1)Ψ.(C10) i\partial_t\Psi=D\Psi-g(\Psi^\dagger\Psi)^{1/(d-1)}\Psi. \tag{C10} i∂t​Ψ=DΨ−g(Ψ†Ψ)1/(d−1)Ψ.(C10)

Hermiticity and the real scalar coefficient give ∂tρ+∇⋅j=0\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot j=0∂t​ρ+∇⋅j=0. For the spatial profile, the sign convention can be checked directly:

∇⋅j=i[ψ†Dψ−(Dψ)†ψ]=0. \nabla\cdot j=i\left[\psi^\dagger D\psi-(D\psi)^\dagger\psi\right]=0. ∇⋅j=i[ψ†Dψ−(Dψ)†ψ]=0.

The current therefore transports conserved field norm under this candidate equation. It has not been shown to transport the electric charge or energy that an experiment measures. The profile (C1) is the zero-temporal-frequency stationary member of (C10). Adding a constant frequency offset could attach a supplied phase clock, but would not derive the kind-specific fundamental or the multiplier C. The internal current calculation and the common-fundamental waveform construction remain two ingredients to join under one law.

6. Consequences for Polarization and Coupling

The depth-four B already entering the Hodge-channel comparison now has an explicit realization in the current of the existing spatial profile. At six the same calculation supports three circulation planes; at ten the distinction between pure and general chiral polarization matters. This strengthens the connection between the shared spinor construction and spatial polarization without deriving independent color or a measured interaction strength.

The reciprocal contact benchmark supplies a separate, specified energy term whose spatial and orientation derivatives respond to both patterns. It also shows why the full component overlap can matter when the two-form current agrees. Its Gaussian controls and quartic contact are not solutions of (C3). Combining that contact with this bubble changes the equation and requires checking the resulting profiles and complete kinetic action. The two calculations cannot simply be added and called the finished mass operator.

The next physical question is now more concrete: which common same-field dynamics preserves a suitable localized profile, its fundamental clock and conserved quantities while producing reciprocal polarization-dependent response? The present calculation supplies the explicit current to use in that test. It does not yet fix physical amplitudes, count selection, photon reconstruction, electron/muon charge equality or neutrino interaction rates.
