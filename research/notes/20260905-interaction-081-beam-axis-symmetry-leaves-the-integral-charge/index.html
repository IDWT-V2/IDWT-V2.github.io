81. Beam-axis symmetry leaves the integral charge free

[Jzdiag,Gq]=0 [J_z^{\rm diag},G_q]=0 [Jzdiag​,Gq​]=0

for every qqq. The induced Λ2\Lambda^2Λ2 actions also commute for every qqq.

It cannot be used to select q=1q=1q=1. Section 82 gives the physical branching; Sections 87--92 give the distinct, conditional fixed-lock criterion.
