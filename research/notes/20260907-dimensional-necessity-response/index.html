A sharp local lower-bound theorem

Let x∈R3x\in\mathbb R^3x∈R3 be visible displacements and y∈Rky\in\mathbb R^ky∈Rk the only eliminated spatial displacements. At a fixed preparation suppose

E2(x,y)=12xTAx+xTBy+12yTKy,K>0.E_2(x,y)=\tfrac12x^TAx+x^TBy+\tfrac12y^TKy,\qquad K>0.E2​(x,y)=21​xTAx+xTBy+21​yTKy,K>0.

Eliminating yyy gives y∗=−K−1BTxy_*=-K^{-1}B^Txy∗​=−K−1BTx and

The factorization R=(BK−1/2)(BK−1/2)TR=(BK^{-1/2})(BK^{-1/2})^TR=(BK−1/2)(BK−1/2)T proves

R≥0,rank⁡R=rank⁡B≤k.R\geq0,\qquad\operatorname{rank}R=\operatorname{rank}B\leq k.R≥0,rankR=rankB≤k.

Consequently a rank-three hidden-mediated response at one fixed preparation requires k≥3k\geq3k≥3, hence d=3+k≥6d=3+k\geq6d=3+k≥6. The bound is sharp: any positive semidefinite rank-rrr response has an rrr-coordinate realization by taking its positive-eigenvalue square root and K=IrK=I_rK=Ir​. To infer exactly six, rather than at least six, additionally require a minimal realization with no unobserved spectator coordinates.

This is also a local theorem for nonlinear energies: if the hidden equilibrium is unique nearby and its Hessian Eyy=KE_{yy}=KEyy​=K is positive definite, implicit differentiation gives the same Schur complement for the effective Hessian at that preparation. No globally quadratic energy is needed. Singular hidden Hessians require a separate treatment.

For a stationary linear response with fixed coordinate coupling BBB, hidden inertia MMM, and damping Γ\GammaΓ, the frequency-dependent mediated matrix is

R(ω)=B(K−ω2M−iωΓ)−1BT.R(\omega)=B\left(K-\omega^2M-i\omega\Gamma\right)^{-1}B^T.R(ω)=B(K−ω2M−iωΓ)−1BT.

Where the inverse exists, its complex rank is still at most kkk; positive semidefiniteness need not survive at nonzero frequency. Direct feedthrough must again be separated. This formula does not cover a periodically rotating coupling B(t)B(t)B(t): sidebands and period averaging can mix different directions, as the rank-one averaging counterexample demonstrates. An orbit's cycle-averaged susceptibility cannot simply be treated as the stationary matrix above. A response-state count also does not become a spatial count without identifying the eliminated variables as physical coordinates.

The sign matters: minimizing a stable hidden coordinate reduces the visible energy Hessian by RRR. Positive definiteness of the total visible Hessian neither implies nor requires rank-three RRR.

A symmetry version of the theorem

This does not assume three hidden directions at the start. It assumes that a nonzero mixed linear coupling exists at a symmetry-preserving state. Covariance of a family of oriented states is weaker and does not give this result. Exact counterexamples to weaker proposed tests

  1. Direct visible response. For k=1k=1k=1 or 222, choose B=(e1,…,ek)B=(e_1,\ldots,e_k)B=(e1​,…,ek​) and positive diagonal KKK. The hidden response has rank kkk, but adding the direct matrix D=I3−RD=I_3-RD=I3​−R gives total response D+R=I3D+R=I_3D+R=I3​. Total rank is not hidden-channel rank.

  2. Averaging. With one hidden coordinate and K=1K=1K=1, choose the prescribed rotating coupling

    b(t)=(2/3cos⁡t,2/3sin⁡t,1/3)T.b(t)=\left(\sqrt{2/3}\cos t,\sqrt{2/3}\sin t,1/\sqrt3\right)^T.b(t)=(2/3​cost,2/3​sint,1/3​)T.

    Every b(t)b(t)Tb(t)b(t)^Tb(t)b(t)T has rank one, but its exact period average is I3/3I_3/3I3​/3. This example includes a prescribed changing orientation; it does not claim that its orientation clock is generated for free by that one hidden displacement. It disproves the inference from averaged rank to instantaneous hidden rank.

  3. Additional internal variables. The same rank proof counts whatever variables were eliminated. Calling polarization or source-matrix entries spatial directions would change the theorem's physical meaning.

  4. Extra dimensions. Appending uncoupled coordinates preserves a model's responses on its original invariant subspace. A lower-bound argument alone cannot exclude such higher-dimensional extensions.

The current cycle-averaged molecular Hessians do not yet isolate RRR under these conditions. Three molecular axes and petal counts therefore do not supply the missing rank-three premise.
