Rotation-neutral Dirac fields in physical space

Updated September 9, 2026

Summary: A full radial Dirac field can preserve an exactly neutral total spatial rotation through compensating orbital and spin motion, even though a projector on neutral spin components discards part of the field.

Keep the position dependence of the polarization

The neutral-doublet calculation shows that projecting the first-order spatial Dirac operator onto neutral spin components removes some momentum responses. Full spatial modes provide a constructive way to retain those components. The conserved rotation acts on position and spin together.

Work on a flat ddd-dimensional spatial model of a sector in the one nested macroscopic manifold. All ddd directions are ordinary spatial dimensions. The finite spinor components below describe the same field on those coordinates. Choosing rotation planes specifies a probe or a physical preparation; it does not divide a particle's dimensions into different kinds.

This note derives neutrality under that specified spatial rotation. Its identification with an electromagnetic interaction remains a separate current calculation. No mediator particle or additional physical dimension is introduced.

The five-dimensional neutral modes remain available

The rest weights are the spin lifts of the chosen spatial rotations. Those fields can have nonzero density throughout all five spatial coordinates.

At depth six the rest module is a selected four-component half-spin representation. Three equal rotation planes give half-integer weights with no zero. The five-plane depth-ten construction likewise has no zero weight. With ordinary single-valued spatial wavefunctions, the full 2π2\pi2π rotation acts as −I-I−I when the number of planes is odd; orbital integer weights cannot turn it into a zero eigenvalue. The rotation-weight note gives the representation assumptions and the other depths, including the even-plane cases which also admit zero weights.

The comparison concerns the same type of geometric operation. It is not a proof that this operation is the photon current or that all five-dimensional particles occupy its neutral modes. Neutral and nonneutral radial polarizations in (3) have the same scalar density and radial equations. Their occupation still requires a dynamical explanation.

Compatibility with the entire nested geometry

The rotation used here can move lower members of a nested chain. Rotations of nested sectors distinguishes preserving each fixed member from transporting the whole chain while preserving its nesting. A physical application must include those embedding motions and both interacting objects' polarizations. Equations (8)–(13) retain full spatial motion but do not select a universal embedding or assign a charge generator to every particle.
