Why this is still an inverse candidate

Equation (3) has the desired shared self/pair algebra and retains the conditional simplex mass frequency. Its positive matrix excess does not on its own sustain the displayed localized core. On the upper-only coherent branch, its first variation has no lower-spinor source, while the spatial Dirac derivative lies in that lower block. Removing the separate sustaining interaction from the prior localized action would leave −iα⋅∇ϕ=0-i\alpha\cdot\nabla\phi=0−iα⋅∇ϕ=0, whose finite-norm flat-space solution is trivial in this ansatz. The previous binding term remains an additional part of the candidate same-field action.

A complete one-law derivation must generate the off-diagonal sustaining source, the shell weights, the common normalization, stable spatial states, and cross-depth response from one physical interaction. The current construction isolates a useful algebraic condition: a positive same-field mass current can have a stable aligned-pair direction without sacrificing the assigned self-frequency, provided the current law uses more than quadratic purity and has a physically valid positive-current lift.
