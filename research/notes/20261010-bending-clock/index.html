A Positive Geometric Wave Pair With a Constant Frequency Gap

Updated October 10, 2026

A sector-fundamental coupling source does not necessarily require a complete harmonic comb. Two dynamically generated branches can remain separated by one clock while both frequencies become large. The following positive local inverse action realizes that possibility and also produces a fourth-order static response. It provides shared mathematics for the frequency and geometric-response investigations; its identification with IDWT's field and source operators remains conditional.

The displacement-and-rotation structure is standard in Timoshenko wave theory. Mori, Xu and Kawashima, equations (1.1)–(1.5), explicitly distinguish equal from unequal characteristic speeds. Their paper includes damping. The calculations here use an independently written conservative action, set damping to zero and extend its scalar/rotation structure to the occupied ordinary spatial depths. Their decay results are not applied to this conservative model.
