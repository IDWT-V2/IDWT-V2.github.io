Updated October 13, 2026

This is a changed physical realization of that action. It includes an explicit center and a rest profile on ordinary ten-dimensional spatial hyperplanes. It is nonlocal on those hyperplanes; no unrestricted local laboratory scalar-field action or interacting completion is asserted.

Verification and Scope
