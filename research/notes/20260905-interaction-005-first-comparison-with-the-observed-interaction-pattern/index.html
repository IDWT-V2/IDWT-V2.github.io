5. First comparison with the observed interaction pattern

5.1 Electromagnetism: very low shared depth, broad reach

The current V2 electromagnetic construction uses the depth-two Spin(2)≅U(1)Spin(2)\cong U(1)Spin(2)≅U(1) projection. Charged particles of depths 3, 4, 6, and 10 can all possess a nonzero depth-two current, while the neutrino projector is required to annihilate the electromagnetic generator.

Thus the qualitative pattern is

many different particle depths⟶one common low-depth electromagnetic channel. \boxed{ \text{many different particle depths} \longrightarrow \text{one common low-depth electromagnetic channel}. } many different particle depths⟶one common low-depth electromagnetic channel.​

This naturally allows electromagnetism to connect particles whose full spatial geometries are very different. The interaction does not require them to coincide in all dimensions either particle possesses.

The important selection datum is charge, not depth coincidence.

So unlike electromagnetism, the charged weak interaction is explicitly an off-diagonal depth-changing operator.

That is qualitatively striking:

4↔3,5↔6,5↔10. 4\leftrightarrow3, \qquad 5\leftrightarrow6, \qquad 5\leftrightarrow10. 4↔3,5↔6,5↔10.

The observed weak interaction really does convert members of quark and lepton multiplets into one another rather than merely exerting a diagonal force. In the V2 geometry, this is exactly the kind of process represented by PaD∞PbP_aD_\infty P_bPa​D∞​Pb​ with a≠ba\neq ba=b.

Thus the depth-changing character survives as a promising structural match, while the detailed transition matrix remains open.

5.3 Colour: not explained by adjacent depth difference alone

The direct quark depths are 3 and 4, whose normal difference is only one dimension and has trivial continuous spin group. Current V2 therefore does not obtain SU(3)SU(3)SU(3) colour merely from the local 4→34\to34→3 normal direction.

Instead, the colour candidate is associated with a larger normal geometry containing

Spin(6)≅SU(4) Spin(6)\cong SU(4) Spin(6)≅SU(4)

and a selected SU(3)SU(3)SU(3) subgroup, with quark projectors transforming nontrivially and lepton projectors as singlets.

This is a useful negative result: the simple rule

force group=Spin(∣b−a∣) \text{force group}=Spin(|b-a|) force group=Spin(∣b−a∣)

is false.

Depth difference supplies a candidate normal structure, but the physical interaction group depends on holonomy reduction and representation selection.

5.4 Gravity: full marginal plus singlet channel

The current polarization module identifies scalar and stress-energy bilinears as normal singlets, making gravity the least representation-sensitive channel.

If that survives the full master dynamics, it would explain why essentially every sector can participate:

This is qualitatively consistent with gravity coupling to all stress-energy rather than only selected charge sectors.
