One Spatial Band for Counts, Contact and Transport

Updated October 9, 2026

A finite band of spatial fields supplies more than a number of modes. Its evaluation map fixes a contact operator, its moving image fixes a connection, and overlaps of those images fix transition strengths. These constructions can therefore be tested together without independently prescribing a count, a connection and a mixing matrix.

The geometric hypotheses below concern ordinary spatial manifolds. A spinor fibre or a finite band describes fields on that space. The round-sphere comparison is a compatible nested control family; the projective calculation is a separate candidate branch with its existing global embedding conditions.

1. Evaluation Gives a Contact Operator

Let HN\mathcal H_NHN​ be a finite spectral band of smooth sections of a Hermitian rank-rrr bundle on a spatial manifold. Use its specified physical L2L^2L2 norm. Evaluation at xxx is

EN,x:HN⟶Fx,BN(x)=EN,xEN,x†. E_{N,x}:\mathcal H_N\longrightarrow F_x,\qquad B_N(x)=E_{N,x}E_{N,x}^\dagger. EN,x​:HN​⟶Fx​,BN​(x)=EN,x​EN,x†​.

Here BN(x)B_N(x)BN​(x) is the diagonal of the reproducing kernel. Suppose a fixed reference band has B0(x)>0B_0(x)>0B0​(x)>0 on the chosen fibre. Define

TN,x=EN,x†B0(x)−1EN,x. T_{N,x}=E_{N,x}^\dagger B_0(x)^{-1}E_{N,x}. TN,x​=EN,x†​B0​(x)−1EN,x​.

Its nonzero eigenvalues are those of B0−1/2BNB0−1/2B_0^{-1/2}B_NB_0^{-1/2}B0−1/2​BN​B0−1/2​. Consequently,

BN(x)=S(n,d)B0(x)⟹TN,x2=S(n,d)TN,x,N=n−1. B_N(x)=S(n,d)B_0(x) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad T_{N,x}^2=S(n,d)T_{N,x},\qquad N=n-1. BN​(x)=S(n,d)B0​(x)⟹TN,x2​=S(n,d)TN,x​,N=n−1.

The nonzero contact eigenvalue is exactly S(n,d)S(n,d)S(n,d), repeated rrr times. This is an evaluation theorem, not the assertion that every band vector has that eigenvalue. The contact's kernel is retained.

A frequency contribution μdTN,x\mu_dT_{N,x}μd​TN,x​ then has the maintained nonzero frequency μdS(n,d)\mu_dS(n,d)μd​S(n,d) under one band-independent coefficient. Deriving that contact contribution, its reference normalization and its occupied state from the same-field action remains the physical problem. A finite-band point form is mathematically well-defined; it does not make a smooth self-source into a delta distribution.

2. Actual Spatial Examples

The full positive band through NNN has dimension rdS(N+1,d)r_dS(N+1,d)rd​S(N+1,d). These are the multiplicities in Bär's sphere theorem.

Homogeneity and isotropy give

BN(x)=S(N+1,d)Vol⁡(Sd)Ird. B_N(x)=\frac{S(N+1,d)}{\operatorname{Vol}(S^d)}I_{r_d}. BN​(x)=Vol(Sd)S(N+1,d)​Ird​​.

At odd ddd the isotropy spinor is irreducible. At even ddd, its two irreducible chiral pieces have equal diagonal coefficients: a nonzero Dirac eigenstate has zero integrated chirality, while homogeneity makes the diagonal chirality trace constant. The two equal-rank pieces therefore contribute equally. Thus the same formula holds at every d≥2d\geq2d≥2.

With B0=I/Vol⁡(Sd)B_0=I/\operatorname{Vol}(S^d)B0​=I/Vol(Sd), the normalized contact has eigenvalue S(n,d)S(n,d)S(n,d) on rdr_drd​ channels. The normalization cancels both the spinor multiplicity and physical volume through a specified reference operator, rather than silently discarding them.

For the separate CPm\mathbb{CP}^mCPm zero-form branch with fixed twist O(m)\mathcal O(m)O(m), the existing spatial spectral-band calculation gives

DN=(N+mm)(N+2mm)=(2mm)S(N+1,2m). \mathscr D_N=\binom{N+m}{m}\binom{N+2m}{m} =\binom{2m}{m}S(N+1,2m). DN​=(mN+m​)(mN+2m​)=(m2m​)S(N+1,2m).

3. Retain the Kinetic Operator and Calculate Its Dressing

At one sphere point, each evaluation polarization has the same coupled radial matrix. In shell coordinates,

HN=diag⁡(ω0,…,ωN)+μvv†,vl=gd(l),v†v=S. H_N=\operatorname{diag}(\omega_0,\ldots,\omega_N)+\mu vv^\dagger, \qquad v_l=\sqrt{g_d(l)},\qquad v^\dagger v=S. HN​=diag(ω0​,…,ωN​)+μvv†,vl​=gd​(l)​,v†v=S.

The other directions in each shell remain explicit uncoupled states. Put b=v/Sb=v/\sqrt Sb=v/S​ and Λ=μS\Lambda=\mu SΛ=μS. The exact coupled frequencies away from the free poles satisfy

1=μ∑l=0Ngd(l)z−ωl. 1=\mu\sum_{l=0}^N\frac{g_d(l)}{z-\omega_l}. 1=μl=0∑N​z−ωl​gd​(l)​.

This follows either from the matrix determinant lemma or the evaluation resolvent. It supplies the correction that an isolated contact eigenvalue omits.

The uniform cumulative state count gives exact kinetic moments:

ωˉ=1R(d2+dNd+1),σω2=dN(N+d+1)R2(d+1)2(d+2). \bar\omega=\frac1R\left(\frac d2+\frac{dN}{d+1}\right),\qquad \sigma_\omega^2= \frac{dN(N+d+1)}{R^2(d+1)^2(d+2)}. ωˉ=R1​(2d​+d+1dN​),σω2​=R2(d+1)2(d+2)dN(N+d+1)​.

If μ>0\mu>0μ>0 and Λ>W=N/R\Lambda>W=N/RΛ>W=N/R, the top frequency has the controlled form

The complementary component of a normalized top eigenvector is at most σω/(Λ−W)\sigma_\omega/(\Lambda-W)σω​/(Λ−W).

The construction now makes their size and mode dependence calculable.

5. Overlap Gives an Exact Mixing Defect

Suppose two canonically normalized frames Ua,UbU_a,U_bUa​,Ub​ have been embedded by explicit maps into the same field Hilbert space. For the overlap vertex K=Ua†UbK=U_a^\dagger U_bK=Ua†​Ub​,

I−K†K=Ub†(I−Pa)Ub≥0,∥K∥op≤1. I-K^\dagger K=U_b^\dagger(I-P_a)U_b\geq0,\qquad \|K\|_{\rm op}\leq1. I−K†K=Ub†​(I−Pa​)Ub​≥0,∥K∥op​≤1.

For equal finite ranks, KKK is unitary precisely when the two frame images coincide. Their bases may still differ. The defect measures amplitude in directions omitted by the receiving band. It cannot be removed by replacing KKK with its polar unitary factor.

This is a concrete geometric test for the overlap contribution to generation mixing. A general physical vertex need not be a pure overlap; its actual kinetic metrics, interaction weights and eliminated channels must be retained. The formula then applies to the explicitly identified overlap part.

6. The Same Spectral Measure Controls Response and Truncation

Eliminating complementary modes gives

Σ(z)=PHQ(z−QHQ)−1QHP. \Sigma(z)=PHQ(z-QHQ)^{-1}QHP. Σ(z)=PHQ(z−QHQ)−1QHP.

Its discrete poles give dressing, while a continuous spectral component gives the imaginary part associated with outgoing response. This reuses the same spectral measure rather than introducing a separate decay kernel. An instantaneous effective interaction needs its own low-frequency and propagation justification.

For a frequency tower μS(n,d)\mu S(n,d)μS(n,d), the multiplicity must be stated. The unweighted reciprocal sum is finite for d>1d>1d>1. With complete oscillator-shell weights, however,

7. Temporal Occupation and the Contact Readout

A spatial band also supplies the operator that reads an occupied temporal spectrum. For harmonic coefficient vectors ara_rar​ and a Hermitian source BBB, its bilinear beat is Jp=∑rar†Bar+pJ_p=\sum_r a_r^\dagger B a_{r+p}Jp​=∑r​ar†​Bar+p​. Orthogonality under an integrated identity source need not imply orthogonality under point evaluation.

In the explicit finite control T=ee†T=ee^\daggerT=ee† with e=(1,…,1)Te=(1,\ldots,1)^Te=(1,…,1)T, both T2=STT^2=STT2=ST and ∑r=1S−1Tr,r+1/[r(r+1)]=1−1/S\sum_{r=1}^{S-1}T_{r,r+1}/[r(r+1)]=1-1/S∑r=1S−1​Tr,r+1​/[r(r+1)]=1−1/S hold. This connects the contact form to the fundamental beat of a specified harmonic preparation. The sawtooth coupling calculation retains the canonical metric and distinguishes its temporal cutoff from the spatial shell index. It does not assign evenly spaced temporal frequencies to the sphere's individual degenerate states without a dynamics establishing that map.

Verification and Use

One calculation verifies cumulative counts, exact first and second moments, projective reference ratios, normalized contact projectors, independent dressed eigenvalues and overlap defects. It compares depths 2 through 12 with the same rules and coefficients.

This construction joins the spatial count, its contact operator, kinetic corrections, geometric transport and transition accessibility. Physical completion supplies the occupied band and common interaction; the formulas specify what those choices jointly entail.
