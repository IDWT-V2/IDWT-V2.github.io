IDOS Energy, Coherence, and Supporting Dynamics

Updated October 16, 2026

This calculation retains its stated assumptions and action. For the current ten-space-plus-time physical picture, common-frequency portions and later developments, see the new particle-geometry and sector-depths case.

The maintained mass law has a direct energy interpretation:

Ed,N=εdDd,N,Dd,N=IDOS⁡d(N)=(N+dd),εd=μdc2. E_{d,N}=\varepsilon_d D_{d,N},\qquad D_{d,N}=\operatorname{IDOS}_d(N)=\binom{N+d}{d},\qquad \varepsilon_d=\mu_dc^2. Ed,N​=εd​Dd,N​,Dd,N​=IDOSd​(N)=(dN+d​),εd​=μd​c2.

The sector scale is the complete energy per counted state, not an additive pedestal beneath an oscillator. Counts include every degree through the cutoff. The following constructions test how this interpretation can be realized. They are conditional classical field models in ordinary spatial dimensions; none replaces deterministic electron trajectories with a probability density.
