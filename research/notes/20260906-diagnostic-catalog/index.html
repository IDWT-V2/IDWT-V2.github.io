exploratory not a module

Summary: Reproduction commands and scope descriptions for every local finite diagnostic and helper.

The scalar Hartree, circulating-field, and fixed-oscillator projection checks verify auxiliary models. Their success does not establish the trajectories or stability of the actual electron orbits in the 6D demo.

Existing checks

  • localized_polarization_stationarity_checks.py compares spatial first variations in five and six dimensions under a separately fingerprinted protocol, retaining the same kernel and omitting compensating channels.

  • frozen_magnetic_texture_checks.py verifies a fingerprinted protocol for spatial polarization textures, independently compares analytic curvature with nonlinear Fourier energies, and reports rejected preparations without treating a scientifically negative outcome as a broken test runner.

  • accurate_cylindrical_continuation.py is an optional-argument shooting tool requiring a fresh --output path; it is not automatically run by the suite.

  • cylindrical_orbit_stability.py takes a corrected candidate and fresh --output path, computing full-coordinate Floquet matrices and optional fixed-Casimir nonlinear growth probes. It is not an automatic suite entry.

  • cylindrical_refinement_checks.py recomputes ten candidate closures with boundary-segmented DOP853 and Radau, plus the exact linear block splitting.

  • dimensional_necessity_checks.py checks response-rank identities, averaging counterexamples, and the same-equation five-dimensional inclusion. Its optional --integrate mode also reproduces three five-dimensional orbits; --output accepts a fresh evidence path. The suite runs the algebraic and field checks without the optional orbit integrations.

  • spin5_selection_checks.py checks universal five-dimensional spinor identities, CP3 source selection, a spectral no-go theorem, and reciprocal conservation. Optional --output writes a fresh evidence report.

  • spinor_response_poles_checks.py derives and verifies the coupled source/spinor frequencies in five and six dimensions, checks the nonlinear variational derivative, and reproduces small-perturbation evolution.

  • dimensional_magnetic_kernel_checks.py compares the existing constrained magnetic kernel in five and six dimensions, checks envelope-anisotropy selection, and verifies the vector channel in five-dimensional scalar exchange.

  • moment_kernel_checks.py checks the scalar coupled moment model's stationary covariance, full Hessian, and mixed-source response. It requires NumPy and does not derive an embedding, spinor lock, or physical frequency.

  • ckm_count_audit_checks_2026_09_05.py checks ranks, degree-loss support, kinetic-metric normalization, unitary completions, and rephasing-invariant CP examples. It does not fit or derive the physical CKM matrix.

  • progress_checks_2026_09_05.py checks static versus frequency-dependent reductions, colour-pair coefficients, projectors, contact spectra, and conditional common-anchor ratios.

  • common_anchor_action_checks_2026_09_05.py verifies the exact Case-A/Case-B count mismatch for the current common-anchor action audit.

  • two_electron_depth6_checks_2026_09_05.py checks the dimensional counts, Gaussian hidden-impact coefficient, and a conditional lock metric example. Its base-kernel exponent N=12N=12N=12 is a trial input, not a derived transfer of the electron mass-band degree to the base-coordinate interaction.

  • two_electron_pair_invariant_check_2026_09_05.py verifies the exact collapse of the complex-structure alignment invariant to the Fubini--Study pair overlap, C_J=(4 nu-1)/3.

  • two_electron_scalar_quartic_check_2026_09_05.py verifies the exact two-packet expansion of the current depth-six scalar quartic and its incoherent two-electron cross term.

  • dimensional_em_green_function_check_2026_09_05.py verifies the three- versus six-spatial-dimensional electrostatic Green-function coefficients used to test full-dimensional electromagnetic propagation.

  • operator_lift_kernel_check_2026_09_05.py verifies the block-operator distinction between direct projection and effective reduced dynamics used in the electromagnetic inverse-lifting audit.

  • hidden_visible_lock_schur_check_2026_09_05.py verifies the exact self-adjoint 3+3 lock block, its three zero/three restoring modes, and its vanishing relaxed Schur complement.

  • free_electron_pair_rotation_check_2026_09_05.py computes R=-J1 J2, its characteristic polynomial and overlap trace from Clifford bilinears. The canonical real split is 2+4; an internal 2+2 display is not canonical.

  • free_electron_pair_quaternionic_check_2026_09_05.py computes the symbolic generic quaternionic identities and solves the active commutant at an exact nondegenerate representative.

  • free_pair_equivariant_operator_check_2026_09_05.py solves the simultaneous commutant equations: symmetric span {P0,P4}, four skew generators, and no commuting mixed maps. This is not a classification of all covariant operators.

  • free_pair_scattering_mixed_rank_check_2026_09_05.py checks minors and exact saturating examples for the specified one- and two-outer-product ansatzes.

  • beam_adapted_frame_link_check_2026_09_05.py verifies the exact relation between Fubini--Study pair separation and relative SO(3) frame-link mismatch in a common beam-selected 3+3 split.

  • two_electron_relative_lock_check_2026_09_05.py verifies the conditional relative hidden/visible separation theorem for two individually locked electrons in a common beam-adapted 3+3 frame.

  • two_electron_com_relative_energy_check_2026_09_05.py verifies the exact center-of-mass reduction of two individual beam locks to one relative restoring energy.

  • electromagnetic_mixed_block_rank_check_2026_09_05.py verifies that a simple 6D bivector has mixed 3x3 block rank at most two, while the direct depth-two electromagnetic lift has zero mixed block in a beam-adapted observed plane.

Reproduce the finite checks

From the repository root, run the registered diagnostic scripts:

python3 -B experiments/run_checks.py

For the independent audit suite without the two long continuation searches:

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -B experiments/run_checks.py --skip-continuation --jobs 3 --report experiments/results/audit-2026-09-07/reproduction-suite.json

Exclusions are named in the output and report. The archived-state refinement test still runs; its diagnostic success does not imply candidate acceptance.

The runner uses the current Python interpreter, reports failures with nonzero exit status, and disables bytecode writes. The checks require SciPy, SymPy, mpmath, and NumPy; they do not install dependencies. electron_pair_algebra.py supplies the shared explicit Clifford construction. correction_checks_2026_09_06.py covers the normalization, topology prerequisites, action/frequency, rank-two Hessian, Clifford-commutator domain, and numerical corrections.

The original two larger audits can also be run individually from the repository root:

python3 experiments/progress_checks_2026_09_05.py
python3 experiments/ckm_count_audit_checks_2026_09_05.py

The check script uses SymPy, already used by the research session. It checks finite algebra and comparison operators. It does not install dependencies, modify the modules, fit an amplitude, solve the V2 master equation, or predict a baryon mass.

Status vocabulary: checked algebra denotes a verified identity or spectrum for the specified operator; conditional mechanism denotes a sufficient construction whose physical premises remain unproved; source audit records what particular inspected passages say; withdrawn inference identifies an earlier conclusion that exceeded its calculation; open denotes a remaining proof obligation.

These labels and the filenames are for readers. This write does not change website discovery, publication settings, or module-index code. Existing physics modules and the earlier investigation ledger remain in place.

Knowledge-base maintenance

Run python3 -B scripts/validate-kb.py from the repository root to check the registry, IDs, tags, topic maps, local file links, and inbound references. It requires PyYAML. URL fragments and external websites are not checked. This is structural QA, not a physics test.
