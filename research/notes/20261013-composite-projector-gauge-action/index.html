A Global Auxiliary Carrier and Composite Curvature Action

Updated October 17, 2026

Conclusion

The auxiliary carrier cannot be obtained by a nonsingular, fibrewise complex-linear rank-preserving projection of the geometric positive half-spin bundle on CP5. Their second Chern characters differ by 48H². The actual tangent bundle also has no global complex 3+2 split. Neither a trace subtraction nor a connection normalization repairs either obstruction.

The minimal bundle change is to retain a separate determinant-trivial coefficient subbundle of the single field, rather than identify it with geometric tangent or spinor directions. An explicit embedding in a trivial rank-175 coefficient bundle proves this change is globally realizable. A conditional single-matrix-field action then gives split-preserving composite curvature, with exact trace indices 2, 2 and 10/3 on its even exterior carrier. Equal trace weighting implies the formal canonical coefficient ratio gY²/g2²=3/5 and g3=g2 on the same base. It supplies neither an absolute coupling nor the physical weak/color response. Its explicit simple background has only Abelian holonomy.

The matrix action is an alternative finite-depth single-field hypothesis. It is not a reduction derived from the existing Psi_infinity action and does not complete its extension to the infinite physical manifold. The obstruction and this distinction are the principal outcome.

1. Exact obstruction and its scope

Let H=c1(O(1)) on the candidate physical CP5. The source supplies

Eg=O(−2)⊕3⊕O(3)⊕2,Fg=ΛevenEg. E_g=O(-2)^{\oplus3}\oplus O(3)^{\oplus2},\qquad F_g=\Lambda^{\rm even}E_g. Eg​=O(−2)⊕3⊕O(3)⊕2,Fg​=ΛevenEg​.

Its rank is 16, and c1(F_g)=0. Its sixteen line exponents are sums of even subsets of (-2,-2,-2,3,3). Therefore

ch⁡2(Fg)=12∑IqI2H2=60H2. \operatorname{ch}_2(F_g)=\tfrac12\sum_I q_I^2H^2=60H^2. ch2​(Fg​)=21​I∑​qI2​H2=60H2.

For the actual geometric positive half-spin carrier, the half-spin weights give

ch⁡2(Σ+CP5)=2∑i=15xi2=2(c1(T)2−2c2(T))=12H2. \operatorname{ch}_2(\Sigma^+CP5)=2\sum_{i=1}^5x_i^2 =2(c_1(T)^2-2c_2(T))=12H^2. ch2​(Σ+CP5)=2i=1∑5​xi2​=2(c1​(T)2−2c2​(T))=12H2.

An everywhere rank-16 linear map from Sigma+ to Fg would be a bundle isomorphism. The unequal characters rule it out. A rank-16 projector inside a rank-16 carrier is its identity; it cannot change this conclusion. A line twist O(k) changes the first Chern class to 16kH, so preserving c1=0 forces k=0. A singular or rank-changing map is outside this obstruction, but then it loses the globally complete 16-component carrier and needs an explicit defect/domain treatment.

This is a CP5 topological obstruction. It does not exclude a local identification on a contractible patch or a different noncompact spatial geometry. Pullback to the standard CP2 and CP3 preserves the nonzero H² mismatch; pullback to CP1 loses this particular fourth-cohomology test. None of those restrictions repairs the original global map. The exterior fibre branching and anomaly sums do not alter the topology or establish occupied modes.

Independently,

c(TCP5)=(1+2H)(1+H+H2)(1+3H+3H2). c(TCP5)=(1+2H)(1+H+H^2)(1+3H+3H^2). c(TCP5)=(1+2H)(1+H+H2)(1+3H+3H2).

A putative rank-two factor must have (c1,c2)=(H,H²) or (3H,3H²). On CP3, expansion in the integral K-theory basis t=[O(1)]-1 gives coefficients (2,1,-1,1/2) or (2,3,0,-1/2). Those half-integral coefficients violate the necessary integral K-class condition. Thus no unitary connection on the literal tangent bundle can preserve a global rank-three plus rank-two splitting. The exact checker verifies the algebra; integrality of this K-theory basis is the mathematical input retained from the source, not something a finite check proves.

The gauge module's older mode-index arithmetic is not used in this derivation. The durable later correction rejects that arithmetic as a physical selection law. We use its gauge branching only under the subsequent global-carrier corrections.

3. Minimal changed bundle premise, realized explicitly

Retain Eg as a coefficient bundle carried by Psi_infinity. It describes mathematical field components, not extra physical spatial dimensions. To obtain it by projection the ambient coefficient carrier must contain Eg (or Fg) as an actual subbundle. The existing rank-16 geometric half-spin carrier is insufficient. No new mediator particle follows from this change.

A concrete sufficient enlargement is elementary. For a local unit homogeneous lift z of [z] in CP5, let v_k(z) be the unit degree-k symmetric tensor z tensor ... tensor z. Its ambient complex dimension is binomial(5+k,k). Define its line projector p_k=v_k v_k†. The projector is globally well-defined although its local unit frame changes by e^{ik theta}. It represents O(-k). The complex-conjugated projector represents O(+k), as a smooth Hermitian bundle.

In the fixed orthogonal coefficient space

Pc=I3⊗p2,Pw=I2⊗p3‾. P_c=I_3\otimes p_2,\qquad P_w=I_2\otimes\overline{p_3}. Pc​=I3​⊗p2​,Pw​=I2​⊗p3​​.

The two projectors act on distinct fixed blocks, so their images are orthogonal and their sum has image Eg. The rank 175 is a sufficient explicit coefficient dimension, not a minimality claim and not a physical spatial dimension. An embedding into an infinite coefficient carrier is consequently also possible. A single globally nonzero vector z would trivialize its own line and cannot be used as a global lift of this degree-one CP5 construction; the projectors, rather than a fictitious global lift, are essential.

Topological enlargement alone is therefore the minimal changed premise that removes the proved obstruction. Selecting this particular bundle from Psi_infinity, producing its non-Abelian curvature and deriving occupied modes remain separate requirements.

6. The constrained field equation is not an independent connection equation

No independent temporal-connection variation is available to import an earlier Gauss constraint. This is the same distinction demanded by the corrected normal-structure sources. If projected matter modes of the same field are later retained, their currents enter the same pullback variation; their equations and their energy cannot be added by name alone.

The master-action closure therefore still needs: a physical functional selecting these projectors from the original field or an accepted changed fundamental field; a common spatial metric and compatible occupied flag; selection of the split and determinant transport; a mechanism producing the required non-Abelian curvature and allowed perturbations; a well-posed complete response; spatial occupied modes and canonical vertices; and cross-depth overlap measures. No curvature trace identity fixes the hadronic frequency renormalization or total action normalization analyzed in the canonical count-response calculation, or the physical eliminated-mode kernels analyzed in the exact normal-response calculation.

Verification and Scope

The portable exact checker, using Python and SymPy, verifies the characteristic mismatch, forbidden rank-two K coefficients, generator trace matrices and mixed commutators, projector polynomials and a nonzero composite-curvature witness. The original 185-condition record also included archival source-hash checks; those operational checks are omitted from the portable version and are not mathematical evidence. Its depth table is an arithmetic consistency check of the source-proved holonomy results, not a new holonomy proof. No numerical particle simulation is performed.

Reusable equations are (1)–(2) for a globally supplied projector carrier, (4)–(6) for conditional trace normalization, and the rank-preserving obstruction in section 1. The substantive unresolved step is the map and dynamics of Psi_infinity, not the finite trace calculation.
