Operator and energy domain

On a flat model of ddd ordinary spatial directions within the one nested physical manifold, consider

H=−Δ+λ∣x∣2,λ>0,H=L2(Rd,ddx). H=-\Delta+\lambda|x|^2,\qquad \lambda>0,\qquad \mathcal H=L^2(\mathbb R^d,d^dx). H=−Δ+λ∣x∣2,λ>0,H=L2(Rd,ddx).

This is a specified scalar confinement operator. Its use for a particular sector requires the corresponding kinetic and binding dynamics. Define its energy through the positive quadratic form

It is complete: a Cauchy sequence converges in H1H^1H1 and its products ∣x∣u|x|u∣x∣u converge in L2L^2L2; local convergence identifies the latter limit with ∣x∣|x|∣x∣ times the former. Compact resolvent

Suppose a sequence is bounded in this form norm by a common constant. For every R>0R>0R>0,

∫∣x∣>R∣u(x)∣2 ddx≤q[u]λR2. \int_{|x|>R}|u(x)|^2\,d^dx \leq \frac{q[u]}{\lambda R^2}. ∫∣x∣>R​∣u(x)∣2ddx≤λR2q[u]​.

On each finite ball, the Rellich compactness theorem supplies an L2L^2L2-convergent subsequence of the H1H^1H1-bounded restrictions. Choose a diagonal subsequence over increasing balls. The uniform tail estimate makes this subsequence Cauchy on all of Rd\mathbb R^dRd: first make both tails small, then use convergence on the chosen ball. Thus Q↪L2\mathcal Q\hookrightarrow L^2Q↪L2 is compact.

The resolvent (H+1)−1(H+1)^{-1}(H+1)−1 maps bounded L2L^2L2 sets to bounded form-norm sets. Composing with that compact inclusion proves compactness of the resolvent. The spectrum consists of isolated eigenvalues of finite multiplicity tending to infinity, with a complete orthonormal eigenbasis. This is the confining-potential case of the spectral theorem discussed in Phan Thành Nam, Functional Analysis II, §2.5.
