9. Consequence for the proposed electron-beam experiment

An electron-electron collider contains at least two conceptually different channels:

Ordinary projected scattering

e6+e6→  a=2  e6+e6, e_6+e_6 \xrightarrow{\;a=2\;} e_6+e_6, e6​+e6​a=2​e6​+e6​,

which probes the electromagnetic marginals and need not be suppressed by unobserved six-dimensional separation.

Genuine six-dimensional contact/excitation

e6+e6→  a=6  reconfigured/excited Q6 state, e_6+e_6 \xrightarrow{\;a=6\;} \text{reconfigured/excited }Q_6\text{ state}, e6​+e6​a=6​reconfigured/excited Q6​ state,

which would depend on the full conditional separation and orientation geometry.

If the second channel exists, its rate relative to ordinary Moller scattering is the cleaner observable:

R6=σ(full-depth electron contact/excitation)σ(ordinary projected electromagnetic scattering). \boxed{ \mathcal R_6 = \frac{\sigma(\text{full-depth electron contact/excitation})} {\sigma(\text{ordinary projected electromagnetic scattering})}. } R6​=σ(ordinary projected electromagnetic scattering)σ(full-depth electron contact/excitation)​.​

IDWT should predict the beam-energy, impact-parameter, and polarization dependence of R6\mathcal R_6R6​.

A threshold, resonance, or anomalous missing/projected inertia signature in this ratio would be much more diagnostic than a generic deficit in the total electron-electron scattering rate.
