Scope

This is the reciprocal response. It does not require equal response coefficients after changing field normalizations or identifying different coordinate spaces.

Remaining physical requirements

The kinetic symbol includes the appropriate geometric connection; replacing covariant derivatives by partial derivatives on a curved sector needs a justified local approximation. Dynamical projectors, metric-dependent measures, and kernels produce additional variations, including contributions to stress-energy. They are held fixed only for the variation above.

For a local phase with generator Q^\hat QQ^​, the displayed scalar densities are invariant if [Pa,Q^]=0[P_a,\hat Q]=0[Pa​,Q^​]=0 and the adjoint transforms compatibly. More general charged bilocal factors need appropriate transport or invariant contractions. Gauge covariance is not implied by the double-integral notation.

The equation is a Dirac mean-field equation. Its reduction to a positive scalar Schrödinger density, the sign of self-binding, a causal completion of the instantaneous spatial kernel, and the physical mass operator remain separate obligations. In particular, the flat scalar oscillator closure cannot be obtained merely by relabelling ΨˉΨ\bar\Psi\PsiΨˉΨ as ∣Ψ∣2|\Psi|^2∣Ψ∣2.
