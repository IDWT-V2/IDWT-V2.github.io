A Pure Spatial Spinor Pencil Links the Four-Plane, Normal Frame and Charge Carrier

Updated October 9, 2026

Two compatible polarizations of a spatial spinor give a common route to several previously separate geometric constructions. Their pure pencil selects a real four-plane. Applying the earlier universal alternating frame rule to its complement then selects six normal directions and a ten-dimensional parent. Fixing one polarization and the other's ray leaves the determinant-trivial block-unitary group whose exterior character gives the familiar one-family charge weights.

These are conditional geometric consequences. The pencil and universal normal-frame prescription are explicit premises; a positive potential below makes the first a possible energetic condition. Neither premise is an observed particle property, and the ambient spinor carrier does not assign particles to depths. The orthogonal normal six-plane must also remain distinct from the nested physical six-dimensional sector.

The ten-dimensional pure-pair stabilizer is established mathematics: Krasnov, Geometry of Spin(10) symmetry breaking, theorem A and sections VII A–C. The present calculation compares other parent dimensions, connects that construction to the existing normal-frame criterion, supplies an alternative positive polarization potential, and tests compatibility with the physical sector flag. It does not adopt an independent Higgs field, mediator particles, or the source paper's proposed gauge-field dynamics.

There is a further force-interpretation constraint. If the normal SU(3)SU(3)SU(3) acts as literal ambient rotations and must preserve that physical Q6Q_6Q6​, it reduces to the line stabilizer U(2)U(2)U(2), of dimension four. Preserving the real-line Q5Q_5Q5​ reduces it to SU(2)SU(2)SU(2), of dimension three. The exact ordinary rotation generators verify both reductions. Thus the full observed color symmetry cannot simply be called the group of ambient rotations preserving every fixed sector. Independent equivalent-mode transport, a moving compatible embedding, or another common field mechanism would need explicit construction.

Global topology also remains a separate problem. The round-four-sphere chiral spin bundle in the earlier curved existence control has nonzero second Chern number. It cannot contain two everywhere independent global sections as a trivial rank-two frame. A local pencil or a suitably twisted pencil subbundle is a weaker datum, and must be checked with the chosen global geometry. The existing projective tangent splitting obstruction is not removed by a pointwise spinor calculation.

Verification

The single checker and report construct actual spatial Clifford annihilators and bilinear rotation structures through parent depth twelve; solve exact real stabilizer equations; rotate the complete pair covariantly; compare non-pencils; compute the positive potential's exact Hessians; compare complete exterior charge characters through complex normal rank ten; and enforce one nested flag. The calculation passes 3,900 checks across 22 cases. Executed code hashes are preserved. No particle simulation, physical amplitude fit or new particle assignment is used.

Independent Observer Spin Is a Further Test

The full normal-color calculation verifies that the six-normal triplet commutes with observer spin and every three/four-dimensional tangent Clifford vector. It also finds that the standard ambient exterior charge generator fails to commute with two observer-spin generators. In an ordinary spatial interpretation, the familiar charge table therefore cannot directly be identified with measured electric charge. A separate normal charge operator with an auxiliary Clifford-volume branch is spin independent and reproduces the earlier weights locally, but its extension beyond the three-sector and its total orbital/domain action remain to derive. The known pure-pair stabilizer theorem and the conditional four-base/six-normal dimension criterion are unaffected.
