Spatial selection under a smooth self-mass law

Updated September 12, 2026

Summary: A common smooth current law preserves simplex ratios on uniform spatial controls but lifts holomorphic shape degeneracy and gives unstable nonconstant controls with strongly probe-dependent responses.

The spatial field must solve the same law

On a coherent coefficient ray, the current hierarchy reduces to the scalar source

FN(J)=μDNJJ2+σ2,ΦN(J)=μDN(J2+σ2−σ).(1) F_N(J)=\mu D_N\frac{J}{\sqrt{J^2+\sigma^2}},\qquad \Phi_N(J)=\mu D_N\bigl(\sqrt{J^2+\sigma^2}-\sigma\bigr). \tag{1} FN​(J)=μDN​J2+σ2​J​,ΦN​(J)=μDN​(J2+σ2​−σ).(1)

The full covariant Dirac equation still determines the physical profile. The fixed mass grades N=12,34N=12,34N=12,34 at depth six are distinct from any radial or angular cutoff used to solve that equation.

The flat radial localization test excludes regular finite-norm stationary fields for this smooth scalar source on infinite flat space. A separate full-current virial condition extends the restriction to nonradial fields in the positive-current cone. Those hypotheses differ from the curved spatial controls calculated here.

The spatial controls below use actual conditional CP3 geometry. Its six directions are ordinary physical spatial directions, and its radius is undetermined; no dimension is compactified. Write dV=VRdνdV=\mathcal V_Rd\nudV=VR​dν with ∫dν=1\int d\nu=1∫dν=1. The computations use a radius unit and this normalized measure. Ψphysical=VR−1/2Ψcode,μ^=Rμ,σ^=VRσ.(2) \Psi_{\rm physical}=\mathcal V_R^{-1/2}\Psi_{\rm code},\qquad \widehat\mu=R\mu,\qquad \widehat\sigma=\mathcal V_R\sigma. \tag{2} Ψphysical​=VR−1/2​Ψcode​,μ​=Rμ,σ=VR​σ.(2)

Thus setting the numerical volume factor to one means choosing density units; it does not independently set the metric volume and radius to two incompatible physical values. Both branches use the same normalization.

Similar response for physically selected probes remains possible, but the probe selection and its actual spatial geometry must be derived. Neither one matching average nor a close mass ratio establishes that result.
