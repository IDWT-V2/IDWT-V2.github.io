From Relative Spatial Curvature to the Charge Generator

Updated November 5, 2026

conditional research

The existing charge weights and the new spatial curvature construction can be joined by measuring relative orientation, rather than total oriented volume alone. The resulting mixed integral gives exactly the same alignment quantity that appears in the maintained charge generator. With the existing determinant branch retained, it produces 0, minus one, two thirds and minus one third. Complementing the orientation and swapping the branch reverses every charge.

This is a mathematical bridge between two constructions. It does not discover new charge values independently of the older rule. Its additional value is to express those values using curvature of an actual spatial profile and to expose what is needed to turn them into a conserved physical source.

Three levels are kept explicit: the exact curvature identity; the complete-profile charge eigenvalue and its conditional Noether interpretation; and a proposed source action in ten spatial dimensions plus time. The first two have bounded exact author checks. The action variation is derived below. Independent scientific review remains pending.

5. Noether Conservation and Its Physical Limits

For the canonical comparison action

ordinary spatial rotations and a common phase are symmetries. Treating the branch shift as a constant symmetry-preserving block label, (8) combines these symmetries. This is an explicitly stated action class; the original static critical profile is not thereby promoted to a nonzero-frequency solution.

Let xi be the vector field of the sum of the normal plane rotations, so L-total=minus i times xi dotted into the spatial derivative. Localizing the symmetry parameter gives the real Noether current

The last term follows because a spatial rotation changes the Lagrangian by a spatial divergence. It must not be omitted by treating the generator as a purely internal matrix. The Euler–Lagrange equations imply continuity for this current, with the usual permitted current improvements.

For an eigenprofile, its integrated charge is q times its total norm. Thus a dimensionless eigenvalue is not an absolute electric-source normalization. A common action unit and a norm-selection mechanism remain necessary if (11) is to give the same measured charge for particles with different counts and unknown amplitudes.

There is another structural test: total angular momentum depends on the choice of origin when momentum is nonzero. Hence (8) does not commute with all spatial translations and cannot simply be declared the universal electric charge. For an isolated moving pattern, replacing total angular momentum by the intrinsic quantity J minus X wedge P removes origin dependence; under a shift of origin, the changes in these two terms cancel. Conservation of that intrinsic quantity then requires the appropriate torque and centre-motion equations. That is a concrete repair to test, not a new proof of a local additive electric current during encounters.

The curvature form offers a different route: translated spatial profiles carry translated curvature densities with the same integral, without inserting a coordinate-origin angular momentum into the charge definition.

6. A Trial Source Action with the Correct Coefficient

A six-form is not yet an electric current in eleven-dimensional spacetime. A conserved vector current there is dual to a closed ten-form. Introduce a closed four-form W describing how the source occupies the remaining four directions, normalized to unit integral on the corresponding spatial slice. This is an additional support premise, to be supplied by the same field.

Then an explicit candidate source is

With the stated factorized normalization its spatial integral is e-star times q. The single e-star sets the common physical unit; no per-family charge coefficient is introduced. For a moving rigid preparation, a normalized W made from four comoving coordinate differentials remains closed and carries the source with the pattern. This is a kinematic construction, not a solution of its motion equations. Uniform infinite free extension does not supply a unit-integral W automatically.

There is an action whose partial variation gives (12). Write A for the connection of (2), A0 for its reference counterpart, F=dA and F0=dA0, and c=one half minus b. Propose

A common temporal phase supplies a temporal component, but changing only that phase is a boundary variation against a closed source and does not by itself produce a force or select a clock. The form degree is four plus one plus six, exactly eleven. These forms are descriptions of the single field on the physical spacetime, not new particle fields. Equation (13) is a proposed effective functional, not a term already derived from the IDWT master dynamics.

7. What Is Actually Unified, and the Remaining Support Test

There is now an exact chain:

spinor polarization ⟶ spatial phase curvature ⟶ mixed alignment integral ⟶ existing charge weights. \text{spinor polarization}\ \longrightarrow\ \text{spatial phase curvature} \ \longrightarrow\ \text{mixed alignment integral} \ \longrightarrow\ \text{existing charge weights}. spinor polarization ⟶ spatial phase curvature ⟶ mixed alignment integral ⟶ existing charge weights.

The same weights also extend to the complete profile through the orbital completion. The neutral depth-five state has a demonstrated intersection with the earlier handed-neutral construction. A proposed eleven-dimensional functional supplies the corresponding closed source kernel, including its reciprocal reference variation.

However, the six-normal integration in (5) requires actual field variation through those six spatial directions. A depth-three or depth-four profile extended uniformly through those normals has no such normal curvature. Its nonzero charge eigenvalue under (8) does not remedy that geometric fact. Similarly, a bound six-profile and the fixed normal six-carrier are not interchangeable solely because both have six directions. The ten-dimensional branch also needs the specified polarization and reference construction.

The constructive next realization is therefore a core-plus-orientation-tail solution: keep the core's maintained bound-depth classification while derive its actual exterior orientation and phase variation in the containing physical space. Then test whether the full same-field configuration supplies the six-form, a finite normalized transverse support and the required reciprocal response without changing the known charge and mass rules. This changes a completely uniform free-extension ansatz if that ansatz was being used. It must be stated and tested as such, including complete energy and kinetic consistency.

One cannot skip that step by integrating a six-form over four coordinates, by introducing another physical manifold, or by normalizing an infinite transverse volume to one. The current result makes the missing realization specific and provides equations it must satisfy. It does not claim that the complete physical electric source has already been derived for every family.
