Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

Both shapes change, and their interaction integral can change even though rigid canonical rotation would preserve its orientation overlap. This is a weak-interaction average on an overlapping round field feature. It is not a separated-particle encounter, a solution for the normal carrier or the complete spatial field equation at arbitrary coupling. All complex coefficients in the stated resonant family are retained; its symmetry and spectral restriction are described below.
