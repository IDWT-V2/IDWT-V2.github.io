Limits of a Casimir penalty

An assumed term κCtot\kappa C_{\rm tot}κCtot​ with κ>0\kappa>0κ>0 has zero energy exactly on available singlets. This is a useful conditional selection operator. It does not derive that term from the master kernel, set κ\kappaκ, or prove spatial confinement, a growing separation energy, or nuclear depth three. If other coloured fields participate, the constraint concerns their total charge too; a valence-only projection requires justification.
