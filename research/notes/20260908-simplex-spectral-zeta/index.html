Specify the positive spectrum

For integer d≥2d\geq2d≥2, consider one eigenvalue per positive integer index, with no additional multiplicity:

Pd(n)=S(n,d)=(n+d−1d)=n(n+1)⋯(n+d−1)d!,Zd(s)=∑n=1∞Pd(n)−s. P_d(n)=S(n,d)=\binom{n+d-1}{d} =\frac{n(n+1)\cdots(n+d-1)}{d!}, \qquad Z_d(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}P_d(n)^{-s}. Pd​(n)=S(n,d)=(dn+d−1​)=d!n(n+1)⋯(n+d−1)​,Zd​(s)=n=1∑∞​Pd​(n)−s.

Comparison with n−dsn^{-ds}n−ds proves absolute convergence for Re⁡s>1/d\operatorname{Re}s>1/dRes>1/d. All eigenvalues in this definition are dimensionless. The integer ddd labels the polynomial sequence; interpreting an actual spatial operator requires its eigenvalues and multiplicities. The associated positive heat sum is developed in Simplex heat trace.
