A binomial compatibility law for actual spatial spinors

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

A family of circulating spinor patterns gives an exact orientation dependence for the declared local current interaction. The individual linear frequencies stay fixed under spatial rotation, while the interaction integral can become very small. Its binomial factor comes from integrating the actual spatial modes, rather than assigning a small coefficient to a particle sector.

The construction uses the conditional round four-sphere feature in ordinary six-dimensional space and the spinor parent already defined in A spatial spinor parent for the curvature frequency. It is one spatial family at depth six. It is not a photon model, a depth-five neutrality mechanism or a calculation of interaction rates in matter. The common surface, normal carrier and endpoint-current action remain assumptions.
