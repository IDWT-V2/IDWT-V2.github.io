What this comparison supplies

The calculation changes a concrete feature of one scalar kernel and recomputes the stationary spatial field and its reciprocal response. The smooth moment kernel avoids the universal weak-coupling radial instability of local contact while retaining a nonuniform coherent branch. It provides a candidate class for a master-kernel derivation. It does not derive that kernel, select LLL, determine the physical radius, identify ordinary electric charge, or establish observed interactions between particles associated with the nested spatial sectors.

The common projective-chain action uses the same unnormalized spatial kernel at every depth. Its single-sector strength therefore includes the factor 1/(m+1)1/(m+1)1/(m+1) when using this note's normalized-kernel solver. The instantaneous interaction remains an effective candidate whose causal master-field origin must be supplied.

The balanced holomorphic family gives a different exact branch of this same moment action. Its isotropic first spatial moment produces a constant potential despite a nonconstant field density. The resulting frequency is exactly linear, and the complete spatial linearization has no exponential modes. This family uses noncoherent holomorphic superpositions; the coherent branches in (6) retain their spatial corrections and the radial stability scope established above.
