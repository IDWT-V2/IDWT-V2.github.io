A Moving Finite-Speed Count Action With Positive Inertia

Updated October 14, 2026

The finite-speed count parent can be embedded in one explicit moving-material action. The same action retains its fourteen supplied frequency assignments, accounts for internal energy transport in the complete momentum, and has a positive joint wave/material kinetic response. Weighting the elastic energy by the wave's own local energy removes a separately imposed carrier mass and an uncontrolled stiffness-to-amplitude ratio.

This is an inverse construction. Its material geometry, component carrier, sector units and radius relation remain constitutive inputs. The frequency approximation is the existing finite-chain result, not a new fit or a forward selection of particle masses. The physical containing space is ordinary macroscopic space. The calculation below describes an active d-dimensional body for d=2,3,4,5,6,10; embedding its finite energy in all containing directions requires a further construction. Field components do not replace deterministic electron trajectories.

One Covariant First-Gradient Action

Let a be a physical material coordinate in a ball of radius R_d, y=a/R_d, w(a)=sqrt(1-|y|²), X(a,t) the physical material map and U(a,t) the retained real field components, including two real quadratures for complex notation. Set F=D_a X, v=X_t and gamma=(1-|v|²/c²)^(-1/2). The rest material derivative and rest strain are

D_A U=partial_A U+gamma²(F^T v)_A U_t/c²,
Cmat=F^T F+gamma² F^T v v^T F/c².

These formulas follow by taking spacetime material labels phi^A(x,t), defining B^{AB}=partial_mu phi^A partial^mu phi^B, and contracting the wave derivative with the rest-spatial dual E_A^mu=partial^mu phi^B(B^-1)_(BA). The material four-velocity is orthogonal to all label gradients. Thus D_A=E_A^mu partial_mu, Cmat=B^-1 and the invariant material measure is w da dt/gamma. Gradients must remain independent and spacelike; the material map must remain invertible.

Let H=k_d H_(M,b), k_d=mu_d M^(d-1)/d!, be the complete all-grade parent in y=a/R_d, with b_0=1 and b_k=b for k>=1. Its physical first-order expression H=sum_A C_A(a)partial_A+M0(a) is in its orthonormal component chart. Its physical derivative coefficients contain R_d, giving s=b k_d R_d. M, b, kappa and r are dimensionless; H, omega, k_d and mu_d have frequency units. Retain all of its weighted lower-order terms. Define H_D by replacing partial_A with D_A and put

W(C)=||C-I||_F²/4+[tr(C-I)]²/8,
S=int da dt (w/gamma){gamma²|U_t|²/2
                 -[1+2 kappa W(Cmat)]|H_D U|²/2}.       (1)

The dimensionless kappa is common across depths. All fields in (1) are retained parts of the proposed common field law, not exchanged particles. Equation (1) contains only first time derivatives. Covariantizing a high-order scalar count polynomial would not have this property; the retained first-order parent is essential to the present proof.

At X=a, H psi=omega psi with psi real gives the exact balanced solution

U=alpha psi exp(-i omega t),   X=a.

Here W(I)=DW(I)=0, the first material variation vanishes, and the internal flux is zero. With q=U_t, z=HU and h=(|q|²+|z|²)/2, this branch has |q|²=|z|²=h. Its rest energy and conserved phase action obey E0=Q omega. With p=L_(U_t), including the measure weight, the sign convention is Q=-int p.JU for U proportional to exp(-i omega t). Q is not identified with electric charge or fixed to an empirical action unit. No amplitude has been chosen to fit a mass.

This controls arbitrary spatially varying material velocities, not just rigid translation. Overall physical amplitude cancels from the sign condition.

With J_X=det F>0, the physical energy density, momentum and energy flux are respectively e/J_X, P/J_X and (e v+F K)/J_X. Covariance gives the exact identity

and a symmetric physical momentum flux. At unstrained rest these reduce to

Internal wave energy transport therefore contributes to complete momentum. A mixed wave with changing flux cannot consistently keep its material momentum fixed. These are local conservation identities for solutions of the coupled equations, without a time average or a common clock imposed on distinct patterns. The zero total rest stress is a consequence of this particular isospectral material law, not a universal field postulate.

Positive Translational Inertia for the Free Symmetry Family

A uniform boost of the stationary solution contracts the material map and adds its simultaneity phase. At infinitesimal velocity V,

The phase structure J is multiplication by i in complex notation. The wave phase cannot be omitted while claiming the boosted solution. A direct check uses [H,f]=C(grad f) with f=V.a/c². Substitution into the complete quadratic wave and material equations gives

delta P(a)=w(a)h(a)V/c²,
delta P_total=E0 V/c².

Thus the complete translational inertia is positive E0/c², without an added carrier mass. The canonical multiplier-domain result and the actual polynomial profiles justify this tangent. The boosted family uses natural free boundary conditions; a clamp does not permit the same translation symmetry.

Ballistic center displacement is consequently an exact generalized zero mode of the free linear system. A norm measuring absolute position cannot stay uniformly bounded for a moving particle. Free stability must compare shape relative to its moving symmetry family and retain conserved momentum. A thin d-dimensional sheet in R10 is not automatically a complete ambient body: strain depending only on F^T F leaves arbitrary normal deformations without quadratic bending stiffness. This is a neutral quadratic kernel, not evidence of instability or decay; generic finite normal graphs have positive quartic strain energy.

Physical Scope

Equation (1) connects retained count frequencies, local momentum balance, positive inertia, causal principal propagation and a controlled clamped linear regime in one common construction. The finite chain still has extra modes and an explicit radius cost R_d=c/[100 b k_d] in the certified example. Its small radius is a consequence of this parent, not a measured size or a universal restriction on alternative constructions. A local causal Hessian bound alone is not that combined evolution theorem. The physical mass-frequency conversion is already known; this work addresses a candidate spatial mechanism and its consistency, not the conversion itself.
