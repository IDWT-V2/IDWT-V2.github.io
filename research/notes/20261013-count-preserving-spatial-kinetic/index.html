A Positive Spatial Kinetic Action That Preserves Count Dynamics

Updated October 13, 2026

Result and Physical Meaning

There is an explicit positive quadratic kinetic completion of a degree-two canonical classical field action. It retains every solution of the original action on its stated positive-energy domain, including the count-frequency solutions and their complete energy and conserved phase action. This provides a constructive compatibility result between the count dynamics and a second-order kinetic law. It does not yet derive the electron orbit from the same physical field.

All field values, derivatives and interactions below occur on ordinary physical space. Spatial integrals are mathematical tools for specifying the action. They do not place motion in a space of quantum states or turn a field intensity into a position probability.

The physical containing geometry has at least ten macroscopic spatial dimensions; whether its total dimension is ten, a larger finite number or infinite is unknown. Depth classifies a field pattern's dimensional involvement; it is not a separate physical sector space. Flat ordinary space is the current working geometry. The completion below acts on a specified spatial action. It does not make separately chosen spherical and projective geometries into one compatible model.

A Shared Flat-Space Parent

6. What Has Been Established

A positive second-order spatial kinetic law and the existing canonical count dynamics are mathematically compatible under an explicit nonlocal completion. This is an action-level result with preserved complete energy and phase action, rather than an assignment of inertia from frequency alone. It leaves the complete physical mass–orbit derivation open.

The new action is not uniquely selected. Its positive transverse kernel can be changed while retaining TG=Phi and the original solutions. Original instabilities along retained trajectories are not automatically removed. The positive-energy domain, source regularity, propagation, total energy under exact uniform extension and stability of the enlarged dynamics remain concrete tests; the companion flat-space parent resolves its own finite-profile and moving-center construction. No observed particle property or preferred sector depth is newly established by this completion alone.

The checker passes 17 exact conditions: a nonlinear canonical example with full metric derivatives, energy and Noether checks, plus actual spatial-integral and pointwise controls. These verify signs and implementation; the general claims follow from the explicit variational proof above. The nonlinear example uses H=(|z1|^4+2|z2|^4+|z1|²|z2|²)/(|z1|²+|z2|²).
