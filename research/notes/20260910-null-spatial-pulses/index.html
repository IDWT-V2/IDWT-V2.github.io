Coupling still needs the full complementary field

The two normalizable modes, exact speed and handedness are useful propagation data. They do not by themselves give a Maxwell current, electric charges, a Coulomb law, or neutrino selectivity.

Two opposite pulses generally require a further spatial deformation. Spatial displacement action derives that residual and the exact action of uniformly tilted and moving profiles. As with general polarization textures, an equation for selected coordinates alone must not replace the full spatial field equation.
