Coherent spatial mode-bundle fields

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: Pointwise coherent fields on the curved tangent-mode bundle can have balanced spatial moments, but local contact and a transported graph pairing retain different band-dependent normalizations.

Keep the physical field and its mode bundle together

The balanced spatial construction gives exact linear frequencies for a specified first-moment interaction. Its field is a section of a spatial line twist. The mass-counting construction uses a second object: the cumulative tangent-mode bundle. Compatibility requires a field on that bundle with its actual connection. An arbitrary constant array of coefficients cannot replace the connection of the curved tangent directions.

Use the specified physical CP3 geometry, with undetermined Fubini–Study radius RRR. Its six directions remain ordinary macroscopic spatial directions, without compactification. The following complex bundle types describe that same real tangent geometry; they do not partition the six directions into preferred real subspaces.

Write T=T1,0CP3T=T^{1,0}\mathrm{CP}^3T=T1,0CP3. The Kähler metric gives a parallel complex linear identification of the (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) cotangent bundle with TTT. Thus the homogenized mode bundle and its degree-NNN carrier have the holomorphic descriptions

W=1⊕T⊕T∗,EN=Sym⁡NW,rank⁡EN=DN=(N+66).(1) W=\mathbf1\oplus T\oplus T^*,\qquad E_N=\operatorname{Sym}^N W,\qquad \operatorname{rank}E_N=D_N=\binom{N+6}{6}. \tag{1} W=1⊕T⊕T∗,EN​=SymNW,rankEN​=DN​=(6N+6​).(1)

The natural connection is the direct-sum and symmetric-power connection induced by the spatial Levi–Civita connection. On a Kähler manifold the tangent Chern connection equals the Levi–Civita connection; its (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) part in a holomorphic frame is ∂ˉ\bar\partial∂ˉ. See Calegari, Section 1.5. The calculation below retains this connection rather than trivializing ENE_NEN​.

The constructive result (14) and the constraint (19) can coexist because their actions contract the source differently. Deriving which contraction the master field supplies is essential to a physical mass operator.

The physical point-evaluation calculation now treats the entire holomorphic zero-form space of this same curved mode bundle. It derives the complete contact spectrum and its sharp spatial norm bound. Those operator eigenvalues are distinct from the auxiliary simplex rank; the coherent construction above does not make the two evaluation maps identical.

The constant-density local construction uses five orthogonal tangent copies to solve the written local scalar equation with a noncoherent spatial field. It also proves that the only constant-norm coherent holomorphic section at this degree-zero line twist is the pure-slack field. This identifies a precise difference between the two spatial patterns and their interaction requirements.

Verification and remaining scope
