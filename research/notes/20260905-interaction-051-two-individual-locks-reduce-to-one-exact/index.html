51. Two individual locks reduce to one exact relative restoring mode

The conditional two-electron lock can be reduced exactly in center-of-mass and relative coordinates.

Start from

Vpair−lock=κ2(∥h1−Q1Tu1∥2+∥h2−Q2Tu2∥2). V_{\rm pair-lock} =\frac{\kappa}{2} \left( \|h_1-Q_1^Tu_1\|^2 + \|h_2-Q_2^Tu_2\|^2 \right). Vpair−lock​=2κ​(∥h1​−Q1T​u1​∥2+∥h2​−Q2T​u2​∥2).

Introduce

U=u1+u22,u=u1−u2, U=\frac{u_1+u_2}{2}, \qquad u=u_1-u_2, U=2u1​+u2​​,u=u1​−u2​,

and

H=h1+h22,h=h1−h2. H=\frac{h_1+h_2}{2}, \qquad h=h_1-h_2. H=2h1​+h2​​,h=h1​−h2​.

After minimizing over the hidden center-of-mass coordinate HHH, and then working in the visible center-of-mass frame U=0U=0U=0, the energy reduces exactly to

Vrel=κ4∥h−Q1T+Q2T2u∥2. \boxed{ V_{\rm rel} =\frac{\kappa}{4} \left\| h-\frac{Q_1^T+Q_2^T}{2}u \right\|^2. } Vrel​=4κ​​h−2Q1T​+Q2T​​u​2.​

Thus the two individual residuals combine into a single relative restoring residual

rrel=h−Au,A=Q1T+Q2T2. \boxed{ r_{\rm rel} =h-Au, \qquad A=\frac{Q_1^T+Q_2^T}{2}. } rrel​=h−Au,A=2Q1T​+Q2T​​.​

The singular values already found for AAA are

σ(A)={1,ν,ν}, \boxed{ \sigma(A)=\{1,\sqrt\nu,\sqrt\nu\}, } σ(A)={1,ν​,ν​},​

where the scalar overlap is

ν=cos⁡2ϕ2. \nu=\cos^2\frac{\phi}{2}. ν=cos22ϕ​.

Therefore the conditional collision model does not require three unrelated hidden restoring coordinates to be inserted separately. Once the two individual frame locks exist, their center-of-mass reduction automatically produces one covariant relative lock whose anisotropy is fixed by the electron-pair invariant.

At projective coincidence,

ν→1,Q1→Q2, \nu\to1, \qquad Q_1\to Q_2, ν→1,Q1​→Q2​,

so AAA becomes orthogonal and the relative lock becomes

Vrel→κ4∥h−QTu∥2. \boxed{ V_{\rm rel} \to \frac{\kappa}{4}\|h-Q^Tu\|^2. } Vrel​→4κ​∥h−QTu∥2.​

This is the same difference-norm structure that produced the exact zero-Schur-backreaction result in the earlier 3+33+33+3 compatibility calculation, now obtained as the relative reduction of two individual locks rather than postulated directly for the pair.

51.1 Status
