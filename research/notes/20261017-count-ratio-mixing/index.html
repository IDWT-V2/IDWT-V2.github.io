A precise projection identity

The brackets specify a classical preparation average. They do not define a probability distribution of electron positions or a Born law. Likewise the trace identity is not yet a transition amplitude. Identifying its square root with an observed mixing magnitude is the physical bridge being proposed.

On a homogeneous band whose diagonal kernels are constant, the ratio is pointwise constant as well. A physical contact must specify whether it samples an integrated, local or otherwise weighted response. These are different mechanisms.

Cumulative bands of all polynomial degrees through NNN supply the required nesting. A single-parity real amplitude-sphere realization has a different property: degree-NNN homogeneous polynomial restrictions contain harmonic degrees N,N−2,…N,N-2,\ldotsN,N−2,…. Opposite parities are orthogonal. The proposed mixing pairs have degrees (0,3)(0,3)(0,3), (2,19)(2,19)(2,19) and (2,71)(2,71)(2,71); they are all opposite-parity pairs. Their direct inner-product contact is consequently zero in that particular realization. Under the proposed direct overlap mechanism, the fitted nonzero mixing favors a genuinely cumulative realization. It does not rule out every sphere construction: a different interaction could mix parity, but would require its own derivation.

Reproduction and next test

The arithmetic follows directly from the mass assignments and scale law. The exact trace calculation is reproducible by choosing any nested orthonormal bases and summing the retained squared components; the ball version additionally requires its actual kernel and integration measure. The original calculation used exact rational checks for the band ratios and parity controls. The finite candidate search and its failed comparisons are retained in the source-pinned research record.

The useful next calculation is a reciprocal same-field transition vertex on a cumulative spatial carrier, with its kinetic metrics and contact weighting varied as part of the action. It must yield these magnitudes while accounting for the third generation, bottom, unitarity and CP phase. A mode-space projection alone does not do that work.
