Sawtooth Harmonics in Geometric Sector Coupling

Updated October 9, 2026

A particle pattern may contain the sector fundamental and every harmonic through its assigned mass-frequency. Under a specified sawtooth preparation, the lower harmonics produce a fundamental-frequency density source whose strength approaches a fixed value as the upper cutoff grows. Combining this source with the existing spatial contact, color and angular operators gives calculable phase forces and response spectra.

The most useful results are an exact fundamental coefficient 1−1/K1-1/K1−1/K, a reciprocal phase-overlap kernel, and a tetrahedral example whose shape remains unchanged while its five positive shape eigenvalues split. A common receiver calculation also gives nearly equal density responses for the electron, muon and tau cutoffs. Small depth-five response occurs for a derivative source, whereas a direct density source remains appreciable. Those alternatives specify different interaction terms and must retain that distinction.

The maintained mass-frequency is the supplied endpoint in this calculation. The physical hypotheses being tested are the harmonic occupation, its spatial readout and the interaction. No particle amplitudes or individual pair coefficients are fitted.
