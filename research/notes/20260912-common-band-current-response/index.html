Exact linear mass and amplitude curvature

There is also a simple general constraint. If a differentiable stationary branch has EN(Q)=mNQE_N(Q)=m_NQEN​(Q)=mN​Q on an open interval of canonical norms, then

ωN=dENdQ=mN,dωNdQ=0.(10) \omega_N=\frac{dE_N}{dQ}=m_N, \qquad \frac{d\omega_N}{dQ}=0. \tag{10} ωN​=dQdEN​​=mN​,dQdωN​​=0.(10)

A nonzero density curvature cannot coexist with that exact linear branch on the same interval. The constraint does not exclude polarization couplings, a physically selected isolated norm or a different stationary branch. It does explain why smoothing the peak generates both a response and a mass correction. The exact entropy energy-frequency defect of (6) must also be retained.

Reconstructing a fixed-order current from a coherent symbol

Verification

The common current probes provide a different joint action with exact target-grade independence for a fixed lower-order probe under stated coherent conditions. Its currents also generate the isolated self-source. The smooth spatial comparison shows why agreement of one broad probe does not establish agreement of the complete spatial response: the two resolved nonconstant controls differ strongly near their nodal region.
