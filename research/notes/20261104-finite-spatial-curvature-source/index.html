Finite Spatial Charge Sources and the Dynamics They Still Require

Updated November 5, 2026

conditional research

In the four-plus-six spatial reference split, the base has four ordinary spatial dimensions and its perpendicular complement has six. Time is a separate coordinate in the infinite-dimensional physical spacetime. This four-plus-six split concerns ten spatial directions in the matter domain.

The curvature charge construction can be made into an explicit finite source on the existing ten-dimensional spatial spinor. Its transverse normalization can be calculated from the same field instead of assigned independently. A wider class of spatial orientation profiles preserves the same charge weights and permits a finite charge radius. It also permits neutral configurations with nonzero signed internal source distributions.

The important dynamical distinction is now calculable: the topological source term preserves the charge but does not, by itself, restore a preferred size in the fixed-boundary family considered here. A separate metric-dependent energy must provide localization. A bounded trial-energy calculation below shows one possible stabilizing balance, with its changed premises and limited scope explicit.

These results are principal-authored conditional research. They are not independently reviewed, and they do not establish a completed physical electromagnetic interaction, selected particle count or all-family solution.

6. What the Topological Action Actually Does on These Fields

The previous trial action contains the terms A wedge F0 cubed and A wedge F wedge F0 squared, multiplied by W. On the static spatial textures with constant temporal phase rates, its radial integrals depend only on the boundary values appearing in (11) and (16).

Changing a, b or the radial interpolation while retaining those boundary values does not change this part of the action. It therefore gives no restoring force for these size variations. A pure common-phase variation is also a boundary term against the closed source. This does not prove that the functional has no effect on more general time-dependent orientation histories, but it rules out claiming that it already sustains these profiles.

The distinction is constructive: geometric charge can stay fixed while metric-dependent energy selects shape. The same charge-preserving family is available to test candidate stabilizing terms without retuning charge to compensate for an energy change.

Because A, its reference and W are induced by the same field, their variations must still be retained together. The partial connection variation in the preceding note is not an independent Maxwell equation. The current has been constructed; the complete response equation remains to be selected and solved.

There is an explicit test of the induced connection contribution. For a normalized coefficient field z and a closed source kernel K, variation of A=minus i z-dagger dz gives

δA=2Im⁡(δz†dz)−id(z†δz). \delta A=2\operatorname{Im}(\delta z^\dagger\mathrm dz) -i\mathrm d(z^\dagger\delta z). δA=2Im(δz†dz)−id(z†δz).

The last term contributes only a boundary term against K. Writing the dual kernel as a vector k-mu, the non-phase variation therefore depends on the projected derivative (I minus zz-dagger) times k-mu partial-mu z. If the kernel is purely temporal and the texture only changes by a common phase, that expression vanishes. Thus a nonzero charge-weighted kernel does not guarantee a nonzero field-shape force. Spatial orientation transport or further time-dependent shape motion can make the projected derivative nonzero; the reference and marginal variations remain additional terms. Once a common action is changed, the conserved current used to define W must also be checked for that action rather than silently borrowed from its predecessor.

8. Physical Scope and Next Closure

The exact ten-dimensional critical source in section 2 and the fast-tail trial in section 3 are two distinct constructions. The first retains the known spatial Dirac solution and its existing clock limitation. The second repairs the charge moments but changes the spatial profile and requires new dynamics. Their successful properties cannot be combined as if one solved field already possessed them all.

A finite texture varying through all ten spatial directions also does not automatically preserve the literal lower-depth particle assignments. To interpret it as a lower-depth core with an orientation tail, one must derive that distinction from the common dynamics. Taking transverse widths to infinity does not solve the problem: the normalized marginal spreads without a localized limit, and the unnormalized uniform extension has infinite full-space norm.

The next closure is therefore an explicit common field equation admitting a persistent core, the required orientation tail, its temporal clock and a reciprocal response. The current work supplies normalized source formulas, charge-preserving shape freedom, a neutral-response diagnostic and energy conditions that such an equation can be tested against. It leaves the simplex count and the family support selection open.
