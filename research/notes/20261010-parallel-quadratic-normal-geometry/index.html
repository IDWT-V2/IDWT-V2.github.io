Parallel Quadratic Normal Geometry and a Distinct Triplet Algebra

Updated October 10, 2026

The quadratic Veronese embedding

ν:CP2⟶CP5,[z]⟼[z⊙z] \nu:\mathbb{CP}^{2}\longrightarrow\mathbb{CP}^{5},\qquad [z]\longmapsto[z\odot z] ν:CP2⟶CP5,[z]⟼[z⊙z]

has a parallel, everywhere invertible complex second fundamental map B:Sym⁡2T1,0CP2→N1,0B:\operatorname{Sym}^{2}T^{1,0}\mathbb{CP}^{2}\to N^{1,0}B:Sym2T1,0CP2→N1,0. Its normal bundle is genuinely Sym⁡2T1,0CP2\operatorname{Sym}^{2}T^{1,0}\mathbb{CP}^{2}Sym2T1,0CP2, with c1=9Hc_1=9Hc1​=9H and c2=30H2c_2=30H^2c2​=30H2. Its normal holonomy is the four-dimensional image Sym⁡2U(2)\operatorname{Sym}^{2}U(2)Sym2U(2), conjugate in U(3)U(3)U(3) to U(1)×SO(3)U(1)\times SO(3)U(1)×SO(3). The connected traceless holonomy is SO(3)SO(3)SO(3), with Lie algebra su(2)\mathfrak{su}(2)su(2), rather than the full eight-dimensional su(3)\mathfrak{su}(3)su(3).

Removing the determinant line yields a globally defined rank-three bundle with a parallel symmetric scalar pairing. This extra invariant distinguishes its spin-one triplet from the fundamental triplet of full SU3. The original normal bundle's pairing is line valued; its full unitary holonomy has no untwisted scalar in N⊗NN\otimes NN⊗N.

There is no normal-section obstruction to placing this four-base in a smooth 5/6/10 spatial flag. A complete ordinary ten-dimensional extension, retaining the exact Veronese metric near the base, supplies a complete occupied 2/3/4/5/6/10 flag. This extension changes the global ambient manifold from CP5 to the total normal bundle. Smooth intermediate normal lines are not parallel lines, and neither assertion proves closed global intermediate inclusions inside unchanged CP5.

2. Why the second fundamental map is parallel

There is a short symmetry proof. At [1:0:0][1:0:0][1:0:0], the unitary map diag⁡(1,−1,−1)\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1)diag(1,−1,−1) of VVV preserves the Veronese image. Its induced projective isometry acts by minus the identity on the tangent directions L⊙QL\odot QL⊙Q, and by plus the identity on the normal directions Sym⁡2Q\operatorname{Sym}^2QSym2Q.

The covariant derivative ∇B\nabla B∇B has three tangent arguments and one normal output. Invariance under this isometry makes it equal to its negative at the fixed point, so it vanishes there. Homogeneity gives ∇B=0\nabla B=0∇B=0 on all of CP2. This establishes parallelity for the actual induced tangent and normal connections, rather than inferring it from constant quadratic coordinate coefficients.

The primary normal-holonomy calculation of Console and Di Scala, Parallel submanifolds of complex projective space and their normal holonomy independently supports this example: Table 1 gives the Veronese normal action as the isotropy action of Sp(n)/U(n)Sp(n)/U(n)Sp(n)/U(n), and Proposition 2.4 identifies normal holonomy with the effective slice action. For n=2n=2n=2, that action is the symmetric-square representation used here. Their theorem concerns the Fubini–Study geometry; it does not assert full U3 for this parallel example.

4. Actual holonomy and the additional pair invariant

The Fubini–Study tangent holonomy of CP2 is U2. For example, its complex curvature endomorphisms, up to an overall nonzero scale, are Eij+δijIE_{ij}+\delta_{ij}IEij​+δij​I; these span the complexification gl(2,C)\mathfrak{gl}(2,\mathbb C)gl(2,C). Parallelity and invertibility of BBB identify normal parallel transport with the symmetric square of tangent parallel transport. Consequently

Hol⁡(N)=Sym⁡2U(2)≅U(2)/{±I}≅U(1)×SO(3). \operatorname{Hol}(N)=\operatorname{Sym}^2U(2) \cong U(2)/\{\pm I\}\cong U(1)\times SO(3). Hol(N)=Sym2U(2)≅U(2)/{±I}≅U(1)×SO(3).

CP2 is simply connected, so the full and restricted normal holonomy groups coincide here. The last group is embedded as scalar phases times the complexified real standard SO3 action. A central tangent phase eiθI2e^{i\theta}I_2eiθI2​ acts on NNN by e2iθI3e^{2i\theta}I_3e2iθI3​. The connected commutator group is SO3, and after removing the determinant connection the holonomy on WWW is precisely SO3 inside SU3. The normal algebra has dimension four and its traceless algebra dimension three. No commutator closure of these three generators produces the five missing SU3 directions.

5. Smooth intermediate sectors and a complete occupied flag

A tubular flag inside the fixed CP5 geometry

The complex normal rank is three and the real base dimension four. A smooth section of NNN transverse to its zero section would have zeros of codimension six. Since six exceeds four, such a section has no zeros. This supplies a nowhere-zero smooth normal section sss. Normalize it using the normal metric.

Its real span ℓR=Rs\ell_{\mathbb R}=\mathbb R sℓR​=Rs and complex span ℓC=Cs\ell_{\mathbb C}=\mathbb C sℓC​=Cs are globally trivial smooth subbundles. A tubular neighborhood of the compact Veronese base embeds a sufficiently small normal disk bundle in CP5. Restricting it to these two subbundles gives global neighborhoods of the entire base with real dimensions five and six:

B⊂Disk⁡(ℓR)⊂Disk⁡(ℓC)⊂Disk⁡(NR)⊂CP5. B\subset\operatorname{Disk}(\ell_{\mathbb R}) \subset\operatorname{Disk}(\ell_{\mathbb C}) \subset\operatorname{Disk}(N_{\mathbb R})\subset CP5. B⊂Disk(ℓR​)⊂Disk(ℓC​)⊂Disk(NR​)⊂CP5.

These are smooth inclusions. The complex line subbundle need not be holomorphic, and its tubular image is not asserted to be a complex submanifold. There is no contradiction with c1(N)=9Hc_1(N)=9Hc1​(N)=9H and c2(N)=30H2c_2(N)=30H^2c2​(N)=30H2: a trivial complex line splits off smoothly, leaving a rank-two bundle with those two nonzero classes. Topological existence of a section neither trivializes the full normal bundle nor supplies a parallel section.

In fact neither the real line nor the complex line is preserved by the normal connection. The irreducible SO3 triplet, together with the central phase in the original bundle, permits no parallel proper normal line. Imposing a parallel line as an additional requirement would exclude this example. A mere smooth nested flag does not impose that requirement.

Open finite-radius tubular sectors generally have incomplete induced metrics. Their existence does not solve a request for complete intermediate sectors inside the unchanged compact parent. No closed global 5/6 inclusion in unchanged CP5 is claimed here.

It demonstrates a complete allowed curved-parent flag, not a complete extension inside the original CP5. It also supplies no claim about the color holonomy of every lower sector or its physical coupling; those require their own specified carrier and restriction rule.

Why the older projective obstruction is different

For the standard CP2⊂CP3CP2\subset CP3CP2⊂CP3, the normal bundle has complex rank one, O(1)\mathcal O(1)O(1), whose underlying oriented real two-plane has nonzero Euler class. A real five-dimensional intermediate manifold containing the whole CP2 would require a real normal line subbundle; over simply connected CP2 such a line is trivial and would provide a forbidden nowhere-zero normal section.

The Veronese normal bundle has real rank six, so that rank-two Euler obstruction does not apply. Likewise the chosen lower S2S^2S2 is a small null-homologous sphere in a coordinate chart, not the projective CP1⊂CP2CP1\subset CP2CP1⊂CP2. Requiring a global real-three intermediate around that projective CP1 would reintroduce its own nonzero normal Euler obstruction. The full flag above does not make that additional choice.

6. What the comparison teaches about color selection

An invertible quadratic normal map and three complex normal directions do not by themselves select full SU3. The parallel curved example has a smaller holonomy and an additional symmetric pair invariant. The maintained flat-ambient graph instead has nonparallel bending and full U3 normal holonomy. Parallelity therefore separates two materially different geometric realizations of the same pointwise rank match.

If full SU3 is the desired traceless normal algebra, maintaining a parallel isomorphism Sym⁡2T→N\operatorname{Sym}^2T\to NSym2T→N at complex tangent rank two prevents it in any ambient geometry: tangent holonomy has dimension at most four and its symmetric-square image remains that small. The canonical curved example realizes this restriction exactly. Full SU3 requires relaxing that parallel identification or changing the proposed carrier/connection; a smooth nested flag alone selects neither possibility.

This is useful geometric discrimination, not a derivation of higher-sector force absence, an observed coupling strength or quark occupation. CP2's compact topology, its complex structure, the Fubini–Study metric and the choice of quadratic Veronese embedding remain conditional geometric premises.
