Entropy-Difference Field Action with Exact Localized Patterns and Global Evolution

Updated October 13, 2026

A second-spatial-order classical field equation can sustain exact finite-norm Gaussian patterns with two preferred widths, continuous spatial orientations and free center motion. Its full nonlinear Cauchy problem has a unique global finite-energy flow. Nearby Gaussian initial fields remain localized within their invariant family. These statements coexist with growing non-Gaussian linear disturbances: persistence within that family is a narrower result than stability of the complete field.

The construction is a candidate same-field support law. The filter lengths, coefficients and preferred widths are inverse inputs. Every maintained width multiplicity has the same support clock; neither the unequal observed mass-frequencies nor their simplex selection is derived. The flat-space count action supplies a different inverse construction with exact count clocks. Its action and localization theorem must be kept distinct from those below.
