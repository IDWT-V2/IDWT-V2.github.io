Complete positive-band source balance and its energy cost

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The fast bands already present in the spatial carrier can supply a source of the opposite sign. There is an exact constructive way to balance all centered sources after time averaging. It also exposes a normalization problem: the resulting energy per canonical wave charge cannot remain equal to the selected small frequency in the tested model.

This calculation retains the full finite carrier, both surface signs, the density-gradient multiplier and all maintained controls at depths 3, 4, 5, 6 and 10. It is an integrated, time-averaged condition. It does not establish a pointwise coherent equilibrium or include the remaining normal continuum. All modes considered here have positive frequency and positive energy in their stated canonical normalization.
