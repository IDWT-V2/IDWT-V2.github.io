Fixed Count Clocks Leave a Testable Curvature Constraint

Updated October 12, 2026

The continuous and locally affine count interactions agree in value and first derivative at every eligible count node. They therefore produce the same stationary fields and frequencies. Their curvature differs, and the full spatial index test can distinguish that difference without changing the mass inputs.

1. One Common Local Interpolation

Let f_0 be the locally affine envelope and f_1 the continuous count function, both with the same diagnostic sector scale. On their common open constitutive domain define

fγ=(1−γ)f0+γf1,0≤γ≤1.(1) f_\gamma=(1-\gamma)f_0+\gamma f_1,\qquad 0\le\gamma\le1. \tag{1} fγ​=(1−γ)f0​+γf1​,0≤γ≤1.(1)

At each eligible x_l, f_gamma and its first derivative agree with f_1. Its second derivative is gamma f_1 double prime. Hence all count clocks and stationary fields remain unchanged, while their exact real source derivative becomes

Bγ=H+γN1.(2) B_\gamma=H+\gamma N_1. \tag{2} Bγ​=H+γN1​.(2)

The complete positive-wave spatial stiffness is B_gamma squared minus omega squared, with the gyroscopic velocity term retained. Squaring a projected B_gamma is not substituted for that operator.

Equation (1) specifies one constitutive interpolation, not a separately adjusted correction for each particle. Its global extension and its physical derivation remain additional questions.

3. A Pair Test Misses the Spatial Constraint

For the restricted polarization pair, the real derivative is omega-2lambda+gamma q_1 and the imaginary derivative is omega-2lambda. Its sufficient non-growth interval includes

Because the positive source increases monotonically with gamma, its positive Schur complement also increases. The negative-block gap has already been bounded at the largest gamma=1. Consequently the strange certificate proves growth throughout [1/2,1], and the up certificate proves it throughout [3/4,1], including the points between the samples.

These are sufficient growth intervals, not exact transition values. The index proof gives existence, with 0<sigma<=omega, rather than a precise multiplicity, lifetime or outgoing decay channel.

4. What This Constrains

The spatial test supplies information that the fitted frequencies and isolated pair do not supply. It identifies an allowed constitutive degree of freedom and places a mathematical constraint on it under the stated spatial preparation.

The zero-curvature envelope endpoint retains its separate nonlinear persistence theorem. The analytic zero-curvature endpoint in (3) has the same complete linearized positive-wave dynamics, but lacks the open affine chart needed to transfer that nonlinear proof. Neither endpoint selects occupied depths or derives physical support, common normalization or complete translational inertia.

All six occupied depths enter the unchanged-clock and pair comparison. Only the displayed depth-three and depth-four full-matrix fields receive full spatial certificates here. The result gives no special depth-five preference and is not a result for minimum-column carriers.
