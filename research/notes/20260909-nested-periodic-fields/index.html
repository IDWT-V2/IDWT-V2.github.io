Two-frequency fields in a readjusted nested pair

Updated September 9, 2026

Summary: Both deformed nested Dirac profiles admit exact opposite-frequency components with independent polarizations, and the containing field can keep the lower field confined at finite frequency when its own mean scalar source vanishes.

Use the source of both occupied fields

The readjusted nested pair describes two projected channels of the one field on Qp⊂QDQ_p\subset Q_DQp​⊂QD​, with D=p+qD=p+qD=p+q. The higher profile deforms because it shares ppp ordinary macroscopic spatial directions with its lower partner. Those shared directions are equivalent in physical kind to its other qqq directions; their grouping is relative to this pair.

The two-frequency construction can be extended to both of these deformed profiles. Its spatial algebra is stronger than a radial-density approximation, because it retains all four components of the anisotropic higher field.
