Closed-Circle Count Fields with Spatial Concentration and Persistence

Updated October 12, 2026

One elementary spatial construction now realizes several of the conditional count model's properties together. It has an exact positive Dirac shell, the prescribed count clock and a calculable concentration of classical energy around a closed circle. Suitable orientations retain the full observer half-spin transformation. Adding a small nodeless mode permits the existing preferred-time nonlinear persistence theorem to apply.

This is a construction on the assumed round spatial sector sphere. It does not establish a mobile localized particle in the containing unbounded space, an observed total spin or a derived physical interaction. The count constitutive action and sector scales remain premises. A literal electron trajectory remains distinct from this internal classical field.

The full sphere Dirac operator commutes with K because it contains Clifford products of even degree. Thus any real positive-shell section is pointwise balanced. 7. Reproducibility and the Remaining Physical Question

The fully varied positive wave action retains the nodeless fields, their count frequencies and conditional local nonlinear persistence, with nonnegative complete energy. Its phase-action normalization differs from the first-order action, and its interaction must be recalculated.

The same-action contact calculation now derives reciprocal orientation energy and a global alignment theorem for distinct branches in orthogonal columns. Strong aligned electron/muon overlap has negative complete energy in that diagnostic action; this is an encounter constraint, rather than a change to the isolated-state persistence proof.

balanced-count-beams.py and its report pass 2,114 evaluations across all fourteen maintained assignments. Exact Gaussian-integer projector products establish monogenicity. Actual spatial jets verify the full Dirac equation, complete count source, pointwise energy and full observer rotation. The spatial shell residual is below 4.8e-16 relatively; the source check uses its large coefficient as scale. Independent beta integrals check concentration and tube widths. The count clock's independent high-precision derivation remains the one in the forward action note.

balanced-beam-persistence.py and its report pass 2,420 evaluations. Exact real-structure identities accompany the direct-sum and coherent spatial checks. A pilot symbolic comparison used floating entries against integer matrices; conversion of the exactly Gaussian-integer Clifford entries fixes that representation error. The generic nodeless theorem uses the dimension argument above, while the persistence theorem uses the stated analytic domain assumptions.

This is a concrete common realization of count frequency, closed-circle energy concentration, appropriate observer transformation and conditional nonlinear persistence. The same law admits other depths and shapes; it does not independently favor the assigned depths. A physical account still needs the effective count action's origin, motion and stress in the containing manifold, and an actual reciprocal interaction. Those questions remain distinct from the verified existence of these fields.
