50. Conditional relative-separation theorem under two individual beam locks

Assume a common beam-adapted split

V6=O⊕H≅R3⊕R3 V_6=O\oplus H \cong\mathbb R^3\oplus\mathbb R^3 V6​=O⊕H≅R3⊕R3

and suppose each electron individually satisfies the beam-relative lock

hi=QiTui,Qi∈SO(3). h_i=Q_i^Tu_i, \qquad Q_i\in SO(3). hi​=QiT​ui​,Qi​∈SO(3).

In the center-of-mass frame write

u1=+12u,u2=−12u,u_1=+\frac12u, \qquad u_2=-\frac12u, u1​=+21​u,u2​=−21​u,

so the visible relative displacement is u=u1−u2u=u_1-u_2u=u1​−u2​. The hidden relative displacement is

h=h1−h2=12(Q1T+Q2T)u. h=h_1-h_2 =\frac12(Q_1^T+Q_2^T)u. h=h1​−h2​=21​(Q1T​+Q2T​)u.

Define

A=12(Q1T+Q2T). A=\frac12(Q_1^T+Q_2^T). A=21​(Q1T​+Q2T​).

Let the relative rotation Q1Q2TQ_1Q_2^TQ1​Q2T​ have principal angle ϕ\phiϕ. An exact calculation gives the singular values of AAA:

σ(A)={1,cos⁡ϕ2,cos⁡ϕ2}. \boxed{ \sigma(A) =\left\{1,\cos\frac{\phi}{2},\cos\frac{\phi}{2}\right\}. } σ(A)={1,cos2ϕ​,cos2ϕ​}.​

Using the beam-adapted identity

ν=cos⁡2ϕ2, \nu=\cos^2\frac{\phi}{2}, ν=cos22ϕ​,

this becomes

σ(A)={1,ν,ν}. \boxed{ \sigma(A)=\{1,\sqrt\nu,\sqrt\nu\}. } σ(A)={1,ν​,ν​}.​

Therefore, decomposing uuu into the relative-rotation axis and its perpendicular plane,

u=u∥+u⊥,u=u_{\parallel}+u_{\perp}, u=u∥​+u⊥​,

one gets the exact hidden-relative norm

∣h∣2=∣u∥∣2+ν∣u⊥∣2. \boxed{ |h|^2 =|u_{\parallel}|^2 +\nu|u_{\perp}|^2. } ∣h∣2=∣u∥​∣2+ν∣u⊥​∣2.​

Consequently

ν∣u∣2≤∣h∣2≤∣u∣2. \boxed{ \nu|u|^2\le |h|^2\le |u|^2. } ν∣u∣2≤∣h∣2≤∣u∣2.​

For the complete six-dimensional relative displacement

ξ=(u,h), \xi=(u,h), ξ=(u,h),

one therefore has

(1+ν)∣u∣2≤∣ξ∣2≤2∣u∣2. \boxed{ (1+\nu)|u|^2 \le |\xi|^2 \le 2|u|^2. } (1+ν)∣u∣2≤∣ξ∣2≤2∣u∣2.​

50.1 Coincidence limit

As the electron-sector points approach one another,

ν→1, \nu\to1, ν→1,

and hence

Q1Q2T→I. Q_1Q_2^T\to I. Q1​Q2T​→I.

Then

A→Q1T=Q2T, A\to Q_1^T=Q_2^T, A→Q1T​=Q2T​,

so

∣h∣=∣u∣ \boxed{|h|=|u|} ∣h∣=∣u∣​

and

∣ξ∣2=2∣u∣2. \boxed{|\xi|^2=2|u|^2.} ∣ξ∣2=2∣u∣2.​

Thus, conditional on the individual beam locks, projective pair approach automatically removes the independent hidden-impact freedom in the coincidence limit.

This is substantially stronger than the earlier independent-hidden benchmark. The cubic hidden small-ball suppression applies only when the individual locks are absent or ineffective.

50.2 Status

The result is exact given

hi=QiTui. h_i=Q_i^Tu_i. hi​=QiT​ui​.

What remains unproved is the dynamical origin of those individual locks for free electrons in a beam. The pair geometry fixes the mismatch Q1Q2TQ_1Q_2^TQ1​Q2T​ through ν\nuν, but current V2 still needs a scattering-state mechanism that makes each residual

ri=hi−QiTui r_i=h_i-Q_i^Tu_i ri​=hi​−QiT​ui​

a restoring mode.
