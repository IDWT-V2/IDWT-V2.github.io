An Amplitude-Homogeneous Finite-Range Count Action

Updated October 13, 2026

The common flat-space count action has exact count-clock families and a bounded-localization theorem, but its global moments couple arbitrarily distant patterns. A different action replaces those global moments by smoothly windowed moments and averages over window anchors. With an explicit common cap, the resulting continuum equation is globally well posed, exactly independent of overall field amplitude, and additive for sufficiently separated compact supports. Its complete initial source can approximate a specified finite set of parent count-clock shells arbitrarily accurately.

These properties coexist in one changed action. The parent stationary solutions and localization theorem are not inherited. In particular, the initial approximation proved below supplies neither an exact new stationary branch nor a particle lifetime.

The coordinates are ordinary physical coordinates in shared flat R10\mathbb R^{10}R10, used as the working geometry. Depth classifies a pattern's dimensional involvement and extracted widths. It does not name a separate physical space. The field below is a classical complex field, equivalently two real quadratures of one field. Spatial integrals and spectral coefficients supply mathematical descriptions, not physical motion in a Hilbert space, a Born law, or mediator particles. An internal field profile remains distinct from an electron's deterministic six-dimensional trajectory and an observer's time-averaged registration.
