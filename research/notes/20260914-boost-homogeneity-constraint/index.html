Research / Mass and normalization

A constraint on smooth homogeneous spinor interactions

Updated September 14, 2026

mathematical result

The retained frame in the regular current model is not merely an awkward way to write a spinor-only invariant. Within a precise class of local potentials, removing it while preserving both global regularity and the existing homogeneity removes the nonlinear interaction itself.

This is a mathematical constraint on that class of actions. It does not forbid nonlinear spinor theories, a retained degree of the same field, or an effective action restricted to a regular current domain. It also does not identify the sign of a potential with the inertia of a particle.

A nonnegative potential makes the restriction stronger

If HHH anticommutes with AAA, its eigenvalues occur in pairs: Hv=λvH v=\lambda vHv=λv implies H(Av)=−λAvH(Av)=-\lambda AvH(Av)=−λAv. Consequently

U(Ψ)≥0 for every Ψ⟹H=0.(7) U(\Psi)\ge0\text{ for every }\Psi \quad\Longrightarrow\quad H=0. \tag{7} U(Ψ)≥0 for every Ψ⟹H=0.(7)

No nonzero nonnegative potential satisfies all the hypotheses. A signed quadratic scalar potential is allowed, and can support positive-frequency particle branches. Equation (7) is not a statement that particle inertia can be negative. The complete branch energy and momentum determine that separate physical response.

For the doubled spinor representations used in the current calculations, the full Hermitian spinor-matrix space anticommuting with all physical AaA_aAa​ has real dimension four at even depth and eight at the tested odd depths. Partner matrices supply additional constant quadratic coefficients. These counts are representation algebra, not spatial dimensions or a particle selection rule. If independent grade phases are also symmetries, the quadratic matrix cannot mix different grades.

The positive density normalization instead uses ρu=u⋅j\rho_u=u\cdot jρu​=u⋅j in a retained future timelike frame. The simultaneous transformation is

Holding uuu fixed while boosting only Ψ\PsiΨ is a different transformation and is not a symmetry of that regularized potential. It therefore lies outside (2). The frame dependence is explicit and mathematically consequential; assigning the frame its own response is still required by that candidate action.

What the constraint leaves for the mass derivation

The homogeneous local action gives the useful stationary identity E=ωQE=\omega QE=ωQ. The classification shows why extending a nonlinear version smoothly across every spinor current while retaining ordinary boost covariance requires additional structure or a changed hypothesis. An effective current law on a regular domain remains possible. So do higher-degree microscopic interactions, derivative interactions, and retained modes with their own transformation law. Their energy, kinetic normalization and source must be included when deriving the particle frequency and inertia.

This is a restriction on proposed reconstructions, not a proof selecting one of those extensions. In particular, the simplex shell weights and their physical spatial carrier are still to be derived. A model with a frame must account for that frame within the same field; the theorem does not introduce another particle or an exchanged interaction.
