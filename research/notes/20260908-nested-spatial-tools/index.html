Nested spatial tools

Updated September 8, 2026

Tools for common time, centred self-binding, cylindrical barriers, and inherited evolution on nested spatial sectors.

All physical coordinates belong to one nested chain of ordinary macroscopic spatial submanifolds. The scalar models and coefficient spaces here are mathematical tools whose physical application requires a specified operator.

  • Shared time coordinate — states the common temporal premise and distinguishes coordinate time from clock readings.
  • Centroid relative self binding — derives a centred scalar functional with zero net self-force.
  • Cylindrical barrier separation — reduces a separated crossing problem at fixed longitudinal energy.
  • Nested sector dynamics — gives the lift and intertwining conditions for exact inherited evolution.
  • Simplex reciprocal sum — provides an exact inverse-count sum and truncation error.
