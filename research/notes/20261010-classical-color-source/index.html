Classical Color-Current Cancellation and Complete Spatial Dynamics

Updated October 10, 2026

The zero-dielectric Gauss constraint requires the actual classical density and spatial currents to vanish. A fibre singlet is sufficient, but is not necessary. The distinction matters when interpreting inactive sectors: a zero source does not, by itself, prove that an occupied field transforms in the trivial representation.

This calculation uses the actual rank-32 Clifford carrier of the selected ten-parent, with its full normal SU3 at depths two through four, residual SU2 at five/six, and no independent normal algebra at ten. All dimensions remain ordinary spatial dimensions. Exterior factors describe field components. No particle identity, localized solution, mass-frequency or physical occupation rule is inferred from these free-mode controls.

The Current of a Positive Spatial Mode

This is a principal-symbol result. It does not guarantee that a superposition of different source-free modes is source-free, because the currents are quadratic.

For an arbitrary spinor function χ(x,t)\chi(x,t)χ(x,t), including interference between different positive-energy momenta, VχV\chiVχ has zero local color currents. A Fixed Pure Triplet Has an Exact Positive-Mode Source Floor

∑AqA2=12(tr⁡ρc2−13)≥112. \sum_Aq_A^2=\frac12\left(\operatorname{tr}\rho_c^2-\frac13\right) \ge\frac1{12}. A∑​qA2​=21​(trρc2​−31​)≥121​.

The lower bound is attained at eigenvalues (1/2,1/2,0)(1/2,1/2,0)(1/2,1/2,0). A pure positive-mode triplet alone cannot satisfy the exact zero-source constraint at depth two. The direct-sum construction above avoids that obstruction by using the complementary exterior representation. At active three/four no zero-source constraint is imposed; their ordinary triplet sources remain allowed.

Residual SU2 at Five and Six

The residual fundamental has four positive spatial spin multiplicities. Choose orthonormal vectors u1,u2u_1,u_2u1​,u2​ in the simultaneous positive spatial-symbol and Tz=1/2T_z=1/2Tz​=1/2 subspace. With T−=Tx−iTyT_-=T_x-iT_yT−​=Tx​−iTy​, define

qA=jiA=0,⟨C2⟩=34,∥ψ+∥2=1. q_A=j_{iA}=0,\qquad \langle C_2\rangle=\frac34,\qquad \|\psi_+\|^2=1. qA​=jiA​=0,⟨C2​⟩=43​,∥ψ+​∥2=1.

A common independent complex amplitude retains a positive kinetic norm. The construction works for arbitrary nonzero momentum directions at both physical depths. It proves existence of source-free positive modes in a nonsinglet carrier, rather than a complete finite-energy localized branch.

Two independently chosen such modes generally acquire nonzero cross-current terms when coherently superposed. The executable checks exhibit this directly. A one-direction transport envelope can retain the cancellation, but gives no localization in the transverse directions. A full spatial wave packet needs further conditions.

There is also a precise fixed-subspace obstruction. This does not exclude a nonlinear source-free solution set, a momentum-dependent constrained field, or the actual residual fibre-singlet branches already proved to preserve all spatial directions.

Antiunitary Pairing Repeats the Symplectic Obstruction

Conjugating the full spatial wave reverses momentum and temporal phase. The positive bare energy of one wave and the negative bare energy of its constrained conjugate cancel. This reality relation therefore does not supply nondegenerate positive first-order particle dynamics. The independent common-amplitude positive mode above has different constraints and avoids that particular cancellation; it does not solve the packet problem.
