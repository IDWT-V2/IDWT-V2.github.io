Updated September 8, 2026

Summary: Restricting a magnetic two-form overlap to the photon's actual two-plane gives a rank-two projector and an explicit polarization-dependent coupling diagnostic.

Spatial setting and conditional interaction

All directions below are ordinary macroscopic spatial directions in one nested physical manifold. Work in a flat tangent chart of a containing sector, with real Euclidean space VVV of dimension d≥3d\geq3d≥3. The photon has an oriented physical two-plane E⊂VE\subset VE⊂V and unit phase direction k∈E⊥k\in E^\perpk∈E⊥. Its complex polarization belongs to E⊗CE\otimes\mathbb CE⊗C. This is the geometry in photon transversality.

The calculation adopts the spatial two-form pairing of the dimensional magnetic kernel. It is one specified interaction term of the single field Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​. It does not introduce mediator particles or establish that this term is the whole electromagnetic response. Electric, orbital, and additional spatial response terms must be retained when the action supplies them.
