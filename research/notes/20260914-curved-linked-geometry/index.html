Research / Mass and normalization

Curvature forces on the linked mass-carrier geometry

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The actual spatial curvature supplies a positive restoring coefficient for the carrier's constant-polynomial deformation at depths 4, 5, 6 and 10. That contribution vanishes at depth 3. The ratio of its leading coefficient to the deformation metric is an exact polynomial in depth, with no independent numerical integral.

The same calculation also finds a force on a different centered shape coordinate. Consequently this restoring coefficient does not make the round reference a stationary occupied particle. A common-radius pressure balance must be accompanied by a balance of the internal shape forces.

These statements concern the declared geometric coefficient functional. Its physical energy and time normalization, its identification with sustaining modes of the one field, and the complete curved wave source remain open. Mass is the particle's selected frequency in equivalent units.

The full first variation and complementary fields

Thus the order-R−1R^{-1}R−1 source is purely a translation in this specified metric. If translation is removed by the corresponding orthogonal projection, there is no order-R−1R^{-1}R−1 force on complementary fields. The usual second-order energy lowering from relaxing such a forced complement therefore vanishes in this particular projected problem.

That projection does not derive physical pressure. A different imposed center constraint, or the occupied wave's actual support stress, can act on the complementary fields differently. The full physical source still needs its own equation. At order R−2R^{-2}R−2, the nonzero quadrupole force already lies within the geometric zero-mode space and remains explicit.

The existing occupied wave also has a nonzero constant-polynomial force, as computed in the reciprocal source calculation. A positive coefficient in (6), by itself, does not cancel that force at α=0\alpha=0α=0. Both the changed equilibrium and the other shape directions must be included before claiming a stationary mass carrier.

Independent spatial verification

nonlinear_moduli_plane.py solves the nonlinear planar equation for both signs of the constant and quadrupole coefficients. Fourier collocation retains their complete angular dependence. The tube weight is folded over the exact symmetry sectors, preserving every angular component that can contribute.

The geometric energy is evaluated directly from covariant spatial kinetic, curvature and potential terms. An independent integral using (3) agrees and improves under radial refinement. At depth ten and R=40R=40R=40, the finite quadrupole derivative is approximately −0.111678-0.111678−0.111678, including the higher inverse-radius terms; at depth three it is zero.

The 357-check report includes every polynomial zero mode at the five depths, arbitrary scalar and connection variations in (10), the translation identity, and nonlinear solutions on 1,201- and 2,401-point radial grids. Exact source hashes and separately retained spatial arrays make the calculation reproducible. It verifies these conditional geometric statements, not the missing physical normalization or a completed nonlinear particle solution.
