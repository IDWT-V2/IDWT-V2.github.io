Selected spinor line current

Updated September 10, 2026

Physical premise (IDWT): There is one infinite-dimensional physical manifold. All sectors form one nested chain of submanifolds within it. Particle depth counts ordinary, literal macroscopic spatial dimensions. “Observed” and “unobserved” describe an observer's access to those dimensions, not different kinds of physical dimension. See spatial meaning and mathematical scope.

Summary: A specified twist embeds the orbit sections in a global spinor line, but their motion requires a gauged orbit-driving term beyond the free Dirac operator.

A weaker bridge that exists

The earlier obstruction excludes identifying the entire spatial half-spin bundle with a constant C4 bundle. It does not exclude a selected spinor line. On CP3, K = O(−4), and the spinor bundle is

Σ=Λ0,∗⊗O(−2). \Sigma=\Lambda^{0,*}\otimes\mathcal O(-2). Σ=Λ0,∗⊗O(−2).

Choose the additional twisting line E_L = O(L+2), with its compatible Chern connection. Then

Σ⊗EL=Λ0,∗⊗O(L). \Sigma\otimes E_L=\Lambda^{0,*}\otimes\mathcal O(L). Σ⊗EL​=Λ0,∗⊗O(L).

Its degree-zero exterior component is a globally defined O(L) subbundle. Thus Φ(t) = Σ_a c_a(t)s_a, with holomorphic sections s_a of O(L), defines a twisted spatial spinor ψ(t) in this component. It uses precisely the time-dependent coefficients of the demo, not a stationary orbital in their place. Sections may have zeros; no nowhere-vanishing trivialization or pointwise full-rank C4 identification is asserted.

This is a chosen global lift, not a master-derived one. In particular, the spin twist is O(L+2), whereas the section degree is L. If the entire twist is identified as electromagnetic, that distinction must be reconciled with the flux-sector module; silently calling both integers L would be incorrect.

The free Dirac term does not generate these orbits

For this Kähler/Chern construction the twisted Dirac operator is √2(∂̄_L+∂̄_L†). The bundle and operator correspondence is given in Jakobson, Strohmaier and Zelditch, section 6, equation 42. A holomorphic degree-zero form is killed by both terms: ∂̄Φ = 0 and ∂̄† lowers degree from zero. Hence every such ψ lies in the spatial Dirac kernel. With only a constant mass term in the product-time Dirac Hamiltonian, this component acquires a common phase, not the orbit's relative phases.

Moreover, every spatial velocity Clifford matrix exchanges even and odd exterior degree. On the pure degree-zero component,

ψ†αaψ=0. \psi^\dagger\alpha^a\psi=0. ψ†αaψ=0.

Prescribing changing orbit coefficients while keeping only this zero spatial current would violate continuity whenever |Φ(t,u)|² changes. Thus neither the free Dirac equation nor a zero-current argument proves a stable, moving, nonradiating electron orbit. A nonconstant nuclear term may mix these modes or take them out of the holomorphic kernel and needs a new calculation.

Gauging the orbit drive supplies transport current

Let K be the real Killing field of Z → diag(e^{iθ},e^{-iθ},e^{-iθ},1)Z. Its divergence in the FS measure vanishes. The unitary lift has generator Ĝ with principal differential term −i∇_K; its vertical connection term makes it a well-defined operator on O(L). Fix the lift so monomial weights are (n₁−n₂−n₃). A common scalar shift has no effect here.

Conditionally add the energy term ℏνĜ to the Hamiltonian. It preserves holomorphic sections and produces the demo's weights {L,−L,−1,0}; the even/odd change of basis gives its Lσ_x exchange block. This term is imposed for the diagnostic, not derived as a nuclear interaction or explained away as a rotating coordinate frame. A physical interpretation must settle that distinction before radiation is assessed in an inertial observer frame.

The unitary flow transports the density n = |Φ|² along K:

∂tn+νK(n)=0,div⁡FSK=0. \partial_t n+\nu K(n)=0,\qquad \operatorname{div}_{\rm FS}K=0. ∂t​n+νK(n)=0,divFS​K=0.

Consequently its transport current is J_K = qνnK. The same contribution follows by minimally gauging the first-order derivative in Ĝ when the charge convention is chosen consistently with the Hamiltonian action. The free Dirac velocity current is zero on this selected line, but the added orbit-driving term's current is not. This yields a conserved sector source for the conditional action; it is not permissible to use the new dynamics with only the old current.

For a fixed physical observer map X: CP3 → R³, its conditional dipole is d_i = q∫X_i n dμ and its current moment is qν∫K(X_i)n dμ. Integration by parts gives ḋ_i = qν∫K(X_i)n dμ, consistently with the projected commutator formula. The electromagnetic observer map remains to be selected physically.

Ring limitation and next test

At L = 0, H⁰(O(0)) is one-dimensional and the projective action is trivial. It does not reproduce the demo's separate three-state ring sector. This lift therefore addresses the two-, four-, and six-petal families, not an unnoticed replacement of the ring construction. A universal model still needs to connect that separate sector to the same physical field and charge.

A later embedding realizes the exact three-state ring generator within H⁰(O(k)) for every k ≥ 2 under the same Killing action. It does not turn H⁰(O(0)) into a three-state space or identify the ring's display label with that positive Chern degree. The algebraic bridge and its selection caveats are recorded in the ring note below.

A second later calculation obtains the Killing generator by compressing a nonconstant scalar potential. Such a potential necessarily couples out of the holomorphic sector. That is a candidate effective origin for the drive, not an exact unmodified vacuum-line solution; its current and frequency corrections must be derived with the omitted modes.

The next note evaluates a simple geometric observer-symbol class exactly. This gives a concrete conditional source and probe calculation while leaving the choice of X, twist, ν, and physical origin of Ĝ visibly open.

A separate stationary test now restores the complete radial Dirac complement of a coherent section. Its normalized spatial branch contains the opposite-chirality correction required by scalar self-contact. That branch is a conditional field-equation benchmark, not the evolving orbit or a derivation of the Killing drive.
