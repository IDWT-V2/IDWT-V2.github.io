Neutrino channel control

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: A neutrino coupling test requires vanishing electric charge, a colour singlet, and nonzero weak vertices, with low-energy suppression distinguished from a zero interaction.

Physical target

The assigned neutrino sector has five ordinary spatial dimensions within the same nested physical manifold as the other sectors. Its rare interactions in ordinary matter provide an important control on a generalized coupling rule. IDWT uses direct dynamics of Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​ and has no mediator particles. That rule must retain the weak interactions through which active neutrinos are detected. The discovery of neutrino neutral-current scattering is a direct example: CERN's account of weak neutral currents.

The target is channel-dependent: zero ordinary electric charge and no colour charge, with allowed weak interactions. Rare scattering is not equivalent to a vanishing weak vertex, and a small mass does not itself specify an interaction strength. The following matrix example is a benchmark for the projected rule, not a derivation of the physical electroweak representation from spatial depth.

Spatial source barriers and the low-frequency proposal

The full periodic polarization calculation now tests actual spatial source operators rather than a depth-only weight. For a specified lower traceless pattern, every finite spectral residue of one scalar rest sideband cancels. Its amplitude opens linearly in normal momentum, and its source-conditioned Gram opens quadratically. Both occupied polarizations, the incoming spatial pattern and common physical rotations are retained. This supplies a concrete geometric momentum barrier in the specified source channel.

The same calculations also show why that result is not yet the neutrino rule. The complete set of scalar inputs has no cancelling second polarization in the tested deformed five-dimensional preparation, while a particular incoming pattern can have a cancelling subspace. Other current readouts and source feedback need not share that zero. The actual photon source must determine which of these spatial operators it samples.

The readjusted periodic pair further shows that reducing a lower object's mean scalar balance does not necessarily make its clock arbitrarily slow: the containing field can maintain its confinement and a finite limiting frequency. The exactly zero signed normalization has an undefined scalar center. Separately, slow collective shape poles retain a canonical spectral sign that prevents identifying their small residues alone with stable weak absorption.

The neutrino simplex spectrum constrains its three assigned mass levels and a common normalization. It does not yet supply the occupied spatial wavefunctions or the photon current needed by these source tests. The scalar diagnostic profiles must therefore remain distinct from those physically assigned neutrino modes until that mapping is derived. The target remains a neutral electromagnetic channel with surviving, normalized weak response from the same field dynamics.

Application to the spatial hierarchy

Complete local scalar response adds a pointwise constraint: a small or zero background scalar does not suppress every source-response direction. Its full derivative and its projected vertex have different nullspaces, and the actual kinetics must preserve any proposed cancellation. This does not identify the ordinary electric generator or the surviving weak channel.

The kinetic scalar-null classes give exact source-free spatial preparations in the minimal module at every odd depth. This includes five but does not distinguish it from three, seven or nine. Their kinetic involution is not a conserved electric generator for arbitrary scalar-coupled fields, and the scalar term supplies no self-binding inside a single class.

For each derived neutrino preparation the calculation should determine:

  1. Whether the electric generator annihilates the retained subspace.
  2. Whether the colour representation is a singlet.
  3. Which normalized charged and neutral weak vertices survive.
  4. What the derived field evolution and kinematics imply for response rates at the energy being compared.

None of these results follows from d=5d=5d=5 by itself. In the scalar common-marginal example, depth five couples exactly as other containing depths do. That is the appropriate geometric control: any physical distinction must be traced to a derived channel or dynamical structure. The existing neutrino mass spectrum fixes neither the generator nor the weak response map.
