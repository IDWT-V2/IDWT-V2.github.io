Research / Mass and normalization

The spatial complement and positive kinetic normalization

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The small mass-frequency branch survives the leading response of the omitted normal modes. Their contribution includes a positive correction to kinetic normalization. It must be retained together with their change to the frequency operator.

This calculation uses the maintained full spatial parent. It supplies a specific improvement over a finite-band assumption, while leaving the infinite-complement remainder and the occupied supporting geometry open. The bounded angular completion then uses the same spatial structure to remove the leading mode-dependent departure from the simplex factors.

Eliminate the spatial modes before squaring

Let PPP be the kernel of the linked geometric operator DDD, including its two canonical blocks, and Q=I−PQ=I-PQ=I−P. At a fixed surface parameter β\betaβ, write the maintained first-order parent as

The spatial inverse is explicit

The polarization part uses the exact identity

D(RΦl)=(K−kl)Φl,R=−iSα⋅x,kl=d−1−2l.(7) D(\mathcal R\Phi_l)=(K-k_l)\Phi_l, \qquad \mathcal R=-iS\alpha\cdot x,\quad k_l=d-1-2l. \tag{7} D(RΦl​)=(K−kl​)Φl​,R=−iSα⋅x,kl​=d−1−2l.(7)

The hopping part is obtained from the positive-chiral radial Green equations of D2D^2D2, retaining both radial components, their connection terms and the subtraction of the retained projection. This is an actual spatial inverse, rather than a scalar denominator assigned to an omitted mode.

The radial fields determine every entry of NNN and AAA. Angular selection then restricts the off-diagonal block of (5): its row-minus-column shift lies between −1-1−1 and 222. If an entry contains γp\gamma^pγp and has shift sss, it obeys p−s≤2p-s\le2p−s≤2.

What the larger spatial comparison establishes

A finite spatial Galerkin space containing PPP, the range of FFF, and the range of YYY reproduces the leading coefficient independently. It retains 6d6d6d complex basis vectors. The normal part has a positive gap, the complete matrix has the required chiral symmetry, and halving ggg gives the expected fourth-order frequency remainder after (8).

The basis is deliberately sufficient to test this order. It is not an exhaustive discretization of the infinite normal plane. Independent radial refinement checks the spatial integrals used in the small eigenvalue; high-precision diagonalization alone would not establish that accuracy.

The result supports a positive, properly normalized reduced wave action under the stated parent. It does not supply the overall physical phase normalization, the same-field source balance, or the total inertia of a localized occupied particle. Those require the supporting action as well.
