Updated October 13, 2026

The construction also exposes an essential domain issue: smooth positive zero-penalty profiles are Gaussians, but positive weak profiles can change width across an interface at zero penalty. Neither the Gaussian classification nor the linear spectrum establishes nonlinear persistence.

This is an inverse support-and-motion construction. Two widths, a clock, a speed and a penalty coefficient enter as constitutive inputs.
