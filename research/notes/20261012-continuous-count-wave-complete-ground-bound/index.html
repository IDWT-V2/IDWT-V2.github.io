Updated October 12, 2026

A short operator argument also excludes exponential linear growth of the complete ground-shell spatial problem under explicit sufficient conditions. The result applies to the actual minimum carriers and every spatial disturbance on the stated sphere.

This ground theorem is a control, not persistence of the assigned excited particle fields. Its constitutive law and ordinary sphere geometry remain premises. It neither establishes containing-space localization nor derives the count action from the master field.
