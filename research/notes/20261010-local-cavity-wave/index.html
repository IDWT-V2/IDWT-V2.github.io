A Local Cavity Wave With a Fundamental Current and Uniform Energy

Updated October 10, 2026

The modal cavity construction identifies positive energies for phase locking and reciprocal beat transfer. A local wave prototype gives a more physical realization of its carrier-independent source. It also separates circulating field motion from a circulating energy lump. This is a construction on a prescribed closed path, not a self-confined particle solution or a derivation of the simplex index.
