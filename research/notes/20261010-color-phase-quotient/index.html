A Dynamical Color-Neutral Pair Phase and Its Central Character

Updated October 10, 2026

Conjugate representation data need not lose their entire canonical dynamics when color currents cancel. The degeneracy of a fixed reality relation is repaired by retaining an independent gauge-invariant pair phase. The resulting reduction is explicit, but its electric interpretation depends on the actual central character. The spin lift cannot be replaced silently by an exterior tensor action.

This extends the classical current comparison. It concerns ordinary commuting field amplitudes in the stated normal carrier, not anticommuting variables, an identified lepton, charge quantization or a derived mass law.

The Pair Clock Is Not Automatically the Particle Clock

For a common positive linear mode clock,

H=ω(∥z∥2+∥w∥2)=2ωR, H=\omega(\|z\|^2+\|w\|^2)=2\omega R, H=ω(∥z∥2+∥w∥2)=2ωR,

the original components vary as e−iωte^{-i\omega t}e−iωt while BBB varies as e−2iωte^{-2i\omega t}e−2iωt. The canonical equations give the same doubled pair phase frequency. Interpreting the bilinear as the observed particle therefore changes which oscillation supplies its mass-frequency. The maintained particle frequency cannot be transferred unchanged merely by forming this invariant. A physical reduction must identify the observed clock and preserve the declared frequency law.

Full Spatial Currents Across the Occupied Sectors

At depths two through four, color acts on the normal factor and every active Clifford matrix acts on the base factor. The common complex-linear paired branch therefore retains arbitrary base spinors and vanishing color currents, as already proved. The Hodge action constrains these currents only at depth two; active three/four also permit ordinary sourced triplets. This quotient does not preferentially select lepton assignments.

At five/six, the residual SU2 has two fundamental copies distinguished by the first active normal occupation. Write their amplitudes as zzz and eiϕεzˉe^{i\phi}\varepsilon\bar zeiϕεzˉ, with the SU2 alternating matrix ε\varepsilonε. Their density and all four base-direction currents cancel. A specially prepared s=0s=0s=0 spinor can evade this test, but the zero-chirality condition and its evolution need to be preserved by the complete spatial dynamics. This is consistent with the earlier canceled positive modes and their packet limitation; it is not a contradiction of the complete residual singlet branches.

At ten there is no independent normal algebra in the selected ten-parent, so this SU3 quotient is not an additional depth-ten constraint. The same Hodge gate is independently recomputed at every occupied depth: two/five/six/ten inactive, three/four active. No calculations at unoccupied depths are needed.

Reproducibility and Use

The checker and report preserve 453 phase, metric, character and full-current checks across 14 cases, plus 192 exact geometry dependency checks. They retain gauge horizontality, a nonzero symplectic form, the actual spin shift, determinant branches, the bilinear frequency and both residual added-direction current norms.

The useful mathematical construction is a nondegenerate gauge-neutral phase derived from paired amplitudes, with an explicit geometric character and metric. A physical charged-lepton mechanism still requires the common electric character, complete occupied spatial dynamics and the correct particle clock. Those are identifiable conditions to derive rather than a reason to discard the dynamical-phase possibility.
