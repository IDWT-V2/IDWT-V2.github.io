Research / Mass and normalization

A stationary collective source for the spatial mass operator

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The bounded normal carrier supplies an actual spatial operator and a nonzero force on its proposed geometry. A collective completion can now make that geometry stationary without shifting the operator's frequencies. Its full finite-dimensional linear response also satisfies a common stability criterion at every maintained particle index, including depths three and four.

The completion is an explicit inverse choice. It retains two geometric mode coordinates of the same proposed field and includes their energy and canonical response. It does not derive their stiffness or inertia from the master field, prove stability in the full spatial complement, or make the carrier's finite-hopping spectrum exactly proportional to the simplex count. Mass remains the selected particle frequency in equivalent units.
