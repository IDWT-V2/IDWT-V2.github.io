Normal Spatial Rotations Give Independent Color on a Four-Base

Updated October 9, 2026

The three-component spatial Hodge source and an independently acting color triplet are different constructions. A symmetric square of a two-component spinor rotates as spin one; its three components do not by themselves give a color action commuting with spatial spin. The ordinary six-normal geometry supplies a separate candidate that does commute with spin and the entire lower-sector Dirac symbol.

In one selected ten-dimensional spatial parent, this candidate preserves every tangent Clifford direction at physical depths two, three and four. The same fixed triplet projection fails at five, six and ten. Three and four are therefore compatible with an independent normal color factor, but depth two is also compatible. This is a concrete partial geometric connection, with the missing depth-two occupation or coupling rule retained explicitly.

The ten-dimensional parent is a finite sector inside the infinite containing manifold. No argument below excludes other normal directions in that larger manifold. The pure-pencil and normal-frame premises supply the selected four-base and six-complement used for this comparison; a different parent or a different carrier is a changed hypothesis.

This is a geometric realization with a prescribed connection and metric. A field action must still determine that connection, its fluctuations, its coupling coefficient and the spatial particle states. It is not a derivation of the strong-force law or of a mediator particle.

6. Charge Bookkeeping Must Also Respect Observer Spin

The ambient pure-pair character gives the familiar YYY and Q=Y+T3Q=Y+T_3Q=Y+T3​ representation weights. In this theory its two-plane is ordinary spatial geometry. These exact checks explain why the familiar ambient charge table is useful algebra without being a direct physical electric-charge derivation in an ordinary spatial Spin(10) interpretation.

This local auxiliary realization therefore does not by itself complete the four-, six- or ten-dimensional charged kinetic field, its determinant-line twist, or its global domain.

A total spatial symmetry can include an orbital Lie derivative, and the earlier normal-circle construction explicitly does. Failure of a pointwise fibre commutant is not a theorem excluding such a total geometric charge generator. The test identifies the extra structure that must be retained rather than silently treating a representation weight as physical charge. Neither charge signs nor conjugate representations settle the open antiparticle interpretation.

A Common Hodge Current Has Now Been Constructed

The adjoint middle-form current joins the independent normal color matrices to actual Hermitian spatial Clifford blades. Hodge contact acts on the form index, while color acts on a separate normal index. Its positive quartic source retains independent color and spin at three/four and vanishes at the other maintained depths. Seven/eight remains active but lacks the fixed normal color kinetic symmetry. Exact Fierz contraction gives the standard color pair operator with scalar and spin-dependent terms retained. This common action does not erase a separately prescribed normal kinetic connection or derive strong-force attraction.

Real Curvature Characters and the Spatial Pair Response

The normal-curvature comparison proves exact cancellation of odd real trace characters between conjugate complex normal carriers. A maximal-degree projected response has the occupied three/four activation rule, but that degree choice remains a premise and complex-structure-weighted alternatives survive. Neither a vanishing curvature term nor the geometric connection alone removes its kinetic source vertex.
