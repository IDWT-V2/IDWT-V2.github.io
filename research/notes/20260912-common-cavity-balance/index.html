Frequency scale and spatial scale

The proposed mass law supplies a frequency unit directly from the fixed particle assignments:

Different particles in one depth test whether this is a common unit. The mass-frequency conversion is already available. Identifying the unit with a cavity's spatial scale additionally requires its spatial operator. For example, if that operator gives

A common characteristic well size does not imply equal spatial extent of every occupied mode. Nor does the cavity terminology require an external wall, compactification, or particles occupying one shared location. The following inverse actions test the common-size premise; their origin in the same master field remains to be established.
