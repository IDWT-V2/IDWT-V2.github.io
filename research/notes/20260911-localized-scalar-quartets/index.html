Summary: Complete three-dimensional packet evolution retains phase-sensitive reciprocal transfer beyond a plane-wave truncation, with a continuum reflection law and independent spatial, temporal and boundary refinements.

From a resonant contraction to the full spatial equation

The resonant quartet calculation identifies a coherent channel through which two complex spatial patterns can exchange positive canonical norm. The full local scalar equation also generates momenta outside that quartet. A localized preparation further contains a continuum of directions and frequencies.

Reproduction and scope

scalar_packet_collision_checks.py constructs (5), evolves all spatial components through scalar_wave_packets.py, and records both chiral norms, full field changes, energy and momentum. For the positive-phase preparation:

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 \
python experiments/scalar_packet_collision_checks.py \
  --packet-size 80 --quartet-cosine .6 --phase .7853981633974483 \
  --step .04 --output-dir experiments/results --save-fields

Change the phase sign for the reciprocal comparison, or set --quartet-cosine 0 for the right-angle control. Without --packet-size, the diagnostic runs 101 checks, including independent full spatial ODE integration, energy variation, time convergence and the complete reflection identities (7)–(8). Reports and optional field archives have timestamped filenames.

The calculation confirms a permitted spatial mechanism of the specified scalar term beyond plane-wave truncation. Bound particle shapes, their common physical normalization, their nested-sector channel maps and their measured interaction rates remain to be derived.
