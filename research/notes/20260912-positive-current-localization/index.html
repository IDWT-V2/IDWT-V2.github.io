Positive current matrices and spatial localization

Updated September 12, 2026

Summary: A scalar Dirac identity and spatial virial exclude localized flat-space stationary fields in the positive-current cone of the convex hierarchy, retaining all spinor and coefficient components.

A full-field condition on the self-mass action

The current hierarchy supplies an isolated self-source and reciprocal interactions from one specified action. Its spatial states still need to exist. The following exact condition tests that question beyond a radial profile or one coherent coefficient ray.

The resulting spatial search condition

This is a full-field conditional nonexistence result. It retains arbitrary spatial dependence, complex phases, coefficient entanglement and spinor polarizations. It does not assume a radial shape or a coherent ray. It does require the flat trivial connection, the stated local convex interaction and admissible virial variation.

At positive frequency, a localized solution of that same action must therefore escape every fixed sign decomposition, or fail another stated hypothesis. An indefinite scalar mode current is insufficient on its own. Spatial change of the sign subspaces, including possible eigenvalue-sign crossings outside the smooth class (13), is a concrete route to examine. The full source, stationary equation and stability still have to be calculated. Removing negative spinor components before doing that search would miss the very directions that could matter.

The radial tail calculation gives a different restriction. For smooth scalar contact on a constant coherent ray, it excludes all regular radial finite-norm solutions, including sign-changing ones, without relying on the above current-positivity condition. Its root extension retains the separate virial hypotheses.

The known curved coherent fields and smooth spatial controls use different global hypotheses. Their full Dirac operator can have nonzero normalized zero fields, and flat dilation is not their geometric variation. Their conditional actual CP3 geometry is not compactification. The present result neither establishes that geometry nor invalidates its explicitly stated solutions.
