Localized polarization stationarity

Updated September 8, 2026

Summary: With the same localized Gaussian envelope and magnetic kernel, uniform five-dimensional polarization has a nonzero spatial first variation whereas six-dimensional pure polarization is stationary.

Separately frozen dimensional control

The control protocol was written after the six-dimensional texture test and before evaluating this comparison. It is not retroactively part of that earlier protocol. Its fingerprint is e0ce516ebe9a85347dffa58e35df581060e2a0bca9076e18e781f9c531f7e362.

Keep the same closed-field magnetic kernel, fixed normalized unit-variance Gaussian envelope in each ambient dimension, and positive bare gradient energy. Use the fundamental Spin(5) spinor or one Spin(6) half-spinor, each in C4\mathbb C^4C4. Do not add the scalar-exchange vector channel. The first three spatial coordinates remain the visible three-plane in both.

This tests the uniform-polarization preparation, not every possible field solution in five dimensions and not the particle-depth assignment.

Exact first-variation distinction

The identity makes the magnetic first variation vanish even for spatially varying coefficients, not only for a uniform rotation. The gradient term also has zero first variation at a uniform field. Thus the six-dimensional uniform preparation is stationary for the stated fixed-envelope model.

What the comparison establishes

This closes a particular loophole in the earlier isotropic-envelope CP3 comparison: being a minimum among uniform rays did not imply being stationary among spatially varying polarization fields. Five dimensions fail that stronger requirement in this magnetic model; six dimensions satisfy stationarity and the inherited weak-coupling texture test.

Six-dimensional stationarity is not unconditional stability: the separate frozen texture test found negative curvature in stronger-coupling cases. The kernel, envelope, leading-order approximation, omitted orbital/electric terms, and unidentified physical observable remain explicit premises. This is a conditional discriminator between specified preparations—not experimental proof that electrons have six spatial dimensions.
