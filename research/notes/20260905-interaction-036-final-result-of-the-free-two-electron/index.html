36. Final result of the free two-electron correlation test

The proposed idea was that visible approach of two depth-six electrons might dynamically correlate their otherwise unseen directions strongly enough that a visible near-collision also becomes a genuine six-dimensional encounter.

After testing every currently available V2-native channel, the result is:

Q6=CP3, Q_6=\mathbb{CP}^3, Q6​=CP3,

ν=∣Z1†Z2∣2. \nu=|Z_1^\dagger Z_2|^2. ν=∣Z1†​Z2​∣2.

The complex-structure alignment invariant collapses exactly to

CJ=4ν−13. C_J=\frac{4\nu-1}{3}. CJ​=34ν−1​.

Thus projective approach and frame alignment are not independent isotropic scalar variables.

36.3 Pre-contact scalar focusing: fails

The current order-zero scalar interaction is local and cannot steer trajectories before overlap.

The historical quadratic coordinate kernel

(ξ1⋅ξ2)2 (\xi_1\cdot\xi_2)^2 (ξ1​⋅ξ2​)2

is not a central function of relative separation and therefore is not the required capture force.

36.4 Naive six-dimensional Maxwell extrapolation: rejected as the wrong test

If one imports the ordinary flat six-dimensional Maxwell/Laplacian operator, one obtains

But this does not test IDWT itself. IDWT must derive the higher-dimensional electromagnetic operator whose projection produces the observed lower-dimensional Coulomb law. The calculation therefore rejects the naive extrapolated operator, not six-dimensional electron geometry.

A depth-two pullback
