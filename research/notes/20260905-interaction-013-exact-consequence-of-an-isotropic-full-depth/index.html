13. Exact consequence of an isotropic full-depth interaction

For

V66=v(cos⁡2θ), V_{66}=v(\cos^2\theta), V66​=v(cos2θ),

we have

dV66dθ=−sin⁡(2θ) v′(cos⁡2θ). \frac{dV_{66}}{d\theta} =-\sin(2\theta)\,v'(\cos^2\theta). dθdV66​​=−sin(2θ)v′(cos2θ).

Near coincidence,

cos⁡2θ=1−θ2+13θ4+O(θ6), \cos^2\theta =1-\theta^2+\frac13\theta^4+O(\theta^6), cos2θ=1−θ2+31​θ4+O(θ6),

so

V66(θ)=v(1)−v′(1)θ2+O(θ4). V_{66}(\theta) =v(1)-v'(1)\theta^2+O(\theta^4). V66​(θ)=v(1)−v′(1)θ2+O(θ4).

Hence coincidence is a local minimum precisely when

v′(1)<0. \boxed{v'(1)<0.} v′(1)<0.​

The local Hessian in the six-dimensional normal bundle is then isotropic:

Hess⁡NΔ6V66=−2v′(1) I6 \boxed{ \operatorname{Hess}_{N\Delta_6}V_{66} =-2v'(1)\,I_6 } HessNΔ6​​V66​=−2v′(1)I6​​

in orthonormal normal coordinates.

This is an important positive result. A genuine SU(4)SU(4)SU(4)-invariant attractive same-depth kernel does not distinguish an observer-visible three-plane from the other three local electron directions. Every relative tangent component sees the same leading restoring curvature.

13.1 Connection with the existing coherent contact kernel

For the already studied coherent overlap potential

VN(θ)=−A(cos⁡2θ)N, V_N(\theta) =-A(\cos^2\theta)^N, VN​(θ)=−A(cos2θ)N,

we have

v′(1)=−AN, v'(1)=-AN, v′(1)=−AN,

and therefore

Hess⁡VN∣Δ6=2AN I6. \operatorname{Hess}V_N|_{\Delta_6} =2AN\,I_6. HessVN​∣Δ6​​=2ANI6​.

This recovers the earlier exact result

VN′′(0)=2AN. V_N''(0)=2AN. VN′′​(0)=2AN.

Its previous failure as a universal hadronic bulk curvature does not invalidate it as a two-ray correlation kernel. It simply says the curvature depends on band degree NNN.

Ne=12, N_e=12, Ne​=12,
