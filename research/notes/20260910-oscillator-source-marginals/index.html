Covariance and verification

The isotropic oscillator band is preserved by common rotations of the physical coordinates. Rotate the containing field, lower inclusion and normal frame together in (3); a change of integration variables gives the same marginal in the rotated lower coordinates. The result does not assign a preferred physical split. Holding the inclusion fixed while rotating only one pattern describes a different preparation.
