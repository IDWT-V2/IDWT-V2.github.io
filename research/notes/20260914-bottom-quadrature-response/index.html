What the common field model still has to supply

Three conditions are required before (2) is a bottom mass mechanism. The physical kinetic projection must give the proposed canonical pair; the same spatial action must supply its two positive coefficients with consistent rotation and frame behavior; and its occupied branch must have the required mass and geometric response.

Selection is not automatic. Applying the adjacent-count rule to down and strange as well would give counts 2 and 700\sqrt{700}700​, replacing their maintained counts 1 and 20. A common action would therefore need an actual branch distinction between paired and unpaired oscillation patterns. The surface identity alone does not provide that distinction.

The quadrature diagnostic checks exact count identities, direct Hamiltonian trajectories, positive energy, unit canonical action, the circle conjugation map, and both complete phase-dependent response signs. It establishes these mathematical targets; it does not establish a spatial bottom solution or an observed coupling rate.
