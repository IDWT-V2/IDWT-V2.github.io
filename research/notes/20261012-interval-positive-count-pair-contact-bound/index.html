A Pointwise Energy Constraint on the Positive Count Interaction

Updated October 12, 2026

The positive contact costs are a general feature of the stated orthogonal- column preparation, rather than a result confined to aligned beams. At the maintained diagnostic scales, a pointwise inequality covers every density ratio and every relative spatial orientation for all maintained distinct same-sector pairs. It includes the actual smooth source through every transition band.

This excludes energy below the isolated reference within that preparation. It does not calculate a physical radial force, exclude local metastable wells or cover coherent fields and time-evolved shape changes.

4. Physical Scope

The positive action retains exact isolated count clocks and its separate persistence theorem. Its orthogonal-column prepared contact nevertheless does not supply energetic binding below the isolated reference at these scales. A positive energy function can still have gradients of either sign or local wells above its asymptotic reference. Neither repulsive force at every distance nor absence of finite-lived composites follows from (5).

Coherent fields change the local interference and are not covered by this inequality. Their existing finite quadratures retain their own limits. Actual encounters can also leave the isolated-shell preparation. The result therefore constrains this specific effective interaction rather than excluding IDWT or the proposed competing-range mechanism.

Reproducibility

The final source explicitly converts rational factors and retains separate names for scalar and list values.
