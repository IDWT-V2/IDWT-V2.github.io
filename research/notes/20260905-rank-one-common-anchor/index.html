Research / Mass and normalization

Rank-one common anchor

Updated September 8, 2026

exploratory not a module

Summary: A conditional source-cancellation mechanism fixes output ratios but not their physical identification.

NOT A PHYSICS MODULE OR COMPLETED SCALE DERIVATION. The source-cancellation calculation below is exact for a specified rank-one response. Its identification with the actual V2 projected reference-frequency dynamics remains unproved.

1. The constructive possibility

The earlier work treated a coupling matrix as though it had to act directly on wave-mode coefficients, then diagonalized it or took its square root. A different role is possible: the matrix can map scalar source currents to scalar Dirac self-energies. In that role, its rank-one image fixes ratios of output coefficients without determining the source magnitudes.

This provides a concrete sufficient mechanism for the square-root coupling ratios. It does not require an oscillator frequency-squared matrix, and it does not require an amplitude measurement. The remaining physical identification is substantial and is kept explicit below.

2. Exact source elimination

A physical cross-depth action must establish the compatible measures and maps; the displayed finite expression does not do that automatically.

3. Coefficient ratios are not automatically pole ratios

The exact result above concerns Σd\Sigma_dΣd​. To conclude

ωd⋆ωe=gddg66, \frac{\omega_d^\star}{\omega_e}=\sqrt{\frac{g_{dd}}{g_{66}}}, ωe​ωd⋆​​=g66​gdd​​​,

one must establish that Σd\Sigma_dΣd​ is the relevant complete occupied scalar rest-frequency coefficient after canonical kinetic normalization and the prescribed mode projection.

Different kinetic factors, additional mass terms, noncommuting operator pieces, spatially varying backgrounds, or frequency-dependent self-energies can prevent this coefficient ratio from being the ratio of physical poles. A common local scalar function is not automatically a common constant in every sector's eigenvalue problem.

The self-contact source, Section 9.1, currently introduces the common-anchor relation conditionally and says the master equation must supply it. The present calculation offers a sufficient response mechanism to test against that obligation. It does not change that documentary status into a completed theorem.

5. What two-sided reference-count weighting would do

Let D⋆=diag⁡(1,15,18564)D_\star=\operatorname{diag}(1,15,18564)D⋆​=diag(1,15,18564). A symmetric primitive response matrix

G~=D⋆−1GD⋆−1=v~v~T,v~=D⋆−1v, \widetilde G=D_\star^{-1}GD_\star^{-1} =\widetilde v\widetilde v^T, \qquad \widetilde v=D_\star^{-1}v, G=D⋆−1​GD⋆−1​=vvT,v=D⋆−1​v,

has diagonal entries

g~dd=gdd/(Dd⋆)2. \boxed{\widetilde g_{dd}=g_{dd}/(D_d^\star)^2.} g​dd​=gdd​/(Dd⋆​)2.​

If this were the primitive response, multiplying its outputs by Dd⋆D_d^\starDd⋆​ would give a vector proportional to vvv, recovering the Case A reference ratios. This is a correct algebraic construction and retains rank one.

It is not a derived V2 normalization. The primitive matrix is no longer the original GGG with the original diagonal entries. Dividing only one side would also change the action's symmetric response interpretation. Calling the two-sided transformation a harmless field redefinition would require transforming the kinetic form, currents, measures, and the meaning of the quoted couplings consistently.

No such projection/kinetic-normalization theorem was established in the inspected passages. The reference counts must not be inserted solely because they recover the desired numbers.

6. Why cross-depth geometry is still an obstruction to automatic factorization

The reciprocity source, Section 5, writes the two variations of the same cross-depth interaction as a lower-depth response to the higher-depth marginal and a higher-depth response to the lower-depth field extended across the additional fibre. The polarization-coupling source, Section 6, separately assigns pushforward to contact, invariant brackets to polarization selection, and the coupling coefficient and field equations to dynamics.

These are valuable geometric and variational structures. They do not automatically make every sector's full response proportional to the same source functional after projection. Even when the numerical coefficients factor as gde=vdveg_{de}=v_dv_egde​=vd​ve​, the full operator can contain sector-dependent kernels, fibre maps, spinor contractions, and kinetic normalizations. Factorization of the coefficient matrix is not yet factorization of all those maps together.

The common-source cancellation is therefore sufficient when the projected response has the proved finite form. Checking that premise in the actual source geometry is indispensable. The bounded searches returned in this session did not establish an independent full projected reference-response action; that search result is not a universal absence theorem.

7. Proof obligations before promoting the mechanism

The immediate task is an identification calculation, not a source-amplitude solve. It must show that the projected scalar interaction has the needed common response after the cross-depth measures and kernels are included; track every Dd⋆D_d^\starDd⋆​ through projection and variation; put the kinetic term into the same normalization used for the frequency extraction; and connect the resulting scalar coefficient to the complete reference pole on the appropriate branch.

If those steps establish Case A, the reference ratios follow without determining CCC. If the action instead establishes Case B, the different ratios are a real consequence of that prescription and must not be hidden by relabeling Σd\Sigma_dΣd​. If additional kernels destroy the common response, their frequency effects have to be calculated rather than silently absorbed into a universal source.

Closing the reference law would still not solve the baryon problem by itself. A composite operator, its color-singlet constraint, and any induced frequency-dependent interactions remain to be derived. The signed color-pair coefficient and exact block reduction in the review note are tools for that next stage, not missing mass factors that can simply be multiplied together.

8. Reproducible status

The regression script differentiates the symbolic quadratic interaction, proves the row annihilator identities for arbitrary sources, calculates both reference prescriptions, and verifies the two-sided count weighting. These tests were checked independently in the session and are packaged for repetition.

Established: a rank-one scalar response can give the desired square-root coefficient ratios with complete cancellation of unmeasured source magnitudes.

Not established: that the written V2 master action realizes this response for canonically normalized occupied reference frequencies, that the reference-count weighting is dynamically selected, that the self-consistent branch exists and is stable, or that a nucleon pole has been predicted.

This is the principal constructive lead from the latest calculations. Its conditional status is part of the finding, not a reason to discard it or to claim more than has been shown.
