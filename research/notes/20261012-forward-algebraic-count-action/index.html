A Direct Variational Derivation of the Conditional Count Clock

Updated October 12, 2026

The reconstructed current interaction admits a short forward derivation. Its algebraic feedback label is varied independently; the spatial field equation then identifies that label with a Dirac eigenvalue. The resulting clock is the geometric cumulative count multiplied by the sector unit. No separate coefficient is adjusted for each particle.

This is a conditional theorem for a specified effective action. Its counting constitutive form was found by inverse research. Writing that form explicitly makes its consequences easy to derive and verify; it does not independently derive the interaction from the master action. The auxiliary representation removes an implicit inverse evaluation, without reducing the model's physical premises.
