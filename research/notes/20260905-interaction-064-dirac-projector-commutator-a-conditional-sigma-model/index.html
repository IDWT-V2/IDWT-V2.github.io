64. Dirac-projector commutator: a conditional sigma-model construction

For D=iγμ∇μ+ΦD=i\gamma^\mu\nabla_\mu+\PhiD=iγμ∇μ​+Φ and a zeroth-order projector PPP, the general commutator acting on a test section is
