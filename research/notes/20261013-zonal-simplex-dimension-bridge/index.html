A zonal differential bridge with exact kinetic pullback

Updated October 13, 2026

Two actual height derivatives connect three occurrences of the same simplex spectral polynomial. The derivative maps change the canonical norm. Their induced metric, or equivalently their normalized polar map, must be retained to transfer a frequency equation. This result concerns zonal scalar functions and the positive linear branch after specified null modes are removed.

The weighted-ball clock is a prescribed material construction in ddd ordinary spatial coordinates. Its Sd+2S^{d+2}Sd+2 representation is an auxiliary mathematical lift in d+3d+3d+3 Euclidean coordinates, not a physical sphere within Rd\mathbb R^dRd or R10\mathbb R^{10}R10. The original bending comparison instead uses a surface Sd−2S^{d-2}Sd−2 embedded in Rd\mathbb R^dRd. No sphere dimension is silently equated with its Euclidean embedding dimension, and no compactification or Kaluza--Klein interpretation is used. Spectral inner products below are integrals of classical spatial functions, not an ontology of physical states moving in a Hilbert space.
