Different generating functions count different things

The ordinary index generating function is also exact:

∑n≥1S(n,d)zn=z(1−z)d+1,∣z∣<1. \sum_{n\ge1}S(n,d)z^n=\frac{z}{(1-z)^{d+1}}, \qquad |z|<1. n≥1∑​S(n,d)zn=(1−z)d+1z​,∣z∣<1.

It weights the index by the count; the heat trace instead places the count in an exponential. Their singular exponents need not agree. Likewise, comparison with n−dsn^{-ds}n−ds shows that ∑nS(n,d)−s\sum_n S(n,d)^{-s}∑n​S(n,d)−s converges absolutely exactly when Re⁡s>1/d\operatorname{Re}s>1/dRes>1/d.

The heat exponent 1/d1/d1/d is therefore a property of this one-eigenvalue-per-index tower. It is not evidence that the corresponding spatial dimension equals ddd. For example, the actual round-sphere Dirac spectrum has eigenvalues linear in its level and nontrivial multiplicities, as detailed in round-sphere Dirac counts. V2's physical depth is the number of ordinary macroscopic spatial directions in its principal-symbol construction. This diagnostic does not replace that construction or change any depth.

What this does not regularize
