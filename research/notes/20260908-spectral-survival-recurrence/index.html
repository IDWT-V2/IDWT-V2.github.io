A specified evolution and preparation

Let HHH be a self-adjoint frequency operator and uuu a normalized field profile. Time evolution is u(t)=e−itHuu(t)=e^{-itH}uu(t)=e−itHu; for an energy operator replace HHH by H/ℏH/\hbarH/ℏ. The return amplitude and weight are

a(t)=⟨u,e−itHu⟩,P(t)=∣a(t)∣2. a(t)=\langle u,e^{-itH}u\rangle, \qquad P(t)=|a(t)|^2. a(t)=⟨u,e−itHu⟩,P(t)=∣a(t)∣2.

These are Hilbert-space diagnostics of the selected linear evolution. They apply to classical complex mode amplitudes as well as quantum states. Their interpretation as an observed survival probability requires a measurement prescription. Mode amplitudes describe fields on the one physical manifold and introduce no further spatial dimensions.
