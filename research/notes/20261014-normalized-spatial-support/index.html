Normalized Spatial Contact and a Supported Profile

Updated October 14, 2026

Combined with a gradient term and competing attractive and saturating contacts, it gives a converging numerical localized profile without an external trap or a prescribed finite band. This is a candidate classical spatial object, not an identified particle or a derived mass family.
