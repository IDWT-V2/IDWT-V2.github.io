1. Question

Test the user's proposed geometric idea:

If particles approach one another in the observer-resolved directions, their unobserved spatial separations need not remain independent. A genuine interaction may reduce one full higher-dimensional separation, just as an ordinary central force in three dimensions reduces all components of one spatial separation vector.

Then ask whether the existing V2 cross-depth contact and polarization structure produces recognizable qualitative interaction patterns among particles of different assigned depths.
