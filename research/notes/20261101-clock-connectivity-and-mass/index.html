Common Clocks, Spatial Connectivity and Mechanical Mass

Updated November 1, 2026

proposed construction

This is a proposed constitutive construction. Its clock, graph and finite algebraic consequences are derived below; they do not establish that the single physical field realizes the proposed action. The new synthesis has 29 passing exact symbolic checks and has not received independent scientific review. Earlier shape and response results retain their own stated domains. All physical geometry belongs to ordinary ten-dimensional space plus time.

1. The physical picture and the precise target

A particle is proposed to be a persistent arrangement of C portions of one field in ordinary ten-dimensional space and time. Each portion advances at one common proper fundamental Omega. Phase offsets may be arbitrary. The sum of one crossing per portion per cycle has average angular tick rate C Omega when coincident crossings count with multiplicity. These are physical portions, not independently resonating Fourier levels. External deterministic electron trajectories are separate from these internal coordinates.

The construction seeks to connect that picture to supported shapes, mechanical inertia, source charge and relativistic motion. The recent calculations provide pieces with different actions and domains. Their conclusions cannot simply be added. The common reduced law proposed below gives explicit clock and force consequences while leaving a defined field-realization problem.

The proposed connection is: C portions joined into one connected structure have C-1 independent graph connections in the rank sense. The already proposed rest functional contains exactly C-1. Replacing that integer by a spectral function of a spatial coupling graph gives an explicit, testable interpretation of the term and a smooth joining cost. This is an interpretation supplied as a new premise, not a derivation of its coefficient from the field.

2. The actual starting point

The compact-family action, with its stated mathematical qualifications, has canonical form

Theta = p dot dR + Theta_internal - sum I_a d beta_a,
P = sum I_a,
H = sqrt(c^2 |p|^2 + B^2),
B = Omega [P + hbar(C-1)] + U.

Here I_a is positive phase action, U is complete internal excess in an admitted canonical chart, and B is mechanical rest energy of this changed law. It is not the old action's energy renamed. On the supplied leaf P=hbar and an admitted zero-excess state, B=C hbar Omega. The old internal coefficient m_g remains independent of m_0=hbar Omega/c^2.

The reviewed finite family supplies U=T_relative+W_0, with source-independent internal kinetic coefficients and a nonnegative positional well. The broader shape theorem supplies a restricted nonnegative excess, with exact normalization, a fixed support ball, translation slice and conserved-charge restrictions. It does not establish that the whole chart is invariant under time evolution.

The provisional covariant construction supplies the physical centroid Y, the complete internal spin S, and

R = Y + c^2 S p/[B(H+B)].

Its frame connection gives the canonical one-form exactly, before source restriction. Its rigid field reconstruction contracts the spatial profiles and transforms the actual spinor. This is the best current reduced kinematic bridge, but not yet an unrestricted local field action.

3. An exact clock-preservation criterion

Use phi_a=-beta_a so its canonical term is +I_a d phi_a. In an independent canonical chart with p fixed under source differentiation,

d phi_a/dt = partial H/partial I_a
           = (B/H)[Omega + partial U/partial I_a].

For the free rigid centroid d tau/dt=B/H. Consequently

d phi_a/d tau = Omega + partial U/partial I_a.

The statement about proper time applies on the centroid branch for which that identification has been derived. On a general spinning, deforming or accelerated field, the polarization and frame connections in the actual clock equation must also be retained.

This yields a useful design condition: in that canonical chart, an added internal interaction preserves every fundamental exactly if its derivative with respect to every I_a vanishes. Source-independent positional and shape terms can therefore coexist with the clock. This is stronger than requiring overall amplitude independence.

For example,

U_bad = lambda (I_1-I_2)^2/(I_1+I_2)^2

is homogeneous of degree zero: sum I_a partial_I_a U_bad=0. Nevertheless its two source derivatives are different at unequal source sharing. It protects an action-weighted average, not the individual common clocks. Conversely, independent phase invariance preserves the individual I_a but does not by itself guarantee the clock: the Hamiltonian's source derivatives still matter.

On a connected open source domain, requiring partial_I_a B=Omega for every a integrates to

B = Omega P + F(other canonical variables).

Thus the clock requirement fixes the source dependence while leaving an entire source-independent geometric rest functional available. It does not choose that functional. This is where shape and connectivity can enter without amplitude mass fitting.

4. A spectral interpretation of the count term

On the fixed-C portion chart, take a real nonnegative symmetric weight w_ab determined by spatial geometry, independent of I and phi in the chosen chart. A trial example is a common finite-range function of squared rest-frame center separation. Deriving this function from Psi remains necessary; disjoint compact densities do not produce a nonzero direct density-overlap integral by themselves.

Define the C by C weighted Laplacian

L_aa = sum_(b != a) w_ab,     L_ab = -w_ab.

For any real vector v,

v^T L v = sum_(a<b) w_ab (v_a-v_b)^2.

Therefore a vector is in the kernel exactly when it is constant on each connected component. If there are K components, dim ker L=K and rank L=C-K. No physical extra dimensions are introduced: L describes the response between portions situated in the same physical space. Its eigenvectors are diagnostic variations, not separately oscillating physical constituents.

The proposed rest functional is

B_graph = Omega P + hbar Omega Tr g(L/epsilon) + U,

where epsilon>0 is a shared response scale, and a concrete twice continuously differentiable function is

g(x) = 0                                  for x <= 0,
       6x^5 - 15x^4 + 10x^3               for 0 < x < 1,
       1                                  for x >= 1.

Its first derivative is 30 x^2(1-x)^2 inside the transition; its first and second derivatives vanish at both endpoints. The piecewise function is C2, not C-infinity. L/epsilon is dimensionless.

If every nonzero eigenvalue of L is at least epsilon, then

Tr g(L/epsilon) = C-K,
B_graph = Omega [P+hbar(C-K)] + U.

For a connected particle K=1, this reproduces the existing rest functional exactly. For K disconnected calibrated clusters with P=K hbar and U=0, its rest value is C hbar Omega. This is a rest-energy statement. Arbitrarily moving separated clusters require a sum of their individual square roots; a single global square root is not cluster-separable mechanics.

The premise assigns one action-energy unit hbar Omega per independent connection. Counting the connections is derived from the Laplacian; assigning them that cost is proposed. Their spectral plateau does not establish a selected C, a selected geometry, or any actual bound state.

5. Joining forces and why the spectral gap matters

For an admitted smooth geometric variation,

delta B_graph = Omega delta P
   + (hbar Omega/epsilon) Tr[g'(L/epsilon) delta L] + delta U.

The trace derivative follows by diagonalizing L: only the diagonal entries of delta L enter the first variation of the spectral trace, and the expression is basis independent, including degenerate eigenspaces. On a spectral plateau the graph term has zero force and zero second variation. Existing local positional and shape restoring terms can then remain unchanged, provided the neighborhood stays on that plateau and satisfies their original domains.

Across a transition the extra force must be retained. For two sites of weight w, the eigenvalues are 0 and 2w:

B_connection = hbar Omega g(2w/epsilon),
d B_connection/dw = (2 hbar Omega/epsilon) g'(2w/epsilon).

At fixed P, turning an absent link into a saturated link costs hbar Omega. If w decreases with separation, this contribution favors separation while it is in its transition region. It is not itself an attractive binding law. A positional well or another same-field contribution must sustain the structure and any transition barrier. Its zero derivative on a plateau does not make the global energy cost disappear.

This construction repairs the hard reference jump into a specified continuous force law at fixed C. It does not yet repair arbitrary relativistic cluster merging or smooth creation/deletion of field portions. Positive individual conserved I_a also prevent simply deleting a portion without a source-transfer mechanism.

Connectedness alone is insufficient for the exact plateau. For an equal-weight cycle the positive spectral gap is 4w sin^2(pi/C), which decreases with C. A fixed epsilon eventually loses some contributions. Thus an exact count law over a large count range needs a proven gap bound on the admitted physical graph family, an appropriately justified graph normalization, or a different physical mechanism. Dividing by the measured smallest eigenvalue would make a formally flat spectrum but would introduce nonlocal, potentially singular feedback; it is not an automatic repair. The sharp rank limit has the original discontinuity.

6. Shape response fits the same variational structure, conditionally

Let q denote admitted shape coordinates after removing translation kernels and enforcing the actual fixed charges. If the reduced excess has a positive Hessian A and a weak spatial load contributes -f dot q, then

U(q) = (1/2) q^T A q - f^T q,
q_equilibrium = A^-1 f,
U_relaxed = -(1/2) f^T A^-1 f.

This is the finite-coordinate form of the reviewed forced-profile inverse. A positive susceptibility lowers the loaded energy after relaxation. It gives a concrete route for shape feedback to alter interaction forces.

For a reciprocal pair, f must depend on both actual configurations and come from the same total energy. Differentiating that relaxed energy then gives the two forces, including derivatives of A and f; a prescribed external field is only a one-sided comparison. Translation invariance of that total interaction implies equal and opposite total forces by differentiation. Lorentz covariance and retardation require more than this static identity.

If A or f depends on I, the relaxed correction also shifts the clock:

partial_I U_relaxed = -(partial_I f)^T A^-1 f
               +(1/2) f^T A^-1 (partial_I A) A^-1 f.

Therefore amplitude-independent geometric forcing and stiffness are sufficient to preserve the source-affine clock in this reduced construction. Whether the real field produces that independence is the physical test. The old force-response calculation used m_g and a boundary-fixed gauge; its numerical susceptibility cannot be imported as the coefficient of this new law without rederiving the kinetic and gauge reductions. In particular its gauge differs from the finite-basin theorem's slice.

7. Charge, spin and alternative source normalization

The shared source leaf retains an economical possibility: an additive bilinear charge is q P, while mass depends on C. At P=hbar the charge can be count independent without assigning each portion action hbar. This remains a specified source convention and calibration, not a derived electric charge operator. General geometric charge matrices and their eigenvalues still need their own physical coupling test.

A recent alternative operator construction tests the alternative P=C hbar. It correctly exposes that norm-bilinear charge and aligned spin then tend to grow with C. Its addendum supplies a narrower parity obstruction for equal-action vacuum/filled singlets with a shared complex structure and the stated maximal observer-spin orientation. Arbitrary polarizations, nonmaximal orientation and other charge laws are different hypotheses. A representation Casimir and a classical spin expectation are also distinct quantities.

Two qualifications matter for assembling the work. First, setting kappa c^2=2 a Omega in a Galilean field kinetic term is a constitutive coefficient relation, even though its desired value is motivated by the known mass-frequency conversion. The conversion alone does not force an arbitrary kinetic coefficient to take that value. Second, the shared-source self-response obstruction uses E_self=b_d sum a_i^(4-d) together with a particular radius-norm relation. Its power of C is valid only when that relation and the same response law apply. The current normalized compact profile has supplied radius independent of N_i. A complete common action must decide which behavior it actually has; the historical law cannot close every shared-source model merely by changing its label.

The transfer of the Clifford and magnetic-response results to this combined action remains unproved. These qualifications concern applicability; they do not overturn calculations within their original hypotheses. The graph proposal keeps the shared leaf because it preserves the count-independent source route. It does not solve spin orientation, color, magnetic moment or their physical coupling.

8. A sharpened assembly of the candidate

For one fixed, isolated, gapped cluster, use the complete covariant reduced one-form of the covariant rigid construction and replace its rest functional by B_graph above. Use positive source-independent U on the admitted finite shape/position chart, with its actual constraints. On a rigid, constant-polarization reference satisfying U=0, P=hbar and the graph plateau:

B = C hbar Omega,
mechanical mass = C hbar Omega/c^2,
every proper phase rate = Omega,
ideal average crossing rate = C Omega/(2pi),
integrated source charge = q hbar.

The clock and source independence follow from the declared canonical law; the rest count follows from the plateau and source calibration; the kinematic covariance follows only within the supplied reduced construction and rigid embedding. These are compatible conditional outcomes, not five independent empirical successes.

The inertial test is explicit. At fixed internal state, partial H/partial p=c^2 p/H and the rest momentum Hessian is c^2 Id/B. Thus the small-velocity inertia is B/c^2. Its agreement with the count-frequency mass is exact on the reference branch. Excited U changes inertia; the formula-particle identification remains the reference branch, not every excited state.

This construction applies algebraically to any C and to spatial graphs embedded in any maintained depth. It produces no distinction at depth five. The current actual compact profiles still fill R10; lower-rank center arrangements do not by themselves realize depth-two, three, four, five or six particles. The spin-module selector and property calculations therefore remain separate conditional constraints until those actual lower-depth field solutions exist.

9. What this changes about the next mathematical problem

The mass-operator question can now be stated more narrowly: can one action of Psi generate (i) a canonical source-affine clock term, (ii) a stable localized spatial structure, (iii) the proposed connectivity rest functional or a simpler replacement, and (iv) the existing physical covariant embedding with no hidden kinetic variables?

In a field reconstruction F(z), any coordinate direction invisible to the field and its legitimate conjugate/history data must not carry independent observable mechanics. One must check the full action's descent through that redundancy, not just reconstruct the stationary field. The current rigid image omits general relative momenta; declaring a finite Hamiltonian does not expose those momenta in Psi automatically. The bare first-order spinor term's p/C translation coefficient is not the momentum of the complete old action, so it cannot settle this question alone.

The graph gives no reason for C=binomial(n+d-1,d), no preferred n, and no source quantization. It describes arbitrary graph counts. A physical simplex closure rule and a field-supported gap are still necessary. Source conservation also retains the merger constraint: two calibrated clusters bring total source 2hbar, whereas one calibrated cluster has hbar. A transition needs an accounted exterior source channel or a different admissibility rule. Spectral smoothing cannot remove a conserved-charge mismatch.

This is a constructive partial synthesis: a single reduced proposal with explicit clock, count, inertia and transition derivatives, plus a precise route for shape response. It replaces several loosely connected requirements with equations that can be tested together. The principal open problem is still their realization and selection by the full field.

Reproduction and interpretation

The exact algebra checker uses Python and SymPy. Run it in a writable working copy with python clock-connectivity-synthesis-checks.py; it writes clock-connectivity-synthesis-checks.json beside the script. It checks the Hamiltonian derivatives, a counterexample to individual-clock preservation by degree-zero homogeneity, connected and disconnected graph ranks, smoothing endpoint derivatives, a two-site transition and the quadratic shape-response formula. These are finite exact checks of the displayed algebra, not continuum evolution, a field-action derivation or empirical particle validation.

The physical count rule and its numerical scale prescription are presented in the particle-geometry case. Volume 11 distinguishes the restricted shape bounds from nonlinear persistence; volume 12 explains complete momentum and inertia. Those distinctions are essential when using this proposal.
