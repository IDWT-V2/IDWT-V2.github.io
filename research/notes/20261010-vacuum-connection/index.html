Occupied Sectors and a Positive Floor

The actual Hodge operator is evaluated at depths two, three, four, five, six and ten. Active three/four have the demonstrated vacuum/probe ambiguity. The compact non-Abelian Gauss trials, in contrast, already have flat exterior vacuum, so vacuum flatness alone does not remove that separate confinement limitation.

Reproduction and the Next Physical Requirement

The checker and report retain 472 passing vacuum, full-equation, work and clock checks across 16 cases, plus 18 Hodge dependency checks. There is no particle-specific adjustment or calculation at unoccupied depth.

The exact-zero coefficient is useful for sector selection but does not yet define a complete physical connection theory. A common geometric reduction must determine how the connection exists and evolves where its deformation carrier vanishes, preserving whatever source and flux results survive that reduction. That requirement is sharper than either asserting causality from a frozen positive coefficient or abandoning the geometric zero without testing a completion.

Actual Induced Geometry Gives a More Specific Completion Test
