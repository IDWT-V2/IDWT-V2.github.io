35. Projector-dynamics audit of the remaining unobserved-steering channel

The previous sections reduced the possible V2-native pre-contact mechanism to mixed or vertical terms in the depth-six projected Dirac operator. A corpus audit now fixes the status of that possibility.

V2 assumes a smooth zeroth-order sector projector

Pd:Σ∞∣Qd→Σd P_d:\Sigma_\infty|_{Q_d}\to\Sigma_d Pd​:Σ∞​∣Qd​​→Σd​

and defines the tangentially compressed sector operator schematically by

Dd=PdD∞∥Pd. \mathcal D_d=P_d\mathcal D_\infty^{\parallel}P_d. Dd​=Pd​D∞∥​Pd​.

Because PdP_dPd​ is order zero, derivatives of PdP_dPd​ enter only in lower-order terms. These are precisely the terms in which one can obtain

∇Pd,A⊥,IId,Hd, \nabla P_d, \qquad A^\perp, \qquad \mathrm{II}_d, \qquad H_d, ∇Pd​,A⊥,IId​,Hd​,

and other normal/extrinsic connection data.

Thus the geometry does provide a legitimate mathematical location for the desired two-electron mixed or vertical curvature.

However, the current V2 action contains no independent variational dynamics for the projector or embedding. In particular, there is no written equation of the form

δSδPd=0 \boxed{ \frac{\delta S}{\delta P_d}=0 } δPd​δS​=0​

or an equivalent field equation fixing

The phrase “dynamically defined projector” is therefore presently a construction requirement, not a completed dynamical law.

35.2 The normal connection does not rescue the argument by itself

The normal connection transforms correctly as a gauge potential,

A⊥↦UA⊥U−1−(dU)U−1, A^\perp\mapsto U A^\perp U^{-1}-(dU)U^{-1}, A⊥↦UA⊥U−1−(dU)U−1,

with curvature

F⊥=dA⊥+A⊥∧A⊥. F^\perp=dA^\perp+A^\perp\wedge A^\perp. F⊥=dA⊥+A⊥∧A⊥.

This proves that higher-dimensional normal geometry can realize gauge-like structure after projection.

It does not prove that a second free electron sources a particular nonzero A⊥A^\perpA⊥ or F⊥F^\perpF⊥.

Transformation law and dynamical field equation are different statements.

Therefore one may not infer unobserved focusing merely from the existence of the normal bundle.

35.3 Exact missing closure

A completed V2 derivation of the proposed beam correlation would require at minimum a master-geometric term whose variation gives a source equation for the projector or normal connection. Schematically, one needs something like

δSgeom[P,Ψ]δP6=0 \frac{\delta S_{\rm geom}[P,\Psi]}{\delta P_6}=0 δP6​δSgeom​[P,Ψ]​=0

or

δSgeom[A⊥,Ψ]δA⊥=J⊥[Ψ], \frac{\delta S_{\rm geom}[A^\perp,\Psi]}{\delta A^\perp}=J^\perp[\Psi], δA⊥δSgeom​[A⊥,Ψ]​=J⊥[Ψ],

with no assumption here that either displayed schematic form is the correct final theory.

That equation would have to determine all of the following without introducing arbitrary fitted functions:

  1. whether a free electron sources normal or mixed curvature;
  2. its sign under electron--electron versus electron--opposite-charge configurations;
  3. its radial/retarded Green function;
  4. its coupling to the three unobserved transverse impact coordinates;
  5. why ordinary electromagnetic propagation retains its observed low-dimensional Coulomb behavior;
  6. whether it conserves or transfers the higher-dimensional transverse Noether charge;
  7. the polarization dependence of the resulting full-depth collision rate.

Until such an equation is derived, the mixed-curvature route remains an allowed slot in the geometry, not a prediction.
