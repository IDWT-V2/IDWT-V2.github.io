22. What observed interaction patterns this begins to explain

The following qualitative pattern now follows from one consistent geometry rather than separate collision stories.

Electromagnetism

A low-depth projected interaction can be common between high-depth charged particles because fibre coordinates are marginalized. It need not force full particle-sector coincidence.

Weak conversion

Cross-depth blocks naturally change particle sector. Large depth mismatch does not produce a huge coordinate-hit penalty because the lower-depth mode spans the extra fibre. The real suppression/selection must come from polarization, chirality, mediator mass, and the transition operator.

Full-depth restructuring

Same-depth local contact is geometrically much more selective because it samples the diagonal in the complete particle sector. It can additionally be suppressed by unobserved impact parameter and fermionic antisymmetry.
