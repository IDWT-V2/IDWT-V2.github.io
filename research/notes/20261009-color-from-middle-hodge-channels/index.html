A Three-Channel Hodge Criterion for the Quark Sectors

Updated October 9, 2026

The maintained down-type quark family occupies three ordinary spatial dimensions, and the up-type family four. Each flavor needs the same color triplet, separate from its spin and flavor. The comparison depths are two, five, six and ten. This investigation asks whether one geometric prescription has a complete three-channel space precisely at the two quark depths.

The result is a conditional dimensional connection. Middle-degree Hodge channels have complex rank three only at depths three and four, over all positive dimensions. An even-dimensional channel restricts isomorphically to its odd-dimensional partner. At four and three, this realizes a chiral four-dimensional structure with a complete three-dimensional shadow. Identifying the channels with color still requires an equivalent-mode interpretation and a common interaction. The rank theorem alone does not make the higher-depth particles color singlets.

A second uniform test reaches the same exceptional pair: the lowest nontrivial adjoint rotation-Casimir channel of a minimal spatial spinor's endomorphisms has rank three only at three and four. These are related geometric tests, not independent experimental observations.

2. What The Four-Dimensional Shadow Actually Does

At m=2m=2m=2, identify a three-dimensional two-form with a vector by its Hodge dual. Each of the two four-dimensional chiral triples is

F±(B)=∗3B±B∧n,B∈Λ1V∗. F_\pm(B)=*_3B\pm B\wedge n,\qquad B\in\Lambda^1V^*. F±​(B)=∗3​B±B∧n,B∈Λ1V∗.

Both restrict to ∗3B*_3B∗3​B. Ordinary rotations of VVV act identically on the two triples. Rotations mixing nnn with VVV act with opposite signs in their three-component representations. Thus a three-dimensional restriction cannot distinguish the two chiral structures through its own rotations, while the additional ordinary spatial direction can distinguish them.

This provides a precise version of a shared three-/four-dimensional geometric origin. It does not require an up-type quark to be present in every hadron. The containing geometry and connection can exist without an up-type constituent. The Ω−\Omega^-Ω− baryon's sssssssss valence content is an important down-type-only control; see the PDG Omega table.

The two triples are alternatives, or require a common diagonal symmetry. Treating them as two unrelated color groups would add another premise. The embeddings concern bundles and geometry, not one particle solution being the restriction of another particle's solution.

3. Why Three Channels Are Not Yet Color

Three equivalent complex amplitudes with a Hermitian norm and a preserved complex volume have maximal mixing group SU(3)SU(3)SU(3). This supplies its fundamental representation and eight traceless Hermitian generators. But real rotation of a geometric triple gives SO(3)SO(3)SO(3), with only three generators. Its induced real geometric connection remains in that smaller algebra unless further field-dependent mode geometry supplies the missing directions.

There is also a spin distinction. On a fixed spatial triple the rotations act irreducibly as spin one. Schur's lemma leaves only scalar complex operators commuting with every such rotation, not an independent color algebra. The eight traceless endomorphisms split into spin-one and spin-two spaces of dimensions three and five under those rotations. They cannot simply be relabeled eight internal color directions. This use of Schur's lemma is standard; see Woit, section 2.1.

A constructive relative-frame completion avoids that particular obstruction. Let RRR be a geometric orthonormal channel frame and BBB its complex pattern amplitudes. Set c=RTBc=R^TBc=RTB. A spatial rotation OOO acts on both:

(R,B)⟼(OR,OB),c⟼c. (R,B)\longmapsto(OR,OB),\qquad c\longmapsto c. (R,B)⟼(OR,OB),c⟼c.

Color can instead act as

c⟼Uc,B⟼RURTB,U∈SU(3). c\longmapsto Uc,\qquad B\longmapsto RUR^TB,\qquad U\in SU(3). c⟼Uc,B⟼RURTB,U∈SU(3).

A separate spinor factor then has commuting actions Si⊗I3S_i\otimes I_3Si​⊗I3​ and I2⊗TAI_2\otimes T_AI2​⊗TA​. More generally the same construction works at channel rank kmk_mkm​; it does not select rank three on its own.

For a varying frame, the relative kinetic derivative is

∂B−(∂R)RTB=R ∂c. \partial B-(\partial R)R^TB=R\,\partial c. ∂B−(∂R)RTB=R∂c.

A physical enhancement needs justification from the actual interaction, not just a choice of frame.

8. What Would Make This A Particle-Property Connection

A useful common law would derive an equivalent-mode bundle from the sector operator, preserve its full observed spin representation, and make the color action scalar on each occupied mass-frequency branch. For a triplet, color invariance gives

If the underlying mass feedback instead uses a total trace counting every color component, that normalization must be rederived rather than silently treating a per-color count as a total.

The concrete progress is an exact full-channel rank criterion selecting three/four and its shared chiral restriction map, together with constructive the lowest nontrivial spinor-endomorphism criterion, and constructive ways of separating relative color from observed spin and generating an eight-direction mode connection. The remaining physical tasks are to derive the equivalent modes and interaction, preserve the full nested spatial geometry, and select singlet branches for the nonquark particles under the same law. No spontaneous dimension-dependent cancellation of a triplet has been established. These linked mathematical consequences are conditional support for investigation, not independent experimental proof of IDWT.
