A Positive Hodge-Gated Dielectric Supports Finite-Width Color Flux at Three and Four

Updated October 10, 2026

One positive action can combine the existing geometric color gate with an ordinary spatial flux guide. Under its explicit neutral-mode constitutive premise, a long tube of commuting color flux has a finite transverse width and positive tension at three and four. The same scaling identity excludes a regular finite-width profile at the other higher occupied depths, while the common Hodge factor removes depth two. The profile is a minimum of its transverse fixed-flux scalar energy, rather than a negative-energy supporting medium.

This is a conditional construction, not a derivation of the master field's dielectric, complete non-Abelian confinement or observed hadron spectra. Its value is that source constraints, the independent geometric color carrier, spatial flux support and complete retained energy can now be examined in one stated action.

Finite Sources Share the Same Linear Energy Bound

The sharp separation comparison now applies the transverse minimum plane by plane. Opposite fixed commuting sources have energy bounded below by tension times their source-free separation; explicit finite caps give an upper bound with the same slope. Completing the full Abelian electric quadratic energy also retains the fixed-flux stability bound. Full non-Abelian screening and dynamical endpoints remain outside this result.

A Neutral Geometric Frame Supplies the Quartic Power

The neutral-frame construction realizes the active dielectric as the squared area of two ordinary deformation directions. Exact area and gradient bounds recover this scalar minimum with coefficient kappa/4, while retaining and testing relative amplitude, angle and orientation modes. Its common-parent reduction and full spacetime tensor completion remain premises; the scalar boost result is not automatically a complete frame boost law. Robustness Under a Quadratic Correction

The mixed dielectric extension adds a positive quadratic invariant while preserving the exact-zero vacuum and occupied-sector Hodge gate. A sharp interpolation bound reduces full scalar shape minimization exactly to two moments. At depth three a finite guide requires flux above a continuous widening threshold; at four a metastable branch precedes finite-width global binding. The fold is not a local minimum, and the upper branch has exactly one negative scalar Hessian direction. These results retain the commuting restriction and do not override the compact full non-Abelian alternatives.
