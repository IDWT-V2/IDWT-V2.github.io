The operator and its multiplicity

For the untwisted complex spinor Dirac operator on a round sphere SdS^dSd of radius RRR, d≥2d\ge2d≥2, the eigenvalues and multiplicities for each sign are

λk±=±k+d/2R,md(k)=2⌊d/2⌋(k+d−1d−1),k=0,1,…. \lambda_k^\pm=\pm\frac{k+d/2}{R},\qquad m_d(k)=2^{\lfloor d/2\rfloor}\binom{k+d-1}{d-1}, \qquad k=0,1,\ldots. λk±​=±Rk+d/2​,md​(k)=2⌊d/2⌋(d−1k+d−1​),k=0,1,….

This is a cumulative eigenstate count with multiplicity, not an individual Dirac eigenvalue. Including both signs doubles this count.

Use in V2

These formulas provide a checked benchmark for the candidate sphere operators. The spinor factor counts field components; it introduces no additional physical spatial directions.

They do not select a sphere as the actual sector geometry, construct its nesting, or derive the law m=μS(n,d)m=\mu S(n,d)m=μS(n,d). The mass law and the individual sphere Dirac eigenvalues remain different quantities. Existing particle depths and the neutrino mass ratios use a separately specified contact mass operator.

The mode counting checks verify the cumulative sums and the explicit S3S^3S3 and S5S^5S5 examples. They check applications of the cited spectrum, not a numerical construction of the sphere Dirac operator.
