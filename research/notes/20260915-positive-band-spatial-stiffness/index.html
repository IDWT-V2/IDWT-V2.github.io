The supporting multiplet's stiffness depends on the full spatial deformation

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The complete positive-frequency multiplet can cancel every centered source after time averaging. That balance alone does not establish an energy minimum. The second variation must include the actual change of the spatial geometry, not only a linear perturbation of its projected matrix.

For the separate bounded multiplier h=z/1+r2h=z/\sqrt{1+r^2}h=z/1+r2​, all tested coordinate curvatures of the balanced averaged energy are positive. For the density-gradient multiplier, the full spatial term produces negative curvatures, including the endpoint direction at every maintained depth. This distinction was absent from the linear matrix model.

These are energy curvatures at fixed positive canonical populations. They are not particle inertias or calculated growth rates. The averaged state has not been shown to be a pointwise coherent particle solution.
