Sharp Plane Bounds Give Linear Energy for Finite Commuting Sources

Updated October 10, 2026

The positive Hodge-gated flux guide can be tested beyond an ideal infinite tube. For fixed, opposite commuting sources with a source-free interval of length RRR, the same action gives a lower bound Tmin⁡RT_{\min}RTmin​R. Finite cap trials give an upper bound Tmin⁡R+CT_{\min}R+CTmin​R+C with an RRR-independent positive constant. The bounds therefore share a linear asymptotic coefficient without requiring an exact finite-end solution.

This is a prescribed-source variational result for a commuting normal-connection channel. It is not a proof about quantum color representations, dynamical source creation or full non-Abelian screening. The same exact Hodge gate and ordinary spatial sector response are retained; no independently adjusted particle coefficients enter.

Verification

The checker and report preserve 231 passing checks across 15 cases, with exact code dependencies, occupied-sector controls, polynomial cap identities, independent complete cap integrals, Gaussian comparisons with the sharp bound, fixed-source separation slopes and directly completed electric second variations. The original tube checker supplies its independently verified ground profiles. No particle simulation, particle-specific retuning or calculation at unoccupied depths is used.

Full Classical Connection Has a Compact Alternative

The explicit non-Abelian Gauss construction supplies finite-energy compact trials for fixed one-signed mean-color source cores under this same neutral dielectric. Two opposite disjoint cores have a separation-independent field-energy upper bound. Their commutator source removes the conserved commuting plane flux used above. These results therefore establish the commuting theorem’s actual domain and exclude transferring its linear lower bound to the unrestricted classical prescribed-source problem. They do not screen quantum fundamental triality or derive a localized matter source. The inactive Hodge source constraints survive unchanged.
