Spatial response of a conformal scalar-current interaction

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: An exact zero-current Killing field has a closed full-variation response to a conformal scalar-current interaction, whose simplex coefficient enters collective frequencies and makes this branch unstable at high harmonic degree.

Test the scalar-current route in the field equation

The conformal scalar operator has a simplex spectrum, but an interaction in which it acts on the scalar current must be varied and solved as a nonlinear field equation. There is an exact spatial background on which this response can be calculated. The simplex value enters the collective response; it is not directly the background frequency or an observed particle mass.
