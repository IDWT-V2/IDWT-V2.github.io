Updated September 8, 2026

Summary: Eliminating stable response modes of the same field gives a Gram correction to the direct coupling, with exact reciprocity and a proportional-profile criterion for rank one.

Direct coupling and reduction of one field

IDWT has one field Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​ and no mediator particles. Its cross-sector interactions belong to that field's own action. Start with a specified background and independent real coordinates for its admissible variations in a finite region:

δΨ∞=Srj+SsX. \delta\Psi_\infty=S_{\rm r}j+S_{\rm s}X. δΨ∞​=Sr​j+Ss​X.

Here jjj describes retained variations and XXX describes other variations of the same field. The maps must respect the actual spatial embeddings, constraints, and normalization. Real coordinates may describe a complex classical field or a specified mean-field energy; they add no physical dimensions. Independent variations do not imply separate physical fields.

Retarded response and a controlled static limit

For linearized equations of the same field, let D(ω)D(\omega)D(ω) be their operator in retained and remaining coordinates. Wherever the retarded inverse of DssD_{\rm ss}Dss​ exists, eliminating the remaining coordinates gives the exact block expression

Deff(ω)=Drr(ω)−Drs(ω)Dss−1(ω)Dsr(ω). D_{\rm eff}(\omega)=D_{\rm rr}(\omega) -D_{\rm rs}(\omega)D_{\rm ss}^{-1}(\omega)D_{\rm sr}(\omega). Deff​(ω)=Drr​(ω)−Drs​(ω)Dss−1​(ω)Dsr​(ω).

The inverse is a response kernel for field evolution. This step assumes no exchanged particles. Both the blocks and their causal boundary conditions come from the original equations.

If that reduction yields harmonic response coordinates with positive inertia MMM, their equation is

MX¨+LX=S(t). M\ddot X+LX=S(t). MX¨+LX=S(t).

With Fourier convention e−iωte^{-i\omega t}e−iωt and retarded boundary conditions,

GabR(ω)=Ba∗[L−(ω+i0)2M]−1Bb.(8) G_{ab}^{R}(\omega) =B_a^*\left[L-(\omega+i0)^2M\right]^{-1}B_b. \tag{8} GabR​(ω)=Ba∗​[L−(ω+i0)2M]−1Bb​.(8)

For real reciprocal coefficients its transpose reverses source indices at the same frequency. Above accessible resonances it need not be a positive static Gram matrix. An instantaneous interaction is the low-frequency approximation to this specified response, not proof of instantaneous physical signalling.
