46. Quaternionic structure of the free-pair active four-space

The apparent 2+2+22+2+22+2+2 form of R12R_{12}R12​ in a convenient basis does not mean that the free pair canonically selects two real two-planes inside its rotating four-space. A stronger invariant structure is present.

Let

R=−J1J2, R=-J_1J_2, R=−J1​J2​,

and decompose its symmetric and antisymmetric parts,

C=12(R+RT),A=12(R−RT). C=\frac12(R+R^T), \qquad A=\frac12(R-R^T). C=21​(R+RT),A=21​(R−RT).

For generic separation 0<θ<π/20<\theta<\pi/20<θ<π/2, define the pair-fixed projector

P0=C−cos⁡(2θ)I1−cos⁡(2θ) \boxed{ P_0 =\frac{C-\cos(2\theta)I}{1-\cos(2\theta)} } P0​=1−cos(2θ)C−cos(2θ)I​​

and

P4=I−P0. P_4=I-P_0. P4​=I−P0​.

The explicit chiral-spinor calculation gives

rank⁡P0=2,rank⁡P4=4. \boxed{ \operatorname{rank}P_0=2, \qquad \operatorname{rank}P_4=4. } rankP0​=2,rankP4​=4.​

The normalized antisymmetric part

Jpair=Asin⁡(2θ) \boxed{ J_{\rm pair} =\frac{A}{\sin(2\theta)} } Jpair​=sin(2θ)A​​

vanishes on the fixed plane and satisfies

Jpair2=−P4. J_{\rm pair}^2=-P_4. Jpair2​=−P4​.

Thus it is an orthogonal complex structure on the active four-dimensional real subspace.

Now restrict the first electron complex structure to that active subspace,

I4=P4J1P4. I_4=P_4J_1P_4. I4​=P4​J1​P4​.

The exact identities are

I42=−P4,Jpair2=−P4, I_4^2=-P_4, \qquad J_{\rm pair}^2=-P_4, I42​=−P4​,Jpair2​=−P4​,

and

I4Jpair+JpairI4=0. \boxed{ I_4J_{\rm pair}+J_{\rm pair}I_4=0. } I4​Jpair​+Jpair​I4​=0.​

Define

K4=I4Jpair. K_4=I_4J_{\rm pair}. K4​=I4​Jpair​.

Then

K42=−P4, K_4^2=-P_4, K42​=−P4​,

and the quaternion relations hold exactly on the active four-space:

I4Jpair=K4,JpairK4=I4,K4I4=Jpair. \boxed{ I_4J_{\rm pair}=K_4, \qquad J_{\rm pair}K_4=I_4, \qquad K_4I_4=J_{\rm pair}. } I4​Jpair​=K4​,Jpair​K4​=I4​,K4​I4​=Jpair​.​

All three operators are skew-adjoint orthogonal complex structures.

Therefore

Erot(4) carries a canonical quaternionic structure generated by the pair (J1,J2). \boxed{ E_{\rm rot}^{(4)}\text{ carries a canonical quaternionic structure generated by the pair }(J_1,J_2). } Erot(4)​ carries a canonical quaternionic structure generated by the pair (J1​,J2​).​

The commutant of two independent quaternion generators on this real four-space has real dimension four, as expected for the opposite quaternionic action. This confirms that the active space is naturally one quaternionic line rather than a canonically ordered pair of real two-planes.
