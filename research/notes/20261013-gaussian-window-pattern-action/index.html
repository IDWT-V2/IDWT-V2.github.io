Gaussian Window Patterns with Global Classical Field Evolution

Updated October 13, 2026

A Gaussian-window continuation of the flat-space count action combines exact localized stationary profiles with a globally regular classical field equation and asymptotic separation of distant patterns. The additional common centroid cap is essential to the regularity estimate. Call this action GC-R1. It differs from both the global-moment parent and the compact-window finite-range action; their stronger stationary, localization or finite-range properties do not transfer automatically.

The physical coordinates are ordinary coordinates in shared flat R10\mathbb R^{10}R10. Depth classifies a pattern's extracted spatial widths, not a separate physical sector. The complex scalar notation represents two classical field quadratures. Squared amplitude, mode coefficients and spatial integrals are not a Born position law. The internal profile remains distinct from the deterministic center trajectory of an electron in six ordinary spatial dimensions and from time-averaged detector registration. Interactions below arise from one field's action, without mediator particles.
