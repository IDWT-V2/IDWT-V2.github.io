The linked carrier's induced geometric response

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The positive collective kinetic coefficient used in the inverse source model must have an origin in the same field. It does not follow merely from allowing the occupied carrier wave to respond. That wave's exact frequency-dependent response can be computed with all its signed modes retained, and its dominant low-frequency time-derivative coefficient has the opposite sign.

This is a calculation of a forced wave response. It is not negative particle inertia. The complete positive-frequency particle and its sustaining motion still require their joint energy and momentum.

Consequence for source reconstruction

Eliminating this occupied wave does not generate the positive, band-independent geometric inertia assumed in the collective completion. It supplies (5), with the dominant contribution (6). A positive complete geometric kinetic term would therefore need the sustaining modes' own contribution, or a reconstruction retaining the full response instead of assigning this coefficient an independent positive value.

This conclusion applies to the retained linear geometric deformation and the full finite carrier. The full normal complement, the physical kinetic law of the supporting texture, higher derivatives of the deformed spatial operator and any additional moving-basis terms have not been included in that claim. They must be computed before identifying a complete geometric source or a physical particle's inertia.
