82.5 Status of the exchange route

The mathematics is exact, but the physical premise must not be smuggled in. The labels OOO and HHH are not automatically interchangeable merely because they have equal dimension.

The scattering-frame functional

∥O^−QH^∥F2 \|\widehat O-Q\widehat H\|_F^2 ∥O−QH∥F2​

is reciprocal under exchange accompanied by

O↔H,Q↔QT, O\leftrightarrow H, \qquad Q\leftrightarrow Q^T, O↔H,Q↔QT,

which makes exchange symmetry a plausible property of the lock interaction. What has not yet been proved is that the stationary Hamiltonian circle acting on the electron spinor must inherit that reciprocity.

Thus the corrected status is

beam SO(2) leaves q free,O↔H reciprocity would force q=1. \boxed{ \text{beam }SO(2)\text{ leaves }q\text{ free,} \qquad O\leftrightarrow H\text{ reciprocity would force }q=1. } beam SO(2) leaves q free,O↔H reciprocity would force q=1.​
