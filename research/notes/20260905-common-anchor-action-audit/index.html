5. Interim verdict

The naive rank-one source-to-self-energy mechanism does not derive the common-anchor law when attached directly to the scalar coefficient already written in V2. With the explicit self-contact ordering it produces Case B.

Case A remains possible only if the deeper projected master dynamics supplies additional structure, for example:

  1. a rank-one equation acting directly on occupied reference frequencies;
  2. an independently derived two-sided reference-count weighting before contact projection;
  3. sector-dependent projected overlap or kinetic factors that cancel the reference counts exactly; or
  4. a nonlinear fixed-point equation in which the reference-band factors enter before the final ratio is formed.

None of those mechanisms has yet been found in the inspected source passages.

The identity

g342=g33g44 g_{34}^2=g_{33}g_{44} g342​=g33​g44​

is therefore insufficient by itself. It establishes rank one for a coupling-coefficient matrix, but does not determine the operator layer on which that matrix acts. Since D⋆vD_\star vD⋆​v is not proportional to vvv, the simplex projection and the proposed rank-one image do not commute in the way required by the anchor law.

The remaining problem is an operator-ordering and normalization problem, not an unknown-amplitude problem.

12. Kinetic-normalization audit: no present rescue of the primitive rank-one anchor

The remaining obvious place for a compensating factor was canonical or kinetic normalization. The current V2 mass module does not provide one.

Its operator ordering is explicit:

  1. the intrinsic Dirac/Laplace operator constructs the mode eigenspace;
  2. the localized contact operator maps the rank of that eigenspace to the rest-energy eigenvalue;
  3. the sector coefficient is μd=κdRdBd, \mu_d=\kappa_dR_dB_d, μd​=κd​Rd​Bd​, and is independent of the band label under the ordinary mass theorem.

No reference-band wavefunction renormalization or kinetic factor proportional to

A separate gauge/polarization module does say that propagation laws and kinetic normalizations of the projected gauge connection remain to be obtained from the master action. The first attempted action-level derivation has therefore failed in a precise way.
