Research / Mass and normalization

A canonical mode-field realization of the collective source

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The collective geometric source has an exact realization in a single canonical coefficient field. The realization specifies the interaction through mode averages. This assumption makes its coherent family invariant and keeps all the additional oscillator modes under control at linear order.

The oscillator indices describe candidate modes of the same field. They are not extra physical dimensions or additional particles. Identifying these modes with the actual spatial carrier remains a separate derivation. In particular, a moment of a whole mode is not automatically a local interaction on the physical manifold.
