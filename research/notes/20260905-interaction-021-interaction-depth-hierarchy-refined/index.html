21. Interaction-depth hierarchy refined

The previous interaction-codimension number

Cint=(b−a)+(c−a) C_{\rm int}=(b-a)+(c-a) Cint​=(b−a)+(c−a)

should not be interpreted as a suppression exponent when the interaction is defined by full marginalization to depth aaa.

A better classification uses two independent labels:

  1. retained geometric depth aaa: how much spatial structure the interaction kernel resolves;
  2. localization rank ℓ\ellℓ: how many relative coordinates remain simultaneously localized after the pullback/pushforward rules are applied.

For a lower-depth carrier-mediated interaction, ℓ\ellℓ can be much smaller than (b−a)+(c−a)(b-a)+(c-a)(b−a)+(c−a) because cylindrical directions carry no independent coincidence condition.

For genuine same-depth full contact of two depth-ddd particles,

ℓ=d \ell=d ℓ=d

at the configuration-space diagonal, or d−1d-1d−1 transverse dimensions in a beam geometry.

For two depth-six electrons in a beam,

ℓ⊥=5, \ell_{\perp}=5, ℓ⊥​=5,

of which ordinary three-dimensional optics controls two, leaving three potentially independent transverse coordinates.

This is the precise origin of the cubic hidden-contact factor in the unlocked model.
