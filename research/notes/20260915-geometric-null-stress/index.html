Null motion and positive inertia of the linked geometry

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The linked spatial geometry admits a positive dynamical completion in which a disturbance can keep oscillating and carry null energy–momentum. The same action gives positive inertia to its supporting structure. No negative kinetic contribution is needed for either result.

This closes the time-to-space normalization within a specified effective geometric action. It does not derive that action from the master field, identify its disturbance with the photon, or solve the occupied massive particle. The coefficients below describe proposed modes of the same field. They are not additional particles or a particle-exchange mechanism. All spatial coordinates are ordinary and uncompactified.
