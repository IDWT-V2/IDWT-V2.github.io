Positive-Frequency Harmonic Density and Occupied-Sector Response

Updated October 9, 2026

The bilinear Hermitian current makes the temporal preparation a physical part of a coupling proposal. A real local field evaluation gives a squared real waveform. A positive-frequency complex evaluation gives its modulus squared. Their fundamental density harmonics agree under equal-mean normalization, but their higher harmonics and conservative responses do not.

This calculation extends the existing complex preparation in Sawtooth Harmonics in Geometric Sector Coupling. It uses the same assigned upper mass-frequency, sector fundamentals, common receiver and unspecified physical amplitude. It does not identify either preparation with a derived particle solution.
