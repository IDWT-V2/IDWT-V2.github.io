The complete second spatial response and its rotation identity

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The first projected polarization source vanishes at the linked radial configuration. Its second derivative does not vanish. It is determined by the moving normal curvature already obtained from the full spatial inverse. This gives another exact relation between the spectral and canonical-response calculations.

The adjacent hopping has additional second derivatives of its texture, connection and mode basis. Keeping those terms is necessary before using a linear matrix model to infer the stability of the actual spatial shape. The calculation uses the declared ordinary unbounded normal plane at depths 3, 4, 5, 6 and 10.

The curvature is a canonical geometric quantity; this relation does not identify it with particle inertia.
