Exact local scalar gap on a round spatial sphere

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: The written local scalar Dirac interaction has an exact nonzero constant-current branch on a conditional round six-dimensional sphere, with complete spatial spectral stability but no derived simplex enhancement or particle mass identification.

A self-consistent local coefficient with a complete spatial solution

The local scalar Dirac interaction admits an exact smooth field with a nonzero constant scalar current on a round six-dimensional sphere. It uses the ordinary spinor bundle and includes both opposite spatial Dirac modes. There is no additional contact kernel in this construction. A harmonic decomposition of its full linearization proves the absence of exponentially growing spatial eigenmodes.

This is a conditional field-theory result. The round sphere is an assumed actual physical spatial geometry with undetermined radius RRR; its six directions are ordinary macroscopic spatial directions, without compactification. The calculation does not select that geometry, derive its projection from the master manifold, or identify the solution with an observed particle.

The scalar coefficient depends on the canonical norm, physical volume and radius. No simplex multiplier or occupied mass-band index follows. The field extends over the whole assumed sphere; localization of a particle and nonlinear orbital stability remain separate questions.
