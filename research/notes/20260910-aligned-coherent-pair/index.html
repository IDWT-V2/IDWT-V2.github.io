Stability does not imply capture into alignment

The conserved rotation moment is S=Nρa+MρbS=N\rho_a+M\rho_bS=Nρa​+Mρb​. A pair of nonparallel coherent rays has a rank-two SSS, whereas an exactly aligned coherent pair has rank one. The former cannot evolve into the latter under these equations while preserving all its moments.

The deformation calculation shows how contact can nevertheless increase: the patterns can depart from the coherent subset and retain their conserved moments in shape modes. An energy extremum alone does not justify an alignment or relaxation law. Physical time scales require the actual kinetic normalization, and a physical capture process requires its own allowed outgoing modes.
