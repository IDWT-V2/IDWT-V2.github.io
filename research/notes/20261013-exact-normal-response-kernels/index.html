Exact Normal Response Kernels and Their Spectral Gaps

Updated October 17, 2026

The useful positive result is an exact localized solution of a geometrically defined finite-width same-field response. It supplies a canonical normal operator, its complete reduced energy, actual bound levels, and responding translation and phase zero modes. Its curvature/depth ratio is sqrt(2/3), while its frozen bound-level spacing/depth is 1/2. A separate exact local-power family can attain the historical ratio 2, but selecting that ratio fixes an otherwise unexplained golden-ratio nonlinearity and produces only one frozen bound level. Thus achieving the curvature ratio does not supply the proposed normal gap. A third, ordinary warped spatial geometry gives exact normal localization at depths 4, 5, 6 and 10, but its tangential modes change the normal well: geometric localization alone is not band independence.
