Physical contact on the tangent-mode band

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: The complete holomorphic tangent-mode band has an exact polarization-dependent physical point-contact spectrum, whose largest eigenvalue differs from the auxiliary simplex count.

Project the actual spatial sections

The coherent spatial mode-bundle construction puts the tangent-mode carrier and the spatial Dirac field on the same curved geometry. Its natural spatial evaluation map can now be computed, including the full holomorphic coefficient space and its kinetic norm.

The fixed-line spatial spectral band is a distinct construction: it adds nonholomorphic spatial harmonics on one line bundle. That band has a different evaluation spectrum and an exact simplex dimension up to a constant. Matching a dimension does not identify it with the tangent-mode bundle considered here.

Use the specified physical geometry Q=CPmQ=\mathrm{CP}^mQ=CPm, with m=3m=3m=3 for six ordinary macroscopic spatial directions. The radius RRR remains undetermined, and no dimensions are compactified. Formulas at other m≥2m\ge2m≥2 are conditional geometric comparisons, not particle identifications.

Verification and consequence for the master projection

tangent_band_contact_checks.py checks complete characters and ranks, the full cohomology algorithm, the independent Euler index, Serre duality, every point eigenvalue and the explicit extremizer integrals.

A separate spatial calculation constructs all 15 holomorphic tangent vector fields on CP3 from traceless matrices. Direct integration normalizes their Gram matrix and gives three point eigenvalues equal to 555. Their 120 symmetric products have an integrated Gram matrix of rank 848484. Using its inverse on that support gives six physical point eigenvalues equal to 141414, in agreement with (13) at N=2N=2N=2. The calculation retains the product relations and uses two independent spatial cubature orders. Counting all 120 generators as orthonormal sections would give the wrong operator.

The natural cumulative inclusions preserve the existing degree blocks and their connections, as required by consistent band contacts. This spatial projection is also distinct from the flat oscillator point form, whose spatial profiles and metric are different.

The calculation derives the evaluation operator once the specified spatial point form and kinetic bundle are adopted. A smooth local scalar current still does not itself impose a point insertion. Deriving the master field's actual interaction, projection and canonical normalization remains necessary before the auxiliary simplex eigenvalue can be identified with a physical mass.
