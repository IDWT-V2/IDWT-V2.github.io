Retarded and static meanings

The stable spectral poles in (12) use the retarded boundary value z=ω+i0z=\omega+i0z=ω+i0. Their two wave factors are the same ones in the uniform rest response. The numerator is a local differential operator, so the response preserves the causal support of those wave propagators. The first-order Dirac system fixes the compatible initial data; rewriting (12) as a fourth-order scalar equation does not add independent initial data. Both response channels are modes of the original field, not mediator particles.

Verification

scalar_plane_response.py implements the complete Fourier symbol and exact responses. scalar_plane_response_checks.py runs 2,594 checks across the linked plane and kinetic-null calculations. For (6)–(21) it checks arbitrary complex rest polarizations in spatial dimensions two, three, five, six and ten, diverse momenta, the full nonlinear source, current and spin boost.

Direct full-spinor resolvents and determinant ratios check the feedback derivation independently of its scalar formula. Both field and momentum are rotated together to test physical rotation covariance. The exact zero-mode vectors are checked separately from the nonzero spectrum. Independently solved nonlinear stationary equations with positive and negative probes verify (20) at fixed norm. The approach to (21) has quadratic error in MMM away from its poles.
