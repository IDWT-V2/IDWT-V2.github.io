Independent correctness audit

Updated September 8, 2026

Summary: Independent recurrence refinement exposes false numerical acceptance, while scope corrections separate valid conditional geometry and conservation tests from physical identification.

Verdict and scope

Not everything was correct. There are valid conditional constructions and reproducible diagnostics, but the periodic-continuation acceptance and several physical-inference statements exceeded their evidence. This audit corrects those statements rather than counting earlier successful script exits as proof of the full theory.

The audit inspected the core module statements and recent orbit, response, geometry, mass-operator, and conservation calculations. The initial sweep ran 57 existing diagnostic scripts, excluding the two expensive continuation searches, and all exited successfully. Independent checks then reintegrated saved continuation candidates and found the failure below. The mass-spectrum program also reproduces its stated fixed-index depth sequence. These are finite checks, not an end-to-end independent proof of every theorem in the historical archive. Earlier research notes not individually rederived remain historical records with their existing correction links.

After the corrections, a fresh suite run completed 58 diagnostic scripts, including the new independent audit, with no failing exit status. The saved suite report contains every script's output, exit status, timing, interpreter version, and the two explicit continuation-search exclusions. The independent audit performed 84 checks and classified all three archived candidates as not accepted. The mass-spectrum program, knowledge-base validator (163 notes), Python syntax checks, Markdown display checks, and the visualization's two inline JavaScript blocks also passed their respective checks. Source checks are not browser rendering or mathematical proof.

  2. The fully cylindrical centre had a removable implementation singularity.   3. Projection does not establish a smeared physical electron. The delta-distribution calculation and experimental distinction are in Electron detection and shape. An additional normalization mistake is corrected in Bound within, free without: a uniform probability density normalized in a fibre box of volume VVV gives an overlap proportional to 1/V1/V1/V, not an automatically volume-independent coupling. A cylindrical field and a normalized uniform wavefunction are different objects; the action must specify which is intended.

  4. A frozen-route ligand minimum is not full molecular stability. The positive ligand Hessians are meaningful for the specified frozen route, fixed centre, and fixed planes. What survives and what comes next

The exact representation algebra, specified analytic orbit constructions, fixed-source chart, and checked conservative equations remain useful. They are positive mathematical results for defined models. They are not a completed derivation of the physical electron, chemistry, or a unique CP3 geometry. Known unstable and rejected branches must remain in the record.

The immediate numerical task is accurate correction and independent closure testing of the fixed-Casimir cylindrical candidates. After that come coupled moving-nucleus recurrence and full perturbation stability. In parallel, the physical identification requires a common action and measurement law tying together the spatial electron, source variables, interaction, and detections. Neither chain can substitute for the other.

Reproduction

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -B experiments/independent_audit_checks.py
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -B experiments/reciprocal_cylindrical_molecule_checks.py
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 -B experiments/run_checks.py --skip-continuation --jobs 3 --report experiments/results/audit-2026-09-07/reproduction-suite.json
python3 -B experiments/idwt-v2.py
python3 -B scripts/validate-kb.py

The independent script consumes archived states without re-fitting them and regenerates its report. Its successful diagnostic checks and the candidates' computed acceptance fields are separate: passing the audit does not mean the orbit candidates passed. Integration-failure negative controls reject failed, partial, and nonfinite trajectories. Full long-run continuation has not been rerun as part of this independent saved-state test.
