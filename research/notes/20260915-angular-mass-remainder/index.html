Research / Mass and normalization

A finite-scale bound for the complete spatial mass branch

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The bounded completion permits more than a formal leading-order statement. Its angular structure and complementary gap give an explicit bound on the remaining mass-frequency correction, including the infinite radial complement.

The theorem below is an operator estimate. Its numerical evaluation uses independently refined radial coefficients, rather than rigorous interval enclosures of those coefficients. The large scale separations used as examples establish a sufficient mathematical regime; they are not selected physical lengths or particle-specific adjustments.

The full complement here is the infinite normal plane at a fixed chosen surface eigenvalue β\betaβ, within the maintained separated carrier. The theorem does not establish that separation in a complete curved physical sector or control interactions with every other surface harmonic.
