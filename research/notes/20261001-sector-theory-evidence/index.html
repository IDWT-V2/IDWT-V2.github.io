Evidence for the Sector Structure of IDWT

Updated October 4, 2026

Abstract

Infinite Dimensional Wave Theory assigns particle families to different depths in one nested spatial manifold. This paper assembles the surviving support for those assignments. The empirical part concerns a simplex mass-frequency formula, shared sector scales, small residuals relative to neighboring count steps, and comparisons with alternative depths at fixed indices. The geometric part concerns explicit spatial spinors, a five-dimensional handed neutral channel, six-dimensional spin geometry, photon polarization, and a common projective construction at depths six and ten. The latter now supplies exact count polynomials and available spin-one-half representations under one specified connection and observer action. These are conditional mathematical results; the physical occupation and selection mechanism remains incomplete. The paper identifies which results share a derivation, distinguishes evaluation from the historical construction of the scale prescription, and states what remains necessary for a common particle law. No combined statistical significance is claimed.

1. Meaning of a Sector and of Evidence

The spatial depth ddd counts ordinary spatial dimensions of the same kind as the familiar three. The sectors form one nested chain in an infinite-dimensional physical manifold. A six-dimensional electron sector can lie within a ten-dimensional tau sector without an electron being a constituent of a tau. Particle patterns remain distinct, and their orientations need not define a universal fixed grid.

Mass is expressed in equivalent frequency units,

ωm=mc2ℏ. \omega_m=\frac{mc^2}{\hbar}. ωm​=ℏmc2​.

It is not identified with field amplitude. Overall amplitudes are left unspecified where they cancel; genuine nonlinear amplitude dependence must still be retained when it affects a solution.

Two kinds of support are presented:

  • Empirical relationships: calculations compared with measured masses or mass-squared splittings, under specified indices and scale assumptions.
  • Conditional geometric support: exact constructions compatible with those same depths and relevant particle properties. Their geometric, kinetic and observational premises are part of the result.

Agreement within a chosen construction is a reason to investigate it. It does not establish that nature realizes the construction, that the assignments are unique under every alternative search, or that its several consequences are statistically independent.
