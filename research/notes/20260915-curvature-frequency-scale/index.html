The remaining lengths and coupling in the frequency unit

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The curvature-moment rule removes an independent spatial function. It does not fix the remaining dimensional scales. Restoring their units makes the missing physical input explicit and gives a useful constraint on the charged-lepton comparison.

Mass is frequency in equivalent units throughout. This calculation asks which physical geometry and coupling produce that frequency; it does not rederive the conversion mc2=ℏωmc^2=\hbar\omegamc2=ℏω.

Restore the lengths already present in the carrier

A common radius, width and coefficient fail the six/ten comparison
