Research / Mass and normalization

A local homogeneous current that closes the spatial mass equation

Updated September 16, 2026

conditional calculation

A local interaction can preserve the selected spatial frequency without requiring the field density to be spatially constant. The construction below uses the direction of a same-field current, while retaining its complete variation. It supplies exact standing waves of a positive spatial action at every maintained depth.

This is an inverse construction. The proposed ordinary spatial geometry and its retained component chain come from the whole-depth carrier. They have not been derived from the original scalar-current master interaction. The construction does not yet select a supporting radius, the physical coupling scales, the required shared six/ten frequency unit, or a complete particle response to motion.

One local current and one generating functional
