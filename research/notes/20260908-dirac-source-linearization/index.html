Spatial coupling between mode families

Let fa(x)f_a(x)fa​(x) be photon-sector test modes and uλ(x)u_\lambda(x)uλ​(x) target variation modes, normalized by the action's kinetic metric. The ordinary and conjugate matrix elements of the interaction derivative are

Interchanging target and photon mode labels gives Hermitian reciprocity for KKK and transpose symmetry for CCC:

For the second equality, use BNT=BNB_N^T=B_NBNT​=BN​ and transpose the scalar pairing. In the doubled mode space these are precisely the symmetries of the real quadratic energy. They do not require treating the conjugate variation as an independent particle.
