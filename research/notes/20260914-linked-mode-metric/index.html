Exact metric identities for the linked spatial deformations

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The bounded normal carrier's geometric deformations have a simple metric once changes of component basis are removed. Its radial equations imply an exact identity between that metric and the second change of the geometric profile. This supplies the normalization needed to compare shape responses without confusing a small coordinate coefficient with a small physical vertex.

The metric is a stated positive kinetic comparison for the spatial coefficients. Its physical coefficient and origin in the one master field remain to be derived. It is not a calculation of the complete particle's inertia. The normal coordinates are ordinary unbounded spatial coordinates.
