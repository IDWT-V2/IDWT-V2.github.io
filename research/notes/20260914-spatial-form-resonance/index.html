Reciprocal evolution within degenerate frequency families

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The complete source calculation identifies resonant forcing of spatial patterns outside the initial two modes. The appropriate leading weak interaction equation therefore retains each entire degenerate family in the stated spatial symmetry class. It follows by averaging the declared quartic action over the relative particle oscillation phase.

This is an interaction calculation on overlapping spherical features. The feature, common gap and endpoint interaction are inverse assumptions. It does not describe separated trajectories or derive their sustaining geometry. The endpoint bilinear is also distinct from the kinetic norm flux, as derived in The kinetic current and the chosen endpoint interaction.

Average the full action, including both objects' currents

Write the field at leading order as

Ψ(t,x)=e−iωatA(s,x)+e−iωbtB(s,x),s=gt.(1) \Psi(t,x)=e^{-i\omega_a t}A(s,x)+e^{-i\omega_b t}B(s,x), \qquad s=g t. \tag{1} Ψ(t,x)=e−iωa​tA(s,x)+e−iωb​tB(s,x),s=gt.(1)

Here A and B are arbitrary complex vectors in their respective positive parent-frequency eigenspaces. The unit-radius notation absorbs the spatial units of g. Each eigenspace includes both signed B polarizations; neither is a particle-antiparticle assignment. The amplitudes describe the evolving spatial pattern and canonical norm. Mass is still the selected particle frequency, not any one of these amplitudes.

Using the equal-endpoint bilinear, define

jA=∑eAe†γAe,jB=∑eBe†γBe,z=∑eAe†γBe.(2) j_A=\sum_eA_e^\dagger\gamma A_e,\quad j_B=\sum_eB_e^\dagger\gamma B_e,\quad z=\sum_eA_e^\dagger\gamma B_e. \tag{2} jA​=e∑​Ae†​γAe​,jB​=e∑​Be†​γBe​,z=e∑​Ae†​γBe​.(2)

The full current is j_A+j_B+exp[-i(omega_b-omega_a)t]z plus its complex conjugate interference term. Integrating its square over one relative phase gives the exact averaged energy

Uav=12∫∣jA+jB∣2dA+∫∣z∣2dA.(3) \boxed{\mathcal U_{\rm av} =\frac12\int|j_A+j_B|^2dA+\int|z|^2dA.} \tag{3} Uav​=21​∫∣jA​+jB​∣2dA+∫∣z∣2dA.​(3)

The currents of A and B need not vanish during the evolution. Let P_a and P_b be the full orthogonal spectral projections onto the retained frequency families. Varying (3) gives both equations:

i∂sA=Pa[(jA+jB)⋅γA+z∗⋅γB],i∂sB=Pb[(jA+jB)⋅γB+z⋅γA].(4) \begin{aligned} i\partial_s A&=P_a\big[(j_A+j_B)\cdot\gamma A +z^*\cdot\gamma B\big],\\ i\partial_s B&=P_b\big[(j_A+j_B)\cdot\gamma B +z\cdot\gamma A\big]. \end{aligned} \tag{4} i∂s​Ai∂s​B​=Pa​[(jA​+jB​)⋅γA+z∗⋅γB],=Pb​[(jA​+jB​)⋅γB+z⋅γA].​(4)

This is a Hamiltonian evolution. Independent phase symmetry in each family conserves Q_A and Q_B separately, while (3) and the common rotation momentum are conserved. It need not decrease an overlap monotonically. Energy can move between the individual currents and their mutual interference, so coupling can weaken and subsequently strengthen without an imposed alignment rule.

For the O(m-1)-invariant basic spatial class, a degree L family contains q=-L,-L+2,...,L and both Hodge signs. This gives 2(L+1) complex canonical coefficients. The depth-six degree-14/36 pair therefore has 104 coefficients, with every allowed relative phase and both signed form polarizations. They are evaluated as actual spatial forms using the normalized Jacobi basis, not as an isotropic density trace.

What makes this an approximation

Equation (3) is an exact phase average of the declared interaction. Using (4) as dynamics is a weak-interaction reduction. Off-resonant frequencies remain in the complete parent and have the explicit first response in Complete form response to the current interaction. Their forcing divided by their detuning must be small for the average to be adequate. A large intermediate gap alone is insufficient, because additional low form families exist.

No nonzero cross-frequency resonance was found in the initial cubic source at the assigned controls, beyond the two complete original frequency families. This finite source calculation is not a nonlinear stability theorem or a classification of every disturbance. The evolving family approximation and the full unrestricted spatial equation remain different levels of calculation.

The degree-two pattern remains a mathematical control.
