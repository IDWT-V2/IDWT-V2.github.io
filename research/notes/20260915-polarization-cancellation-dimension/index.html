Research / Mass and normalization

Generalizing polarization cancellation and testing its inertial meaning

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The depth-two result supplies an exact cancellation of coherent shape forces. It motivates a stronger test: can phase or polarization also remove the collective inertia, and does that possibility disappear at higher physical depths?

Two calculations separate the relevant effects. Extending the available polarization channels permits force cancellation at higher depths without changing the frequencies. The complete positive quadratic energy of the retained occupied modes nevertheless remains positive and phase independent. Thus the current model supplies a polarization compatibility rule, but does not yet turn that rule into cancellation of inertial mass.

The subsequent actual normal-mode classification finds no independent repeated factor in the existing normal chain. Its geometric modes have different rotation weights. The complete linked stress instead supplies a null traveling disturbance above a background with positive inertia, without cancelling positive kinetic contributions.

The two-channel threshold is not automatically a dimensional law

The actual two-block calculation has two isospectral chiral sectors with different source matrices. At depth two, placing its two occupied frequency bands in opposite blocks removes their beat term. With three or more bands and resolved distinct beat frequencies, the exact endpoint determinant prevents all pair sources from vanishing.

What a physical cancellation mechanism must additionally provide

The current construction needs a physical polarization module, rather than an assumed channel count. It also needs the complete same-field stress and kinetic normalization. A viable masslessness mechanism must give null total energy–momentum for the photon while preserving its physical motion, and retain the intended nonzero mass-frequencies in the other sectors. The formal depth-two zero is still lifted by an allowed shape deformation, so its protection is a separate unresolved test.

The shared and distinct photon-mechanism possibilities remain open. Neither the force-cancellation threshold nor the positive-energy calculation alone settles the complete physical theory.
