Exact Spatial Disturbances Beyond the Inverse Count Pair

Updated October 12, 2026

The inverse count action gives exact spatial fields and conserved energy with the supplied frequencies. Its retained pair persists under its own nonlinear dynamics. The broader spatial calculation below shows why that persistence cannot be transferred to arbitrary disturbances: a different, exactly reducing spatial harmonic has growing oscillations. This is a dynamical constraint on the fitted action, without requiring ignorance of measured masses.

The calculation retains the conditional ordinary sphere, its radius and connection, both Dirac signs, and the stipulated balanced matrix carrier. It supplies no replacement for literal deterministic electron trajectories. It does not identify this carrier or its unstable ground branches with an observed particle, nor derive a decay channel or lifetime.

Assigned Excited Shells Still Need Convergence

All fourteen maintained assignments have been projected into the broader rotational family using one epsilon per depth and no amplitude fitting. The explicit diagnostic prescription is epsilon_d=0.3/max P_d'(h_assigned), with the maximum over maintained assignments in that depth. It probes radii while keeping all frequencies of the same fitted constitutive construction; it is not a derived radius law. One run uses cutoff l+10 and another l+24 with finer quadrature.

Both projections show growing candidates, but several maxima move substantially. For the electron they move from approximately 8.217 to 19.785 in inverse-radius units. For the tau they move from 14.521 to 31.801. These are unconverged finite-projection diagnostics, not certified growth rates or electron-instability theorems. Increasing the basis can expose additional channels as well as change a previously leading root. The exact ground blocks do not have this truncation qualification.

A separately labeled comparison replaces n by rho. That changes the action and removes the vector-current Hessian; it is not permission to freeze a derivative of the original action. Its finite projections reduce many growth candidates, but do not establish persistence of the assigned excited states. The comparison selects a preferred time direction and does not supply a Lorentz-invariant replacement by itself.

Reproduction and Scope

inverse-current-radial.py checks exact radial differential identities, actual full Clifford projections, Gram normalization, the stationary equation, both complete real Hessians, and the old pair's invariance. On the ground shell its quadrature-projected matrices agree with the independently derived exact blocks (7). Its dated reports retain separate cutoff and action controls.

inverse-current-ground-proof.py derives the rational characteristic polynomials, discriminants and Sturm certificates without numerical eigenvalue signs. Independent normalized matrices agree with their coefficients.

inverse-current-radial-variation.py tests independent nonlinear action and source variations with arbitrary complex matrix coefficients at 120-digit precision. Its 112 evaluations cover every maintained assignment, two spatial points and both complete actions. Action and Hessian discrepancies are below 3.4e-65 and 6.8e-66, respectively. Exact reports retain the executed source hashes and errors.

The new result narrows a concrete inverse mechanism: fitting the clocks and proving pair persistence do not establish spatial persistence. The exact ground instability does not exclude IDWT's count law or its sector assignments. It does not establish an observed decay, a localized particle or a preference for the proposed depths. No new supporting claim for the sector-case document follows from this calculation alone.

The matrix scalar-current continuation adds a Lorentz-invariant polarization response while retaining every balanced count background. Exact first-harmonic inequalities show that its natural positive blends do not remove growth at the two stated ground preparations.

The unrestricted spatial repair analysis settles the real blend and its even-depth invariant extension. A distinct shell-preserving constitutive construction retains the exact clocks while changing the pair law between shells; its full persistence result requires the stated preferred-time density action.
