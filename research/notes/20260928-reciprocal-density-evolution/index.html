Reciprocal Contact With Deforming Spatial Patterns

Updated September 28, 2026

conditional calculation

The scale-covariant contact action admits an exact two-grade invariant subsystem. Evolving it tests whether reciprocal contact survives when both patterns can deform. These are separate same-depth experiments in ordinary spatial dimensions three and five; they do not model a cross-depth encounter.

Thus weak integrated contact can coexist with fast initial local deformation. This Taylor-curvature timescale is neither a decay time nor an eigenfrequency. The short-time approximation is nonuniform at large count. Thirty-six initial-integral cases cover depths 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 10 with heavy counts 10, 35 and 126 at separations 2 and 8. At count 126 and separation 8, leading asymptotes differ from direct integrals by less than 0.27%. That comparison does not establish spatial stability; the inherited depth-six profile remains unbound.

What This Establishes

The declared action gives nonzero reciprocal momentum transfer while both field patterns change shape. A fixed-overlap interaction misses a substantial part of that response. The calculations do not establish completed scattering, nonlinear stability, polarization acceptance or an observed coupling rate. Exterior graph components remain zero in the invariant preparation; general pair perturbations have not been evolved.

The separate conserved charges and the continuous isolated-profile family remain. Energy exchange alone does not choose their values or a unique mass-frequency. Radiation might change a localized remnant while preserving total charge in its grade, but no long-time selection result is established here. The count-selection analysis explains why that remains a distinct requirement.

Reproducing the Calculation

Download the solver text, frozen profile inputs and run histories and comparison records. Save the solver as reciprocal-density-evolve.py beside reciprocal-density-inputs.json. It requires Python, NumPy and SciPy. For example:

OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 reciprocal-density-evolve.py --d 3 --h .04 --dt .002 --end 2 --R 2 --zmax 12 --rmax 10 --cross 1 --tag reproduced-d3

Use --h .08 for the coarse control, --d 5 for the depth-five pair, and --cross 0 for isolated controls retaining the self interaction. The time control uses --dt .001; larger coarse domains use --zmax 16 --rmax 14. Run cases sequentially on a memory-limited machine.

This publication edition replaces binary-profile loading and local paths with portable JSON inputs. The evolution and diagnostic formulas are unchanged. Recorded hashes distinguish the executed original solver from this packaging revision; the archived numerical results are not represented as fresh executions of the revised file.
