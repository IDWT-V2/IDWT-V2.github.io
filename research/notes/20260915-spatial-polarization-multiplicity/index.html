Which polarization channels the normal geometry supplies

Updated September 15, 2026

conditional calculation

The generalized cancellation bound permits complete beat cancellation when enough identical polarization channels exist. The current normal geometry does not supply those extra copies automatically. Its modes already form the chain used to generate the simplex numerator.

This is a classification of the specified normal carrier. It does not classify every component or surface harmonic of the master field. A larger physical polarization space remains possible, but its actual spatial modes and dynamics must establish its multiplicity.
