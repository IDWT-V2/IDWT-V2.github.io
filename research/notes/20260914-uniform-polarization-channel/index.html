A spatial instability of the uniform polarization source

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The positive scalar-current square has a growing channel even when its normal field occupies a complete isotropic spinor multiplet. The result holds for every positive current coefficient and both positive occupations. Adding nonnegative constant Dirac gaps moves the unstable momentum interval without removing it. This is a statement about a uniform source on ordinary noncompact flat space. It does not classify localized sources or a derived discrete spatial spectrum.
