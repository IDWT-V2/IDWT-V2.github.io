Sector geometry premise audit

Updated September 10, 2026

Physical premise (IDWT): There is one infinite-dimensional physical manifold. All sectors form one nested chain of submanifolds within it. Particle depth counts ordinary, literal macroscopic spatial dimensions. “Observed” and “unobserved” describe an observer's access to those dimensions, not different kinds of physical dimension. See spatial meaning and mathematical scope.

Summary: The sphere and projective sector assignments occur in V1 and remain conditional candidates in V2; spatial depth, topology, phase rules, and interaction dynamics require separate justification.

Fixed physical meaning

All sectors are nested submanifolds of the one infinite-dimensional physical manifold. This is a fixed IDWT premise. Every sector direction is macroscopic and spatial. In particular, CP3 denotes the electron's six-dimensional physical submanifold, not an internal state space. Three are shared with us, but that makes none of the six different in kind. Similarly, the electron's six dimensions are six of the tau's ten; the electron is lower-dimensional, or “flat,” relative to the tau. Neither comparison gives the containing particle an inherent physical split.

The three-dimensional nuclear sector is the proposed basis of ordinary matter's three-dimensional experience. A particular observer-adapted frame calculation does not establish that general claim. The earlier attribution of three-dimensional experience to nuclear 3+3 locking is withdrawn.

An incompatible coordinate construction, candidate topology, or effective model must be corrected to respect this premise. It cannot be rescued by reinterpreting a sector as an independent internal space or by abandoning nesting. The audit below distinguishes the intended physical meaning from what the written mathematics actually establishes.

Nesting is a geometric requirement

The named candidate list in the polarization module does not establish its required smooth inclusions. There is a concrete obstruction in that list. Suppose an embedding of CP2 into S5 existed. Both are oriented, so its real normal line would be trivial. Restricting the sphere tangent bundle would give

i∗TS5=TCP2⊕1. i^*TS^5=T\mathbb{CP}^2\oplus\mathbf1. i∗TS5=TCP2⊕1.

The sphere tangent bundle is stably trivial, hence its second Stiefel–Whitney class vanishes. The Whitney sum formula would then require the same for the CP2 tangent bundle. But the projective-space calculation gives

w2(TCP2)=c1(TCP2) mod 2=3H mod 2≠0. w_2(T\mathbb{CP}^2)=c_1(T\mathbb{CP}^2)\bmod2=3H\bmod2\ne0. w2​(TCP2)=c1​(TCP2)mod2=3Hmod2=0.

Here HHH is the hyperplane class. This contradiction rules out that smooth nesting of the proposed CP2 and S5 assignments. The characteristic-class tools are given in Ralph Cohen, The Topology of Fiber Bundles, chapter 3. The conclusion here is an application of those tools to the candidate list.

Therefore that list needs a geometry correction; the requirement that all sectors are nested remains fixed. This audit does not choose a replacement topology or a different electron depth. The Hopf map from S5 onto CP2 is a quotient and does not supply the missing inclusion.

More generally, a nested inclusion does not automatically provide a global product, retraction, or marginalization map. Contact calculations using local tangent–normal coordinates retain that conditional domain until a compatible global construction is supplied. Compact topology also supplies no metric length by itself and gives no reason to call a spatial direction microscopic.

Provenance found, physical derivation not established

The listed depth geometries remain conditional candidates. The historical forces document, section 0.8b, already lists CP1, S3, CP2, S5, CP3, and CP5 at depths 2,3,4,5,6,10. This establishes their presence in V1, not a complete edit-by-edit copying history and not the correctness of the accompanying force claims.

Section 12 of the V2 polarization module gives an inverse reconstruction conditional on:

  1. a smallest complex amplitude carrier;
  2. fixed normalization;
  3. a chosen physical or redundant status of common phase;
  4. transitive unitary action; and
  5. an already prescribed real spatial dimension.

Within that ansatz, retaining phase gives S(2r−1), and quotienting it gives CP(r−1). The dimension arithmetic is valid. It does not show that a particle's actual configuration is this amplitude space, that its norm is constrained in this way, or that its common phase has the assumed physical status. The written reconstruction does not derive these premises from the master interaction or establish that alternative geometries are impossible.

Three questions must remain separate

Question What would support it What does not settle it
Spatial depth d Independent kinetic derivative directions in a derived sector operator Number of display controls or complex coefficients
Geometry of Q_d A derived metric, constraints, identifications, and global structure Real dimension alone or a convenient homogeneous-space construction
Interaction and observed orbit Derived source, dynamics, physical projection/current and perturbation response A chosen generator with a trajectory-shaped readout

The particle-depth module already identifies depth with the number of local spatial derivative directions. Even if that count were established, it would not select a unique global topology or metric. Its projected-symbol theorem itself requires a sector embedding, projector, and compatibility assumptions; writing a d-direction operator after assuming the sector is not a measurement or a derivation selecting d.

The local symmetric-mode count is likewise conditional on independent quadratic directions and the specified mode construction. It is not a unique signature of CP3, S5, or another global manifold.

Consequences for the current orbit investigation

  • CP3 precession and the finite response model are valid conditional constructions on a chosen six-real-dimensional geometry. They do not establish that geometry for the electron.
  • The earlier SO(4)/U(2) comparison varied carrier dimension, not V2's proposed particle-base dimension. Its minimum-carrier theorem cannot establish a minimum-six particle depth.
  • The exact three-amplitude restriction on candidate CP2 and S5 reproduces the prepared ideal demo with the restricted original readout. It is a counterexample to uniqueness inside these candidate constructions, not evidence that CP2 or S5 is the correct lower-depth geometry.
  • A response-control rank calculated in the assumed CP3 model is not yet an empirical dimension witness. Source state, readout, phase sensitivity, and memory must be accounted for before interpreting it as spatial rank.

The five-depth reminder is therefore not resolved by merely substituting S5 into every calculation. Depth five stays in scope even if the S5 assignment is eventually rejected. The same applies to CP3 at depth six.

Scope of the geometric construction

The working mass-formula depth assignments are fixed inputs, including an electron at depth six. Its geometry, local kinetic symbol, source coupling and physical readout require derivation from the proposed master equations. Explicit geometric models provide conditional examples; their conclusions remain distinct from statements that depend only on dimension.

A first target is an interaction that selects the CP3 complex-structure constraint from unconstrained six-dimensional variables, with controlled fluctuations and a justified kinetic reduction. This concerns the geometry at the assigned depth, not a new test of that depth. No new geometry is selected in this audit itself.
