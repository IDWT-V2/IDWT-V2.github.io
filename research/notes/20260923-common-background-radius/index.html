A common background action and its radial stability threshold

Updated September 23, 2026

conditional calculation

The positive-background construction previously canceled the angular force at first order but left the geometric Hessian unspecified. This calculation gives one complete radial inverse action for the maintained finite carrier. It derives the radius force, field--radius mixing and radial curvature from that action, and identifies exactly what remains undetermined for nonuniform geometry. The separate whole-depth conformal-current candidate does specify its metric dependence and has its own calculated two-direction Hessian; the two carriers are not one action and their curvatures cannot be combined.

Why angular stability remains unselected

The force-canceling rule fixes only the first geometric derivative. For an angular coordinate qqq, write a carrier with the same reference operator and first derivative as

H(q)=H0+qC+q22D+O(q3),B(q)=−qv⟨Ψ,CΨ⟩.(7) H(q)=H_0+qC+\frac{q^2}{2}D+O(q^3),\qquad B(q)=-\frac qv\langle\Psi,C\Psi\rangle. \tag{7} H(q)=H0​+qC+2q2​D+O(q3),B(q)=−vq​⟨Ψ,CΨ⟩.(7)

The force and field--geometry mixed block cancel at q=0q=0q=0 for every choice of DDD, while the bare angular curvature is ⟨Ψ,DΨ⟩\langle\Psi,D\Psi\rangle⟨Ψ,DΨ⟩. Changing DDD changes its sign without changing the frequency or first variation at the reference. This proves that the previously established first-order angular force and transparent background do not determine the angular stability calculation. The full conformally transported spatial carrier, background geometry and their second derivatives must be specified. Choosing DDD solely to pass the stability test would be a fit.

The same caution applies to (1): adding a term quadratic in R−R0R-R_0R−R0​ can change (4) while preserving (2). Equation (1) is the minimal linear-in-radius background completion that was actually tested. Its successful radial cancellation does not establish the master-field origin, photon behavior, a common compatible geometry across depths, nonlinear stability or reciprocal particle coupling.

The unified calculation passed 356 conditions at this radial stage. Its radial-stage report contains every maintained mode, two common gap controls and the exact source fingerprints.
