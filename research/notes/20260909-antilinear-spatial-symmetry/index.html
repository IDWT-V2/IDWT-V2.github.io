When a two-frequency spatial symmetry exists

Updated September 9, 2026

Summary: A constant antilinear reduction requires a specific involution of the complete spatial Dirac matrices, and its existence, scalar interference and compatibility with nested field projections can be checked directly.

A special solution family need not be a symmetry of arbitrary fields

The radial two-frequency map constructs full solutions from arbitrary complex radial profiles. Extending that map to arbitrary spatial perturbations requires an additional matrix identity. The number of field components alone does not supply it.

A positive square gives a controlled nonlinear map

When (2)–(3) hold, define

Exact radial and real anisotropic two-frequency families can still exist through their own spatial identities. They do not require a global transformation of every field perturbation.

Include the existing normal direction before using the symmetry

A symmetry of the bound equation must also satisfy BY∗=YBBY^*=YBBY∗=YB to include its normal derivative. In the tested three-dimensional bound representation, the involution instead has BY∗=−YBBY^*=-YBBY∗=−YB. At normal momentum qqq, it therefore obeys

BH−q∗+HqB=2qYB,Hq=Hb+qY.(10) B H_{-q}^*+H_qB=2qYB, \qquad H_q=H_b+qY. \tag{10} BH−q∗​+Hq​B=2qYB,Hq​=Hb​+qY.(10)

Its bound-space reduction fails as soon as that normal propagation is retained. In the tested five-dimensional case, the negative-square partner respects the normal derivative but still fails the arbitrary-field scalar-interference condition. These are distinct requirements, checked separately in the full spatial source.

The occupied polarizations also matter. A background generated by (6) must have Yϕ2=Cϕ1Y\phi_2=C\phi_1Yϕ2​=Cϕ1​, or ϕ2=YCϕ1\phi_2=YC\phi_1ϕ2​=YCϕ1​. That is a specific correlation between the two occupied profiles. It cannot be presumed for independent polarizations.

For the deformed three-dimensional profile in a 2⊂32\subset32⊂3 pair, this correlated preparation cancels all scalar rest sidebands. The full normal response still opens them. The analogous orthogonal preparation in the deformed five-dimensional profile of a 3⊂53\subset53⊂5 pair has nonzero scalar sidebands even at rest. Orthogonality alone therefore does not substitute for the full spatial symmetry calculation. The reciprocal periodic response retains these channels directly.

A containing-space involution need not preserve a projected sector

Suppose a higher representation has C2=IC^2=IC2=I, and PPP selects a lower field copy while retaining its lower spatial Clifford operators and β\betaβ. If CP=PCCP=PCCP=PC, restricting CCC to that copy would give a positive-square solution of (2) there. That is impossible when the selected minimal lower representation requires C2=−IC^2=-IC2=−I.

The containing involution must then leave that selected copy. This can be checked explicitly in the 646464-component representation with ten bound derivatives and one common normal derivative. Commuting rotation projectors on the other containing directions select a lower minimal copy of rank 888 for bound depth five or rank 161616 for bound depth six. For the checked projectors,

PBP∗=0,∥(I−P)BP∗∥F2=rank⁡P.(11) \boxed{PBP^*=0,\qquad \|(I-P)BP^*\|_F^2=\operatorname{rank}P.} \tag{11} PBP∗=0,∥(I−P)BP∗∥F2​=rankP.​(11)

The transformation's full norm goes into complementary field components. Those components still describe the same nested physical manifold. This is a compatibility condition on a symmetry and a chosen field projection, not an obstruction to the nesting of the spatial sectors. The spatial reflection analysis provides related constraints on their polarization multiplicities.

Verification and scope

antilinear_dirac_checks.py constructs (4)–(5), checks every spatial matrix relation, and independently solves the complete complex linear intertwiner equations at the smaller ranks. It checks scalar interference, the real kinetic form, the inverse and number norm on arbitrary complex field vectors.

Complete centered nonlinear source evaluations test the map beyond a stationary ansatz. Independent prepared spatial resolvents test the correlated-polarization sidebands. Explicit containing-space projectors verify (11). Reports are timestamped and checks remain active with Python optimization.

The obstruction concerns constant coordinate-preserving antilinear maps in the specified representations. Additional field multiplicities, spatial reflections, nonlocal transformations and special solution families require their own analysis. The full periodic source equation remains available without assuming this reduction.
