A nonzero same-field background and the joint support bound

Updated September 18, 2026

conditional calculation

The spectrally transparent angular construction requires a nonzero supporting background. This note gives the minimal realization of that requirement and computes its complete quadratic consequence. A retained amplitude can supply the missing metric force while leaving the reference field equation unchanged. Eliminating the amplitude always subtracts a positive Schur term from the geometric stiffness, so force cancellation alone is not stability.

This is an inverse construction. It identifies an admissible local quadratic structure and an exact test for any proposed realization in the one field. It does not derive the background, its potential, or the projected inverse angular operator from Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​.
