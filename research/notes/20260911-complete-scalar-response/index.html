Consequence for physical coupling tests

The classification of scalar-null fields identifies constant linear preparations whose zero source survives every spatial derivative. The full null-plane response separately determines whether perturbations outside an exact zero-source background stay bounded. Its stability threshold shows why invariance alone is insufficient.

The local scalar term admits real source nullspaces at particular fields and exact mismatched traveling solutions. Its nonzero-scalar response is nevertheless full rank before projection, and its null cone is not invariant under arbitrary spatial motion. A protected physical suppression therefore requires the actual occupied solution, its full kinetic evolution and the derived channel map.

These statements do not establish electric neutrality or eliminate weak vertices. The neutrino channel control keeps those physical requirements separate. No special depth-five zero, band-independent mass coefficient or observed rate follows from (5) or (11).
