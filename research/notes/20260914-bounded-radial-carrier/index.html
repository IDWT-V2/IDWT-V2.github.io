Radial freedom in the gapped simplex carrier

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The equally spaced polarization values of the normal Dirac carrier do not require the unbounded coefficient W=z^n. They follow from a radial boundary identity that remains valid for a much larger profile family. This permits a bounded spatial coefficient while preserving the simplex numerator. The profile is a coefficient of the inverse spatial operator; its physical source and total supporting inertia remain open.

Use the four-component ordinary planar Dirac operator defined in the holomorphic carrier, now with W=w(r) exp(i n theta), where w is real, positive away from zero, regular as r^n at zero, and supports decaying zero modes. Write q=n-1. The negative-chiral zero equations are

A smooth bounded profile and a full gap

The complementary response must also change

For (5), the positive-chiral Green inverse is a coupled radial two-component inverse. The monomial carrier's separate scalar Green functions are no longer valid. The complete coupled calculation retains every forced angular sector and both spinor components.

At n=6 and gamma=10000, the refined partial wave momentum response divided by frequency is approximately (265079.42,-265077.76) for the electron branch and (44061.20,-44059.54) for the muon branch. These signed responses belong to a wave in prescribed coefficients. They do not describe negative physical particle inertia. The phase of a partial response and the momentum of the complete sustaining field are separate calculations.
