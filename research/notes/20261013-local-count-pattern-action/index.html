A Local Inverse Action with Exact Gaussian Count Clocks

Updated October 13, 2026

A constitutive energy depending on local spatial derivatives can support a whole family of exact positive localized patterns with the supplied simplex frequencies. Its full variation, rather than a Gaussian ansatz variation, gives the stationary equation. The same energy has a nonnegative spatial Hessian in every smooth compact density direction. A frequency-following current term then supplies exact moving histories and positive complete inertia in all ten spatial directions.

The construction is inverse: the count function, width sequence, sector frequency units and positive restoring penalties are chosen. What follows is their coexistence in one local field equation. The raw infinite family has a restricted derivative domain. A second, globally smooth constitutive potential repairs that domain for a declared finite band and preserves all fourteen maintained frequency controls. These are distinct versions of the action, with different domain and large-width behavior.

The field is classical on ordinary shared R10\mathbb R^{10}R10. Density is squared field amplitude, not a position probability. Depth classifies broad directions of a spatial pattern; it is not a separate physical space. No mediator, compact dimension or particle-exchange mechanism is introduced. An internal profile, its center, and an electron's deterministic trajectory in six ordinary spatial dimensions remain distinct. The Gaussian construction does not itself derive that electron trajectory, observed spin or charge.
