A rank-independent current excess for same-field pair response

Updated September 24, 2026

conditional calculation

The cubic current excess gives a positive prepared-pair angular cost but changes the isolated source when a lower-rank current is simply inserted in a larger matrix. The following polynomial removes that algebraic defect without choosing a rank in its definition. The insertion is a conditional map of representation coefficients; the physical spatial map between nested sectors remains undetermined.

Polynomial and source

Conditional cross-depth insertion

Suppose a rank-sss lower current σPs\sigma P_sσPs​ is zero padded in a rank-rrr parent. Equation (2) gives zero source on the occupied lower subspace for every s≤rs\le rs≤r, unlike the cubic excess. For a nested commuting prepared pair C=ρPr+σPsC=\rho P_r+\sigma P_sC=ρPr​+σPs​, the exact cross term is

K(C)=s(r−s)ρ(ρ+σ)σ2.(4) \boxed{K(C)=s(r-s)\rho(\rho+\sigma)\sigma^2.} \tag{4} K(C)=s(r−s)ρ(ρ+σ)σ2.​(4)

The batch checks the isolated identity and (4) for all fifteen ordered depth pairs. The largest absolute formula error in those double-precision controls is below 2.3×10−132.3\times10^{-13}2.3×10−13. This is an algebraic condition on a specified representation embedding. It is not a derivation of how independent physical particle patterns in nested spatial sectors couple. In particular, (4) begins quadratically in the lower amplitude and has no orientation dependence for rotations entirely inside a filled parent projector. With equal local amplitudes, the rank factor in (4) is 12 for a depth-two rank-2 current inside depth six's rank 8, but 16 for a depth-five rank-4 current there. The rank-only comparison therefore does not yield weak neutrino engagement. Different spatial support, occupied modes or a derived geometric map would have to carry any such suppression.

Frozen partner threshold and physical limit

At a unit-density coherent rank-rrr current, the traceless partner-split Hessian of Tr⁡C+ηK(C)\operatorname{Tr}C+\eta K(C)TrC+ηK(C) matches the earlier trace-current metric with q=2ηrq=2\eta rq=2ηr. At local density ρ\rhoρ, qloc=2ηrρ3q_{\rm loc}=2\eta r\rho^3qloc​=2ηrρ3. Its frozen high-momentum partner block therefore requires

2ηrρ(x)3<1(5) \boxed{2\eta r\rho(x)^3<1} \tag{5} 2ηrρ(x)3<1​(5)

for the bounded real-frequency side of that block. This is a local necessary condition, not a whole-core stability theorem. Complete finite real-linear controls at depths 2, 4 and 6, with one or two oscillator columns, core gaps 0 and 1, and momenta 0.1, 1 and 10, found no resolved imaginary frequency above 9×10−159\times10^{-15}9×10−15 at one quarter and three quarters of the threshold. At 1.25 times the threshold, the largest imaginary frequency was about 0.484666 in particle-frequency units at each tested depth, approaching the analytic high-momentum limit 0.5.

The polynomial is a stronger conditional coupling lead because the same expression preserves isolated occupied sources across projector ranks and gives a nonzero angular pair cost. It is not the physical coupling law or mass operator. The relative strength, the complete cross-depth spatial embedding, permitted polarization states, and encounter dynamics are unselected. The inherited upper-only localized core still has the constrained energetic saddle: its coherent energy is flat under norm-preserving dilation while the lower-spinor mixed source is nonzero. In fact, any correction that vanishes on every coherent current ρP\rho PρP is identically zero along this full upper-only dilation, so it contributes no scale-scale curvature. Its occupied first derivative also vanishes when it is projector-transparent. No strength of (1) can cure that particular energetic saddle without changing the occupied core or another part of the action. A complete same-field law must also supply spatial support and a positive full response in one normalization.
