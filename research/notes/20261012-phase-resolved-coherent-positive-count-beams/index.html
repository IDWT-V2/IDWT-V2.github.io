Relative Phase in Coherent Positive Count Fields

Updated October 12, 2026

The orthogonal-column pair has no relative-phase dependence. Coherent spatial fields restore it without adding another coupling coefficient. This calculation uses the same positive count action and an explicit full matrix carrier, rather than claiming that the minimum one-column fields have been classified. The results are prepared energies, without inferred radial forces or encounter trajectories.

Verification
