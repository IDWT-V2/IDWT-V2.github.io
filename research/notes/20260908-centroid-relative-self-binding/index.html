Centroid-relative self-binding

Updated September 8, 2026

Scope and reason for this addition

Binding a particle's structure about its own centre differs from trapping its centre at an external origin. This distinction fits the V2 spatial premise: an electron has six ordinary macroscopic spatial dimensions, treated alike by its self-binding. Its shared directions with a lower-depth observer acquire no preferred status.

The existing self-consistent functional explicitly leaves a correctly varied centroid prescription open. Here is that prescription for its flat scalar model. The calculation below is a checked completion of this auxiliary model; it is not a recovered derivation of the master Dirac dynamics, a global construction on physical CP3, or the actual Coulomb-plus-precession orbit.
