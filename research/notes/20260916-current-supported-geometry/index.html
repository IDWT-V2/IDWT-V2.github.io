Exact scale balance and the remaining spatial supporting force

Updated September 16, 2026

conditional calculation

The local current action gives an exact oscillating field on the specified spatial geometry. A further calculation is needed if that geometry is to be sustained by the occupied field itself. Its size and its nonuniform deformations must both satisfy the complete variation.

The results here concern stated supporting models. They do not exclude the common characteristic size within a depth, which remains a physical working premise, or require a common size across different depths.

Exact pressure of the finite carrier

Thus the finite carrier interpolates from a radius exponent near one to the leading simplex exponent ddd. This is an exact statement about the whole finite matrix, not an assumption that its leading determinant term is already accurate.

Independent angular stresses constrain local curvature support
