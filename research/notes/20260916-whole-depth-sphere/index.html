What this alternative improves and what it still needs

The common local polynomial now uses all ddd physical dimensions, has the maintained spectral magnitude at odd and even depths, and avoids the zero-dimensional surface at depth two. The inverse causal parent uses fixed geometric shifts and one common gap within each depth.

This does not derive a self-supported particle. The round geometry, the origin of the retained chain, the physical scales, occupied-state selection and complete particle inertia remain open. The periodic-support test also shows why a frequency-preserving mode frame is insufficient without its temporal connection and physical supporting energy. Neither construction has yet derived the observed coupling filters or neutrino suppression.
