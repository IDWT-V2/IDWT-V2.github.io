Radial instability is inherited

Let u0e−iMtu_0e^{-iMt}u0​e−iMt be a radial stationary solution at coefficient g0sg_0sg0​s. A growing perturbation of its radial equation maps through (2) into a perturbation of the full two-frequency field. At n=1n=1n=1, equation (8) preserves its norm at every time. A radial exponential growth rate is therefore preserved by the map, including an oscillating growing mode. The negative-frequency component cannot remove that instability.

This statement is conditional on the radial instability being present. It does not assert that every scalar Dirac source is unstable. The zero-bare-mass moment branches already have such modes. Direct nonlinear evolution of the five- and six-dimensional radial equations confirms their transfer through (2), while both the full number norm and energy remain conserved.

A phase rotation of the stationary background should be removed when comparing a nonlinear perturbation with a tangent mode. If its real linearization has eigenvalue λ\lambdaλ with nonzero imaginary part, the physical radial tangent evolves as Re⁡(eλtv)\operatorname{Re}(e^{\lambda t}v)Re(eλtv) in real coordinates.
