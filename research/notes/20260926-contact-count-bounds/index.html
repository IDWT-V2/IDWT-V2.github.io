Canonical contact normalization and finite combinatorial bounds

Updated September 26, 2026

Counting can constrain a proposed operator without being a derivation of its frequency. This calculation combines canonical normalization with the exact dimension of a space of angular functions. It rules out specific scalar-contact reductions under a shared normal profile, while leaving derivative interactions and dynamically changing normal profiles open.
