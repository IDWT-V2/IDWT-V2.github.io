What Decay Trees Reveal About Particle Energy

Updated October 5, 2026

A particle decay is a measured rearrangement of energy. Following its products through further decays gives a tree whose energy must balance at every split. For IDWT, this offers a practical way to test how an oscillating field pattern becomes other patterns, their motion and radiation.

The first calculation covers 19 selected channels involving muons, taus, W and Z bosons, the Higgs, and pion and kaon controls. It gives absolute kinematic energies and ratios. It does not yet measure the underlying field amplitudes or derive sector stiffness.

Follow One Branch to Its End

For the positively charged sequence, with the corresponding antiparticles and neutrinos,

Alternative branches are alternatives, not simultaneous products. Along the pion branch, the intermediate rest masses cancel:

That is the final total kinetic energy in the original tau rest frame, under the stated neutrino and radiation approximations. It is not obtained by adding daughter energies calculated in different rest frames. A moving intermediate transfers inherited motion to its products. The required boost is

Three Products Require a Spectrum

The Bridge to Stiffness and Amplitude

A specified harmonic field coordinate has

Eosc=12KA2,ω2=KI, E_{\rm osc}=\frac12KA^2,\qquad \omega^2=\frac K{\mathcal I}, Eosc​=21​KA2,ω2=IK​,

where AAA is its amplitude, KKK its restoring stiffness and I\mathcal II its modal inertia. Once KKK and the internal oscillation energy are supplied by a physical mode model, the amplitude follows. Relative amplitudes can be calculated without an absolute field scale when that scale cancels.

The energy tree helps establish which energy is available for that inference. A muon emerging from pion decay and one emerging from kaon decay have very different kinetic energies but the same intrinsic rest-frequency. Recoil energy cannot simply be counted as additional intrinsic muon oscillation.

The next IDWT calculation must supply the spatial shapes and their kinetic and restoring integrals, then reproduce the tree's energy distributions with one interaction law. The tree supplies a concrete empirical ledger for that calculation. Its conservation identities are standard physics; the proposed same-field explanation and stiffness inference remain the research questions.
