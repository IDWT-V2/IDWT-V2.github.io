Relation to projective ray contact

The projective contact calculation uses H0(CPd,O(N))H^0(\mathbb{CP}^d,\mathcal O(N))H0(CPd,O(N)) with a homogeneous unit-volume Fubini–Study measure. Its point contact has constant strength Dd(N)D_d(N)Dd​(N). The point in that construction is a mode ray; the point XXX in (1) is a position in an actual flat spatial chart. An equality of finite dimensions does not identify the two evaluation operators or measures.

There is an elementary vector test. Under the usual normalized homogenization of occupations, the spatial vector at X=0X=0X=0 has a nonzero ground coefficient, zero degree-one coefficients and nonzero degree-two coefficients whenever N≥2N\ge2N≥2.
