A smooth two-current polynomial with conditional mass and reciprocal pair response

Updated September 24, 2026

conditional calculation

At canonical norm one, its quartic energy is half its standing frequency. A nonhomogeneous current function can remove that algebraic discrepancy at a density stationary under support-volume variation. The following construction is an inverse action candidate, not a derivation of the physical particle state or master interaction.
