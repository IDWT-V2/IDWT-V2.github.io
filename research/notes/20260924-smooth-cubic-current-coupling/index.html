A smooth cubic current excess for prepared pair coupling

Updated September 24, 2026

conditional calculation

The scalar-moment law joins a nonzero coherent mass source to a stable prepared-pair alignment, but its degree-one current formula has no differentiable source at full current cancellation. This calculation asks whether a smooth local polynomial can retain those two useful properties. It does not derive the master mass operator or a measured interaction rate.

Why a globally nonnegative smooth correction is flatter

Compare the globally nonnegative quartic current polynomial H4,r=Tr⁡[C2(C−tI)2]H_{4,r}=\operatorname{Tr}[C^2(C-tI)^2]H4,r​=Tr[C2(C−tI)2]. On the same prepared pair, its exact value is

which begins at order θ4\theta^4θ4. More generally, suppose a smooth function of every Hermitian current is nonnegative and zero on every coherent rank-rrr current. Its gradient vanishes there. Its Hessian along a tangent rotation of the coherent projector also vanishes, since its value is identically zero on that coherent manifold. The prepared-pair path has the same first-order tangent at alignment. Its angular excess therefore has no θ2\theta^2θ2 term. The cubic (1) escapes this restricted result because it is nonnegative only on the positive-current cone; a coherent current lies on that cone's boundary in the full Hermitian space.

This is a restriction on an excess that vanishes on all coherent currents. It is not a no-go for every smooth full action or a reason to impose global energy positivity on an indefinite Dirac carrier.

Physical status

The cubic excess is a smooth algebraic candidate for same-depth prepared coupling. It leaves the conditional simplex mass branch intact and supplies a reciprocal alignment cost with one coefficient per current rule. It is not yet the coupling law sought for actual moving particles: η\etaη's physical scale, the cross-depth map, the permitted polarization dynamics, and encounter rates are not derived. The mass branch still uses stipulated shell weights and separate spatial binding. The inherited coherent upper-only core has the constrained energetic-saddle obstruction even though finite frozen frequencies can be real. The master mass operator and common physical normalization therefore remain open.
