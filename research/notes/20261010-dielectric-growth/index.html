Dielectric Growth, Critical Concentration and the Three/Four Guide Window

Updated October 10, 2026

The mixed quadratic/quartic law supports finite-width commuting guides above derived thresholds. Its robustness has a precise boundary. In the active four-dimensional sector, a positive dielectric term growing faster than the sixth power admits smooth fixed-flux configurations whose complete tension tends to zero. A small coefficient does not protect an unrestricted polynomial law at arbitrarily large deformation.

This observation constrains a candidate constitutive action. It does not disprove a small-deformation expansion, and it does not establish an empirical force law. The neutral two-frame area-squared construction supplies a geometrically motivated quartic invariant; its large-deformation completion still needs to be specified.

These bounds alone are not a compactness or existence theorem for the nonlinear completion.

This is a coherent repair to test if higher local powers arise from a reduction of the same field. Its new deformation scale and bounded constitutive function remain explicit assumptions. A polynomial extrapolated outside its valid amplitude range and a global polynomial action are different proposals.

Scope and Verification

The growth analysis sharpens which geometric flux-guide claims are robust. The quartic neutral-frame invariant and its positive quadratic extension occupy a controlled range; an arbitrary positive higher polynomial is not harmless. The same sharp-inequality and scaling mathematics supplies the thresholds, critical escape and completed-growth bounds.

These results retain the independently varied, commuting-flux restriction. They do not remove the vacuum connection ambiguity or the compact full non-Abelian Gauss alternatives. Neither an energetic escape sequence nor an attained scalar profile identifies an observed quark, a physical charge unit or the master-field action.

The checker and report preserve 165 checks across 48 cases: all occupied depths, exact critical integrals, explicit eighth-power concentration at three coefficient sizes, cutoff-bubble convergence, critical capacity convexity, exact crossover, sextic depth-three roots and bounded nonlinear growth. No particle evolution, fitted amplitude or measured force calibration is used.
