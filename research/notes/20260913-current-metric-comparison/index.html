The freedom left by the frequency calculation

The localized current action produces its simplex frequencies on an isotropic partner current carried by a coherent oscillator ray. Its Hilbert–Schmidt current norm is one inverse choice. The frequency calculation does not fix how the action curves away from that ray. That curvature matters for perturbations and coupling.

Reciprocal coupling retains the common-target identity

For two independently solved cores of distinct grades N>MN>MN>M at one depth, let ρ(x),σ(x)\rho(x),\sigma(x)ρ(x),σ(x) be their local partner densities and s=∣z†w∣2s=|z^\dagger w|^2s=∣z†w∣2 their coherent-ray overlap. Their initial root cross energy becomes

It depends on both rays and remains independent of the larger grade. Every shared current and trace is identical when that target grade changes. The complete finite probe source and reciprocal initial translation forces therefore retain the common-target identity. The unchanged binding also retains its nonzero shape response for aligned coincident cores. Subsequent target deformation and encounter trajectories need not be identical.

Equation (21) is a same-depth initial interaction, not an observed gauge vertex or a cross-depth photon–neutrino calculation. The new norm changes its angular dependence while preserving the assigned masses.

Scope and verification

partner_current_metric_checks.py checks arbitrary indefinite and singular-eigenvalue currents, complete potential mixed second variations on the actual smooth core, full oscillator source variations, and the finite interaction of two solved cores. It verifies the complete frozen unitary reduction in depths four and six, the exact characteristic compression, direct matrix exponential gains, and the positive conserved form. The implementation is partner_current_metric.py, used by localized_partner_tower.py.

The higher angular calculation also tests the revised complete Hessian on the localized electron and muon branches. Its finite scans do not prove full spatial nonlinear stability. The physical mode carrier, shell weights, common normalization, core-frequency relation and master origin of the interaction remain open. The separate principal defect of the polarized rest-frame carrier is a kinetic issue and is unchanged by this potential replacement.
