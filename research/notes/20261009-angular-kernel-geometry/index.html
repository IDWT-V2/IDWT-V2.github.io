Angular Mode Kernels Select Shapes and Their Small Oscillations

Updated October 9, 2026

The angular addition theorem and the directed-state construction can be combined into an explicit energy. Its minima determine direction sets, and its second variation determines small-oscillation frequencies. This supplies more than an allowed bond angle: it gives a testable response spectrum under one stated effective interaction.

An observer's angular space does not replace the particle's spatial sector.
