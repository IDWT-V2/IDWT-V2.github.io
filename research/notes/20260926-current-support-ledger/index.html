Research / Mass and normalization

Same-field current support, causal response and complete energy

Updated September 26, 2026

conditional calculation

An interaction can change the time kinetic coefficient of an oscillating field while its supporting components respond reciprocally. A useful local candidate uses a current built polynomially from derivatives of those components. This avoids division by a current that can vanish. It supplies testable equations, but its local characteristic calculations and imposed background wells do not establish a self-supported particle.

Equality is an additional normalization target when a model identifies this particular phase frequency and charge with the complete particle energy. A positive E−ωQE-\omega QE−ωQ tests that identification; by itself it is not a no-go theorem for localized massive states.

The physical conversion ωm=Erest/ℏ=mc2/ℏ\omega_m=E_{\rm rest}/\hbar=mc^2/\hbarωm​=Erest​/ℏ=mc2/ℏ is unchanged. Remaining physical bridge

The constructive result is a reciprocal local current interaction with an explicitly checked causal window, including negative signed binding. It still needs one smooth finite-energy stationary texture and wave, the actual simplex spectrum, the complete principal symbol on that inhomogeneous solution, its relaxed stability, physical normalization, boost inertia and a particle-pair response. The local block does not yet derive a physical mass or coupling operator.

Independent verification checks the characteristic factorization at depths 2,3,4,5,6,10 and the exact negative-pair witness and cofactor bound; the remaining identities above were reviewed by direct variation and homogeneous scaling, without a new PDE run.
