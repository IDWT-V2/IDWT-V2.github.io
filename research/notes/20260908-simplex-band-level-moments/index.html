Physical scope

The coordinates being counted describe ddd equivalent ordinary spatial oscillator directions in an auxiliary flat model. Equal weighting of all states in the cumulative band is explicit model data. A single occupied shell, a coherent state, and a nonuniform mixture generally have different moments.

Applying these moments to a physical energy correction requires the perturbing operator and the relevant physical state. The counting mixture is a mathematical benchmark for those calculations.

The mode counting checks compare these expressions with direct rational shell sums and explicit weak-composition enumeration.
