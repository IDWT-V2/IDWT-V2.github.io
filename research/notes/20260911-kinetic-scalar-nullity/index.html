Spatially invariant scalar-null fields

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: The minimal massless spatial Dirac module admits complete kinetic scalar-null classes at every odd spatial depth, while even depths have no such constant linear class; this parity result does not select a particle or a physical charge.

State the dynamical question

The complete source derivative distinguishes a zero background scalar from a zero response to every perturbation. A further question is whether spatial motion preserves a proposed family of zero-source fields.

Use one minimal irreducible complex module of their full Clifford algebra, of dimension n=2⌊(d+1)/2⌋n=2^{\lfloor(d+1)/2\rfloor}n=2⌊(d+1)/2⌋. Components of this module are field amplitudes over ordinary space. The calculation introduces neither compactification nor a choice of global physical topology.

Completeness and the even-depth result

The usual complex Clifford classification states that an even number of generators gives one full matrix algebra, while an odd number gives two simple blocks. See the complex case in Etingof's Mathematical Ideas and Notions of Quantum Field Theory, section 11.3. Here its application concerns the kinetic generators alone, inside the larger module that also contains β\betaβ.

Propagation through containing spatial directions

The dimension ddd in (1) counts all derivatives retained in that equation. Applying it to a lower particle while omitting its permitted normal propagation is a further restriction. Use the containing Clifford module when that propagation is present, as in normal Dirac propagation.

For an odd set of lower directions, form CdC_dCd​ from their αj\alpha_jαj​. Every additional normal kinetic matrix αa\alpha_aαa​ anticommutes with it:

More generally, a smooth ambient field constrained to the fixed lower class for all times would have to satisfy DNΨ=0D_N\Psi=0DN​Ψ=0. This concerns the stated smooth ambient extension and fixed projector. It does not exclude a field defined on a lower spatial submanifold, other confinement dynamics, or a complete containing-space symmetry.

In particular, an odd-dimensional containing space has its own kinetic involution. That complete class can preserve scalar nullity while the lower class mixes. A full positive-frequency plane also has zero scalar at every spatial depth. Lower-class mixing alone is therefore not a proof of nonzero scalar interaction. The positive-frequency vertex calculation tests both the complete angular current and these containing-direction qualifications.

Physical scope

This is a parity result; depths three, five, seven and nine share it. It does not single out the depth-five assignment. Within either class the written scalar term supplies no self-binding, so the theorem does not construct a localized particle.

Exact invariance also differs from stability against perturbations outside the class. The complete null-plane response has a density-dependent instability even though its background scalar vanishes identically.

The operator CCC anticommutes with β\betaβ. It therefore fails the general scalar-potential charge condition in conserved spatial charge. Its sign has not been identified with electric charge or an observed weak representation. The neutrino control still requires electric neutrality and nonzero weak vertices. The two signs are not assigned particle and antiparticle meanings.

This kinetic involution also differs from the even-bound-depth Ξ=βΓb\Xi=\beta\Gamma_bΞ=βΓb​ in collective translation waves, which commutes with β\betaβ, and from the rest Spin(6) polarization in the massive current projection. They solve different spatial representation problems.
