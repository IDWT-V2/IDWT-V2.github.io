Retained Normal Rotation Structure and Compatible Transport

Updated October 10, 2026

A retained orthogonal complex structure supplies a precise unitary normal carrier and a positive local orientation energy. It penalizes the complement of U(q), while leaving SU(q) and its central U1 untouched. It neither selects three/four alone nor resolves the exact-zero dielectric vacuum ambiguity. For the actual induced connection, arbitrary real bending need not admit a parallel continuation of the original J. In particular the reviewed depth-four electric wave fails that continuation already at first order. A projected J-preserving metric connection is explicit, but adopting it changes the ordinary induced normal transport.

This is a bounded geometric comparison, not a solved graph evolution or a particle state. All normal directions below are ordinary spatial directions in the selected ten-dimensional parent. Their endomorphisms and orientation coordinates do not add physical dimensions. Global existence of J on an arbitrary normal bundle is itself a reduction premise; the comparison is local.
