Updated September 8, 2026

Boundary of the result

This remains a prescribed-route diagnostic with a fixed central nucleus and fixed nuclear support planes. The electron route has not been solved or relaxed as a function of the ligand coordinates, so this is not a derived Born--Oppenheimer energy surface. It establishes a local ligand minimum only for that restricted energy. It does not establish recurrence or Floquet stability of the reciprocal system, where the electron route, angular source, nuclear motion, and plane rotors all respond together. Conversely, the regular-tetrahedron correction should not be erased by calling the calculation incomplete: within the stated approximation, the transverse force is nonzero and the stable minimum is the reported disphenoid.
