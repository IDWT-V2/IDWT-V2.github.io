74. Orbit generator as a polynomial of the total locked axial spin

The corrected section 72 expressed the traceless orbit generator in the product-spin basis as

G~=14σz⊗I+14I⊗σz+34σz⊗σz. \widetilde G =\frac14\sigma_z\otimes I +\frac14I\otimes\sigma_z +\frac34\sigma_z\otimes\sigma_z. G=41​σz​⊗I+41​I⊗σz​+43​σz​⊗σz​.

This initially made the coefficient 3/43/43/4 look like an independent two-frame bilinear that might have to be related to 3g663g_{66}3g66​. There is a cleaner exact formulation.

Define the total axial generator of the diagonally locked spin frames,

Jz=12(σz⊗I+I⊗σz). \boxed{ J_z =\frac12\left(\sigma_z\otimes I+I\otimes\sigma_z\right). } Jz​=21​(σz​⊗I+I⊗σz​).​

Since

Jz2=12(I+σz⊗σz), J_z^2 =\frac12\left(I+\sigma_z\otimes\sigma_z\right), Jz2​=21​(I+σz​⊗σz​),

the orbit generator is exactly

G~=−34I+12Jz+32Jz2. \boxed{ \widetilde G =-\frac34I +\frac12J_z +\frac32J_z^2. } G=−43​I+21​Jz​+23​Jz2​.​

The scalar −3I/4-3I/4−3I/4 is projectively invisible, so the projective generator is equivalently

G~proj∼12Jz+32Jz2. \boxed{ \widetilde G_{\rm proj} \sim \frac12J_z+\frac32J_z^2. } Gproj​∼21​Jz​+23​Jz2​.​

74.1 Diagonal-spin basis

Under the locked subgroup,

(2,2)↓Spin(3)diag=3⊕1. (\mathbf2,\mathbf2) \downarrow_{Spin(3)_{\rm diag}} =\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf1. (2,2)↓Spin(3)diag​​=3⊕1.

Use the standard coupled basis

∣1,1⟩,∣1,0⟩,∣1,−1⟩,∣0,0⟩. |1,1\rangle, \quad |1,0\rangle, \quad |1,-1\rangle, \quad |0,0\rangle. ∣1,1⟩,∣1,0⟩,∣1,−1⟩,∣0,0⟩.

Then

Jz=diag⁡(1,0,−1,0), J_z =\operatorname{diag}(1,0,-1,0), Jz​=diag(1,0,−1,0),

while the orbit generator becomes

G~=diag⁡(54,−34,14,−34) \boxed{ \widetilde G =\operatorname{diag} \left( \frac54, -\frac34, \frac14, -\frac34 \right) } G=diag(45​,−43​,41​,−43​)​

up to the ordering of the two zero-mmm states.

The two m=0m=0m=0 states — the triplet ∣1,0⟩|1,0\rangle∣1,0⟩ and singlet ∣0,0⟩|0,0\rangle∣0,0⟩ — have exactly the same orbit-generator eigenvalue. Therefore the generator does not need the total-spin Casimir J2J^2J2 to distinguish the 3\mathbf33 and 1\mathbf11 sectors.

Indeed, solving

G~=aI+bJz+cJz2+dJ2 \widetilde G =aI+bJ_z+cJ_z^2+dJ^2 G=aI+bJz​+cJz2​+dJ2

forces

d=0. \boxed{d=0.} d=0.​

So the orbit generator depends only on the axial quantum number mmm, not separately on whether the state lies in the singlet or triplet representation.

74.2 Why this is stronger than the 3g663g_{66}3g66​ interpretation

The coefficient 3/43/43/4 in the product basis is not an invariantly separate physical coupling. It is partly a consequence of rewriting the quadratic axial operator Jz2J_z^2Jz2​ back into the uncoupled tensor-product basis.

Therefore the relation

34=3g66 \frac34=3g_{66} 43​=3g66​

must not be interpreted as evidence that three copies of the depth-six coupling are dynamically summed into an axial bilinear. Section 73 already showed that an actual isotropic three-axis sum produces the Heisenberg exchange operator instead.

The more invariant statement is

G~proj∼12Jz+32Jz2. \boxed{ \widetilde G_{\rm proj} \sim \frac12J_z+\frac32J_z^2. } Gproj​∼21​Jz​+23​Jz2​.​

This shifts the proof obligation away from deriving an isolated coefficient 3g663g_{66}3g66​ and toward deriving a linear-plus-quadratic function of the total locked axial generator.

74.4 What remains unexplained

This reformulation removes a misleading candidate coupling identification, but it does not yet derive the polynomial coefficients

12,32. \boxed{ \frac12,\qquad\frac32. } 21​,23​.​

The existing amplitude/clock uniqueness theorem fixes an equivalent generator once its five premises are assumed. What has not yet been shown is that the scattering-selected locked electron must realize precisely that polynomial of JzJ_zJz​.

The next useful test is therefore whether primitive projective closure, time-reversal/orientation reversal, and the required unit clock gap uniquely determine the lowest-degree polynomial

f(Jz)=a+bJz+cJz2 f(J_z)=a+bJ_z+cJ_z^2 f(Jz​)=a+bJz​+cJz2​

on the four locked spin states. If those physical constraints force b:c=1:3b:c=1:3b:c=1:3, the factor three will have been derived from the locked-spin spectrum without invoking g66g_{66}g66​ at all.
