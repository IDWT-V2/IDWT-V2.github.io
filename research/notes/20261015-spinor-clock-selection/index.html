Real Spinor Clocks, Depth Selection and the Common-Field Test

Updated October 17, 2026

The dimension-property comparison has a constructive dynamical extension. The non-real spinor types are precisely the minimal real spin modules that admit a nondegenerate rotation-invariant alternating kinetic form. Such a form generates internal classical oscillation. Within dimensions one through ten, it exists at exactly two, three, four, five, six and ten.

This is a conditional mechanism, not an established particle selector. Restriction of one common ten-dimensional spin carrier supplies repeated modules and restores the missing forms at seven, eight and nine. A successful selection mechanism therefore needs a dynamical reason for an isolated minimal module, not just the Bott-periodic table. Neither representation reality nor this kinetic criterion identifies antiparticles.

All matrices below act on real field components. Positions remain ordinary coordinates in shared flat spatial space. No complex spatial dimensions, mediator particles or position probabilities enter. Spatial-spin carriers are hypotheses about field transformation, not derived observed particle spin.

The dynamical construction and common-carrier test below are additional calculations.
