5. What the Sign Does and Does Not Establish

Equation (14) identifies actual accessible selected-shell currents and their signed leading background response. In particular, the electron and muon have negative directions in this constrained quadratic response. A negative direction alone is not an exponentially growing mode, a decay channel or a negative complete particle inertia. The orientation tangent itself remains a symmetry direction, and the symplectic pairing of shape and conjugate disturbances must be retained in the evolution.

The two directions in this calculation have not been shown to form an invariant subspace of the full coupled dynamics. Other zero-shell polarizations and nonzero-frequency particle/background modes remain necessary for a persistence result. Higher-order excited background self-feedback also mixes harmonics through its variable density. The known mass targets and inferred indices are preserved throughout.
