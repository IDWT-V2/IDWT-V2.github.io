Homogeneous actions with coherent simplex masses

Updated September 12, 2026

Summary: A specified family of local homogeneous actions gives exact coherent simplex frequencies, canonical energies and full spatial linear stability, while its normalization, master-field carrier and physical selection remain assumptions.

A constructive inverse result

There is an action-level route to the coherent mass multiplier that does not require a rank-one mass endomorphism. Start with the existing quartic coherent-current integral, take its square root, and supply the explicit product-rank normalization. The resulting nonlinear Dirac action has exact nonconstant coherent solutions on the specified physical spatial geometry. Their frequency and energy per canonical norm are both μDN\mu D_NμDN​.

This is an inverse construction: the action is specified to meet the mass condition, and its consequences are then derived. The original master interaction has not been shown to produce either this functional or its canonical mode-bundle field. Those are substantive remaining assumptions.
