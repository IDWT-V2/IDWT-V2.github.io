Why the first combination failed

The completed petal Hamiltonian was originally written about a fixed nuclear origin. Adding moving nuclei without changing that source makes its magnetic completion depend on the absolute electron coordinate and destroys visible translation invariance. A failed momentum-derivative check exposed this before the model was used for shooting.
