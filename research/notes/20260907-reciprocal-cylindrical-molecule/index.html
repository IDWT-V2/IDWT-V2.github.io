Reciprocal cylindrical molecule

Updated September 8, 2026

Physical premise (IDWT): There is one infinite-dimensional physical manifold. All sectors form one nested chain of submanifolds within it. Particle depth counts literal macroscopic spatial dimensions. The electron's CP3 sector has six; nuclear 3+3 locking constrains ordinary matter and observers to an experienced three-dimensional sector. Unobserved spatial directions remain physical. See spatial meaning and mathematical scope.

Summary: A 6D electron, relative angular source, moving finite cylindrical nuclei, and flexible nuclear-plane rotors form one translation-invariant reciprocal Hamiltonian with verified energy and Noether-charge conservation.

Why the first combination failed

The completed petal Hamiltonian was originally written about a fixed nuclear origin. Adding moving nuclei without changing that source makes its magnetic completion depend on the absolute electron coordinate and destroys visible translation invariance. A failed momentum-derivative check exposed this before the model was used for shooting.

The correction is to define the source coupling using the electron--central- nucleus relative coordinate

s=q−(R0,0). s=q-(R_0,0). s=q−(R0​,0).

The completed kinetic term is then

He=12me∣p−meλCs∣2+⟨C,C⟩2IC. H_e=\frac1{2m_e}|p-m_e\lambda Cs|^2 +\frac{\langle C,C\rangle}{2I_C}. He​=2me​1​∣p−me​λCs∣2+2IC​⟨C,C⟩​.

The central nucleus receives the opposite visible component of the source force. It receives no unobserved translational momentum because it remains a depth-three object.

Remaining periodic-orbit problem

This calculation establishes the system to shoot; it does not claim that the arbitrary nonlinear example is molecularly periodic. The next solve should start from the exact trigonal orbit and the restoring finite-profile radius, fix centre-of-mass and global-rotation gauge directions, and solve a relative-periodic boundary condition. The monodromy calculation must retain electron, source, nuclear-centre, and plane-rotor perturbations. Stable radial force balance alone is not sufficient.

The uniform-ball nuclear profile and dimensionless masses, charges, and inertias remain diagnostic inputs. A successful branch would establish existence within that controlled model, not yet derive those inputs from V2.
