Induced Normal Color Geometry on One Nested Spatial Flag

Updated October 10, 2026

A normal connection can be calculated from the bending of actual spatial sectors, rather than introduced as independent matrices. One common nested construction produces a full rank-three unitary normal connection at depth four. Its traceless part is the eight-generator SU3 algebra. Pulling that connection back to depth three retains all eight generators. This is a concrete geometric realization of the proposed color carrier, with the odd-sector pullback and the choice of embedding stated explicitly.

The result does not establish a physical strong force. Higher normal derivatives can generate larger non-Abelian algebras at depth two, and replacing an independently varied connection with an induced one changes the field equations. Those controls identify what a physical sector-selection law still needs to establish.

Physical Scope and Reproduction

The common construction supplies an actual spatial U3 carrier and a conditional four-base/six-normal quadratic dimension criterion. It keeps every occupied depth in one nested geometry and makes the additional three-sector pullback visible. To turn it into a color mechanism, a common action must select the geometric class, determine its dynamics and show how the surviving connection couples to the same field.

In particular, restricting variations to induced connections removes independent A0A_0A0​ variation. The earlier source constraint DiDi=ρ\mathcal D_iD_i=\rhoDi​Di​=ρ and its exact-zero consequences must be derived anew; they cannot be carried over from the independently varied action. Canonical Orientation Transport Is an Explicit Alternative

The canonical orientation connection is determined by a normal rotation structure relative to a fixed flat reference. This connection is generally different from the induced graph transport above.
