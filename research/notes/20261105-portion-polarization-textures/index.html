Common-Fundamental Portions and Rich Spatial Polarization

Updated November 5, 2026

conditional research

A field pattern can carry much more polarization information than a single rotation plane. Its component directions, relative phases, handedness and variation with position all matter. The same count of portions can therefore support different spatial textures without introducing portions that resonate at separate physical frequencies.

The constructions below describe one field on infinitely many ordinary spatial dimensions plus time. The displayed finite-dimensional coordinates describe the matter domain, involving up to ten spatial directions. Complex notation records real oscillation quadratures; it does not introduce a separate physical space. The electron's centre trajectory remains distinct from its internal texture.

This is an exact construction of vertex data, rather than continuous motion along polygon edges. A continuous physical profile could smooth the staircase, but its smoothing law must be stated. Its Fourier coefficients would acquire the smoothing kernel's Fourier factor. A several-plane staircase similarly samples a toroidal figure; continuity and a knot interpretation require further construction.

The portion schedule has CCC reset slots per period. Reading each portion's reset additively retains the proposed count rate C/TC/TC/T. A particular vector observable can suppress a reset or combine unresolved ones, so its shortest period is a separate question. Uneven phase offsets also preserve that mean event count when every portion still contributes one registered event per fundamental cycle. This statement concerns the stipulated event readout, not a claim that every vector signal oscillates at C/TC/TC/T.

3. Polarization Freedom and Spatial Circulation

A nonzero complex NNN-component amplitude has 2N−22N-22N−2 real shape coordinates after removing overall magnitude and common phase. This is a continuous shape count, not the number of independent basis polarizations. For example, two complex light components give two real normalized-shape coordinates, while three complex components would give four. Comparing either number with a basis-state count without that distinction is inappropriate.

Spinor counts must specify their representation. A doubled odd-dimensional module has twice the complex dimension of the minimal odd-dimensional Clifford module. Its commuting plane-sign labels therefore occur with a different multiplicity. Those mathematical conventions cannot silently be interchanged when assigning physical degrees of freedom.

5. Connection to Charge and Contact

For three equally weighted orthogonal planes in the stated aligned preparations, an agreeing-plane fraction is h/3h/3h/3. The existing charge table can be rewritten as the positive agreeing fraction or the negative disagreeing fraction, with the branch parity choosing between them. This is an exact rewrite of that table. Identifying its exterior degree with a count of agreeing physical planes is an additional interpretation to test through the actual reciprocal response.

Finite contact-ended spans also retain phase-dependent residual overlaps that an infinite or full-period average can remove. Thus handedness and shape provide concrete interaction data, while a zero under a special averaging convention is not a universal absence of interaction.

The constructive result is a rich common-fundamental texture language with explicit count, spectrum, current and matching identities. Sustaining a particular texture, selecting its count and deriving its physical force remain dynamical calculations. The sector-structure case and polarization account explain the existing geometric property constructions to which it connects.
