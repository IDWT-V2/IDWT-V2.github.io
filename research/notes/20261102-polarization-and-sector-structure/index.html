Polarization Connections to IDWT Sector Structure

Updated November 3, 2026

conditional research

Polarization can connect the sector results through the geometry of an oscillating pattern. The most useful distinction is between the number of participating portions, which supplies the proposed mass-frequency multiplier, and the directions and relative phases of their motion, which can govern directional response. Those are different properties of the same physical pattern.

This note develops exact algebraic connections and candidate physical interpretations. Claude independently reviewed the initial identities; the subsequent spatial-current calculation was cross-checked by both authors with explicit corrections. The review scope and reproducible sources are recorded below. These results do not establish an observed new particle property. All spatial constructions belong to the same ten ordinary spatial dimensions plus time. Field components, matrices and complex phase notation supply descriptions, not another physical manifold. An electron's centre trajectory remains distinct from its internal field pattern.

The baseline is the particle-geometry case, especially its spin profile, invariant clock, Hodge channel and differential charge constructions. The underlying physical field remains Psi_infinity; no exchange particles are introduced.

1. The Empirical Targets and the Two-Dimensional Photon

Light has two independent transverse polarization components. Their relative magnitude and phase produce linear, circular and elliptical polarization; these are continuous families of preparations, not merely two possible orientations. The usual plane-wave component calculation is summarized by Fitzpatrick.

Electron spin polarization is also measured, for example with the Jefferson Lab Hall A polarimeter. Massive W and Z polarization, including longitudinal and transverse components, is measured through decay distributions; see the ATLAS joint-polarization measurement. These are empirical comparisons. Their conventional exchange-particle interpretations are not IDWT premises.
