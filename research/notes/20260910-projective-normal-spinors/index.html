Normal spinors in projective nesting

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: The standard physical CP3 inclusion in CP5 has an explicit global normal-spinor decomposition and Abelian normal holonomy, with twist-dependent parallel factors that sharpen the six-to-ten coupling test.

The specified spatial embedding

Consider the candidate physical inclusion

CPa↪CPa+q,[Z]⟼[Z,0],(1) \mathbb{CP}^a\hookrightarrow\mathbb{CP}^{a+q},\qquad [Z]\longmapsto[Z,0], \tag{1} CPa↪CPa+q,[Z]⟼[Z,0],(1)

with the same FS metric scale and its induced connections. Here a,qa,qa,q are complex dimensions: the lower spatial depth is 2a2a2a, the higher depth is 2(a+q)2(a+q)2(a+q) and the normal spatial depth is 2q2q2q. The electron–tau comparison is a=3,q=2a=3,q=2a=3,q=2.

These are ordinary spatial submanifolds of the same physical manifold. The split is relative to the chosen lower submanifold, not a division into different kinds of dimensions in the higher particle. No dimension is compactified. The global projective geometries, embedding and metric are explicit hypotheses, rather than consequences of depth alone.

The pointwise branching S10+=(S6+⊗S4+)⊕(S6−⊗S4−)S_{10}^+=(S_6^+\otimes S_4^+)\oplus(S_6^-\otimes S_4^-)S10+​=(S6+​⊗S4+​)⊕(S6−​⊗S4−​) does not yet specify the bundles or their connection. Equation (1) allows those data to be computed.

The complete normal spinor factors

For even qqq, c1(N)=qHc_1(N)=qHc1​(N)=qH is even and the normal bundle is spin. Using the complex structure and its unique spin structure gives

ΣN=Λ∗N⊗(det⁡N)−1/2=⨁k=0qO(k−q/2)⊗ΛkW.(5) \Sigma N=\Lambda^*N\otimes(\det N)^{-1/2} =\bigoplus_{k=0}^{q} \mathcal O(k-q/2)\otimes\Lambda^kW. \tag{5} ΣN=Λ∗N⊗(detN)−1/2=k=0⨁q​O(k−q/2)⊗ΛkW.(5)

Here the Hermitian metric identifies the usual antiholomorphic cotangent description of spinors with Λ∗N\Lambda^*NΛ∗N. Exterior parity specifies chirality. For a=3,q=2a=3,q=2a=3,q=2, the two chiral normal bundles are

It is a splitting along the lower spatial submanifold. It does not split TCP5T\mathbb{CP}^5TCP5 into rank three and rank two over all of CP5, which the global tangent obstruction excludes.

Full normal Clifford derivatives mix that component, and complete form-degree matching constrains a shared construction at both depths.

Global intermediate spatial depths

The normal characteristic obstruction adds a constraint to (2): its real normal Euler class is HqH^qHq. When q≤aq\le aq≤a, the specified inclusion cannot factor through a smooth odd-dimensional intermediate space. For CP3 inside CP5, this excludes intermediate depths seven and nine. A parallel normal spinor doublet is compatible with that obstruction: it is not a frame of ordinary normal vectors. The direct six-to-ten inclusion and its branching remain valid; the missing intermediate embeddings cannot be inferred from them.

What the geometry permits and what it selects

Parallel normal endomorphisms may mix equal holonomy weights. Their complex dimension in (5) is

dim⁡End⁡Hol(ΣN)=∑k=0q(qk)2=(2qq).(11) \dim\operatorname{End}_{\rm Hol}(\Sigma N) =\sum_{k=0}^{q}\binom qk^2=\binom{2q}q. \tag{11} dimEndHol​(ΣN)=k=0∑q​(kq​)2=(q2q​).(11)

For q=2q=2q=2, this is six: two scalar blocks on Σ+N\Sigma^+NΣ+N and the four-dimensional matrix algebra on the parallel doublet Σ−N\Sigma^-NΣ−N. Thus parallel transport alone permits a full two-by-two matrix there; it does not determine that matrix or its eigenvalues.

There is a second symmetry constraint. Normal rotations of WWW are ambient isometries fixing the entire embedded lower subspace. Their SU(2)SU(2)SU(2) action on the parallel doublet is irreducible. An interaction invariant under all those rotations acts as a scalar on the doublet. It cannot choose a unique line or split its two eigenvalues. Selecting a normal pattern can break that symmetry, but then the pattern and its response are additional dynamical data of the same field.

The standard FS normal connection itself remains Abelian. A non-Abelian normal response needs a changed geometry or a derived field-dependent connection; the existence of an SU(2)SU(2)SU(2) frame action does not supply it. The separate gauge-carrier construction makes the same structure-group/holonomy distinction on a different bundle.

Equations (8)–(10) sharpen the charged-lepton test: the proposed pair's twists, form components and normal current must match a global factor. The resulting vertex still needs its spatial matrix element and canonical kinetic normalization. Neither the parallel multiplicity nor the invariant-endomorphism count fixes a mass ratio, an observed weak coupling or neutrino neutrality.
