Five-depth orbit comparison

Updated September 8, 2026

Physical premise (IDWT): There is one infinite-dimensional physical manifold. All sectors form one nested chain of submanifolds within it. Particle depth counts ordinary, literal macroscopic spatial dimensions. “Observed” and “unobserved” describe an observer's access to those dimensions, not different kinds of physical dimension. See spatial meaning and mathematical scope.

Summary: V2's actual S5 and CP2 sectors reproduce the prepared demo through an exact three-amplitude restriction, correcting the earlier carrier-dimension comparison and identifying a conditional perturbation test.

Correction: compare the actual V2 sectors

Geometry qualification: "actual V2 sectors" here means the configuration spaces named in the present documents, not physically established spaces. The user subsequently flagged these assignments as potentially unreliable V1 inheritance. All CP2/S5/CP3 identifications in this note are conditional candidates; neither the trajectory restriction nor the control-rank test derives the correct geometry at any depth.

The user's reminder that there is a depth-five sector exposed a mismatch between two meanings of dimension. The sector construction module specifies Q4=CP2, Q5=S5, and Q6=CP3 as actual configuration manifolds. The particle-depth module defines depth by their real base dimensions and kinetic derivative directions.

The previous dimensional-precession note varied the vector-carrier dimension D in SO(D)/U(D/2). At D=4 that space is a two-real-dimensional sphere CP1, not V2's four-dimensional CP2. The D=6 identification with CP3 has the right base dimension, but this coincidence does not extend to D=4. Odd D being incompatible with J squared equal to minus the identity does not exclude the S5 sector. The previous static obstruction is a valid theorem about its expressly restricted carrier family, not a V2 particle-depth exclusion. The proposed finite-response follow-up on that smaller carrier must not be presented as excluding actual Q4 or Q5 particles.

This correction changes the comparison, not the established CP3 equations. No particle assignment or experimental identification follows from the lower-depth trajectory construction below.

Let b=sqrt(r²+1/L), alpha=r/b, beta=1/(sqrt(L)b), and define the complex linear isometry E from C3 into C4 by

E(v1,v2,v3)=(v1,αv2,βv2,v3),v0=(r,b,1)/D. E(v_1,v_2,v_3)=(v_1,\alpha v_2,\beta v_2,v_3),\qquad v_0=(r,b,1)/\sqrt D. E(v1​,v2​,v3​)=(v1​,αv2​,βv2​,v3​),v0​=(r,b,1)/D​.

Then E dagger E=I3, E v0=Z0, and, with G3=diag(1,−1,0),

G4E=EG3,Eexp⁡(−iθG3)v0=exp⁡(−iθG4)Z0. G_4 E=E G_3,\qquad E\exp(-i\theta G_3)v_0=\exp(-i\theta G_4)Z_0. G4​E=EG3​,Eexp(−iθG3​)v0​=exp(−iθG4​)Z0​.

Thus the normalized vector v(theta) lives on V2's S5, and its ray [v(theta)] lives on V2's CP2. Its image is exactly the previously constructed CP3 trajectory, not merely a similar rose curve. The embedding E is fixed along each trajectory, but depends on L and the chosen A/B preparation. One common fixed E has not been shown to accommodate all preparations or all control settings of the visualization. This is an exact lower-dimensional reproduction of the prepared ideal flow, not a universal compression of CP3.

The existing readout survives unchanged after restriction

Use the existing selected coherent map, not a newly invented circle detector:

fL(Ev)=(v1L,αLv2L,L βv2v3L−1,v3L). f_L(Ev)=\left(v_1^L,\alpha^L v_2^L, \sqrt L\,\beta v_2v_3^{L-1},v_3^L\right). fL​(Ev)=(v1L​,αLv2L​,L​βv2​v3L−1​,v3L​).

Its weights under G3 are still (L,−L,−1,0). It equals the original f_L(Z) point by point, including normalization and the even/odd basis change. The selected coefficient trajectory and all functions of it, including the current petal readout, therefore agree exactly. Four selected monomials do not imply four independent geometric amplitudes.

The extra common phase of v is retained on S5. The current readout is blind to it because f_L(exp(i phi)v)=exp(i L phi)f_L(v), and the displayed radius and clock are phase-invariant. This does not permit discarding that phase from every possible physical coupling of Q5; it only describes this readout.

The unitary equation dot v=−i G3 v defines an autonomous norm-preserving flow on S5 and descends to the Hamiltonian ray flow on CP2. No nondegenerate symplectic form on odd-dimensional S5 is assumed. The sphere flow preserves the natural one-form −i v dagger dv because the evolution is unitary; its phase dynamics are part of the normalized-vector flow.

This is a geometric kinematic competitor. Deriving its generator, nuclear coupling, physical current, spatial spinor equation, and preparation from V2 remains necessary, just as the source-to-demo bridge remains necessary for the six-dimensional construction. It does not transfer the full CP3 reciprocal response equations to CP2 or S5 automatically.

A conditional route to distinguish the full state space

The exact restriction fails when dynamics excite the one complex direction orthogonal to im(E). In the existing response model, perturbations have operators sigma_i tensor sigma_j. For the L=2, A=.6, B=.8 preparation, sigma_z tensor sigma_x has

∥(I−EE†)(σz⊗σx)Z0∥≃0.76344136. \|(I-EE^\dagger)(\sigma_z\otimes\sigma_x)Z_0\| \simeq 0.76344136. ∥(I−EE†)(σz​⊗σx​)Z0​∥≃0.76344136.

A short pulse therefore produces a missing amplitude at first order. The time-independent perturbed generator G4+0.13 sigma_z tensor sigma_x has a four-dimensional complex Krylov span from Z0, unlike the unperturbed three-dimensional span. At this preparation the nine proposed response directions span all six real horizontal CP3 tangent directions.

This gives a sharper task than watching the unperturbed petals: derive accessible source perturbations and a common calibrated readout sensitive to the missing direction, then compare the responses of Q4, Q5, and Q6. CP2 has four real tangent dimensions; S5 has five, with its common phase unresolved by the current readout. A suitable independently calibrated six-direction response could discriminate these simple state-space models.

The calculated control rank is not a measured response rank. Neither the availability of each physical control nor detector observability has been established. Extra source state, nonlinear readout, or memory can complicate dimensional inference. Leaving a fixed embedded CP2 copy alone does not rule out every alternative S5 model or every preparation-dependent map.
