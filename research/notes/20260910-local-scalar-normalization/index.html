Relation to the spatial moment model

The existing scalar Dirac moment branch uses a nonlocal centered quadratic spatial moment. Its interaction scales as λ−4\lambda^{-4}λ−4 under norm-preserving dilation, giving a different virial and a massless frequency proportional to g01/5g_0^{1/5}g01/5​. Equations (3) and (8) instead follow from local scalar contact. The physical kernel determines which normalization law applies.

Canonical coupling normalization must also precede comparison of different retained mode families or spatial sectors. An oscillator dimension count cannot replace these profile and kinetic calculations.

The specified CP3 scalar branch gives a curved spatial comparison with its own physical volume and metric scale. It restores the full radial Dirac components and computes the normalized frequency through cubic order. Its different global hypotheses are explicit; the flat scaling and tail arguments cannot be transferred to it.

The nonlinear spatial reduction eliminates the complementary Dirac modes together with their norm. It derives the effective stationary action, distinguishes its normalized frequency and energy corrections, and gives a source-preservation condition for an exact linear contact branch under explicit positivity hypotheses.
