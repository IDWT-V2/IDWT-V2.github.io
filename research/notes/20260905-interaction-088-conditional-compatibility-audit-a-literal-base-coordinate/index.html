88. Conditional compatibility audit: a literal base-coordinate reading of the published magnetic visualization orbit is not the fixed frame-lock orbit

Section 87 deliberately tested the Hamiltonian family against the scattering-selected locked frame. Before using that result to constrain the published magnetic orbit, one possible reading is to identify the evolving four-component trajectory in the magnetic module directly with the same base homogeneous coordinate used by the frame-lock construction. The source modules do not actually fix the magnetic coordinate basis to the product-spin basis used below, and section 89 shows a deeper base-versus-section distinction. Therefore the calculation in this section is a conditional audit of that literal identification, not a basis-independent contradiction.

Under the additional identification of the displayed four components with the product-spin basis used throughout sections 72--87,

Z=(z1,z2,z3,z4)∈CO2⊗CH2, Z=(z_1,z_2,z_3,z_4) \in\mathbb C^2_O\otimes\mathbb C^2_H, Z=(z1​,z2​,z3​,z4​)∈CO2​⊗CH2​,

a normalized pure state has concurrence

C=2∣z1z4−z2z3∣, \mathcal C=2|z_1z_4-z_2z_3|, C=2∣z1​z4​−z2​z3​∣,

and the frame-lock invariant is

s=1−C2. s=1-\mathcal C^2. s=1−C2.

Thus the maximally locked orbit is

s=0⟺C=1. s=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\mathcal C=1. s=0⟺C=1.

88.1 Test the published orbit

The magnetic module uses

z1=Ae−iθ/4cos⁡(Lθ), z_1=Ae^{-i\theta/4}\cos(L\theta), z1​=Ae−iθ/4cos(Lθ),

z2=−iAe−iθ/4sin⁡(Lθ), z_2=-iAe^{-i\theta/4}\sin(L\theta), z2​=−iAe−iθ/4sin(Lθ),

z3=B2e3iθ/4,z4=B2e−iθ/4, z_3=\frac{B}{\sqrt2}e^{3i\theta/4}, \qquad z_4=\frac{B}{\sqrt2}e^{-i\theta/4}, z3​=2​B​e3iθ/4,z4​=2​B​e−iθ/4,

with

A2+B2=1. A^2+B^2=1. A2+B2=1.

Its two-qubit determinant is

z1z4−z2z3=AB2[e−iθ/2cos⁡(Lθ)+ieiθ/2sin⁡(Lθ)]. z_1z_4-z_2z_3 =\frac{AB}{\sqrt2} \left[ e^{-i\theta/2}\cos(L\theta) +i e^{i\theta/2}\sin(L\theta) \right]. z1​z4​−z2​z3​=2​AB​[e−iθ/2cos(Lθ)+ieiθ/2sin(Lθ)].

At the initial point,

C2(0)=2A2B2. \boxed{\mathcal C^2(0)=2A^2B^2.} C2(0)=2A2B2.​

Therefore no choice of the published normalization parameters places the initial magnetic-orbit state on the maximally locked s=0s=0s=0 frame orbit.

Equivalently,

s(0)≥12. \boxed{s(0)\ge\frac12.} s(0)≥21​.​

88.2 Consequence

This means the phrase ``internal lock'' in the magnetic/orbit construction cannot presently be identified without qualification with the nucleus-induced maximally entangled frame lock of the electron--nucleus module.

The two constructions are using the same C4\mathbb C^4C4 carrier and the same eventual generator, but the displayed magnetic visualization trajectory is not itself a trajectory on the fixed scattering-frame minimum in the product basis used for the frame-lock theorem.

Thus Section 87 is a valid theorem about compatibility of GqG_qGq​ with a fixed selected frame, but it must not be used as though the existing visualization orbit had already been shown to live on that frame-lock submanifold.

88.3 What survives

The following exact statements remain intact:

  1. q≠1q\neq1q=1 contains the relative-frame generator KzK_zKz​;
  2. q=1q=1q=1 is the unique member of the family with no such relative-frame term;
  3. a fixed scattering-selected frame is stationary under the family only at q=1q=1q=1;
  4. the invariant-norm selector also independently chooses q=1q=1q=1.

What fails is the stronger identification

published magnetic visualization orbit=fixed s=0 frame-lock orbit. \text{published magnetic visualization orbit} =\text{fixed }s=0\text{ frame-lock orbit}. published magnetic visualization orbit=fixed s=0 frame-lock orbit.

88.4 Revised interpretation

There are now two logically distinct structures:

frame selection: Qscat and s=0, \boxed{ \text{frame selection: }Q_{\rm scat}\text{ and }s=0, } frame selection: Qscat​ and s=0,​

and

projective orbital trajectory: the A,B,L,θ flow used by the magnetic observer map. \boxed{ \text{projective orbital trajectory: the }A,B,L,\theta\text{ flow used by the magnetic observer map}. } projective orbital trajectory: the A,B,L,θ flow used by the magnetic observer map.​

The missing bridge is an explicit construction showing how the latter is represented relative to the former. It may be that the magnetic orbit describes motion in a fibre or associated representation over a locked background rather than motion of the locking coordinate itself. That possibility is consistent with having a stationary frame lock and a nontrivial electron orbit simultaneously, but it is not yet written as such in V2.

88.5 New proof obligation

The next task is therefore to factor the electron dynamics into

locked background frame×orbital degrees of freedom that preserve that background. \boxed{ \text{locked background frame} \times \text{orbital degrees of freedom that preserve that background}. } locked background frame×orbital degrees of freedom that preserve that background.​

Representation-theoretically, this asks whether the physical orbital generator should belong to the stabilizer/associated algebra of the selected frame while the displayed CP3 coordinates are an observer representation rather than the frame coordinate itself.

If that factorization is derived, Section 87 becomes directly relevant again and q=1q=1q=1 is selected by the requirement that the gross orbit not rotate the locked background frame.

If no such factorization exists and the same CP3 coordinate is simultaneously the frame-lock coordinate and the displayed orbital coordinate, then the current magnetic trajectory and the s=0s=0s=0 binding claim are mutually incompatible and one of the two constructions must be revised.
