Two-frequency spatial Dirac currents

Updated September 9, 2026

Summary: Opposite-frequency components of the same spatial Dirac field give exact current oscillations at twice the bound frequency, with a common scalar source and explicit normalization, size and response constraints.

Construct a spatial clock from the bound field itself

A stationary field's common phase cancels from its bilinear observables. Nevertheless, the scalar Dirac model admits exact fields whose spatial currents oscillate at a frequency fixed by their bound profile. This provides a concrete construction to examine when asking whether slow particle oscillations can weaken coupling.
