What the Geometric Color Construction Currently Establishes

Updated October 13, 2026

The strongest current result is a connected mathematical construction, rather than a color count alone. In a selected ten-dimensional ordinary spatial parent, the four-dimensional carrier has six real normal directions. Their unitary rank-three geometry supplies a triplet, a complete chiral polarization metric, a canonical connection with full U3 holonomy, and a determinant phase line. A separate middle-Hodge prescription activates the proposed curvature energy at the assigned three/four pair. Under an independently varied connection, that action supports finite-width commuting flux guides with complete stress and positive transverse inertia.

These results supply conditional reasons to investigate the sector assignments. They do not yet derive observed color as a complete force law. The distinction matters because adopting a connection determined by geometry changes both its sources and its energy: earlier independent-connection Gauss constraints and fixed-flux solutions cannot simply be reused.

One Ordinary Spatial Geometry, Several Linked Consequences

The finite comparison selects a ten-dimensional parent and even carriers of dimensions 2,4,6,10; odd occupied sectors use a declared neighboring-even pullback. This is an explicit prescription. It does not establish uniqueness among arbitrary containing geometries, nor an orthogonal complex structure on an entire odd-rank actual normal bundle.
