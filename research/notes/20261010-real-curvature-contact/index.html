Real Normal Curvature Cancels Odd Characters and Permits Color Pair Response

Updated October 10, 2026

The ordinary normal spatial connection admits an exact conjugate cancellation. It also supplies a possible spatial response with a color pair sign different from the positive local current contact. These two facts are useful together, but neither proves that higher-depth particles are colorless.

1. Real Rotation Geometry Gives an Exact Cancellation

Write the curvature of the selected metric-compatible six-normal connection as F=dA+A∧AF=dA+A\wedge AF=dA+A∧A. In a real orthonormal frame its matrix coefficients obey FT=−FF^T=-FFT=−F. Exterior products of two-forms have even degree, so reversing their order introduces no exterior minus sign. Therefore

(Fq)T=(−1)qFq,tr⁡RFm=0for odd m. (F^q)^T=(-1)^qF^q,\qquad \boxed{\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbb R}F^m=0\quad\text{for odd }m.} (Fq)T=(−1)qFq,trR​Fm=0for odd m.​

This proof applies to noncommuting curvature coefficients: transposition reverses matrix order, and the complete power includes both orders of its two-form factors.

The realification of a complex normal triplet has complexification 3⊕3‾3\oplus\overline33⊕3. Consequently

tr⁡RFm=2Re⁡tr⁡CFm. \operatorname{tr}_{\mathbb R}F^m =2\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}_{\mathbb C}F^m. trR​Fm=2RetrC​Fm.

Odd characters of a skew-Hermitian curvature are imaginary and cancel between conjugates. Quadratic characters add. This is a precise version of a conjugate-pair cancellation. It is not a singlet in 3⊗33\otimes33⊗3, two particles composing an electron, or an inherent split of the electron's six spatial directions.

The relevant characteristic-form and transgression identities are standard mathematics. Chern and Simons, Characteristic Forms and Geometric Invariants (1974), Propositions 3.2, 3.8 and 4.3, establish their differential, variation and orthogonal-connection properties. The cancellation here concerns differential forms and real characteristic classes; it is not a claim that every possible integral torsion class vanishes.
