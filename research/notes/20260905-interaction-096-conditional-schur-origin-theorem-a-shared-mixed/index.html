96. Conditional Schur-origin theorem: a shared mixed transition channel generates positive sss stiffness and the Procrustes orientation term at second order

Section 94 corrected the overly direct attempt to identify the ordinary Levi--Civita mixed spin connection with the full oriented-lock Hamiltonian. There is, however, a cleaner second-order route that respects the distinction between the electron's base coordinate and its Dirac field.

The electron sector coordinate is

q=[Z]∈Q6=CP3, q=[Z]\in Q_6=\mathbb{CP}^3, q=[Z]∈Q6​=CP3,

and determines the mixed complex-structure block

The cross-depth Dirac block acts on field sections, not directly on ZZZ. Therefore any Schur/Feshbach argument must be interpreted as producing a qqq-dependent spectral energy shift after field degrees of freedom are eliminated. An eigenvector of a field-fibre operator must not be silently identified with the base point qqq.

With that type distinction enforced, the mixed block itself gives an exact invariant target for the qqq-dependence of such a transition.
