Projective nesting and intermediate spatial depths

Updated September 11, 2026

Summary: Normal characteristic classes obstruct odd intermediate depths in standard projective nesting and obstruct a full sequence of one-dimensional depth increments between any embedded CP spaces whose lower complex dimension is at least two.

A partial inclusion is not a complete spatial chain

IDWT requires its physical sectors to be nested submanifolds of one ordinary spatial manifold. The geometry premise audit already distinguishes that requirement from assigning a name to each spatial depth. Here a normal-bundle calculation determines when the standard projective inclusions can contain intermediate spatial depths.

The calculation concerns actual spatial embeddings, not projectors on field coefficients. It assumes the stated global projective topologies. It introduces no compactification or small spatial scale. Changing the metric radius, interaction strength or occupied spinor does not change the characteristic classes used below.

Consider the standard inclusion

CPa↪CPa+q,[Z]⟼[Z,0],a,q≥1.(1) \mathrm{CP}^a\hookrightarrow\mathrm{CP}^{a+q},\qquad [Z]\longmapsto[Z,0],\qquad a,q\ge1. \tag{1} CPa↪CPa+q,[Z]⟼[Z,0],a,q≥1.(1)

Odd intermediate depths are obstructed

Suppose (1) factored through an embedded intermediate spatial submanifold YYY of dimension 2a+r2a+r2a+r, where 0<r<2q0<r<2q0<r<2q:

CPa↪Y2a+r↪CPa+q.(4) \mathrm{CP}^a\hookrightarrow Y^{2a+r} \hookrightarrow\mathrm{CP}^{a+q}. \tag{4} CPa↪Y2a+r↪CPa+q.(4)

e(NR)=e(A)e(B)=0.(6) e(N_{\mathbb R})=e(A)e(B)=0. \tag{6} e(NR​)=e(A)e(B)=0.(6)

This contradicts (3) whenever q≤aq\le aq≤a. Consequently

q≤a⟹no odd-dimensional intermediate Y in (4).(7) \boxed{q\le a\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{no odd-dimensional intermediate }Y\text{ in (4)}.} \tag{7} q≤a⟹no odd-dimensional intermediate Y in (4).​(7)

The Euler product and odd-rank facts follow from Thom classes; see Cohen, The Topology of Fiber Bundles, Corollary 3.12 and Proposition 3.13. Equations (5)–(7) apply those standard results to the actual spatial inclusion. The intermediate space need not itself be projective or have a prescribed metric.

For codimension two, this has a simple geometric meaning. A one-dimensional intermediate normal direction would be a real line subbundle of O(1)R\mathcal O(1)_{\mathbb R}O(1)R​. Over the simply connected lower base that line would be trivial and would give a nowhere-zero normal vector. Its nonzero Euler class prevents such a vector. A normal direction exists in every coordinate chart, but these directions cannot be chosen consistently over the entire lower space.

Every one-dimensional increment requires a real normal frame

There is a stronger condition if every intermediate depth is required:

Md=Yd⊂Yd+1⊂⋯⊂YD=XD.(8) M^d=Y_d\subset Y_{d+1}\subset\cdots\subset Y_D=X^D. \tag{8} Md=Yd​⊂Yd+1​⊂⋯⊂YD​=XD.(8)

Restrict each successive normal line to MMM. Their sum is NMXN_MXNM​X. If H1(M;Z2)=0H^1(M;\mathbb Z_2)=0H1(M;Z2​)=0, each line is trivial, so

A full chain (8) implies NMX≅1D−d as a real bundle.(9) \boxed{\text{A full chain (8) implies }N_MX\cong \mathbf1^{D-d}\text{ as a real bundle}.} \tag{9} A full chain (8) implies NM​X≅1D−d as a real bundle.​(9)

Conversely, a real normal frame and a tubular neighborhood give intermediate images of MMM times coordinate balls of every dimension. This supplies smooth intermediate submanifolds near MMM; it does not require them to be closed or geodesically complete, or turn the whole ambient space into a product. The criterion concerns existence of the stated embeddings, not their dynamics or physical selection.

For the standard projective inclusion with a≥2a\ge2a≥2, p1(NR)=qh2p_1(N_{\mathbb R})=qh^2p1​(NR​)=qh2 already contradicts (9), including when q>aq>aq>a and the top Euler class vanishes for dimensional reasons. Vanishing of the Euler obstruction alone is not sufficient.

A constructive control where intermediate spaces do exist

The lower-dimensional exception is useful. For CP1⊂CP3\mathrm{CP}^1\subset\mathrm{CP}^3CP1⊂CP3, the real normal bundle underlying O(1)⊕O(1)\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O(1)O(1)⊕O(1) has an explicit global orthonormal frame, despite being nontrivial as a complex bundle.

Consequences for the coupling construction

The complete CP1 normal-frame criterion

The same construction classifies the standard inclusions with lower space CP1\mathrm{CP}^1CP1. Their normal bundle is O(1)⊗Cq\mathcal O(1)\otimes\mathbb C^qO(1)⊗Cq: here qqq is complex codimension, and the ordinary real normal dimension is 2q2q2q. For q=2mq=2mq=2m, group its complex lines into mmm pairs. Each pair has the real frame (12)–(13), so their direct sum gives a global real frame of rank 2q2q2q:

NR≅⨁j=1m(O(1)⊕O(1))R≅12q.(15) N_{\mathbb R}\cong \bigoplus_{j=1}^{m} \left(\mathcal O(1)\oplus\mathcal O(1)\right)_{\mathbb R} \cong\mathbf1^{2q}. \tag{15} NR​≅j=1⨁m​(O(1)⊕O(1))R​≅12q.(15)

For odd qqq, triviality is obstructed. The equatorial transition of O(1)\mathcal O(1)O(1) over CP1≅S2\mathrm{CP}^1\cong S^2CP1≅S2 is one full rotation of its real two-plane. For qqq copies, the lift of this transition loop to Spin⁡(2q)\operatorname{Spin}(2q)Spin(2q) ends at (−1)q=−1(-1)^q=-1(−1)q=−1: each plane contributes its spin half-angle sign. It is therefore not a contractible loop in SO(2q)SO(2q)SO(2q), whereas a trivial real bundle has a contractible clutching loop. Equivalently its second Stiefel–Whitney class is w2(NR)=qh mod 2≠0w_2(N_{\mathbb R})=qh\bmod2\ne0w2​(NR​)=qhmod2=0. Together with (15), this proves

CP1⊂CP1+q has a trivial real normal bundle exactly when q is even.(16) \boxed{\mathrm{CP}^1\subset\mathrm{CP}^{1+q} \text{ has a trivial real normal bundle exactly when }q\text{ is even}.} \tag{16} CP1⊂CP1+q has a trivial real normal bundle exactly when q is even.​(16)

Equation (9) and the tubular construction then give the corresponding criterion for a full sequence of intermediate depths near CP1. In particular, CP1 inside CP5 permits such tubular intermediate spaces; it does not make its depth-six intermediate a global CP3. The full CP3-to-CP5 obstruction above remains unchanged. These existence statements neither select physical sectors nor establish completeness of the tubular intermediate spaces.

Keep the actual nesting hypotheses

The standard CP3-to-CP5 pair remains a valid partial inclusion with its computed normal connection and reciprocal specified kernel. It cannot simultaneously be declared the complete spatial chain through all the intervening depths. If only selected depths are retained in a calculation, its scope must say so.

The obstruction at 2→3→42\to3\to42→3→4 and at 4→5→64\to5\to64→5→6 directly affects the alternating sphere/projective candidate list. Merely replacing the odd-dimensional sphere by another smooth odd-dimensional manifold does not fix a standard projective inclusion on either side. Compatible geometries and embeddings need a new construction.

The parallel two-state normal spinor factor in the six-to-ten branching also does not provide a real normal frame. For that inclusion, Σ−N\Sigma^-NΣ−N is a trivial complex doublet while e(NR)=h2≠0e(N_{\mathbb R})=h^2\ne0e(NR​)=h2=0. Spinor components and ordinary spatial normal directions are different bundles.

On a contractible spatial chart these global obstructions disappear. Using such a chart is a different domain from the entire projective space and needs its own field domain, boundary conditions and normalization. Local mode counting by itself supplies neither the missing global embeddings nor a physical geometry.

Verification

projective_nesting_checks.py runs 1,385 checks. Exact tangent-bundle quotients and real complexification recover the characteristic classes independently. Finite differences of the actual normal connection and improper spatial curvature integrals verify the primitive characteristic numbers and their independence of RRR. The code also checks the global phase covariance, real orthonormality, explicit inverse and induced metric of the constructive tube.

The topological conclusions rely on the proofs above and the cited bundle theorems. A finite scan of dimensions or integer degrees is a consistency check, not a substitute for the all-degree argument. No empirical geometry or particle coupling is identified by this result.
