3. Electron-electron scattering is therefore not the same as electron-electron full-depth contact

Scattering can result from the interaction of extended tails at nonzero centre separation. No additional mechanism is needed merely to allow interaction before the centres meet.

A calculation must predict the changes in the two patterns' motion, orientation and field structure from their common dynamics. It must retain causal propagation of changes, energy and momentum balance, and the response of both patterns.

The photon's assigned depth does not by itself specify the dimensionality of the electron-electron force or a projection used by a detector. An electromagnetic reduction remains open until derived. An additional excitation or restructuring process must likewise follow from the actual field evolution; it is not defined by imposing exact centre coincidence.
