Profiles on ordinary spatial coordinates

Berry connection and oscillator width

With convention A=−i⟨ψ,dψ⟩\mathcal A=-i\langle\psi,d\psi\rangleA=−i⟨ψ,dψ⟩, the connection vanishes throughout this globally positive real family. Consequently its Berry curvature dAd\mathcal AdA is zero. The metric is nonzero because neighboring normalized profiles differ even when their relative phase is fixed. Multiplying every profile by a smooth parameter-dependent common phase changes the connection by an exact differential and leaves both metric and curvature unchanged.

For the ground state of H=−Δ+λ∣x∣2H=-\Delta+\lambda|x|^2H=−Δ+λ∣x∣2, Σ=I/(2λ)\Sigma=I/(2\sqrt\lambda)Σ=I/(2λ​). At fixed center,

Σ−1dΣ=−dλ2λI,gλλ=d32λ2. \Sigma^{-1}d\Sigma=-\frac{d\lambda}{2\lambda}I, \qquad g_{\lambda\lambda}=\frac{d}{32\lambda^2}. Σ−1dΣ=−2λdλ​I,gλλ​=32λ2d​.

If a specified self-consistency model imposes λ=(g/2)2/3\lambda=(g/2)^{2/3}λ=(g/2)2/3 with g>0g>0g>0, the chain rule gives

ggg=d72g2=d288λ3. g_{gg}=\frac{d}{72g^2}=\frac{d}{288\lambda^3}. ggg​=72g2d​=288λ3d​.

This is a metric on the chosen family of normalized profiles. A kinetic term for time-dependent width or a physical response coefficient must be derived from the action and its other modes.
