The complete shape source and surface-branch balance

Updated September 14, 2026

conditional calculation

The two surface-sign branches can cancel some geometric forces, but they cannot cancel the complete source of the symmetric linked carrier. At all maintained depths there is an even-order deformation driven with the same sign by both positive-frequency branches. The proposed geometric cost has zero first derivative in that direction.

This is a necessary source-balance test of a specific trial configuration. It does not exclude other shapes, other sustaining motion or the broader IDWT proposal. A source coefficient is not a signed particle inertia. The surface signs are not assigned a particle–antiparticle interpretation.

What the source-balance test excludes

The ordinary round tube has weight J=(1+y1/R)d−2J=(1+y_1/R)^{d-2}J=(1+y1​/R)d−2. Its restricted geometric first derivative vanishes whenever k>d−2k>d-2k>d−2. In particular,

Thus no mixture of these positive-frequency surface branches balances this direction at the symmetric profile by adding a scalar multiple of GJG_JGJ​ and a radius-only supporting term. The geometric integral is understood in the same localized full-plane tube approximation as the curvature calculation; a finite-tube boundary supplies its separately bounded tail correction. Equation (12) is exact for that declared trial functional, rather than a claim about an unspecified boundary force.

The geometric Jacobi carrier changes the hopping integrals and removes the reflection symmetry between their two ends. It nevertheless retains negative endpoint coefficients and two negative penultimate entries in the verified controls. Its largest even source remains positive on both surface signs. The common-operator simplification therefore does not itself solve the occupied geometry.

The missing step is a complete same-field equilibrium or relative motion with its energy, momentum and canonical normalization retained. This calculation identifies a definite force that such a construction must supply; it does not assign a negative inertia or infer a neutrino rate.
