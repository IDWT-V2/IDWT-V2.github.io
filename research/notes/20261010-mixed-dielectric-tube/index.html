Geometric Flux Guides Survive a Quadratic Dielectric Correction

Updated October 12, 2026

The finite-width commuting flux result does not require the dielectric to be exactly quartic. Adding a positive quadratic deformation term preserves localized guides above a calculable flux threshold. One action gives different onset behavior at the two active occupied depths: the depth-three guide widens continuously at threshold, whereas depth four has a finite-width binding transition and a metastable branch.

The result concerns the neutral deformation and independently varied connection action. It does not restore full non-Abelian confinement or resolve its zero-dielectric vacuum ambiguity. Its geometric value is a robustness test of the conditional three/four guide mechanism, including all scalar shapes rather than only a chosen radial family.
