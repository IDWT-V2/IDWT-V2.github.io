Chirality gives a channel zero, not a depth-five exception

At odd total spatial dimension, the kinetic involution commutes with every massless PpP_pPp​ and anticommutes with β\betaβ. Its positive-frequency eigenspaces each have dimension n/4n/4n/4. Writing the restricted involutions as CpkinC_p^{\rm kin}Cpkin​ and CqkinC_q^{\rm kin}Cqkin​ gives

Thus the scalar vertex vanishes between two equal kinetic chiralities for every pair of momenta. Between opposite chiralities, its singular strength is still τ\sqrt\tauτ​. The complete vertex remains consistent with (5): its nonzero response is in the opposite output block.

This occurs at every odd total spatial dimension, including three, five, seven and nine. It supplies no unique depth-five selection and no electric-charge assignment. The neutrino control continues to require a derived neutral electromagnetic channel and surviving weak vertices. Both kinetic signs refer to components of the same field; their particle-antiparticle meaning remains open.

If a field also propagates through containing spatial directions, use the complete containing kinetic operator. A lower odd-dimensional involution anticommutes with every additional normal αj\alpha_jαj​. The lower class can therefore mix under normal motion. The corresponding protection question is treated explicitly in spatially invariant scalar-null fields.

Verification

scalar_wave_packets.py implements (12) on a full three-dimensional Cartesian grid with all four complex components. Its incoming Fourier Gaussian is projected onto the complete positive-frequency band once, then evolved with the exact local reaction and exact free Fourier propagator in a symmetric time splitting. The numerical periodic box is a regulator for localized ordinary spatial fields, not a physical compactification.

Axial spatial Dirac packets retain transverse localization and complete complex polarization vectors in an exact angular family at odd total spatial dimension. Their radial and axial equation comes from the same local scalar, with both patterns evolving and the full angular spinor dependence reconstructed explicitly.

A right-angle cancellation for one polarization per selected chiral band is removed by allowed complete higher-dimensional vectors; counting the components cannot determine that contraction.
