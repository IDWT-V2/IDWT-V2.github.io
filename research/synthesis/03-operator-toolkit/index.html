An actual spatial band has the simplex count

On physical CP3\mathbb{CP}^3CP3 with the specified line connection O(3)\mathcal O(3)O(3), retain every zero-form spatial spectral shell from 000 through N=n−1N=n-1N=n−1. The complete band has dimension

Its physical point evaluation in the zero-form polarization is rank one and has nonzero eigenvalue DN/V\mathscr D_N/\mathcal VDN​/V, where V\mathcal VV is the physical volume. Thus a specified point contact has an exact simplex coefficient with a single factor of twenty independent of the band.

This is a different space of spatial fields from the holomorphic tangent-mode band: the line degree here stays fixed while nonholomorphic spatial harmonics are added. It also uses a different connection from the O(2)\mathcal O(2)O(2) derivative polynomial. The two calculations should not be combined without a map between their bundles and actions.

The count and evaluation operator can be derived. A smooth local scalar current has not thereby become a point insertion. The explicit reproducing state below has a different self-contact expectation and drives complementary components.

The geometry is conditional actual CP3 with ordinary macroscopic spatial directions, no compactification and unknown FS scale RRR. The calculations at CP5 below use a separately specified actual ten-dimensional geometry. Line and spectral labels are field data, not additional physical dimensions or charge assignments.
