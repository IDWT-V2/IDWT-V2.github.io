The remaining physical bridge

A successful scale construction must connect the declared count clocks, class-dependent units, actual spatial gradients, reciprocal coupling and complete energy normalization in one action. It must retain the neutrino calibration freedom until it actually resolves it, and it must account for bottom's two-rung count and the common lepton unit across depths six and ten. They do not merge different candidate actions or turn a radius fit, mode norm or supporting-response coefficient into a completed mass operator.
