Composite and hadronic mass

Updated November 5, 2026

conditional synthesis

A composite's complete rest energy can differ substantially from the sum of its constituents' isolated rest energies. Its field also contains relative motion, supporting strain, interaction energy and extended tails. IDWT seeks those contributions from the same field law that sustains the constituent patterns and governs their reciprocal encounters. It does not introduce gluons, other mediator particles or a separate exchange-force mechanism.

The measurable target is the complete composite rest frequency, equivalently its mass. A decomposition into constituent terms is useful after the complete energy is derived, but an allocated residual is not automatically another observed oscillator frequency. No IDWT proton or neutron field solution or complete baryon-frequency prediction has yet been established.

Physical spacetime has ten ordinary spatial directions plus time. The depth-three and depth-four quark assignments classify involvement of that common space. A three-body configuration space, color tensor and mode-coefficient space are mathematical descriptions, not additional physical manifolds or substitutes for those assignments. Internal hadronic fields do not replace the electron's literal deterministic external motion around a nucleus.
