conditional synthesis

IDWT proposes that particle patterns of one field have different dimensional involvement in one spacetime with ten ordinary spatial directions plus time. Its maintained depths are two, three, four, five, six and ten. No additional physical manifold, compactification or particle-exchange force is used. A physical identification must connect a proposed field pattern to measured frequencies, inertia, charge, spin, response and detection, with the same action and normalization throughout.

The present evidence combines a constrained numerical mass structure, conditional dimensional discriminators and explicit higher-dimensional field constructions. These have different evidential roles. A result working at six and ten can support a shared lepton property without choosing either depth. A selector must exclude alternatives under the same stated rule. A fitted formula can be useful without being a forward derivation. This volume collects the current comparisons and the remaining mathematical bridges; it does not claim the physical mass operator is complete.

7. Inertia, causality and persistence are separate requirements

Positive total inertia concerns the complete particle and supporting field, not the signed momentum response of a wave in prescribed coefficients. For a genuinely isolated positive-energy solution with the appropriate full stress balance and covariant dynamics, its boost response should reproduce the corresponding complete energy–momentum relation. A partial response of opposite sign may be real mathematics, but it does not establish negative particle mass. A phase explanation of that response also needs phase-resolved complete dynamics.

The relevant kinetic form is the constrained one after actual algebraic equations and absent coordinates are treated. A rank-deficient unreduced matrix at an inactive edge is not automatically a ghost. Conversely, a normalization that divides by a vanishing gate coefficient can make a frozen matrix look regular without producing a bounded physical perturbation norm through the edge. The necessary checks include:

  • Positive time inertia on retained physical dynamical directions, complete spatial stiffness where required, and real complete characteristic roots.
  • Propagation in every physical direction and angle, with both signed cone comparisons and actual carrier polarizations; radial and transverse endpoint checks alone can miss mixed directions.
  • The actual solved transition rather than only a reference core, plus the changing-rank constraints and transmission conditions.
  • Complete raw-energy and charge-preserving comparisons rather than an unjustified identification of the principal action Hamiltonian with raw energy.
  • Lower-order spectrum and nonlinear evolution on a stated norm/domain, separately from frozen principal health.

The accepted local origin health theorem covers a short actual inner interval, not an entire determinant transition or endpoint-uniform nonlinear evolution. Local positive margins and exactly luminal Cartesian blocks are not a strictly positive all-block cone margin. A causal principal symbol also does not by itself establish nonlinear persistence or a measured lifetime.

Persistence must match the particle. The electron calls for comparatively persistent localized motion; a tau or muon may be a finite-lived resonance. A negative energy direction or growing linear mode is neither a universal rejection of unstable-particle candidates nor a derivation of their observed decay. Decay requires a timescale, available outgoing channels and complete conserved energy, momentum and charges. The known particle frequency remains the mass-frequency target; a slower shape disturbance must be named separately.

10. What would count as completion or further evidence

A physical mass-operator claim should provide one explicit action and a traceable chain from its geometry and retained modes to the following results:

  1. A spatial mechanism selecting the common-frequency portions and their maintained counts, with all sector units and action conversions identified as supplied, fitted or derived.
  2. An actual complete spatial state for each claimed branch, including sustaining stress, exterior and source-free matching; physical depth must follow from the state rather than coefficient rank alone.
  3. Complete energy and momentum for the actual action, all its relevant charges and positive total inertia, with normalization and finite-cut boundary work retained.
  4. Evolution and persistence appropriate to the branch, including causal/transmission domains and, for unstable particles, actual outgoing decay channels and timescales.
  5. Reciprocal unlike-particle encounters using each object's clock, polarization and response, with force/current/rate normalization and depth controls.
  6. The shared empirical properties of the identified particle: charge and conjugate, spin and detector interpretation, response strengths and any lifetime or branching comparison actually claimed.
  7. Further consequences that distinguish the law from other actions fitting the same masses, with a declared benchmark, candidate family, uncertainty and null procedure where statistical claims are made.

A full mass family need not precede every useful result. A rigorously constructed particle-like state, a selective geometric constraint or a new measured consequence under one coherent action can advance the theory without completing it. Reviewed core, exterior, finite shape-energy and probe-response results sharpen these targets. The new cluster law supplies a scoped mechanical construction with explicit premises; the covariant rigid embedding and spectral connectivity proposal retain their pending independent-review status. No unresolved result becomes accepted because it would close the chain.

The next explanatory success must preserve the useful common structure already obtained while supplying a missing physical bridge. Agreement with selected numbers, exact algebra and conditional spatial capability each contribute evidence in their own scope. None alone identifies the whole proposed theory with nature.
