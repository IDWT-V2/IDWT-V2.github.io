Physical premises and nested spatial sectors

Updated November 10, 2026

conditional synthesis

IDWT investigates particles as persistent or finite-lived structures of one field, Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​, in infinitely many ordinary spatial dimensions plus time. Matter occupies up to ten spatial dimensions; the remaining directions are unbound for every particle. Flat R10\mathbb R^{10}R10 is the spatial baseline for the ten-dimensional matter domain. All spatial patterns, frames and trajectories belong to this one spacetime.

“Ordinary” describes the physical kind of each direction: coordinates specify real positions, separations, gradients and motion. It does not mean that the containing space is three-dimensional. The familiar three dimensions belong to the observer's accessible view. The remaining directions are neither compactified nor replaced by a configuration space, a list of field components or a space of mode coefficients. IDWT has no Kaluza–Klein tower and no mediator-particle force mechanism. Interactions must arise from the dynamics of the same field.

A particle depth ddd names the ordinary spatial directions involved in its bound pattern. The layer or nested-submanifold picture remains an admissible description of this involvement in one shared manifold. It does not supply separate containing worlds, a confinement mechanism, a global metric or a fixed preferred orientation. Those physical structures must be constructed and tested. The spatial-geometry module distinguishes these premises from its conditional mathematical realizations.

What is spatial, and what describes a field?

Four objects must remain distinct:

A spatially extended field does not make a particle's position indeterminate. A squared field norm, a normalized coefficient share or an integration measure does not establish a Born position-probability law. Configuration space records several physical positions; events and forces still occur in the shared physical manifold.

Real amplitude and shape descriptions are the current convention. Complex notation may package two real quadratures or a rotation, but does not turn a coefficient ray into physical position. Older complex projective constructions retain additional mathematical assumptions. Their curvature, topology and finite-volume normalization are conditional comparisons; they are not established physical geometry.

The Dirac spinor is a working underlying-field proposal rather than a completed derivation or an irrevocable premise. Other classical field or spacetime constructions remain candidates when their effective Dirac behavior, localization, spin, frequency and interaction response are checked explicitly. A field-value metric is not automatically the metric of physical space.

Literal electron motion around a nucleus

The six-dimensional electron itself follows a literal deterministic trajectory around the nucleus. The orbit is external motion through surrounding physical space; it is not motion inside the electron and is not replaced by a stationary orbital wavefunction or probability cloud. The existing orbit-plus-precession construction is the baseline. Its position q(t)q(t)q(t), the electron's six-dimensional internal field pattern and a detector's accumulated image are separate descriptions.

The orbit is calculated in its four directions. It is not a three-dimensional Bohr or Kepler orbit with a fourth coordinate added. The electron's internal motion does not affect the orbit, and a spinor rotation belongs to the electron's own six-dimensional pattern, never to its external path. The light of a spectral line has the frequency of the orbit's precession, that is, of its motion along the fourth direction; it does not have the in-plane orbital frequency. In a molecule the electron's orbit goes around all the nuclei involved, and multiple bonds are electrons on equal orbits kept apart by phase. Volume 18 gives the calculations.

The neutron's charge is negated completely, in all four directions. Its opposite quark charges spread around one circle cancel in the three observed directions; the up quark's pull along the fourth direction would remain unless the negation is complete, and the deuterium line shows that it is complete to better than a part per million.

Free extension ends at contact

A free extension is uniform over its span and ends where it makes contact. It is not infinite, so the divergent-energy question of the infinite cylinder does not arise for it. Nothing travels along it: the object is already spread across the distance, so a connection through a free direction is already in place. This premise supplies the span, and the tension TTT of a span is calculated in volume 12.

A pattern that is uniform along a free direction and also vibrates with its bound profile is linearly unstable to necking at every depth below ten that has been tested (volume 11). That calculation copies the vibrating profile along the free direction. It says nothing yet about a contact-ended or non-vibrating free extension, whose own stability is open.

The fourth direction as the path of alpha decay

Alpha decay needs no tunnelling and no imaginary time. The fourth direction is the natural path for that motion and the equation works the same way: for a field steady in time, a real spatial direction enters exactly where imaginary time enters the scalar equation. The alpha's down quarks are already spread along the fourth direction, so only its up quarks have a position to change there. The prediction is faster alpha decay with no electrons present. Photon geometry and phase transfer

The photon is the explicit depth-two transverse control. Its two bound oscillation directions are distinct from its free directions in the shared higher-dimensional space. Linear, circular and elliptical polarization are physical spatial patterns to compare; the existence of an abstract two-state space is not a derivation of those patterns. The photon's massless case need not impose its transversality law on every higher-depth massive pattern.

The current spanning picture treats a photon as one source-to-target connection, with its line-like, spreading structure already present along its span and phase advancing through it. Observed timing is to be explained through phase accumulation, rather than the transit of a point photon. This is a physical hypothesis requiring a same-field derivation. A phase difference at a single frequency does not determine a delay; the frequency dependence and the full energy, stress, recoil and detection balance must be calculated.

Where the connection ends is likewise fixed by the field's actual configuration, not by an added probability law or a race of partially filled absorbers. The field's real configuration, relative phases and spatial suitability must supply that endpoint and the subsequent reconfiguration. Geometric coverage or a nonzero tail alone does not establish the required transfer channel, instantaneous completion or observed causal timing. The photon module develops the polarization comparison under its explicit assumptions.
