Physical premises and nested spatial sectors

Updated November 1, 2026

conditional synthesis

IDWT investigates particles as persistent or finite-lived structures of one field, Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​, in ten ordinary spatial dimensions plus time. Flat R10\mathbb R^{10}R10 is the spatial baseline. The symbol ∞\infty∞ in the field's name does not add physical directions. All spatial patterns, frames and trajectories belong to this one spacetime.

“Ordinary” describes the physical kind of each direction: coordinates specify real positions, separations, gradients and motion. It does not mean that the containing space is three-dimensional. The familiar three dimensions belong to the observer's accessible view. The remaining directions are neither compactified nor replaced by a configuration space, a list of field components or a space of mode coefficients. IDWT has no Kaluza–Klein tower and no mediator-particle force mechanism. Interactions must arise from the dynamics of the same field.

A particle depth ddd names the ordinary spatial directions involved in its bound pattern. The layer or nested-submanifold picture remains an admissible description of this involvement in one shared manifold. It does not supply separate containing worlds, a confinement mechanism, a global metric or a fixed preferred orientation. Those physical structures must be constructed and tested. The spatial-geometry module distinguishes these premises from its conditional mathematical realizations.

What is spatial, and what describes a field?

Four objects must remain distinct:

A spatially extended field does not make a particle's position indeterminate. A squared field norm, a normalized coefficient share or an integration measure does not establish a Born position-probability law. Configuration space records several physical positions; events and forces still occur in the shared physical manifold.

Real amplitude and shape descriptions are the current convention. Complex notation may package two real quadratures or a rotation, but does not turn a coefficient ray into physical position. Older complex projective constructions retain additional mathematical assumptions. Their curvature, topology and finite-volume normalization are conditional comparisons; they are not established physical geometry. In particular, the current electron premise does not require that its physical space be CP3\mathbb{CP}^3CP3.

The Dirac spinor is a working underlying-field proposal rather than a completed derivation or an irrevocable premise. Other classical field or spacetime constructions remain candidates when their effective Dirac behavior, localization, spin, frequency and interaction response are checked explicitly. A field-value metric is not automatically the metric of physical space.

Photon geometry and phase transfer

The photon is the explicit depth-two transverse control. Its two bound oscillation directions are distinct from its free directions in the shared higher-dimensional space. Linear, circular and elliptical polarization are physical spatial patterns to compare; the existence of an abstract two-state space is not a derivation of those patterns. The photon's massless case need not impose its transversality law on every higher-depth massive pattern.

The current spanning picture treats a photon as one source-to-target connection, with its line-like, spreading structure already present along its span and phase advancing through it. Observed timing is to be explained through phase accumulation, rather than the transit of a point photon. This is a physical hypothesis requiring a same-field derivation. A phase difference at a single frequency does not determine a delay; the frequency dependence and the full energy, stress, recoil and detection balance must be calculated.

Deterministic selection of the best-matched target is likewise a proposed field consequence, not an added probability law or a race of partially filled absorbers. The field's real configuration, relative phases and spatial suitability must supply that selection and the subsequent reconfiguration. Geometric coverage or a nonzero tail alone does not establish the required transfer channel, instantaneous completion or observed causal timing. The photon module develops the polarization comparison under its explicit assumptions.
