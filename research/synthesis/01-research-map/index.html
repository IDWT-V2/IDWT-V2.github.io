This twenty-volume set collects the useful physical picture, equations, numerical comparisons and conditional constructions. Physical meaning of the dimensions

The physical setting has infinitely many ordinary spatial dimensions plus time. Matter occupies up to ten spatial dimensions; every remaining direction is unbound for every particle. The spatial baseline is flat. “Ordinary” describes the kind of direction, not a claim that the containing space has only the familiar three. Lower-dimensional particle patterns may be described as confined to nested layers or submanifolds of this same higher-dimensional space. Their confinement must be established by the dynamics. An electron is not a constituent contained inside a tau.

The assigned depths are two, three, four, five, six and ten. In a direction where a pattern is uniformly extended, translation leaves it unchanged; its distinguishable center moves where its structure varies. A pattern can change its orientation relative to another pattern without rotating an entire containing space. A coefficient-space sphere, a mode index or a classical field component is not an extra physical spatial direction.

The electron itself follows a literal deterministic trajectory in six spatial dimensions around a nucleus. Physical position, internal classical field structure, mode coefficients and time-averaged detector registration must remain distinct.

There are no mediator particles or particle-exchange force mechanisms in IDWT. Interactions must follow from the single field's dynamics. No compactification or Kaluza–Klein tower is assumed. Conventional equations may be used as attributed mathematical or empirical comparisons; their physical interpretation does not thereby become an IDWT premise.

Interactions, photons and literal binding

The proposed interaction is compatibility and reciprocal deformation of actual spatial patterns in the same field. Both participants carry orientation, polarization and phase. Unlike particles begin with their own resonance frequencies; a common imposed frequency is only a control. Phase locking, shifts or sidebands must follow from the interaction. Overlap by itself gives neither a calibrated force nor repulsion. A response coefficient, a transition vertex and an observed encounter rate are different quantities.

The working photon picture is an extended transverse connection present along its span, with advancing phase. The proposed deterministic completed transfer selects the uniquely best-matched target under the actual field configuration. This is not an independently traveling point object or a race of partial energy loads. The same-field geometry, energy and recoil, selection rule and observed timing still need calculation. A phase shift at one frequency does not itself establish a delay; its frequency dependence and the observable transfer process matter.
