The maintained mass-frequency rule uses the cumulative simplex count, interpreted in the current physical proposal as the number of portions sharing one fundamental. The unit is shared within a kind of pattern; the electron, muon and tau share it across depths six and ten. Its numerical restrictions are substantial, but they answer a different question from the field dynamics that must sustain the patterns. Depth describes a pattern's involvement in ordinary physical spatial directions. Different dimensional layers can belong to this one nested manifold. A coefficient count is not itself proof of confinement to a layer. No compactification, additional configuration-space dimensions or mediator particles enter the count law.
