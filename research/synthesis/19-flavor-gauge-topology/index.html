Flavor, charge and gauge geometry

Updated October 18, 2026

conditional synthesis

The mass-count assignments constrain eigenvalues. They do not by themselves select electric charge, color, flavor mixing or a physical gauge response. Those properties require field carriers, normalized operators, occupied states and interactions. Several exact geometric constructions now make the missing requirements concrete.

All physical dimensions here are ordinary spatial directions in one shared higher-dimensional manifold, with at least ten directions and an unknown total dimension. Particles may occupy nested dimensional layers; the dynamics confining them remain open. Complex vector spaces below describe paired real components, compatible normal-frame structures or mathematical bundles. They do not replace literal physical position. Projective global geometry is an additional conditional premise wherever used, not compactified physical space.

IDWT has no mediator particles or particle-exchange forces. Conventional color, charge and CKM labels identify empirical comparison targets. A connection or effective transition block must ultimately follow from the dynamics and mode reduction of the one field Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​.

The combined physical requirement

The geometry is promising because some common structures constrain more than one property: the same six-normal carrier relates charge weights and framed planes, the same Hodge rank distinguishes the quark depths, and cumulative counts suggest a normalized mixing comparison. These connections are conditional, not statistically independent proofs of the particle assignments.

A complete same-field construction must select the relevant carriers, generate the connection and its physical sources, fix charges and couplings after canonical normalization, and retain the correct occupied mass and flavor states. It must explain why the quark response differs from higher-depth responses while preserving shared lepton properties at six and ten. The exact geometric tables, available channels and fitted mixing targets supply constraints for that task; they do not complete it.
