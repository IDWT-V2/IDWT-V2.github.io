Photon geometry and physical polarization

Updated November 1, 2026

conditional synthesis

IDWT's working photon picture is an extended transverse connection between material patterns of the single field Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​. It is present along its span, with an advancing phase. The proposed photon is not a point object traveling from source to target. Its two bound spatial directions, its extension through other directions and its completed energy-transfer event are separate parts of the hypothesis.

This volume develops the geometric and algebraic results available for that picture. A transverse plane gives explicit polarization mathematics; it does not by itself derive a finite connection, deterministic target selection, observed timing, energy transfer or protected zero rest mass. The earlier separated-wave, Maxwell and finite-matrix calculations remain useful conditional comparisons, with their additional premises stated.

The connection and the event

The proposed connection spreads transversely in a line-like wedge or triangle-shaped structure. Its bound plane belongs to the same shared higher-dimensional physical space as both endpoint patterns. “Free without” refers to its extension along a direction outside that bound plane. It does not compactify a dimension or introduce a mediator particle. The physical spacetime has ten ordinary spatial dimensions plus time.

In the proposed deterministic event, the actual field configuration has one best-matched target. Completing the transfer changes the configuration for later events. Successive connections can differ in their origin, orientation, phase and polarization. Their apparent statistics are to come from these unmeasured real-valued conditions, not a postulated position-probability law. This is not a race in which several absorbers accumulate partial loads until one fills first. Geometry favors closer suitable targets over otherwise equivalent distant ones; the actual selection and its quantitative distance dependence still require the same-field equations.

The working interpretation assigns one connection to one target, so more light means more connections. It associates the large resonant target area with the extended photon's scale rather than the dense atom's size. Contact phase matters: a crest or trough is proposed to engage differently from a zero crossing. These are physical hypotheses to be tested, not results of the projector calculations below.

The proposed completed transfer may have zero duration when the relevant process occurs entirely through the patterns' free spatial directions. The same hypothesis can apply to other entirely free-direction links; it does not assert that changes of bound profiles or bound-direction energy transport are instantaneous. Contact preparation, selection, phase accumulation, subsequent bound response and observed timing must be distinguished. The actual equations must still satisfy conservation and observed signalling constraints. Neither a nonzero field tail nor a static overlap proves that a photon event occurs. A coherent model must account for endpoint energy, supporting stress, recoil and the full field balance. It cannot assume those quantities vanish because no traveling particle is introduced.
