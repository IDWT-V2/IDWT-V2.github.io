Updated November 10, 2026

conditional synthesis

Neutrinos test both parts of the IDWT proposal: the count-frequency relation and the compatibility of spatial patterns. The maintained mass states occupy depth five, meaning five ordinary physical spatial directions within the single ten-space-plus-time spacetime. A depth label does not establish their support size, occupied polarization, electric generator or weak response. The target is channel-dependent. A neutrino has zero ordinary electric charge while weak interactions remain nonzero. Rare encounters are not complete decoupling, and a small mass does not determine a scattering rate. IDWT must derive those distinctions from the same field Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​, without mediator particles, compactification or an arbitrary small coefficient attached to the entire depth-five row.

Capture and the layers of the charged leptons

The two members of a weak doublet differ by one bound direction, and a pattern of depth ddd is a stack of patterns of depth d−1d-1d−1 (volume 5). A weak transition is a pattern gaining or losing that direction: it becomes the pattern it is a layer of, or it drops onto one of its own layers. The shapes decide which transitions exist. The masses decide which way they run.

The first neutrino, with 10 levels, is the tenth layer of the 13-level electron. The second and third, with 15 and 22 levels, are larger than the electron and are not among its layers. What a neutrino realization still has to establish

A viable spatial neutrino calculation must identify the actual occupied mass-state pattern and derive its support, persistence, complete energy/momentum and inertial response. It must identify the ordinary electric and color generators, retain normalized nonzero weak channels, and connect mass-state/flavor preparation to those channels. A neutrino may have an extended or differently localized field structure. That possibility must be assessed through its same-field dynamics. Tails can carry energy and stress; their overlap alone does not establish a photon connection or its timing. The available results therefore provide a concrete count constraint and several channel-specific tests.
