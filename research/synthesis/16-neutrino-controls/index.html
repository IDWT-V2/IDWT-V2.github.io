Neutrino frequency and interaction controls

Updated November 5, 2026

conditional synthesis

Neutrinos test both parts of the IDWT proposal: the count-frequency relation and the compatibility of spatial patterns. The maintained mass states occupy depth five, meaning five ordinary physical spatial directions within the single ten-space-plus-time spacetime. A depth label does not establish their support size, occupied polarization, electric generator or weak response. Their physical field support may differ greatly from that of other particles; the extent of an internal field structure must also remain distinct from the extent of a prepared wave packet.

The target is channel-dependent. A neutrino has zero ordinary electric charge while weak interactions remain nonzero. Rare encounters are not complete decoupling, and a small mass does not determine a scattering rate. IDWT must derive those distinctions from the same field Ψ∞\Psi_\inftyΨ∞​, without mediator particles, compactification or an arbitrary small coefficient attached to the entire depth-five row.

What a neutrino realization still has to establish

A viable spatial neutrino calculation must identify the actual occupied mass-state pattern and derive its support, persistence, complete energy/momentum and inertial response. It must identify the ordinary electric and color generators, retain normalized nonzero weak channels, and connect mass-state/flavor preparation to those channels. Its encounter rate then requires actual incident modes, relative phases/orientations, target occupation, spectrum and flux. A tiny static overlap is not yet a rare event rate, and a narrow resonance can respond strongly even to a small vertex.

A neutrino may have an extended or differently localized field structure. That possibility must be assessed through its same-field dynamics rather than removed by imposing a compact trial profile or chosen absolute mass. Tails can carry energy and stress; their overlap alone does not establish a photon connection or its timing. The proposed photon phase-transfer picture and literal deterministic electron trajectories remain separate physical hypotheses, rather than interpretations silently supplied by these scalar/Dirac diagnostic modes.

The available results therefore provide a concrete count constraint and several channel-specific tests. They preserve electric neutrality without complete decoupling as the target, but do not derive the full physical neutrino, its mixing matrix, measured scattering rate or a completed common mass operator.
