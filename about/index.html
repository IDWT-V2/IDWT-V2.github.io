About This Reading Edition

This site presents IDWT V2 as connected research pages. Its physical proposal is one shared higher-dimensional space with at least ten spatial dimensions. Particles occupy layers of different dimensionality within it, described by nested submanifolds. The dimensions are physical spatial directions of the same kind as the familiar three. The full space is not restricted to our three-dimensional view. Whether its total dimension is ten, a larger finite number or infinite remains open.

The introduction explains that picture. The sector case connects the supporting mathematics and empirical comparisons; the research status records current results and limitations. Earlier curved constructions remain conditional on their stated geometry.

Written by Fedge No with the contributors acknowledged in the source material.
