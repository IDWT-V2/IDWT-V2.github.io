About this reading edition

This site presents the IDWT V2 modules and research record as connected reading pages. The physical premise is one infinite-dimensional manifold with all sectors nested within it. The electron occupies six macroscopic spatial dimensions; CP3 denotes its proposed physical submanifold. Nuclear 3+3 locking constrains ordinary matter and observers to an experienced three-dimensional sector. The modules develop this construction; the notes preserve calculations, corrections, and open problems.

The research status is the starting point for interpreting the record. Historical excerpts retain their provenance and should be read with the audits that cite them.
